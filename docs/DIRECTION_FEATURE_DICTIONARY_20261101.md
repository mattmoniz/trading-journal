## Cue A: delta divergence at a new extreme (DF, our reading)
Series: the 500-contract bars the tell uses (per-bar delta = ask-side minus bid-side volume, already in the session files).
Bullish DF at bar t: low[t] < min(low over the REFERENCE window of prior bars) AND delta[t] > 0 AND (optional reversal filter) close[t] > open[t]. Bearish DF mirrored (high above the reference, delta < 0, close < open).
OPEN, frozen in the prereg and fixed from existing numbers, no search: REFERENCE window N in {10, 20} bars (the tell's own FLOW_N = 10 and its stride window 20); filter on/off. Four variants at most, all reported. Variant DF-D: delta thresholded at zero exactly as described (no new cut).
Use at a tell fire: DF direction within the last M bars before the fire (M = 10, the tell's FLOW_N); a bullish DF votes LONG, bearish SHORT. Questions: does the DF side predict the first-break side / signed 15 and 60-minute move better than the tell's own side, and do tell-side and DF-side disagree in a way that matters.

## Cue B: absorbed / defended area (the repo absorption studies)
Definition source: `scripts/amt_absorb/absorb.py` (persistent absorption marks: the pressing side keeps pushing with volume at or above its trailing median, both halves of the window net the pressing side, no reward in the last half, at least two tries, then confirmation; "strong" via the dominance test) and `scripts/amt_absorb/shelf.py` (a 25-bar compression at or under the 25th percentile with at least two tries, merged), plus the shelf path study `scripts/amt_marks_path/run_shelf_path.py`. Caveats that must be carried: these detectors use 1-MINUTE RTH bars only (09:30-16:00, no Globex; thresholds are quantiles of the prior 20 sessions' same clock bar), the tell uses 500-contract bars (join by timestamp, prefix-safe, with the parity harness); the detector thresholds were adjusted by eye on explore sessions during the trader's chart reviews (in-sample for the explore window); earlier tests of the absorption marks as entries were NOT SHOWN (`PREREG.md` results in `scripts/amt_absorb`, `run_shelf_path.py`, claims in the ledger), so this cue is tested as a SIDE CHOOSER at tell fires, not as an entry.
Feature at a tell fire (OPEN until the dictionary is frozen): the most recent confirmed absorption mark or shelf zone within the prior 60 minutes whose price lies on one side of the current price; direction rule: absorbed selling below price (support) votes LONG, absorbed buying above price votes SHORT; distance to the zone in ATR20 units as the strength measure.
