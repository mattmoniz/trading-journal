# Pre-registration: a layered, probability-based forecast of the next day (balance assumed until the evidence says otherwise) (written 2026-11-04 BEFORE any feature was built or any outcome read)

STATUS: FROZEN. User (2026-11-04): assume the day is in BALANCE until the watcher or the forecast says otherwise; study higher-level patterns and work them into intraday decisions; "had the market fallen a lot, is it in balance now, maybe it is getting ready to fill the single prints it left behind"; consider recent bigger and smaller picture; cumulative buying and selling; probabilities. Research only, nothing live. Explore window first (522 sessions, features from the 500-contract bars and 1-minute RTH bars); ONE hold-out look only if the lead rule below is met. Ledger family `daytype_forecast_20261104`, counted tests 4.
## 1. Targets (final day, defined on the day's own initial balance, TPO-doc day types)
IB = RTH high / low of the first hour; extension up / down in IBR units at the close. IMBALANCE DAY = total extension > 1.0 IBR (the range at least doubled the IB: the doc's variation-large / trend); BALANCE DAY = total extension <= 1.0 IBR. (Conventions, untuned; base rates printed first.) RANGE RATIO = RTH range / the median RTH range of the prior 20 sessions.
## 2. Features (all causal; scales from the prior 20 sessions; ADR = prior-20 median RTH range)
BIG PICTURE: 5-day and 20-day net change of the RTH close and the 20-day range position of yesterday's close (ADR units); streak of consecutive sessions with overlapping value (prior value areas overlapping); 5-day sum of RTH delta (ask-side minus bid-side volume from the bar files) divided by the 5-day volume; distance (ADR units, signed) to the nearest UNFILLED unfinished business of the last 5 sessions (single-print zone edge or poor high / low from the TradingView TPO rules in `carried_levels.py`; unfilled = not traded through since it was made).
PRIOR DAY AND OVERNIGHT: prior-day value-area midpoint and bimodality (`profile_shape.py`), prior-day range ratio and IB-extension total, today's open vs the prior value area, overnight range ratio, net move and 60-minute drive, overnight delta / volume.
TODAY (stage features): IB range / ADR (from 10:30), the IB extension X at 12:00 and 13:00, the watcher state at those times (BALANCE_CONFIRMED indicator and drive flags), running RTH delta / volume, developing POC position in the day range.
## 3. Models and stages (ridge, shrunk to the base rate; no trees)
Binary: ridge logistic (penalty by inner time-ordered CV over {0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100}) for IMBALANCE DAY; continuous: ridge for log RANGE RATIO. Expanding walk-forward, warm-up 120 sessions, refit every 5. STAGE 0 = big picture + prior day + overnight (known at 09:30); STAGE 1 = + IB range (10:30); STAGE 2 = + the 12:00 stage features. Baselines: the base rate + weekday + prior-day range ratio. Score: log-loss (binary) and squared error (range); calibration table (predicted probability decile vs realised frequency) printed.
## 4. Counted tests (4) and the lead rule
T1 STAGE 0 log-loss improvement over the baseline for IMBALANCE DAY; T2 STAGE 0 squared-error improvement for log RANGE RATIO; T3 STAGE 2 log-loss improvement over STAGE 0 for IMBALANCE DAY (does the day itself add anything the morning does not); T4 the user's hypothesis: after a large 5-day FALL (the lowest quintile of the 5-day net change over the sessions before the day, cut from earlier sessions only) the share of BALANCE days and the fill rate of the prior sessions' single-print zones / poor lows within the next 3 sessions versus volatility-matched controls (paired difference, day-block CI). Each with week-block / day-block 95 percent CIs, a circular-shift placebo (500 draws) for T1-T3. A LEAD = the CI lower bound of the improvement above 0 AND the observed above the zero-centred placebo 97.5th percentile AND the same sign in both halves; only then is the hold-out spent once. The detectable improvement is printed first (UNINFORMATIVE if above 10 percent of the baseline loss).
## 5. Printed, uncounted
Per-feature standardised coefficients (never used to select), calibration, the same stages for the P / b vs D letter and the multi-hump flag, rates by the 5-day change quintile and by the streak of balance days, rates split by direction of the prior move (fall vs rally).
## 6. Limits
Descriptive research; targets are defined by conventions; the earlier overnight-only study found no shape information (range yes), so the new layers (multi-day context, unfinished business, delta) are the untested part; DB 1-minute volume is unreliable in mid-2025 so volume and delta come from the tick-built bar files only; a forecast improves decisions only if a pre-registered decision test later shows it.
