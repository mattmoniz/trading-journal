# Bar-level context studies — Part A: overnight-to-RTH (pre-registration, 2026-10-25)

Scope doc: `docs/NEXT_STEPS_SCOREBOARD_AND_BAR_STUDIES_SCOPE_20261025.md` (Part 2). This is the first of three pre-registrations (A overnight-to-RTH, B multi-timeframe trend at level touches, C regime variable). Committed BEFORE any outcome is computed. These are **market-behaviour** hypotheses tested on raw one-minute bars (CLAUDE.md: bar history first, then setups). Nothing here changes live behaviour; a survivor only earns a real stop/target replay and then a separate user-approved step.

## Data and exclusions
`price_bars_primary`, `symbol='NQ'`, one-minute bars with bid/ask volume. Density check run 2026-10-25: about 1,100–1,175 bars per date from 2023-03 on (Dec 2022–Feb 2023 is one bar per day and unusable). Window: **2023-03-06 .. the last complete RTH date before the run** (pinned in the script, reused verbatim by every script of this thread). Excluded and counted: 2025-09-28..2025-11-19 (sparse-bar window, KNOWN_ISSUES #16), NQ roll weeks (`isInsideNqRollWeek`, on the trade date and the prior date), and any date failing a density screen (RTH bars < 95% of 390, or overnight bars < 80% of that weekday-type's median). The pinned end date and the exclusion counts are printed in the result.

## Definitions (all ET, all scale-free; thresholds come from the prior-period distribution, never a fixed number)
- RTH(D) = bars of date D with minute-of-day in [570, 960). Open `O` = open of the 09:30 bar. Return at horizon k = close of the bar at 09:30+k−1 minus `O` (k = 15, 30, 60) and "to close" = close of the 15:59 bar minus `O`, all in **ATR20 units**, ATR20 = mean `rth_range` of the 20 RTH sessions strictly before D (`daily_rth_stats`).
- ON(D) = bars after the prior RTH date PD's 16:00 and before D 09:30 with minute-of-day ≥ 1080 or < 570 (the system's Globex window; the 16:00–18:00 post-close stretch is excluded). ON range `[onL, onH]`, ON net move = last ON close − first ON open, ON volume = sum of bar volume.
- Prior value area `[VAL, VAH]`, prior close: from PD's RTH bars via the shared `computeProfile` (spreads volume over each bar's high–low), prior close = close of the 15:59 bar. ON POC: `computeProfile` over the ON bars (same function).
- "Unconditional" = the same outcome over all valid days, never zero (multi-year NQ has drift).

## Hypotheses and cells (33 cells counted up front)
**A1 overnight inventory.** State ABOVE = `onL >= VAH`, BELOW = `onH <= VAL`, else INSIDE. Bias = long for ABOVE, short for BELOW. Outcome: bias-signed RTH return at 15/30/60 min. 2 states × 3 horizons = 6 cells. (Mean-reversion claim = negative signed return.)
**A2 open vs overnight range.** ABOVE_ON = `O > onH`, BELOW_ON = `O < onL`, else INSIDE. Outcomes: RTH range / ATR20 (1 cell per state = 2) and the open-side-signed return at 15/30/60 (continuation claim; 6 cells) = 8 cells.
**A3 open vs overnight POC.** ABOVE_POC = `O > POC_on`, BELOW_POC = `O < POC_on`. Outcome: side-signed return at 15/30/60 and to close. 2 × 4 = 8 cells.
**A4 overnight volume.** HIGH = ON volume at or above the 67th percentile of the ON volumes of the prior N=60 sessions (strictly prior; sensitivity N=20 and 120 reported, not tuned). Outcomes: RTH return at 60 and to close signed by the ON net-move direction (continuation claim; 2 cells) and RTH range / ATR20 (1 cell) = 3 cells.
**A5 8:30 release reaction.** NOT TESTABLE: `macro_events` has 19 forward-looking rows (2026-06..12) and no history. Recorded as untestable; no external dates are sourced from memory.
**Globex analogues** (the hard rule requires both windows; A2/A3 are RTH-open events and have no Globex analogue, recorded as structurally RTH-only). G1: the first half of the overnight (18:00–00:00) range vs the prior VA, state ABOVE/BELOW, outcome = bias-signed drift over the second half (00:00 to 09:29) at +60/+180 min and to 09:29 → 2 × 3 = 6 cells. G4: first-half volume HIGH (same 67th-percentile rule against prior first halves) → continuation sign of the second half at +180 min and to 09:29 → 2 cells.

## Test, placebo, and pass rule (frozen)
1. Days are split chronologically: **explore** = earliest 60% of valid days, **hold-out** = latest 40%. All screening is on explore only.
2. Per cell: `delta = mean(outcome | state) − mean(outcome | not state)` in ATR units, with a day-blocked bootstrap 95% CI. Because the unit is one day, "day-blocked" is a bootstrap over days. Labels are autocorrelated (regimes persist), so the placebo is a **circular shift of the label sequence** (1,000 random offsets, the real group sizes preserved), giving a permutation p-value for `|delta|`.
3. Multiplicity: Benjamini–Hochberg at q = 0.10 over the explore p-values of all 33 cells. The expected number of false positives at 5% before correction is about 1.7; the corrected survivors count is reported either way.
4. A cell is **SHOWN** only if it is a BH survivor on explore, the hold-out delta has the same sign with a one-sided permutation p < 0.05, and its top-5-day share is under 60%. A BH survivor that fails the hold-out is **NOT SHOWN (explore only)**. A cell that does not survive on explore is **NOT SHOWN**. The report states, for every null, what could not be shown (the smallest effect the sample could resolve = roughly half the CI width).
5. Pooled verdicts are questions: every state is tested separately (ABOVE and BELOW are never merged), and any SHOWN cell is also split by year to check for opposite-signed eras (the fade/continuation sign flipped between 2023–mid 2025 and the last ~16 months in earlier studies).

## Next steps and limits
Survivors only: a real bar-by-bar stop/target replay with the market-fill rule and a gambler's-ruin placebo for asymmetric geometry, then a walk-forward with the cell chosen on explore only. Limits: one observation per day, so about 870 usable days at most and about 520 in explore; effects smaller than about 0.1 ATR will not be resolvable. Independent re-derivation: after the primary run, Gemini re-implements A1 and A3 from this document alone (not from the script) and the headline numbers must agree, because two builds that share a loader cannot catch a shared bug.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-25, committed BEFORE any outcome was computed) — from state counts and the DeepSeek design critique

Inputs: the dataset builder's state frequencies (no outcome statistics were looked at) and `reports/deepseek_bar_context_prereg_A_2026-10-26.md`. What changed and why:

1. **Test statistic (DeepSeek S1, the critical finding).** The original wording ("side-signed delta vs not-state") is drift-dominated and cannot tell continuation from reversal. Replaced everywhere by **conditional minus unconditional excess**: for a cell with state days `S`, sign `s_i` (+1 long-bias, −1 short-bias; +1 for unsigned level outcomes) and outcome `y_i` in ATR units, `excess = mean over S of s_i × (y_i − μ)` where `μ` is the mean of `y` over ALL valid days of the same block (explore or hold-out). Continuation of the state's bias gives a positive excess, mean reversion a negative one, and drift cancels because it is subtracted. The raw split (`mean y | state`, `mean y | all`) is printed per cell. The circular-shift placebo shifts the signed state sequence and recomputes the same excess (all `L−1` offsets, exhaustively, no random draw, within the block).
2. **A2 redefined (found from state counts).** "Open outside the overnight range" is empty by construction (1 of 772 days): the 09:30 open is within a tick of the 09:29 close. New A2: **location of the open inside the overnight range** (upper third / lower third, signed continuation) and **overnight range/ATR20 in the top third of the prior same-type sessions → RTH range expansion** (renamed "range expansion", not a trend-day claim). A1 and A2 gain a "to close" horizon (A1 had none). No `day_type` outcome; it is reported as a split for any SHOWN cell instead.
3. **Power floors.** A cell is evaluated only with ≥ 30 state days in explore and ≥ 20 in hold-out, else "NOT EVALUATED / UNDERPOWERED" (not "not shown"). Realised sample: 772 valid days (explore 463, hold-out 309); A1 ABOVE/BELOW have 50/30 explore and 26/18 hold-out days, so BELOW cannot be hold-out-confirmed on its own; a **pooled one-sided-inventory cell** (bias-signed, above and below together) is added next to the two separate cells, and the opposite-signed-subgroup check still applies. The realistic resolvable effect for these states is about 0.2–0.3 ATR, not 0.1.
4. **Concentration guard replaced.** At one observation per day the top-5-day share is vacuous. Replaced by a **leave-top-5-out influence check**: drop the 5 state days with the largest |s×(y−μ)|; SHOWN requires the same sign and at least half of the full excess.
5. **Gap-and-range matched control (DeepSeek Q3).** For every BH survivor: each state day is matched to its 5 nearest non-state days (same block) on |gap|/ATR20 (`O` vs prior close) and prior-day RTH range/ATR20, standardised; the control excess is `mean over S of s_i × (y_i − mean y of its controls)` with a day-blocked CI. A SHOWN cell is labelled **gap-controlled** only if this has the same sign and a CI excluding zero; otherwise **SHOWN (gap-confounded)** (likely gap-fill or drift, not the stated mechanism).
6. **Volume features.** A4/G4 use volume **per bar** (Monday sessions start Sunday evening and are longer), and the high-volume flag is the top third of the prior **same-type** sessions (Monday: prior 15 Mondays; Tue–Fri: prior 60), strictly prior; the N=20/120 sensitivity is dropped (not run, no SHOWN eligibility). The A4 directional cell uses a state only when |ON net move| / ATR20 is at least the prior same-type median (near-zero moves get no sign). G4 keeps only the +180 min horizon.
7. **Globex analogue trimmed.** "To 09:29" is dropped from G1/G4 (it sweeps the unidentifiable 8:30 release); horizons are +60 and +180 min. G1/G4 are descriptive of overnight microstructure, not tradable.
8. **8:30 contamination, stated.** Releases cannot be excluded (no history) and drive the overnight features on release days. For any SHOWN cell a proxy split is reported: days with overnight range/ATR20 in the top decile of prior same-type sessions vs the rest.
9. **Session calendar.** `PD` = the previous row of `daily_rth_stats` (actual RTH dates, holiday-aware), never `D − 1`. The first six months (2023-03..2023-09) are reported separately for any SHOWN cell (tick-rebuild-only contracts).
10. **Honest expectation, stated up front.** The hold-out is the most recent 40% (2025-04..2026-10), which sits on the earlier-documented fade/continuation sign flip, so any effect that exists only in 2023–2024 will end as "NOT SHOWN (explore only)". That is a finding about persistence, not an almost-finding. DeepSeek's direct read, accepted as the prior: this thread most likely describes the market (gap fill, drift, volume-volatility link) rather than yields a tradable edge for 1 MNQ contract; a null is the expected result.

**Cell count after the amendment: 39** — A1 12 (ABOVE, BELOW, POOLED × 15/30/60/close), A2 9 (UPPER, LOWER × 4 horizons + range expansion), A3 8, A4 3, G1 6, G4 1. BH at q = 0.10 over the evaluable cells' explore p-values is kept (positive dependence makes it conservative, not liberal; the effective independent surface is well below 39).
