**Raw fractal pivot detector is far too noisy to be useful directly — found and partially addressed 2026-09-09/10**: `findSwingPoints(bars, 5)` (the live default) placed 64 confirmed pivots over just 2 days (2026-09-08/09) — roughly one every 40-45 minutes, all day.
