## Definitions (frozen; all causal at their own bar)
- Thrust onset = an ROC10 expansion onset (|Z_ROC10| > 2, SMA50 +- 2 SD, on one continuous gap-masked 500V series), direction = sign of ROC10 (`scripts/vacuum_sim/features.py` + `v2.py`, the live detector's code, hr-022).
- Window W for a tell entry at time t on date d, clock hour h: W = 10 x the median 500V bar duration of hour h over the 20 prior sessions (10 bars is the ROC10 look-back, so W is the measure's own time scale: derived, not tuned, hr-003). Bar counts equivalent to W are printed.
- CAUSAL thrust flag (the only one used by the split test and any future gate): a same-direction thrust onset in [t - W, t]. LEAD/LAG view (descriptive only, outcome-blind): the signed offset in bars from the entry to the nearest same-direction onset within +-W; onsets AFTER the entry cannot be used by anything live (hr-020).

## Step 1: coincidence (outcome-blind; no tell outcome is read)
For each scored tell entry compute the causal thrust flag and the signed offset. Base rates by random RTH bars, 1,000 draws, direction assigned to match the tell's long/short mix, the same flag computed:
- Base 1: matched on date and clock hour.
- Base 2 (the by-construction control): matched on date, clock hour AND the bar's pace tercile (the tell's `fast` ingredient), so a lift that survives Base 2 is not merely "fast tape".
Report: coincidence rate, lift = rate / base rate with a day-blocked CI for both bases, the lead/lag histogram of the tell against the base histogram, split by side and by clock hour. Per cv-005, count every thrust episode in a session, not just the first.

## Counted tests (2; Holm, family alpha 0.10, one-sided; day-blocked bootstrap, 4,000 draws, seeded)
- T1: coincidence lift over Base 2 > 1 (RTH tell entries). The only counted claim about coincidence; it is outcome-blind.
- T2: V1 net dollars per entry (`net` in the entries file, as scored by the shadow-book replay) of tell entries WITH the causal thrust flag minus entries WITHOUT it, day-blocked CI. Placebo: 2,000 permutations of the flag across entries within clock-hour buckets; the observed difference must exceed the 97.5th percentile of the permuted differences. Report the median alongside the mean, the >100-point-stop group separately (the runner population, `docs/TELL_FLOOR_TARGET_PREREG_20261105.md` section 5), and sum(pnl)/N per group, with the CI only for the difference (hr-062).
Reported, not counted: side split (long/short), hour split (10:00-10:59 vs later), chronological halves, flag with a window of 0.5 W and 2 W (sensitivity, not tuning), top-5-day share.

## PASS / lead rule
T1 lead: lift CI lower bound > 1 against Base 2. T2 lead: CI lower bound > 0 at the Holm-adjusted level, the placebo condition, both chronological halves the same sign, and not INCONCLUSIVE (LONG and SHORT differences of opposite sign each beyond 0.5 standard errors, cv-033). A lead is a HYPOTHESIS for a forward tag on the live tell fires (log the causal flag at each fire; judged at the tell's own evidence floor of about 150 trades), never a gate by itself. Anything else is NOT SHOWN, with the minimum detectable effect printed first (2.8 x SE); a null is "not shown at this power" and parks the idea (co-025).

## Failure autopsy (co-022)
If T1 passes but T2 does not: coincidence without a conditional edge, so the thrust marks "momentum present" for both winners and losers; report which stop-size and hour groups carry the sign. If T1 fails against Base 2: the coincidence is the shared pace ingredient, record that plainly. Either way record the claim, the salvage (the lead/lag view as a chart annotation for discretionary reading) and the next step.

## Step 1 RESULT (2026-11-09 evening; script `scripts/vacuum_sim/tell_roc_step1.py`, output `scratch/vacuum_sim_20261108/tell_roc_step1_output.txt`, look 211, claim `tell_roc_coincidence_step1_20261109`; no tell outcome read)

W median 219 s (about 9 bars). A causal same-direction ROC10 onset in [t - W, t] is present at 37.9 percent of tell entries versus 16.6 percent of date-and-hour matched bars (lift 2.28) and 18.1 percent of pace-matched bars (lift 2.10; day-blocked one-sided 90 percent lower bound 2.01, 95 percent CI [1.97, 2.23]). T1 = LEAD: the coincidence survives the pace control, in both sides (long 2.17, short 2.03) and every hour (1.88-2.25). It is STRONGER in slow bars (lift 3.33) than in fast ones (1.85), so it is not the fast-pace ingredient. Lead/lag (descriptive): 37.8 percent of entries have a same-direction onset BEFORE the decision bar within 15 bars (past side), 3.0 percent on it, 12.3 percent after it; 46.9 percent none within +-15. (Correction 2026-11-09 evening: an earlier draft of this paragraph quoted detail figures from the first run, which used the wrong entry set of 1,744 rows; these are from the final run of 1,448.)
