# Node ladder before the breakout: does price leaving volume nodes behind inside the IB predict the break? (frozen 2026-10-28)

Committed BEFORE any outcome is read. Origin (user, 2026-10-28): "we should be in the trade before it happens to breakout. The way I would tell that it's going to breakout is that it keeps moving in the desired direction leaving behind volume nodes. A breakout is usually acknowledged once price leaves overnight levels or initial balance levels." The earlier volume-by-price tests (`docs/VBP_ACCEPTANCE_REENTRY_PREREG_20261027.md`) looked at the moment AFTER a node exit; this one asks whether a state INSIDE the range, before any exit, leads the break. My definitions are an operationalisation of the user's words (the user's own node sensitivity is unknown), not the user's own rule.

Signal-level screen only (the project checklist step 4a): no stop, no target, no trade. EXPLORE only: the 529 eligible sessions to 2025-05-16 (the same ones the other AMT studies used, so any lead is a hypothesis); the 340 sessions after it stay SEALED and unloaded. If a lead appears it earns trade machinery (entry at the ladder, stop behind the last node) only after a new frozen prereg.

## Definitions (chosen before any result)
- **Node left behind:** the volume POC of each completed 30-minute bracket of the RTH session (bracket b covers bars 30b..30b+29 from 09:30, volume spread across each 1-minute bar's high-low tick range at 0.25, the engine's `profile`; first maximum). Bracket b is usable from its last bar's close.
- **Ladder (direction d, d=+1 up, -1 down):** at the end of bracket b (decision at its last close), POC[b-2], POC[b-1], POC[b] step in direction d, each step at least S, AND the bracket's last close is beyond its own node in direction d (close[b] > POC[b] for up). S = the median absolute POC step between adjacent brackets, pooled over every adjacent pair in the explore sessions (an outcome-free quantity, printed before any outcome is read, frozen; a data-derived separation so "left behind" means a real step, not tick noise).
- **Decision points:** bracket ends b = 2..7 (11:00 through 13:30 ET; b=2 is the first with three completed nodes). Outcome horizon 120 minutes after the decision, never past 15:55.
- **Before the break:** only decision points where price has not already left the IB in that direction: no 1-minute close above IBH (up) / below IBL (down) at any bar from 10:30 up to the decision. IB = high/low of the 09:30-10:29 bars.
- **Break (primary outcome):** any 1-minute close beyond IBH (up) / IBL (down) strictly, within the 120 minutes after the decision. Secondary: close beyond the combined range, max(IBH, ONH) up / min(IBL, ONL) down (ONH/ONL = 18:00-09:29 extremes, >= 500 bars). Signed 60-minute move: d x (close of the bar 59 minutes after the decision open - the open of the first bar after the decision), in ATR20 units, ATR20 = mean RTH high-low range of the 20 prior RTH sessions. No slippage (signal screen).
- **Distance stratum:** distance to the boundary in ATR20 units, (IBH - last close)/ATR20 up, (last close - IBL)/ATR20 down; terciles cut on all decision points pooled (outcome-free). Distance is the main driver of whether a break happens at all, so every comparison is within strata.

## Primary statistic (one)
The ladder-state break rate minus the break rate of decision points WITHOUT a ladder in that direction, averaged over the three distance strata weighted by the number of ladder events in each stratum; pooled over both directions (each decision point contributes once per direction); day-blocked bootstrap (resample sessions, 3000 iterations, seed 20261028); per-arm rates are plain counts over N (not day-weighted); the one-sided 97.5 percent lower bound of the difference must exceed 0. One counted cell.

## Controls and secondaries
- **Price-momentum control (same-selection-minus-signal):** decision points where the last close moved at least S in direction d over the last two brackets (close[b] - close[b-2], signed, >= S) but the POCs do NOT ladder. If the ladder does not beat this, volume nodes add nothing beyond price drift.
- **Signed 60-minute move:** ladder minus no-ladder and ladder minus price-momentum control, same stratified weighting and bootstrap. Also the break within the combined range. Descriptive: long vs short, bracket time, how many ladder events fall in the sparse part of the session, event counts per arm printed FIRST.

## Reading (frozen)
A lead only if the primary lower bound > 0 AND the ladder's break rate beats the price-momentum control (point estimate, same strata) AND the signed 60-minute move difference vs no-ladder has a positive point estimate. Otherwise "not shown" (never "no effect"), reported with the resolution reached. Under 150 ladder events or 60 distinct dates = underpowered. A lead earns a new frozen trade-machinery prereg and one sealed hold-out look only with the user's approval.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-28, committed BEFORE the first run's output was read) — the flow tell and a second counted cell
User, mid-run: "Not every trade is a breakout but higher orderflow back and forth with price moving in the desired direction is the tell." So the tell is the node ladder PLUS heavy two-sided order flow, and the thing to predict is the move in that direction, not only the IB break. The first run (the frozen primary above) had already been started; nothing in it is changed or tuned by this amendment, its output is reported as is, and the new arm is computed in a separate script (`run_flow.py`). Disclosure: the first run was in progress when this was written, but no outcome had been read.

**Flow arm F (frozen definitions, no tuning):** F = ladder (as defined above) AND HIGH FLOW AND BACK-AND-FORTH, measured over the last three completed brackets (the same 90 one-minute bars that make the ladder):
- HIGH FLOW: total volume of those 90 bars >= the median of the same-bracket 90-bar volume over the 20 prior sessions in the data (no lookahead; sessions with fewer than 20 prior sessions are dropped from the F arm only).
- BACK-AND-FORTH (two-sided): |sum of 1-minute bar delta| / total volume over the 90 bars <= the median of that ratio pooled over all decision points (outcome-free cutoff, printed before any outcome). Low net-delta share with high volume means both sides traded heavily while price still stepped one way.
**Counted cell 2:** signed 60-minute move (ATR20 units, as above), F minus no-ladder points, strata-weighted by F events, day-blocked bootstrap (3000, seed 20261028), one-sided lower bound must exceed 0 at the Bonferroni level for the TWO counted cells of this whole study (98.75 percent one-sided). Reported alongside, not counted: F minus ladder-not-F (does flow add to the ladder?), F minus the price-momentum control, the break rate of F (primary-outcome definition) minus no-ladder, F event counts per direction and stratum printed FIRST. Reading for F: a lead only if its lower bound > 0 AND its point estimate beats both the price-momentum control and ladder-not-F on the signed move. Under 150 F events or 60 dates = underpowered (likely, F is a subset of the ladder; stated, not a reason to loosen the cutoffs).
