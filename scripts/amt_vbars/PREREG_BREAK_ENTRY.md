# Pre-registration: use the tell as an ARM, let the market choose the side (wait for the directional break) (written 2026-11-01 BEFORE any outcome)

Origin: user 2026-11-01, "what if you get the signal and wait for the directional break? Or some other signal or filter." Motivation: the expansion test (`amt_tell_is_expansion_detector_side_not_shown_20261101`) found the tell marks WHEN price travels further, not which way. This tests whether arming on the tell and entering only after a close beyond either range edge makes money, against the same break-entry rule without the tell. Explore only (516 sessions to 2025-05-16, RTH 10:00-14:30 decision points); the AMT tell hold-out is not extracted. Script `run_break_entry.py`, one run.

## Definitions (existing code, no new thresholds)
- Decision points: the rows of `decision_points_explore.csv` (every 10th completed 500-volume bar 10:00-14:30). ARMED point = a (date, bar) where either direction has ncomp == 5 (the tell). CONTROL point = a (date, bar) where neither direction does. A point counts only if BOTH range sides are still inside (`trade.inside_range` for +1 and -1; a FRESH break is required, never an already-broken side).
- Window: bars after the decision bar until 120 minutes after it or the 15:55 flatten.
- Break event: the first 500-volume-bar close beyond either boundary (`trade.boundary`: overnight high/low widened by the IB once complete) within the window; the side of the first close sets the trade direction d. Entry at the open of the next bar plus one tick adverse (the research simulator's own SLIP). Events are de-duplicated by (date, entry bar, d); an event reachable from an ARMED point belongs to ARMED and is removed from CONTROL.
- Stops (two arms, both reported, none chosen after seeing results): S_EDGE = the broken boundary price minus/plus one tick (a failed-break stop); S_NODE = `trade.stop_price(ctx, P, j, d, 2)` at the break bar j (the tell's own second-LVN structural stop). An event whose entry is not beyond its stop, or with no node stop, is skipped and counted.
- Exit: `trade.run_position` variant V3 semantics: hold, the stop, or the 15:55 flatten; gap fills at the open; no target, no moving stop; $2 per point, $2 round trip.

## Statistics (per-trade means, sum/N; day-blocked bootstrap only for intervals, 2000 draws, seeded)
Per arm and stop: N, dates, mean net per trade, WR, win/loss means. Armed minus control per-trade mean difference weighted to the ARMED events' entry-time mix (three bins by minutes since 09:30: under 90, 90-180, 180 and later; an entry-time confound control, not a tuned number), day-blocked CI of the weighted difference. Also the armed arm's own absolute mean with its day-blocked CI. Chronological halves by median date. Counts first, including class counts (events skipped, points excluded for an already-broken side).

## Reading rule (stated before the run)
A stop arm "shows a tell-armed break entry" only if ALL hold: >= 200 armed events on >= 100 dates; armed minus control > 0 with the CI excluding zero; armed absolute mean > 0 with its own CI lower bound > 0 (the user needs money, not only relative skill; the hold-out lesson of 2026-11-01 was a relative pass with negative dollars); same sign of the difference in both halves. Two stop arms are tested; one pass is a LEAD for one frozen look at the sealed AMT-tell hold-out (not extracted, needs the user's go), not a result. If the absolute mean is negative the result is NOT a rule whatever the difference. Resolution (CI half-width) is reported either way.

## Not tested here, recorded
The user's half-off at the IB high with a runner and straddle/two-sided variants (exit/management rules, separate prereg and realistic fills); using the first break of a delta flip or an absorbed-area test as the arm; Globex (watch-only Globex arm untested); a stop that is not at the edge or a node (swept stops are a grid; avoided here to keep the number of looks at two).
