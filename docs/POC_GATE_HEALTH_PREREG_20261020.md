## Population
The 3.5-year RTH level-fade replay with touch times and realistic fills (`scratch/rotation_gate_replay_rows_ts_20261020.json`, 21,298 AGREE/DISAGREE-tagged trades). The roster being "gated" is its AGREE trades. All results use per-trade means (sum/N), day-blocked bootstrap CIs (resample sessions), explore 2023-03-06..2025-05-31 and hold-out 2025-06-01..2026-10-16, cut points frozen on explore only.

## Candidate A: label stability (the mechanism)
The AGREE/DISAGREE label is the direction of the in-progress 65pt leg. It is only as good as that leg is settled. Point-in-time features at the touch bar (legs confirmed by that bar only): LEGAGE = minutes since the current leg was confirmed; FLIPS120 = number of legs confirmed in the previous 120 minutes. Each is ranked against the previous 60 sessions at the same clock time (no fixed minute counts).
- A1 (gap by stability): the AGREE-minus-DISAGREE per-trade gap in thirds of LEGAGE and of FLIPS120 (cuts from explore). Hypothesis: the gap is smaller (gate worse) when the leg is young / flips are frequent.
- A2 (a gate on the gate): flag AGREE trades taken when LEGAGE is in the youngest third OR FLIPS120 in the highest third (frozen cuts); "stepping aside" removes them. Metric: mean pnl of flagged AGREE minus unflagged AGREE (negative flagged = helpful), day-blocked CI, placebo of 1000 random flag sets of equal rate within session, hold-out sign, per-direction signs.
## Candidate B: day-level giveback breakers (the outcome)
On the chronological AGREE trades of each session: R_k = once the running session P&L has fallen from its session peak by more than k x M, take no further AGREE trade that session, where M = the median absolute loss of AGREE losers on the explore half (data-derived, not a chosen dollar number), k in {2, 3, 4} (all reported); S_n = after n consecutive AGREE losses stop for the session, n in {3, 4, 5}. Metric: the mean P&L per session of the SKIPPED trades (negative = the rule saves money), summed saving per session, day-blocked CI, a placebo where the trigger bar is drawn at random with the same number of triggers, explore/hold-out signs, number of sessions triggered, and the saved give-back on the days it triggers against what it forgoes on days where the roster would have recovered.

## Verdict rules (each candidate judged separately; nothing is wired live)
USEFUL only if (i) the helpful-direction CI excludes zero, (ii) it beats its placebo 95th percentile, (iii) the hold-out has the same sign and at least half the explore size, (iv) the sign holds for both directions (A) / for at least 2 of 3 k or n settings (B). Otherwise "not shown", written as what was and was not shown. A SUPPORTED result licenses a forward-monitor rule and a DeepSeek review, nothing live.
