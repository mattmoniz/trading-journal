# Pre-registration: do shadow fires on the side of an ACTIVE tell do better than fires against it? (register row 1, 'the tell as direction context'; Opus ranked idea 2; written 2026-11-04 BEFORE any fire outcome was joined to a tell side)

STATUS: FROZEN. Origin: user, 2026-11-04 ('step 2' after the Opus revised review: use the tell as a direction read for OTHER setups instead of trading it). Research only, nothing live. Ledger family `tell_direction_context_20261104`, 3 counted tests.
## 1. Fires
`active_setups` rows with origin_status IN ('ACTIVE','SHADOW') (the would-be-live fires; BACKFILL excluded as synthetic, hr-006), fired 2026-07-09 to 2026-10-19 (the span the tell replay covers with the same machinery as the live detector), RTH only and 10:00-14:30 ET by the fire time (the tell's own decision window; Globex fires have no tell: hr-002 gap RECORDED, untested), with an `actual_pnl`, no `*_basis` flag, PRIMARY LENS = `is_cluster_primary` (hr-007; the all-fires numbers are printed too). Direction of a fire = LONG if its stop level is below its entry zone, else SHORT (checked against the _LONG / _SHORT setup-type suffix; the agreement rate is printed). Dollars per fire as stored (1 MNQ).
## 2. The tell's side at the fire (causal)
Tell entries: the V1 'split' tell replay of the hold-out era (`scratch/tell_context_20261103/watcher_stop_sealed_rows_sealed.csv`: date, dir, end_sec = the entry bar end, seconds since 18:00 of the previous calendar day; 305 entries on 72 dates in the fire span). ACTIVE TELL at a fire = the most recent tell entry whose time is at or before the fire time and no more than 60 minutes earlier (60 minutes = the longest expansion window studied for the tell, a convention, not tuned; 30 minutes and 'any earlier tell today' are reported as sensitivity); its side is the tell's `dir`. Fires with no tell in the previous 60 minutes are NONE. A fire is AGREE when its direction equals the active tell side, AGAINST when opposite.
## 3. Counted tests (3), day-block bootstrap CI at 98.33 percent (Bonferroni 3, 4,000 draws, seed 20261104), both chronological halves, detectable effect (2.8 x SE) printed first
T1: mean dollars per fire, AGREE minus AGAINST (the user's hypothesis; fires with an active tell only). T2: AGREE minus NONE. T3: AGAINST minus NONE (does going against an active tell cost). PLACEBO for T1: each fire takes the tell state of a RANDOM OTHER DATE at the same clock time (the tell timeline moved to another day, 1,000 draws; keeps the tell's frequency and the time-of-day pattern, breaks the link to the day); the real contrast must exceed the 97.5th percentile of |placebo|. LEAD only if the CI excludes 0, both halves share the sign and (T1) the placebo gate is beaten.
## 4. The drift control (the confound that matters) and what is reported, not counted
A fire on the tell's side may simply be on the side the day is moving: the same contrast is computed with the MOMENTUM side (the sign of the price change over the previous 60 minutes, 1-minute closes) in place of the tell side; a tell effect that is only momentum shows the same contrast. Also reported: the contrast within momentum-agree and momentum-against fires (does the tell add beyond momentum), LONG and SHORT fires separately, by setup family (N >= 30), by 30 and 'any earlier today' windows, the counts of AGREE / AGAINST / NONE fires and days first, the all-fires lens next to the declustered one.
## 5. Limits stated now
About 70 trade dates, so far fewer independent observations than fires (day-block CIs); one regime (Jul-Oct 2026); the tell's replay, not live fires, supplies the side (the live detector exists only since 2026-10-28); the tell is an expansion detector with a coin-flip side (`amt_tell_expansion_5_15_30_60min_windows_20261101`), so a null here would be expected; the hold-out era of the tell was already read once (look 143), this uses its entries as a context feature, not as a trade. A LEAD would be a hypothesis for a forward shadow comparison, never a gate.
