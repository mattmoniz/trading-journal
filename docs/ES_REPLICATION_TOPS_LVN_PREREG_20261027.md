# Pre-registration: ES replication of the NQ "short the defended top toward an LVN" lead — 2026-10-27

Written BEFORE any ES event, outcome or tick-volume profile was computed or seen. The lead (from `reports/multiscale_run_poc_lvn_result_20261026.txt`, claim `multiscale_run_poc_lvn_targets_not_shown_20261027`): in the NQ test of sequential defended runs, the SHORT side (tops) was positive under every target and, in the non-overlapping subset, with the LVN targets: DEEPEST LVN excess +32.7 points [+8.0,+57.8] (243 events), NEXT LVN +20.0 [+1.3,+40.0] (243); but the counted explore half did not clear zero and the lead was found after looking at many views. This run asks one question: does the same frozen rule show the same thing in ES?

## Honest limits (stated before the run)
ES and NQ are highly correlated (the same days, mostly the same moves), so this is NOT an independent test; it checks that the rule is not a quirk of NQ's own prices and volume profile. A positive result is weak confirming evidence, a negative result is a real strike against the lead. The ES history covers 2023-02-24..2026-10-26 (the NQ history was used to find the lead, so there is no clean out-of-sample period here either).

## Rule (frozen, identical to the NQ run except where ES requires a change)
Events: `scripts/lib/multiScaleRuns.mjs` on ES 1-minute bars (`price_bars_es_front`, front contract per trade date): sequential defended runs at 60/120/240-minute bars, band 0.25 x the scale's mean bar range of the 20 prior days, run of 4+ bars, came from above, prior reference required, merged to one per episode; TOPS (short) are the counted side, floors descriptive. Excluded: events within 5 trade days of an ES contract roll (`es_front_calendar.is_roll_day`) because the stitched series jumps by the calendar basis, and events whose 20-day LVN composite window spans a roll (the composite is built from real tick volume at price of ONE contract). Trade: entry next open, stop `0.5 x H4` beyond the run extreme (H4 = mean four-hour bar range of the 20 prior days), 2,879-minute cap (lenient), realistic fills, cost 0.4 ES points (the NQ cost of 1.5 points as the same fraction of price). Targets: DEEPEST LVN and NEXT LVN, Sierra Volume-by-Price valley rule (volume strictly lower than N=3 bins above and no higher than N=3 below, edges excluded; bins `0.1 x H4`; composite = the last 20 trade dates, all sessions, from the ES .scid tick files, at least 17 of 20 dates present; eligible 1R to 6R beyond the entry). Controls: blind same-clock entries with identical point distances (30 draws, other dates), fresh-extreme births at the same side within 30 minutes of the clock (pooled over all dates other than the event's own), mirrored-direction run. ES tick volume is built from the raw `.scid` records and reconciled per date to the database ES session volume (within 3 percent), as for NQ; dates that do not reconcile are skipped and counted.

## Counted cells = 2 (Bonferroni over 2), no explore/hold-out split (the rule is frozen from NQ and ES is a new data set)
ES TOPS with DEEPEST LVN target and ES TOPS with NEXT LVN target. "REPLICATES" = all of: (i) EXCESS vs blind entries, all events, date-blocked CI at 97.5 percent with lower bound above 0; (ii) absolute net mean per trade above 0; (iii) EXCESS vs fresh-extreme births (events having it) with point estimate above 0. Otherwise "DOES NOT REPLICATE". Both cells must be reported whatever happens. Printed beside each: n, distinct dates, mean and median net, stopped/target/time shares, top-5-trade share, the non-overlapping subset (events at least 2,880 minutes apart), the smallest detectable effect (CI half-width), and for context the NQ result for the same cell.

## Descriptive only
ES floors (both targets); scale split; the fixed-3R target on the same events; the share of ES top events whose trade date also has an NQ top event (overlap with the NQ sample, so the reader can judge independence); the chosen LVN versus ES period POC lines is NOT computed (no ES period-line set exists).

Dates excluded are counted and printed.
