# Pre-registration: on a heavy Globex night, read the overnight range, then (a) buy a held range low or (b) trade a clean break — 2026-10-26

Written BEFORE running anything. Origin: user, 2026-10-26: "I want it to wait for a break before firing. I want it to get a clear nuance or direction... when I woke up this morning I saw clear support on the four-hour chart as a glaring item. So maybe it gets tripped and then sees where the big picture stands, looks at the past few days and the current multi-hour situation and then fires off a long from the lows of that range? Maybe it assesses probabilities from there." The overnight tripwire (`server/services/overnightTripwire.js`, `docs/OPEN_THREADS.md` 2026-10-26) supplies "tripped" (HEAVY = tonight's cumulative volume AND 65-point swing count both >= the 90th percentile of the prior 60 nights at the same 30-minute checkpoint). Not tested before: the floor/ceiling studies used STORED prior-period levels (PW/3M/PM/PD/PY value areas), not tonight's own running overnight range, and none was conditioned on a heavy night. Related negatives to keep in mind (all in `docs/OPEN_THREADS.md` 2026-10-26): floor entry NOT worthwhile at stored levels, volume/volatility/calendar contexts empty, volume-confirmed defended-level breakouts negative (2026-09-10), overnight order-flow signal not better on heavy-volume evenings.

## Events (no lookahead; NQ 1-minute bars 2023-03-06 to the last complete date, printed; Globex bars only, elapsed >= 120 minutes since the 18:00 open, until 09:29)
Scale `A15` = mean 15-minute bar range over the 20 prior trade days (as in the floor studies); zone `p = 1.0 x A15`; turn `m = 0.25 x A15`; window `W = 50` minutes.
**Range** at bar i: running low `Lr` and high `Hr` of the night so far (bars before i). A range is ESTABLISHED when both extremes were set at least 60 minutes ago and `Hr - Lr >= 2 x A15`.
- **S1 SUPPORT-HOLD LONG (and the mirror R1 RESISTANCE-HOLD SHORT):** a TEST begins when a bar's low is within `Lr + p` after the previous bar was above it; the regime resets (new `Lr`) if any bar's low is below `Lr - p`. FIRE at the first bar within W whose close is `m` above the lowest low since the test began (the parent fire rule), entry at the next bar's open. One event per test.
- **B1 BREAK-UP LONG (and the mirror B2 BREAK-DOWN SHORT):** the FIRST close beyond the established range edge by `m` (after the elapsed >= 120 and 60-minute-old extremes conditions); entry at the next open; one event per edge per night.
**Context flag** at the fire bar: HEAVY or NOT, from `overnight_checkpoint_stats` and the tripwire's own `classify()` at the last completed 30-minute checkpoint (same baseline rule, 60 prior nights). Nights without a baseline (before 2023-06) are excluded.

## Outcome and controls
Primary outcome: ret60 in A15 units from the entry open (close of the bar 59 minutes later), direction-signed. Control: **same-direction blind entries** on OTHER nights of the same HEAVY flag, at a clock time within 30 minutes of the event's entry and elapsed >= 120, up to 10 random draws (never the event's own night); E1a = event ret60 minus the mean control ret60. Descriptive and next to it: ret240, share of events with ret60 > 0 and ret240 > 0, quartiles of ret60 (the base-rate "probabilities" a shadow mode would show), and MFE/MAE.

## Cells and rule (same screen as the context studies)
4 event types (S1, R1, B1, B2) x {ALL, HEAVY, NOT-HEAVY} on E1a, plus the HEAVY-minus-NOT contrast per type = **16 counted cells**. Explore = earlier 60 percent of nights, hold-out = later 40 percent (a screen only: the parent studies already looked at this period). CANDIDATE = explore night-blocked 95 percent CI lower bound > 0; CONFIRMED = candidate AND hold-out point > 0 AND hold-out CI lower bound > 0 AND explore point > 0; UNDERPOWERED if < 20 distinct nights. Printed: tested cells, candidates, expected by chance (tested x 0.025), confirmed, CI half-widths. The user's two long ideas are S1 and B1 on HEAVY nights; R1/B2 are their mirrors, reported separately (a pooled verdict is never given).

## Wording and next step
CONFIRMED = "screening survivor, worth a shadow-only trial on the tripwire", never a rule to trade. A shadow trial would log, for each trip, the range snapshot (high/low/tests/multi-day levels at the edges), the would-fire event and these base rates, with no orders. NOT SHOWN is worded with the CI half-width.

## Limits stated in advance
A heavy night is rare (about 14 percent of nights), so HEAVY cells will be thin; the range is defined from the night's own bars, so it is partly a function of the same volatility that trips the wire; events within a night are correlated; the user's "four-hour chart support" and "past few days" are not in the event definition (multi-day levels at the range edge are recorded only descriptively: share of events whose range edge is within 1 A15 of a stored PD/PW/PM/3M level); the whole thing is another forking path after eight nulls, stated plainly.
