## 1. Data and construction (causal, the real functions)
- The same 1,760 explore entries (`scripts/amt_vbars/trades_V1_explore.csv`), V1 management unchanged, entry-price-only control built in (hr-042).
- 1-minute NQ bars (`price_bars_primary`, RTH 09:30-16:00 ET, open, high, low, close, volume) for every explore trade date, exported by `scripts/tell_context/research_20261103/export_rotation_vwap_20261104.mjs`. Legs come from the REAL `detectRotationLegs` (`server/services/rotationDetector.js`, R = 65, intrabar highs and lows, run from the 09:30 bar), never re-implemented (hr-022). The exporter writes, for the end of every completed minute i: the direction of the last confirmed leg (none before the first confirmation), its anchor index and anchor price (the pivot), the volume-weighted anchored VWAP (typical price (high + low + close) / 3 weighted by the minute's volume, from the anchor minute to minute i inclusive) and the equal-weight anchored average of the same typical prices (the volume-inflation control, hr-070), and the session VWAP anchored at 09:30.
- The state used for a fire is the state at the last COMPLETED minute strictly before the fire's signal bar end (no lookahead; a leg confirmed on the fire's own minute is not yet known). A fire before the first confirmed leg has NO leg and is reported in its own bucket, never merged.
- Window of the question: fires whose signal bar ends before 11:30 ET (the user's 'first hour or 2' after the 09:30 open); fires from 11:30 on are the descriptive comparison.

## 2. Variables (frozen definitions)
- **AGREE**: the fire direction equals the current confirmed leg direction (long in an UP leg, short in a DOWN leg); AGAINST otherwise.
- **WITH_RV**: the entry price is on the supported side of the rotational VWAP (long above it, short below it); AGAINST_RV otherwise. The same with the session VWAP (WITH_SV) and with the equal-weight average (WITH_RT).

## 3. Counted tests (4; verdict CI 98.75 percent, Bonferroni 0.05 / 4; day-block bootstrap resampling trade dates, 4,000 draws, seed 20261104; two-sided; the predicted sign is positive for T1-T3)
- **T1 (leg direction):** mean net dollars per trade, AGREE minus AGAINST, fires before 11:30 that have a leg.
- **T2 (rotational VWAP side):** WITH_RV minus AGAINST_RV, same fires.
- **T3 (VWAP beyond the direction):** WITH_RV minus AGAINST_RV WITHIN the AGREE fires only (same-side control, cv-094): does the VWAP side add anything once the fire already agrees with the leg.
- **T4 (stop beyond the pivot, AGREE fires, same window):** stop = one tick beyond the current leg's pivot (the anchor price: for a short in a DOWN leg the high that started the leg, plus a tick; mirror for a long), but never closer than the tell's own stop (the farther of the two); paired difference (arm net minus V1 net) on the AGREE fires whose stop changed, re-simulated with the repo's own `shadow._run` exactly like the stop-placement arms. This is a STATIC stop at the fire; a stop that moves as the trade works is register row 12, a separate prereg.
- **Reading rule:** SEPARATES (T1-T3) / PAYS (T4) = the lower bound of the 98.75 percent CI above 0 AND the difference positive in both chronological halves of the dates AND positive in at least 2 of 3 chronological thirds. Positive mean with a CI including 0 = LEAD for a forward shadow only. Anything else = NOT SHOWN with what the data could not have shown: the day-blocked standard error of each difference is printed first and the detectable effect is 2.8 times it.
