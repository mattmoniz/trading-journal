# Brief for Argon High: the idea, what was tested, how, and what we may have missed (DRAFT; the real-depth results are inserted when the run finishes)

## 1. The idea (the user's own words, saved in docs/ORIGINAL_NOTES_500V_LIQUIDITY_VACUUM_20261108.md and docs/USER_SIERRA_INDICATOR_SETTINGS_20261108.md)
A solo NQ/MNQ trader (exactly 1 MNQ, $2/pt, about 2 pt all-in cost per round trip) reads Sierra Chart 500-volume bars. The notes say it is "a way to determine how book makers are managing order books and capitalizing on opportunities, pockets where books are in flux", and the objective is "to figure out where institutionals are getting involved by judging the pace that 500V bars are finishing". Three studies: Ratio = EMA10(True Range) / EMA10(Bid & Ask Depth Bars, Sum, Close subgraph) with SMA50 +- 1.5 SD bands (spike above = liquidity vacuum / air pocket, below the lower band = absorption wall = no trade); Bar Time Duration (graded; RTH <= 15 s is fast institutional sweeping; Globex relative: dt <= 0.65 x SMA50 and <= 120 s); ROC10 = Close - Close[-10] with SMA50 +- 2 SD bands (breakout = velocity thrust; zero line and middle band = continuation anchor). Entries: large EXPANSIONS (Ratio above its top band while ROC breaks its outer band on fast bars), Wave 2 re-ignition after a pullback that holds the trend side of ROC zero; exits: ROC hooks back inside its band by 0.20 sd while the Ratio falls 0.25 sd from its peak, absorption divergence (Wave 2 Ratio under 75 percent of Wave 1's peak), ROC crossing zero. Worked example (Nov 6): a short at about 12:36 with Ratio 0.040 -> 0.052, ROC10 under -100, bars of seconds; price 27,150 -> 27,006; the 13:00-14:37 stall had Ratio under 0.030 and 45 s bars.

We restored the original and test it with the user's exact Sierra settings.
