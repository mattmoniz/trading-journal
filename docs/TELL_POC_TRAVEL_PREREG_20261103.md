# Pre-registration (descriptive): does the point of control (POC) travelling across the day's range explain why the flip loses where the tell wins? (written 2026-11-03 night BEFORE any POC measure was computed or any outcome of this test read)

STATUS: FROZEN. Descriptive, hindsight outcomes, explore window (the 1,760 tell trades of `scripts/amt_vbars/trades_V1_explore.csv`), ledger family `tell_poc_travel_20261103`, tests = 6. Nothing here changes what the system trades.
Origin (user, 2026-11-03, after `docs/TELL_TRADES_BY_DAY_SHAPE_PREREG_20261103.md`): on AGAINST fires the tell made +40 dollars/trade on days that ended two-humped while the flip lost 25 (and the reverse on other days). User: "I do wonder if the difference is that the POC shifts greatly from the bottom or the top or vice versa."
## 1. Measures at the fire (causal: only bars completed through the signal bar; POC = the live definition, `context._profile` + `value_area`, 1-point bins on the RTH profile)
- POC points: the developing RTH POC at every completed 30-minute checkpoint (10:00, 10:30, ...) at or before the signal bar, plus the POC at the signal bar itself.
- RANGE = RTH high minus RTH low so far at the signal bar. TRAVEL = (highest minus lowest POC point) / RANGE: how much of the day's range the POC has covered ('from the bottom to the top or vice versa'), scale-free. NET = (POC at the signal bar minus the POC at the first checkpoint) / RANGE, signed by the fire direction (positive = the POC has moved the way the fire trades; long fire, POC up).
- BIG TRAVEL = the top tercile of TRAVEL over all fires (the cut uses no outcome); REST = the others. POC moved WITH the fire = signed NET > 0, AGAINST it = NET < 0.
## 2. The six counted contrasts (all with day-block 95 percent bootstrap CIs, 2000 draws; N below 20 is not a result)
T1 AGAINST fires, book A (tell) mean dollars/trade: big travel minus rest. T2 same, book C (flip). T3 AGAINST fires, flip minus tell (C - A) per fire: big travel minus rest. T4 all fires: share whose day ended multi-hump (bimodality >= 0.365), big travel minus rest. T5 AGAINST fires, book A: POC moved with the fire minus against. T6 same, book C.
Printed, not counted: the travel terciles for AGAINST fires (tell and flip means and N), the same big-vs-rest split within fires before 12:00 and from 12:00 (travel grows with time), and the first/second half of the window.
## 3. Reading rule
A contrast is a LEAD only if its CI excludes zero AND the sign is the same in both halves of the window; otherwise NOT SHOWN, with what could not be shown. A lead is a hypothesis for a pre-registered test with its own confirmation on unseen data; nothing is gated on it. Travel is not independent of the clock and of the range, hence the time split; the flip's loss on two-hump days is already a hindsight-shape result, so a POC measure matters only if it is visible at the fire and separates those cases.
