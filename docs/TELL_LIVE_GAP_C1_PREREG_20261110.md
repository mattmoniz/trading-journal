- S4 (uncounted): session and direction split of the live trades (RTH vs Globex by entry time; long vs short), day-level contribution (how much of the total is the worst 2 days).

- NEXT-STEP TREE: S1 < 0.05 and S3 placebo near zero -> the detector's live behaviour differs (look at S2 / S4 for scale or session); S1 < 0.05 and S3 placebo equally negative -> environment; S1 >= 0.05 -> luck not excluded, keep collecting (floor 150 trades / 60 dates) and report the gap with its day-clustered interval; any scale finding -> a scale-free restatement of the stop and cost in a separate pre-registered row. ## RESULT (2026-11-10 night; look 267; `scripts/tell_context/c1_gap.py`, output `scratch/tell_live_gap_c1_run_20261110.txt`): THE GAP IS NOT SMALL-SAMPLE LUCK ALONE; THE STOP DID NOT SCALE WITH 2026 VOLATILITY; THE LIVE LOSS IS ALL IN LONGS DURING A 1,500-POINT DECLINE (descriptive)

READING: three things stack: (1) the stop is structural and stayed near 30 points while the day range almost doubled, so 81 percent of trades stop out; (2) a falling market made longs lose (the explore years show the tell's mean varies by year from -2.6 to +7.8 dollars per trade, so regime sensitivity is normal); (3) two bad days dominate. S1 says luck alone is unlikely, so (1) and (2) are the live candidates, and both are checkable without changing the detector: a scale-free restatement of the stop (stop in ADR20 units) and the long / short split by the trend of the prior 20 sessions, both as separate pre-registered rows.
