# Pre-registration (explore): does the overnight mechanism work in RTH? The flow sign on HIGH-ROTATION mornings (Opus idea 1; register row 16; written 2026-11-04/05 BEFORE any outcome was computed)

STATUS: FROZEN. Origin: the user, 2026-11-04 ('continue with the opus idea', after the Opus revised review, brief section 10). The one directional edge here lives inside a high-activity regime (flow sign on the overnight nights with 6+ rotation legs: +239 per trade). This asks whether the SAME structure holds in RTH: on mornings with an unusually high rotation count, does the order-flow sign so far pick the rest of the day? Research only, nothing live. Ledger family `rth_regime_flow_20261104`, 3 counted tests. Explore dates with tick data only (<= 2025-05-16); later dates have no tick extraction (the DB 1-minute volume is unreliable in mid-2025, hr-070), so a LEAD would need a tick extraction of the later era for ONE sealed look.
## 1. STEP 0 (done before this document, features only, no outcome): the qualifying-day count and a definition repair
Rotation legs by 11:00 = the real `detectRotationLegs` on the 1-minute RTH bars 09:30-10:59 (`export_rth_legs_20261104.mjs`, output `scratch/ovn_orderflow_20261104/rth_legs_by_11.json`). Qualifying = the count at or above the EXPANDING 90th percentile of earlier days (at least 60 earlier days). Fixed 65-point legs: 164 of 519 explore days qualify (31 percent, not 10): the leg count RISES with the price level (mean legs by 11:00: 1.9 in 2023, 3.0 in 2024, 6.1 in 2025, 10.0 in 2026), so an expanding percentile goes stale. Legs SCALED to the market (reversal size = 0.1786 x ADR, the live 65 points in units of the 364-point ADR, nearest grid size 20-200 step 10, ADR = the median RTH range of the prior 20 sessions): 67 of 519 explore days qualify (12.9 percent), 14 / 34 / 51 / 34 by year 2023-2026. SCALED is the primary definition (67 days is above the 40-day floor Opus set; below 40 the test would not be run). The fixed-65 version is reported as a sensitivity and is NOT trusted (drift).
## 2. The trade (on a qualifying day, same fills as the overnight research, the real `sim()` of `ovn_sim.py`)
Entry at the 11:00 bar open plus one tick against, direction = the sign of the 09:30-10:59 up-tick minus down-tick volume (Sierra's trade-price rule, `scratch/updown_1m_20261104/`, summed over the RTH minutes 09:30-10:59), stop = 1.0 x ADR_t points (the prior-20-session median RTH range: a wide stop, data-derived, no model: the HAR-X scaling test showed a width model adds nothing over ADR), exit at the 15:55 open minus one tick, a stop beyond the market fills at the bar open, a touched stop at its price minus one tick, $2 per point, $2 round trip, 1 MNQ. Sensitivity (uncounted): stops of 0.5 x and 1.5 x ADR.
## 3. Controls on the SAME days (so the flow sign is judged against what any rule would earn)
RANDOM direction = the mean of the long and the short result of the same entry (exact, no draw); PRICE MOMENTUM = the sign of the 09:30-10:59 price change (close of the 10:59 bar minus the 09:30 open); ALWAYS LONG. Also the same trade on the NON-qualifying days.
## 4. Counted tests (3), week-block bootstrap CI at 98.33 percent (Bonferroni 3, 4,000 draws, seed 20261104), both chronological halves, detectable effect (2.8 x SE) printed BEFORE the verdicts
T1: flow-sign minus RANDOM on qualifying days, mean dollars per trade (the direction skill of the flow sign). T2: flow-sign minus PRICE MOMENTUM on qualifying days (does flow beat momentum). T3: the qualifying-minus-other-days contrast of T1's quantity (does the regime matter: (flow-sign minus random) on qualifying days minus the same on all other scored days). PLACEBO for T1: the flow sign circularly shifted across qualifying days (1,000 draws, a random offset of at least 3 qualifying days, the stop and the exit unchanged), the real T1 must exceed the 97.5th percentile of the zero-centred placebo. A test is a LEAD only if its CI excludes 0 (lower bound above 0), both halves are positive and the placebo is beaten. ADDITIONAL CONDITIONS for the whole idea to count as a LEAD (Opus's stop rule): the flow trade's ABSOLUTE mean on qualifying days is above 0 with the CI lower bound above 0 (relative skill with negative dollars is not a rule), flow-sign minus ALWAYS LONG has a CI lower bound above 0 (printed, not counted), and the qualifying days number at least 40.
## 5. Limits stated now
67 qualifying days, so only a large effect is detectable (printed); the regime is mostly a late-2025 to 2026 phenomenon (the overnight edge too) and explore ends 2025-05-16, so a null here is partly the regime not existing in explore: it would NOT prove the mechanism absent in 2026 (it would mean 'not shown in explore'; the forward race and a tick extraction of 2025-05 to 2026-10 are the routes). The flow sign of the first 90 minutes of RTH is a short window. The scaled-leg threshold (0.1786 x ADR) is the live 65 points expressed in ADR units, a convention, not tuned. A LEAD is a hypothesis for ONE sealed look (needs tick data for the later era) and a forward shadow arm, never a gate.
