# Pre-registration: $0 time-expiry audit, True-MTM decomposition by setup / level family / touch sequence, and macro trend-day variables — 2026-10-27

## Premise checks recorded before the run (so they cannot be adjusted afterwards)
1. The $2.90 per trade figure is the TRAIN gap between the flat-0 and mark-to-market conventions of the same-engine control. On Hold-Out the gap is about $0.30; most of the Hold-Out difference between the stored baseline (+$2.6) and the same-engine control (-$0.4) comes from the stored simulation's exact stop and target fills versus gap fills and the entry-bar rule. The claim "true MTM expectancy is near zero" refers to the pooled Mode 1 book as re-simulated, at $1 fee.
2. "Win rate matches the random-walk barrier probability": with a median 76-point stop and 41-point target the driftless barrier probability is about 65 percent; the stored rate is about 60 and the same-engine control about 63-64 percent, i.e. at or slightly below the barrier value. That is consistent with a near-zero gross edge but is a geometry statement, not a test.
3. "No filter can extract edge if the pooled mean is zero" is not strictly true: a zero pooled mean can hide positive and negative subsets, which is exactly what Part 2 tests, with a multiple-comparison control. It is also why the pooled result cannot be read as evidence about any single setup.
4. Read from the code before writing this document (no numbers): the BACKFILL generator `scripts/backfill_unified_levels.mjs` sets each level's stop to the p75 of MAE and the target to the p50 of MFE measured on the SAME touches it then resolves, and scores `TIME_EXPIRED` at 0. By the repository's own standing rule a target set to a percentile of the tested population makes the win rate partly a construction artifact, and a p75-MAE stop makes the stop-out share about 25 percent by construction (Bucket C was 25.5 percent). This is part of the Part 1 audit.

## Engine (identical to `study_geometry_sweep_depth_20261027.mjs`; the Part 2/3 control must reproduce Train -0.9 and Hold-Out -0.4 at the $1 fee on the 1,723 Mode 1 trades)
1-minute NQ bars; entry at the stored trigger price on bar `t`; the stop is checked on the entry bar (conservative), the target is not credited there; from bar `t+1` a stop gapped through fills at the bar open, a stop touch fills at the stop, then the target, stop before target; expiry bar = stored `expires_at` capped at the 15:59 bar; **trades unresolved at expiry resolve at the expiry bar's close (True MTM)**. Gross = `d x (exit - entry) x 2`; `Net_PnL_$1 = gross - 1`, `Net_PnL_$4 = gross - 4`. Deviation from the request's wording ("from t_entry+1 forward"): the entry-bar stop check is kept; it affects the 45 trades where the stored baseline resolved on the entry bar and is the more conservative reading. Same 5,324 trades, 336 dates, Train through 2026-01-02 (3,160 trades), Hold-Out the rest; Mode 1 pooled book rebuilt (1,723; the check line must print it). Within-group Mode 1 uses the stored exit bar `te` for the overlap test, as the pooled Mode 1 did.

## Part 1 — audit of the $0 time-expiry convention (descriptive, no test)
Method: (a) a scan of `scripts/` and `server/` for every statement that assigns `pnl = 0` (or `THEN 0`) next to `TIME_EXPIRED`, with file and line; (b) a classification of every query that includes `TIME_EXPIRED` in a resolution list and reads P&L as BLENDED (no `origin_status` / `REAL_TRADE_FILTER` / `BACKFILL` exclusion within the query text), REAL-ONLY, or DECIDED-ONLY (resolution list without `TIME_EXPIRED`, which omits expiries from expectancy instead); (c) the scheduled scripts (`run_daily_calibration.sh`, `run_weekly_backtests.sh`) among them and the `performance_audit` signal_type each writes; (d) whether server code reads that signal_type; (e) the size of the effect: for every BACKFILL `TIME_EXPIRED` row, the mark-to-market P&L at the expiry close (bars exist), aggregated to the per-setup_type shift in blended EV and the number of latest `SETUP_STATUS` rows whose blended EV crosses 0 or the -5 dollar bar; (f) the BACKFILL stop/target construction facts (generator header and code, plus the observed stop-out / target-hit / expiry shares). The classification is a text heuristic (±12 lines around each match) and is reported as such; flagged items are "worth a look", not proof.

## Part 2 — True MTM decomposition
Groups: `setup_id` = `setup_type`; `level_family` = a fixed parse of the level name (`setup_type` minus `_FADE_LONG|SHORT` and `_GAP_UP|DOWN`) into: IB_EDGE (IB_HIGH, IB_LOW, PD_IB_HIGH, PD_IB_LOW), IB_MID (IB_MID_SCALP, PD_IB_MID, 10D_IB_MID), OR_EDGE (OR5_HIGH, OR5_LOW), OR_MID (OR5_MID, PD_OR_MID, 5D_OR_MID), VAH (PD_VAH, PW_VAH, PM_VAH), VAL (PD_VAL, PW_VAL, PM_VAL), POC (PD_POC, PW_POC, PY_POC), PRIOR_HIGH (PD_HIGH, PW_HIGH, PM_HIGH), PRIOR_LOW (PD_LOW, PW_LOW, PM_LOW), PD_CLOSE_MID (PD_CLOSE, PD_SESSION_MID), OVERNIGHT (ONH, ONL), PERIOD_OPEN (DAILY_OPEN, WEEKLY_OPEN, MONTHLY_OPEN), CAMARILLA (CAM_R1-4, CAM_S1-4), FLOOR_PIVOTS (FLOOR_PIVOT, FLOOR_R1-3, FLOOR_S1-3), PERIOD_PIVOTS (WPP, WR1, WR2, WS1, WS2, MPP, MR1, MR2, MS1, MS2); any unmatched name is `Unlabeled_Level`. The stored `structural_level_type` column is reported for coverage only. `Touch_Seq`: over the full 5,324 trigger universe (not the deduplicated book), a trigger is `1` if no trigger with an EARLIER entry minute, same date, same direction has a level price within 15.0 points of its level price, else `2+` (triggers in the same minute share one touch; the gap in minutes to the nearest earlier same-zone trigger is also printed so near-in-time repeats are visible).
Views: View 1 = Within-group Mode 1 (the 5,324 trades deduplicated inside each group alone); View 2 = the 1,723 pooled Mode 1 trades partitioned by group. Table 1A (setups, View 1; View 2 compact), Table 1B (the 1,402 ACTIVE live-model rows by setup, Within-Setup Mode 1, split at the 2026-10-11 18:00 ET confound boundary; these rows were never subject to the $0 convention, their stored P&L is already marked to market, and the fee basis in the stored P&L is reported from the TARGET_HIT residual, so the $4 column is computed from that basis), Tables 2A (families) and 2B (Touch_Seq on the pooled Mode 1 book).
**Pass gate (fixed now), applied separately to setups, families and Touch_Seq groups:** candidates are groups with Train N >= 20 and Train True-MTM mean at the $1 fee > 0 (selected on Train only). For each candidate with Hold-Out N >= 20 the statistic is the Hold-Out t = mean/(sd/sqrt(n)) at the $1 fee. Null: within-date permutation of the group labels among the View 1 trades (trades keep their dates and outcomes; Train and Hold-Out are permuted independently; the same Train selection rule is applied to the permuted labels), 2,000 permutations, max-T over the selected candidates; a group passes if its Hold-Out mean is > 0 and its max-T adjusted p < 0.05. The 95 percent date-blocked interval of the Hold-Out mean is printed. Groups with Hold-Out N < 20 are marked UNDERPOWERED, not negative.

## Part 3 — macro trend-day variables (pooled Mode 1 book, same engine, strictly information up to bar `t-1`)
Exactly the four variables as specified: Session_VWAP_Anchor_Dist_ATR, Opening_Drive_Net_Move_ATR, Prior_Day_Range_Break_State (PD_Break_Dist_ATR), IB_Extension_Against_ATR (only for entries at or after 10:30). `Daily_ATR_14` = mean of the full RTH ranges (09:30-15:59 bars) of the prior 14 completed RTH sessions found in the loaded bar history (a session counts as completed with at least 330 RTH bars; expanding mean when fewer than 14 exist). `Session_VWAP` = cumulative typical price x volume from 09:30 through `t-1`. `PDH/PDL` = the previous completed RTH session. Table 3A: quartiles with Train cutoffs, count, win rate and mean at $1 and $4, Train and Hold-Out. Table 3B: the veto tail is the side the hypothesis names as toxic: Var 1 the LOW tail; Vars 2, 3, 4 the HIGH tail. On Train choose the tail share (15 or 20 percent of Train values) that minimizes the vetoed trades' mean True-MTM P&L at the $1 fee; freeze the cutoff; apply to Hold-Out. Printed: vetoed count, vetoed mean, all-trade mean, difference with a date-blocked 95 percent interval AND the one-sided 98.75 percent upper bound (Bonferroni over 4 variables), random-veto placebo band (5th-95th percentile of same-count random subsets, 2,000 draws), and the Hold-Out flip: the vetoed trades flipped at the stored trigger price with the original stop distance and 1.0R / 1.5R targets, True-MTM expiry, mean at $1 and $4 with the placebo of same-count random trades flipped identically. **Pass gate:** the Hold-Out one-sided 98.75 percent upper bound of (vetoed minus all-trade mean) is < 0 AND the vetoed mean is below the placebo 5th percentile. A flip is "viable" only if its Hold-Out lower 95 percent bound at $4 is > 0 and it exceeds the flip placebo 95th percentile.
