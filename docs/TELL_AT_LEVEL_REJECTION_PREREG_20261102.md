# Pre-registration: the tell as a launch pad at known levels, direction from the LEVEL reaction (written 2026-11-02 BEFORE the script exists or any outcome is read; user: "test the tell against known market levels ... the tell fired a long just beneath VWAP, where I would have waited and fired short off the next 1-3 500v candles")
Prior work (hr-052): the tell was conditioned only on the OPEN location (`open_location_join_tell_not_shown_20261028`), the tell side was never shown (14 direction tests) and a wait-for-flip entry was not shown. Not done before: using the tell only as an ACTIVITY flag near an intraday level and taking the direction from whether the level REJECTS price in the next 1-3 bars. Explore sessions only (516 sessions to 2025-05-16, 500-contract bars with volume at price); the sealed window is not read. Prior about 6 percent; the user's reading is a discretionary one, this is its mechanical version.
## Levels (causal, from bars completed at or before the bar in question; RTH only, 10:00-14:30 signals)
VWAP = the cumulative volume-weighted average price of the RTH bars so far (from the volume-at-price rows: sum of price x volume over sum of volume, from 09:30); ONH and ONL (overnight range); IBH and IBL (initial balance, only once the first RTH hour is complete). 
## Events (one per signal bar and nearest qualifying level)
Population TELL = every V1 simulated signal bar i (`trade.simulate`, 10:00-14:30). CONTROL = decision-grid bars (every 10th bar index, 10:00-14:30) with price moving and no tell in either direction, the earlier controls. For a bar i and level L: NEAR = |close[i] - L| <= 1 x the median (high - low) of the last 20 bars (a scale-free distance, as in the bracket test); the side s = below (close < L) or above. REJECTION within the next 3 bars: the first bar j in i+1..i+3 whose range touches L (high >= L - 1 tick for a level above, low <= L + 1 tick for a level below) AND closes back on the original side of L (rejected); a bar that touches and closes beyond L is a BREAK, not a rejection (counted and reported, not traded). Entry = the OPEN of bar j+1 (one tick adverse slip), direction AWAY from the level (short for a level above, long below). Nearest qualifying level wins; one event per signal bar. Cooldown: one event per level per 10 bars.
## Outcome and tests
y_H = direction x (close of the last bar completed within H minutes of bar j+1's open - the entry price) in points, H = 15, 30, 60, only for complete windows; gross, cost 1.25 points + 0.25 slip.
Counted tests (family `tell_at_level_20261102`, ledger tests = 3, BH q = 0.10): for H = 15, 30, 60: TELL events pooled over the levels, difference in mean y between TELL and CONTROL events (day-blocked bootstrap CI, 3000 draws), plus the TELL absolute mean. CANDIDATE = BH survivor, difference CI excludes zero, the TELL absolute mean above 1.5 points (cost) with its own CI above zero, same sign in both halves, at least 100 TELL events on at least 60 dates, else UNDERPOWERED.
Uncounted, printed: per level (VWAP, ONH/ONL, IBH/IBL) counts and means; tell direction toward vs away from the level (the user's case: a tell long just below VWAP) and the outcome of each; touch-then-break counts; rejection rate TELL vs CONTROL (does the tell change how often a level rejects, not just the move after); by year; time-of-day bin.
Resolution, stated now: a few hundred tell events resolve a mean move of about +/-4 to 5 points at 30 minutes (the 30-minute SD is 42 points), so a cost-sized edge is NOT resolvable for the tell arm; the control arm (thousands) sets the baseline. A pass is a lead for a forward test only; a null reads "no large tell-at-level effect".
