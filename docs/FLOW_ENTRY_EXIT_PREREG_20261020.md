Written and committed BEFORE any outcome of this test. Origin: the user (2026-10-20): the bubbles are only a visual proxy for the behavior behind them; use that behavior to prevent bad counter trades and to stay in good trades beyond the target when the volume backs it up. Priors to respect: aggregate flow as a "stay in" signal did NOT hold up earlier when properly normalized (order flow looked like a better "get defensive" signal than a "trust it" signal; `docs/OPEN_THREADS.md` 2026-10-04 entries), the T1-floor runner (stop snaps to T1, ride toward 1.5x) is already a real shipped shadow mechanism, and the standing rule that an exit test must not replace a banked win with a gamble (floor-preserving only), with realistic fills (a stop beyond the market fills at the bar open).

## Data and features (aggregate flow, no ticks: complete 2023-03..2026-10 on every minute bar)
`price_bars_primary` bid_volume (aggressive sells) and ask_volume (aggressive buys). Population: the 3.5-year RTH level-fade replay (`scratch/rotation_gate_replay_rows_ts_20261020.json`, 21,480 trades, entry = the touch bar close, stop/target from `OPTIMAL_STOP` as in the replay). All features are ranked against the previous 60 sessions at the same clock time (no fixed volume numbers), windows W in {5, 15, 30} minutes ending at the decision bar, max/min over W with a permutation null (scores permuted within calendar month, 1000 draws).
- IMB_adv(W): net aggressive flow AGAINST the trade, (adverse - favorable)/(adverse + favorable) (long trade: sells minus buys).
- STUFFED(W): rank(our-side aggressive effort) x rank(price result against us) (our side pushed and price still went against us).
- ABSORBED(W): rank(adverse aggressive effort) x rank(price result toward us) (the other side pushed and price still went our way).

## F1: prevent bad counter-trades (decision = the touch bar, known at its close)
Hypotheses (directional): Spearman(IMB_adv, pnl) < 0; Spearman(STUFFED, pnl) < 0; Spearman(ABSORBED, pnl) > 0. Bonferroni over the 3 (each at the 98.3/1.7th percentile of its null). Also as a gate: flag trades in the top third of the predicted-bad feature (cut from the explore half 2023-03-06..2025-05-31), diff = mean pnl unflagged minus flagged with a day-blocked CI, a 1000-draw random-flag placebo, hold-out 2025-06-01..2026-10-16 same sign with at least half the explore size, LONG and SHORT separately, and separately inside the rotation-gate AGREE roster and the DISAGREE trades (is it incremental to the POC gate?).
## F2: stay in winners (decision = the bar that first reaches T1)
Population: replay trades that reach T1 (their target) before their stop. Floor-preserving runner: after the T1 bar, the stop snaps to the T1 price (a long's stop fills at min(T1, bar open); a gap below T1 is a real loss versus banking, kept) and the runner rides to 1.5 x the original target distance or the session end (mark to the last close). EXTRA = runner pnl minus the banked T1 pnl, per trade (the quantity the extension adds or loses). Flow at the T1 bar in the trade's direction: IMB_fav(W) = favorable minus adverse over total, and ABSORBED/STUFFED analogues. Hypotheses: EXTRA is larger when IMB_fav is high (Spearman > 0, max over W with a null). As a rule: "extend only when IMB_fav is in the top third (explore-derived cut)" versus "always extend" versus "never extend (bank T1)": per-trade means with day-blocked CIs, placebo (random third), hold-out, LONG/SHORT. The always-extend baseline is computed in the same pass (the structural-advantage rule: an entry/exit difference must be compared to the same-exit baseline).
