# Pre-registration: variations of OVERNIGHT_ORDERFLOW that target its four named weaknesses (written 2026-11-04 BEFORE any variation outcome was computed)

STATUS: FROZEN. Trigger (user, 2026-11-04): the SHADOW trade OVERNIGHT_ORDERFLOW_SHORT (id 155007) fired at 23:59 after a ~470-point fall, was stopped at 28,340.5 (400 points, -$802) at 03:10 and the market ended even; user: "find variations that can improve the weaknesses". Research only, nothing live. Ledger family `ovn_orderflow_variations_20261104`, counted tests 4. Any survivor is a LEAD to be added as its own forward SHADOW setup (the repo's convention, CLAUDE.md cv-065), never a live rule.
## 1. The setup as built (baseline B0, `server/services/overnightOrderflowEntryDetector.js`, calibration `scripts/calibrate_overnight_orderflow_entry.mjs`)
Population: >= 6 confirmed 65-point rotation legs by midnight ET (`detectRotationLegs` on the 18:00-23:59 1-minute bars). Direction: the sign of the 18:00-21:00 ET order-flow net (ask volume minus bid volume of the 1-minute bars; positive = LONG). Entry at the 00:00 bar open plus 1 tick against, stop 400 points (the calibrated cell: stop 400, exit 14:30), exit at the 14:30 bar open, flatten fills as in the tell code (a stop beyond the market fills at the bar open, stop fills at its price minus 1 tick), MNQ 2 dollars per point, 2 dollars commission. The sample is every trade date 2023-03-07..2026-11-03 with >= 425 overnight and >= 380 RTH one-minute bars (the live calibration used only 60 days from 2025-11-20 because of an old data-cleanliness cutoff; the explore / hold-out bar files were validated against the tick data for price, and the sign of the flow does not depend on the 2025 volume inflation).
## 2. The four weaknesses and the variations (all causal at 00:00; cuts come from EARLIER sessions only, expanding quantiles)
W1 BLIND TO STRENGTH (the flow lean was only -3.5 percent of volume): F1 = trade only when |flow share| is at or above the expanding median of earlier qualifying sessions. W2 BLIND TO WHERE PRICE IS (it had already fallen 1.3 ADR): F2 = skip when the move since the 18:00 open IN THE TRADE DIRECTION (c at 23:59 minus the 18:00 open, in the trade direction, in units of the prior-20 median RTH range, ADR) is above the expanding 67th percentile of earlier qualifying sessions. W3 CHOP (41 rotations is balance, not trend): F3 = skip when the rotation count by midnight is above the expanding 67th percentile. W4 RISK (400 points = 800 dollars, 40 percent of the 2,000 dollar trailing loss): the stop sweep {100, 150, 200, 300, 400, 700} with the exit at 14:30, reported as mean dollars, worst loss and the share stopped, and the stop that keeps the loss under 300 dollars (150 points) against 400.
Combined F12 = F1 and F2; F123 = all three (printed, uncounted).
## 3. Counted tests (4) and reading
T1 F1: mean dollars of the retained trades minus the dropped trades, day-block 95 percent CI (2000 draws); T2 F2 the same; T3 F3 the same; T4 the 150-point stop minus the 400-point stop, mean dollars per trade of the baseline set, paired day-block CI (the price of limiting the risk to 300 dollars). LEAD for T1-T3: CI lower bound above 0 on the whole sample AND the same sign in both chronological halves; T4 is a cost, read with the worst-loss table. Printed, uncounted: the baseline's own numbers (N, win rate, mean, hit rate of the following RTH direction, the live calibration's claims for comparison), exit-time sweep {09:30, 12:00, 14:30, 15:55}, the sample split by year, the 2025-11-20 onward subset, the weak-lean / chased / choppy subsets on their own.
## 4. Limits
About 230 qualifying trades in total, so every contrast has wide intervals; the filters are the user's weaknesses made measurable, not tuned; quantile cuts are conventions (median, 67th percentile); the later half overlaps the live calibration's window; a filter that helps here is a hypothesis for forward shadow testing, and nothing in this thread changes the live detector or its SHADOW status.

## 5. ADDENDUM A1 (2026-11-04, after the 87-night results; written BEFORE the all-nights run): give the variants a data-derived basis by using EVERY night
Reason: only 87 of 878 exported nights satisfy the >= 6-legs rule, so no filter could be tested with power, and the user asked what the variants are based on (answer: the user's observation and a post-hoc 1.0 ADR cut on N=5; the pre-registered percentile filters showed nothing). The order-flow thesis (the 18:00-21:00 flow sign predicts the day) does not itself depend on the legs rule, so the SAME trade (00:00 entry in the flow-sign direction, 400-point stop, exit 14:30, same fills) is run on ALL nights with a non-zero flow and >= 15 prior sessions for the ADR. Counted tests (2): T5 the flow-sign trade on ALL nights versus a random-direction placebo (does the thesis hold beyond the legs rule); T6 legs >= 6 minus legs < 6, mean dollars per trade, day-block CI. Printed, uncounted: mean dollars, win rate and RTH-direction hit rate by rotation-count bucket {0-2, 3-5, 6-9, 10+}, by flow-strength quintile, by the move already made in the trade direction (terciles and the 1.0 ADR cut), by year, LONG vs SHORT. Quintile and tercile cuts are over all nights (a descriptive map, not a trading rule); any rule that comes out of it still needs its own pre-registration and a forward SHADOW record.

## 6. ADDENDUM A2 (2026-11-04, after the all-nights result; written BEFORE this run): replace the leg count by data-derived volatility-regime measures, and add the missing control arm
Why: on all 863 nights the flow-sign trade works only where the rotation count by midnight is high and only in 2026 (legs >= 6: +239 vs +7). The leg count is probably a stand-in for 'the market is volatile now'. Candidate measures, all causal at 00:00 and in ADR units (ADR = the prior-20 median RTH range): R1 = the overnight range so far (18:00-23:59 high minus low) / ADR; R2 = the ADR itself as a percentile of its own earlier history (is the market in a high-volatility era); R5 = the prior RTH range / ADR (prior-day turbulence, the earlier next-day-type study's strongest predictor). A night is 'ON' when its measure is at or above the EXPANDING 90th percentile of all EARLIER nights (the first 60 nights have no cut and are not scored; an expanding cut keeps the era information: a volatile era turns many nights ON, a quiet one almost none). The leg count (>= 6) is the comparator on the same scored nights.
CONTROL ARM (hr-042 / cv-026, missing before): on the same nights, same entry, stop and exit, trade (M1) in the direction of the overnight PRICE move (c at 23:59 minus the 18:00 open) and (M2) against it; if the flow sign does not beat plain overnight momentum the flow adds nothing.
Counted tests (4): T1-T3 for R1, R2, R5: mean dollars per trade of the flow-sign trade, ON nights minus OFF nights, day-block 95 percent CI; LEAD = CI lower bound above 0 and the same sign in both chronological halves. T4: on the legs >= 6 nights, flow-sign trade minus the momentum arm M1, paired per night, day-block CI. Printed, uncounted: overlap of each ON set with legs >= 6, ON counts and means by year and by quarter (does the measure switch OFF in 2023-2025 and warn when the regime fades), the stop sweep {150, 200, 300, 400} inside the best ON set, win rate and RTH-direction hit rate, combinations (R1 ON and legs >= 6, R1 ON or legs >= 6).

## 7. ADDENDUM A3 (2026-11-04, after the regime result; written BEFORE this run): a SCALED rotation threshold (the fixed 65-point reversal size is a static threshold that drifts with the price level)
Result of A2: none of R1 / R2 / R5 reproduces the leg-count effect (ON minus OFF +56 / +25 / +67, CIs include 0) while legs >= 6 gives +231 (CI +99..+365); the flow-sign trade also beat the overnight-price-momentum control (+186, CI +38..+343). Every definition earns its money in 2026 (legs ON: 2023 0 nights, 2024 4, 2025 19, 2026 64). Observation that motivates A3 (not yet tested): NQ went from about 12,000 (2023) to about 29,000 (2026), so the SAME 65-point reversal is a 0.5 percent swing in 2023 and 0.2 percent now; six such legs by midnight are far easier now than then, i.e. the leg count mixes 'many swings' with 'a higher index level', and CLAUDE.md hr-003 says thresholds are derived from the data's own scale, not fixed.
Variant: the rotation reversal size is SCALED, R* = c x ADR with c = 65 / 364 = 0.1786 (the live setting in units of today's ADR; ADR = the prior-20 median RTH range at each night), the confirmed legs by midnight are counted with the real detector at the grid size nearest to R* (grid 20..200 step 10, exported), and a night is ON when scaled legs >= 6 (the live count threshold unchanged). Counted tests (3): T1 scaled-legs ON minus OFF, mean dollars of the flow-sign trade (stop 400, exit 14:30), day-block CI, LEAD = CI lower bound above 0 and the same sign in both chronological halves; T2 the scaled ON nights of 2023-2025 only: the flow-sign trade's mean versus a circular-shift placebo of the flow sign within that subset (does an edge exist outside 2026); T3 on the scaled ON nights flow-sign trade minus the overnight-momentum arm M1, paired day-block CI. Printed, uncounted: ON counts and means by year for the fixed-65 and the scaled rule, their overlap, the legs-at-scaled-R distribution by year, the stop sweep inside the scaled ON set.
