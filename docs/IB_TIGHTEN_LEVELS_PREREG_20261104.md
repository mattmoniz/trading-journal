## A. IB-extension day types as a state known during the day (explore, 522 sessions, 500-contract bars) [family `ib_daytype_20261104`, tests 3]
IB = RTH high / low of the bars completed by 10:30; IBR = IBH - IBL. At t in {11:00, 12:00, 13:00} extension up = max(0, high so far - IBH), down = max(0, IBL - low so far), both in IBR units; a side is 'extended' if its extension exceeds 0.1 IBR (convention for 'inside the IB', close to the doc's 85 percent rule). STATE: NORMAL (no side extended), NEUTRAL (both sides extended), VARIATION (one side, extension <= 2.0 IBR), TREND (one side, extension > 2.0 IBR). X = total extension in IBR units (continuous). Counted: T1 AUC of X at 13:00 for P/b versus D (final letter); T2 strong B versus strong D at 12:00: AUC of X minus AUC of the watcher's BALANCE_CONFIRMED indicator (a paired week-block CI), i.e. does the IB state separate the two kinds of balance-looking day better than the watcher; T3 stop width: among the tell entries from 10:30 on, (net at 2.0 x the node stop minus net at 1.0 x) for entries made in a NORMAL state minus the same for entries made in any other state (day-block CI), from `tell_ladder_rows.csv`. Printed: state frequencies and final-shape rates by state at each t.

## C. Carried-forward levels: do they attract price more than equal-distance random levels? (explore, 1-minute bars) [family `carried_levels_20261104`, tests 3]
Prior session's RTH profile: volume levels (PD POC, VAH, VAL from `profile_shape.py`); TPO levels from 30-minute blocks per the TradingView TPO rules (row size auto ~80 rows with the doc's increment rounding, 70 percent value area built row by row from the POC with the doc's tie rules); poor high / poor low (extreme row with more than one TPO block); single-print zones (runs of >= 2 consecutive non-extreme rows with exactly one TPO block, the edge nearest the open is the level). EVENT per level per day: signed distance d = level - today's open (|d| >= 1 point); REACH = price trades through the level between 09:35 and 16:00 (also by 12:00, printed). CONTROL: every OTHER day's reach of the level at the same distance in units of that day's prior-20 median RTH range from that day's own open (volatility-normalised); paired difference = event reach - mean control reach, day-block bootstrap CI (2000). Counted: T1 PD volume POC / VAH / VAL pooled; T2 poor highs and lows; T3 single-print zones. Printed, uncounted: the TPO-based POC / VAH / VAL, PD high / low, by level, reach by 12:00. A paired difference with a CI above 0 means the level attracts more than distance alone; below 0 means it is avoided.
