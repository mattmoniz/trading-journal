# Production & Research Specification (v2 — Lean Unified `M = 2` Architecture): Liquidity Vacuum Pullback & Continuation Engine (`NQ` Signal / `1 MNQ` Execution)

## 1. Scope, Governance, `int64` Causality & Compiled Runtime Architecture
Engineer a deterministic, zero-lookahead, byte-replayable research and execution sidecar for intraday Nasdaq-100 futures across **two liquidity-matched volume-bar tracks (`M = 2`: `RTH VB = 500`, `Globex VB = 100`)**, pairing a **lean 4-state single-leg continuation engine** with full institutional execution and research rigor (`G0`–`G4`):

- **Signal vs. Execution Ground Truth (`server/config/instruments.js`):**
  - **Signal Tape (`signal_symbol`):** Front-month `NQ` (`tick_size = 0.25`). Volume bars (`VB_RTH = 500` contracts in RTH; `VB_GBX = 100` contracts in Globex, matching ~5:1 CME book depth so bar cadence and 10-bar order-book sweep physics align 1:1 across sessions) are built strictly from `NQ` `.scid` logical trades.
  - **Execution Sizing & Commission (`LIVE_INSTRUMENT`):** Strictly **`1 MNQ` contract** (`dollarsPerPoint = $2.00`, `commissionPerRoundTrip = $2.00`, i.e., **`$1.00/side` on every filled entry or exit**—the user-confirmed ground truth in `server/config/instruments.js`). Every trade is a **single-leg runner** (`1 trade == 1 cycle`, `$2.00` round-trip commission). Define instrument round-trip friction in points directly from `LIVE_INSTRUMENT`: $\text{cost\_rt\_pts} = \frac{\text{commissionPerRoundTrip}}{\text{dollarsPerPoint}} + 2 \cdot \text{tick\_size} = 1.50\text{ pts}$. (Institutional `1 NQ` scaling and alternative broker fee schedules may be logged strictly as non-gating descriptive context.)
- **Bit-Exact `int64` Timestamps, `float64` Price Cast & Causal Observability (`obs_ts_us`):**
  - Store **all** timestamps and wire-latency offsets as **`int64` UTC epoch microseconds** (`start_ts_us`, `end_ts_us`, `obs_ts_us`, `delta_lat_us = 15_000`). Convert `(end_ts_us - start_ts_us) * 1e-6` to `float64` **strictly** inside $\Delta t_{\text{raw}, t}$, and compute ET wall-clock times via IANA `America/New_York` (never fixed UTC offsets).
  - **Mandatory `float64` Ingestion Cast:** Immediately convert raw `.scid` `float32` fields (`o, h, l, c`) via `round_to_tick(float(val) / 100.0)` into `float64` at ingestion (preventing NumPy 2.0 `float32` preservation).
  - **Record-by-Record `TradeMerger` Ingestion (`obs_ts_us`):** **Forbid** chunked `read_ticks()` in batch mode. Both batch and live must iterate `_raw_records()` 40-byte tuples one-by-one into `TradeMerger.feed(...)` so every emitted logical `Trade` carries **`obs_ts_us`**: the `int64` UTC timestamp of the raw `.scid` record whose ingestion flushed that logical `Trade` (capturing `pending_unbundled` delay). Maintain `bars_sealed_by_event` (0, 1, or multiple bars on large sweeps) and iterate `for k in bars_sealed_by_event:` in ascending bar order—never overwrite `{m_emit: k}`.
- **Compiled CSR Fast-Path Engine (Mandatory Runtime Requirement):**
  - Pre-extract per-session CSR arrays (`int64 obs_ts_us`, `float64 px/bid/ask`, `bar_event_offsets[k]`, and `[bar_min_px[k], bar_max_px[k]]`—the min/max `tick_px` on **`exec_stream`** across `(obs_ts_us[k-1], obs_ts_us[k]]`) once, and execute all backtest and `5,000`-draw control passes in **`Numba @njit` (or Cython/Rust)** using an **exact $O(1)$ bar-bounding fast path**: skip intra-bar ticks when `state` $\in \{\text{FLAT}, \text{PULLBACK\_ARMED}\}$, or when `state == IN_POSITION` has `ratchet_due_us < 0`, `bar_end_obs_ts_us < t_flat_us`, and `[bar_min_px[k], bar_max_px[k]]` strictly clear of `stop_px` (and strictly clear of `dll_flatten_px` when a `dllGate` overlay is active). **Whenever intra-bar ticks are skipped in `IN_POSITION` (with $\ge 1$ `exec_stream` print in slice $k$), update `pos_ext_px = max(pos_ext_px, bar_max_px[k])` (`+1`) / `min(pos_ext_px, bar_min_px[k])` (`-1`)** so `mfe_R` and Chandelier ratchets match brute-force tick iteration identically; drop into the intra-bar tick slice whenever any boundary can fire. Gate Stage 0 on a unit test proving **0-bit (`0.00`) divergence** against brute-force every-tick iteration across a 10-session slice.
- **Canonical Infrastructure Reuse & Live Operational Safety:**
  1. **Backwards-Compatible `(TradeMerger, SessionBars)` Layer:** Reuse `scripts/tick_microstructure/scid_reader.py` (`TradeMerger`, `SCID_PRICE_SCALE = 100.0`) and extend `scripts/amt_tell_live/barbuilder.py` (`SessionBars(overflow='split', vb=500)`—preserving default `vb=500` backwards compatibility for `amt_tell_live` while passing `vb=500` for `RTH` and `vb=100` for `Globex`, plus `int64` microsecond timestamps; `overflow='whole'` run as Stage-0 invariance check). Run **separate** instances for `RTH` and `Globex` (plus synchronized `MNQ.scid` in coupled/live mode), reading whole 40-byte records (`header_size + 40 * ((file_size - header_size) // 40)`), holding `pending_unbundled` across live polls, calling `merger.flush()` **only** at track close, and **discarding** `< vb` partial bars at track close. Configure Sierra Chart `.scid` flush `< 100 ms`.
  2. **Order Routing, Grace-Window Reconciliation & `CancelAllOrders` Safety (`G2+`):** Route exclusively through `server/services/sierraChart/` (`orderSweep.js` extended with **Limit IOC / Fill-And-Kill (`FAK`)** entry routing and server-side **OCO** linking resting Stop-Market orders with Market flattens; if a stop-ratchet modification crosses current touch or rejects as marketable, immediately convert to Market `placeExitOrder`).
     - **Entry-Only Staleness & Clock-Skew Halt:** Suppress **new entries only** (`-> FLAT`) when `wall_clock_us - obs_ts_us[k] > 500_000` or `nq_tailer.backlog_bytes() >= 40` or `mnq_tailer.backlog_bytes() >= 40` (**never** suppress exits, flattens, or stop ratchets). If `obs_ts_us[k] - wall_clock_us > 100_000` (`CLOCK_SKEW_HALT`), issue **`CancelAllOrders` + Market flatten if `broker_pos != 0`**, force `FLAT`, and disarm.

After grace expiry: if `broker_pos == 0` while `position != 0`, issue `CancelAllOrders`, fail-closed to `FLAT`, and disarm; if `broker_pos != 0` while `position == 0` (or sign/qty disagrees), **issue `CancelAllOrders` + emergency Market flatten, force `FLAT`, and disarm (`RECONCILIATION_HALT`)**.
  3. **Research Ledger Layer:** Log every outcome-reading run via `scripts/lib/look_ledger.mjs` (`logLook`, `spendSealedLook`), compute diagnostics via `server/services/rigorDiagnostics.js` (`computeRigor`, `computeReplication`, `collapseClusterSiblings`, `dayBlockedBootstrapCI`, `dayBlockedBootstrapDeltaCI`), record claims via `scripts/record_claim.mjs`, update `docs/IMPROVEMENT_REGISTER_20261104.md` (`monitorNextSteps.js` if surfaced), and rebuild `docs/RESEARCH_INDEX.md`.

## 3. Lean, Scale-Free Feature Definitions (Whole-Track & `ATR_20d` Normalized)

Let nominal lengths be $L_{\text{imp}} = 10$ (10-bar impulse window: `5,000` `NQ` contracts in RTH, `1,000` `NQ` contracts in Globex), $L_{\text{tr}} = 10$, $L_{\text{reclaim}} = 2$, $L_{\text{delta}} = 3$, with dynamic warmup **`warmup_bars = max(30, L_imp + L_tr)`** (`30` bars nominal: ~10 min in RTH, ~30–40 min in Globex).
**Homogeneous Daily EOD Prior Pipeline (`17:00–18:00` ET):** Select up to 20 most recent completed non-holiday same-track sessions with $N_{\text{bars}, s} \ge 60$ ($K_{\text{qual}} \in [1, 20]$). Define the **normalized session-equal-weighted `step` empirical quantile** $\text{Quantile}^{\text{eq}}_q$ with bar weights $w_{s, b} = \frac{1}{K_{\text{qual}} \cdot N_{\text{valid}, s}}$ ($\sum_{s, b} w_{s, b} \equiv 1.0$). Compute homogeneously per track:
1. $\text{ATR}_{\text{track}, 20\text{d}, d} = \text{Mean}_{s \in K_{\text{qual}}}(\max_{b \in s} H_{\text{adj}, b} - \min_{b \in s} L_{\text{adj}, b})$.
2. $\Delta t_{\text{floor}, d} = \text{Quantile}^{\text{eq}}_{0.01}(\{\Delta t_{\text{raw}} > 0\})$, $\Delta t_b = \max(\Delta t_{\text{raw}, b}, \Delta t_{\text{floor}, d}, 10^{-3})$, and **whole-track session median duration** $\text{BaseDur}_{\text{track}, d} = \text{Median}_{s \in K_{\text{qual}}}(\text{Median}_{b \in s}(\Delta t_b))$ *(never normalized by clock hour—dead midday lunch chop and quiet Asian drift naturally fall below threshold while active institutional flow wakes up automatically)*.
3. Whole-track quantiles for $\text{Activity}_b = \frac{\text{BaseDur}_{\text{track}, d}}{\Delta t_b}$ and $\text{CLV}_b$, plus Level-2 `max_pb_bars` (§4.1).

### 3.1 True Range & Whole-Track Tape Velocity (`Activity_t`)
Re-seed `EMA_TR[0] = TR[0]` whenever `track_bars_since_open == 0` ($\alpha = \frac{2}{L_{\text{tr}} + 1}$):
$$TR_t = \begin{cases} H_{\text{adj}, t} - L_{\text{adj}, t} & \text{if } \text{track\_bars\_since\_open}_t == 0 \\ \max(H_{\text{adj}, t} - L_{\text{adj}, t}, |H_{\text{adj}, t} - C_{\text{adj}, t-1}|, |L_{\text{adj}, t} - C_{\text{adj}, t-1}|) & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}, \quad EMA\_TR_t = \text{EMA}(TR_t, L_{\text{tr}})$$
$$\Delta t_t = \max\left((\text{end\_ts\_us}_t - \text{start\_ts\_us}_t) \times 10^{-6},\; \Delta t_{\text{floor}, d},\; 10^{-3}\right), \quad \text{Activity}_t = \frac{\text{BaseDur}_{\text{track}, d}}{\Delta t_t}$$
- **RTH Active Entry Window:** $\text{is\_fast\_tape}_t \iff \text{Activity}_t \ge \text{Quantile}^{\text{eq}}_{0.60}(\text{Activity}_{\text{RTH}, 20\text{d}})$.
- **Globex Active Entry Window (outside `[08:28:00, 08:31:00)` ET):** $\text{is\_fast\_tape}_t \iff \text{Activity}_t \ge \text{Quantile}^{\text{eq}}_{0.75}(\text{Activity}_{\text{GBX}, 20\text{d}})$.
- **Elsewhere:** $\text{is\_fast\_tape}_t = \text{False}$.

### 3.2 `ATR_20d` Impulse Displacement, Directional Efficiency & Reclaim Flow
For `track_bars_since_open >= L_imp`, let $d_{\text{cand}, t} = +1$ if $C_{\text{adj}, t} \ge C_{\text{adj}, t - L_{\text{imp}}}$ else $-1$:
1. **Scale-Free Impulse Displacement (`Disp_OK_t`):**
   $$\text{Disp}_{10, t} = \frac{|C_{\text{adj}, t} - C_{\text{adj}, t - L_{\text{imp}}}|}{\text{ATR}_{\text{track}, 20\text{d}, d}}, \quad \text{Disp\_OK}_t \iff |C_{\text{adj}, t} - C_{\text{adj}, t - L_{\text{imp}}}| \ge \max\left(0.10 \times \text{ATR}_{\text{track}, 20\text{d}, d},\; 2.5 \times EMA\_TR_t,\; 10.0 \times \text{cost\_rt\_pts}\right)$$
2. **True Liquidity Vacuum (`Vacuum_OK_t` — One-Way Slicing + Aggressive Delta Imbalance):**
   $$\text{Efficiency}_{10, t} = \frac{|C_{\text{adj}, t} - C_{\text{adj}, t - L_{\text{imp}}}|}{\max\left(\sum_{j=0}^{L_{\text{imp}}-1} TR_{t-j},\; \text{tick\_size}\right)} \ge 0.45 \;\land\; \text{DeltaImb}_{10, t} = \frac{d_{\text{cand}, t} \cdot \sum_{j=0}^{L_{\text{imp}}-1} \text{delta}_{t-j}}{\sum_{j=0}^{L_{\text{imp}}-1} \text{volume}_{t-j}} \ge 0.15$$
   *(Rejects two-way FOMC/news whipsaws where `TR` is huge in both directions, capturing genuine one-sided order-book vacuums with zero nested `EMA/StdDev` collinearity.)*
3. **Wave-1 Impulse Qualification:** $\text{Impulse\_Qualifies}_t \iff \text{Disp\_OK}_t \land \text{Vacuum\_OK}_t \land \text{is\_fast\_tape}_t$.
4. **Reclaim Order-Flow Alignment (`Flow_Aligned_{dir, t}`):** With $\text{CLV}_t = 0.50$ if $H_{\text{raw}, t} == L_{\text{raw}, t}$ else $\frac{C_{\text{raw}, t} - L_{\text{raw}, t}}{H_{\text{raw}, t} - L_{\text{raw}, t}}$ and $\Delta_{3, t} = \sum_{i=0}^{L_{\text{delta}}-1} \text{delta}_{t-i}$:
   - $\text{Flow\_Aligned}_{+1, t} \iff (\text{CLV}_t \ge \text{Quantile}^{\text{eq}}_{0.60}(\text{CLV}_{\text{track}, 20\text{d}})) \land (\Delta_{3, t} > 0)$
   - $\text{Flow\_Aligned}_{-1, t} \iff (\text{CLV}_t \le \text{Quantile}^{\text{eq}}_{0.40}(\text{CLV}_{\text{track}, 20\text{d}})) \land (\Delta_{3, t} < 0)$

## 4. Lean 4-State Continuation FSM & Complete State Table

### 4.1 Uncensored Pullback Duration Scanner (`max_pb_bars`) & State Table
**Uncensored Pullback Scanner (EOD over `K_qual` sessions, resetting at every session close/halt):** Single-slot pass over `track_bars_since_open >= warmup_bars` calling §5.3.2's **exact `PULLBACK_ARMED` update & reclaim step** with `max_pb_bars = inf` (uncensored until session close): arm on `Impulse_Qualifies[k]` (tracking `imp_peak_adj` and resetting `pb_bars = 0` on pre-25% extensions, aborting to `FLAT` if `imp_bars > 30`); compute `retrace_frac` unconditionally on every bar, aborting immediately without recording if `retrace_frac > 0.50` or `dir * (C_adj - wave_origin_adj) <= 0` or session close; record `pb_bars` and reset to `FLAT` when `pb_reached_25pct`, `pb_bars >= 2`, `dir * (C_adj - Ext_2) > 0`, `dir * (C_adj - imp_peak_adj) <= 1.0 * EMA_TR`, and `Flow_Aligned` hold. ## 5. Two-Stage Protocol, Merged Event Loop & Runner Trail Mechanics

### 5.1 Stage 4a — Exit-Agnostic Forward-Drift Screen (`M = 2` Arms, Pooled-Day Hour-Stratified)
Log `tests = 2` jointly (`RTH_CONT_VB500`, `GBX_CONT_VB100`; **immediately reset `PULLBACK_ARMED -> FLAT` upon scoring a Wave-2 Reclaim on bar $t$**, enforcing a 30-bar per-direction cooldown from $t$) at primary horizon **`h = 30` bars** (`h = 10, 60` descriptive only), with $b_{\text{flat}}(d)$ the last bar having `end_ts_us <= t_flat_us(d)` and raw unslipped `NQ` $P_{\text{exec, adj}, t} = \text{tick\_px}[e_{\text{exec}}] + \text{cum\_roll\_offset}_d$ at $\tau_{\text{us}} \ge \text{obs\_ts\_us}[t] + 15\_000$:
$$\tilde{D}_t = \text{dir}_t \cdot \frac{C_{\text{adj}, \min(t + h,\; b_{\text{flat}}(d))} - P_{\text{exec, adj}, t}}{\text{ATR}_{\text{track}, 20\text{d}, d}}$$
In each bootstrap iteration $b \in \{1 \dots 10\_000\}$, **pool triggers across the resampled day-set $\{d^*_1, \dots, d^*_{|D|}\}$ by `hour_et` $h$**, restricting to joint non-empty strata $H^* = \{h : N^*_{\text{cand}, h} > 0 \land N^*_{\text{flow}, h} > 0\}$ with renormalized weights $w^*_h = N^*_{\text{cand}, h} / \sum_{j \in H^*} N^*_{\text{cand}, j}$ to compute $\sum_{h \in H^*} w^*_h (\bar{D}^*_{\text{cand}, h} - \bar{D}^*_{\text{flow}, h})$ against **`FAST_FLOW_CONTROL`** ($d_{\text{cand}} \cdot (C_{\text{adj}, t} - C_{\text{adj}, t - L_{\text{imp}}}) > 0 \land \text{is\_fast\_tape}_t \land \text{Flow\_Aligned}_{d_{\text{cand}}, t}$, 30-bar cooldown). Retire (`"not shown"`) any track failing `M = 2` BH-FDR $q \le 0.05$ or two-sided 95% CI $Q_{0.025} > 0$.

### 5.2 Merged Event Stream, Velocity-Scaled `slip_ticks(Activity)` & Tick Resolution Loop
Define scale-free **`slip_ticks(Activity)`**: `1` tick if $\text{Activity} \le 2.0$, `2` ticks if $2.0 < \text{Activity} \le 4.0$, and `3` ticks if $\text{Activity} > 4.0$. Merge in-track logical trades ordered by **`(obs_ts_us, stream_priority, seq)`**: in coupled/live mode (`exec_stream = MNQ`), **`stream_priority = 0` for `MNQ` execution events** (resolving active fills/stops/exits at identical microsecond **before** `NQ` bar-close emission) and **`stream_priority = 1` for `NQ` signal events**; in `NQ`-only mode, `exec_stream = NQ`. Define touch-anchored execution base prices on `exec_stream` event $e$ (using valid fresh BBO `0 < bid <= ask`, age `<= 1_000_000 us` when present, else `tick_px[e]`):
- `buy_base_px(e) = max(tick_px[e], ask[e])`, `sell_base_px(e) = min(tick_px[e], bid[e])`.

Process each chronological event $e$ in strict order:
1. **On `exec_stream` events while `state == PENDING_ENTRY` (strictly after trigger event, `entry_due_us > 0`):**
   - If $\tau_{\text{us}, e} \ge t_{\text{flat, us}}$ $\rightarrow$ cancel to `FLAT` (`trade_id = None`, `entry_due_us = -1`).
   - Else if $\tau_{\text{us}, e} \ge \text{entry\_due\_us}$: let `base_px = buy_base_px(e)` (`+1`) / `sell_base_px(e)` (`-1`), $P_{\text{impact}} = \text{round\_to\_tick}(\text{base\_px} + \text{dir} \cdot \text{slip\_ticks}(\text{entry\_activity}) \cdot \text{tick\_size})$, and set `entry_due_us = -1`. If $\text{dir} \cdot (P_{\text{impact}} - (\text{entry\_ref\_px} + \text{dir} \cdot \text{entry\_prot\_pts})) > 0$, expire **`UNFILLED_PROTECTION` $\rightarrow$ `FLAT` (`trade_id = None`)**; else fill at `fill_px = P_impact` (`$1.00` commission), set `position = dir`, `state = IN_POSITION`, `stop_px = entry_stop_ref`, **`R_pts = entry_init_risk_pts`** (`>= 4.0 * cost_rt_pts > 0`), and `pos_ext_px = fill_px`.
2. **On `exec_stream` events while `state == IN_POSITION` (strictly after fill event, using causal `Activity_prev` of last sealed `NQ` bar):**
   - Update `pos_ext_px = max(pos_ext_px, tick_px[e])` (`+1`) / `min(pos_ext_px, tick_px[e])` (`-1`).
   - If `ratchet_due_us > 0` and $\tau_{\text{us}, e} \ge \text{ratchet\_due\_us}$: set `stop_px = pending_stop_px`, `ratchet_due_us = -1`.
   - Evaluate in OCO priority (letting `mkt_exit_base = sell_base_px(e)` for `+1` / `buy_base_px(e)` for `-1`):
     a. $\tau_{\text{us}, e} \ge t_{\text{flat, us}}$ (or optional `dllGate` overlay): exit at `mkt_exit_base - dir * slip_ticks(Activity_prev) * tick_size` (`$1.00` commission), reset `ratchet_due_us = -1` $\rightarrow$ `FLAT`.
     b. `dir * (tick_px[e] - stop_px) <= 0`: exit at `min(tick_px[e], stop_px)` (`+1`) / `max(tick_px[e], stop_px)` (`-1`) (or `mkt_exit_base` if breached immediately upon ratchet activation) `- dir * slip_ticks(Activity_prev) * tick_size` (`$1.00` commission), reset `ratchet_due_us = -1` $\rightarrow$ `FLAT`.
3. **On `NQ` signal events:** Feed `NQ` logical trade $e$ into `SessionBars` and iterate **`for k in bars_sealed_by_event[e]:`** in ascending bar index order through §5.3 (if an exit/stop occurred at this exact `obs_ts_us`, forbid arming or entering on bar $k$).

### 5.3 Bar-Close FSM Handlers (4-State Pullback Reclaim + Chandelier Runner Trail)
Define **`Common_Entry_Gates[k]`**: inside §2.2 Active Entry Window (`track_bars_since_open >= warmup_bars`, outside `[08:28:00, 08:31:00)` ET on Globex), `is_fast_tape[k] == True`, `dllGate` unlocked, and (in coupled/live mode) staleness & §6.1 spread/basis gates clear. Set $\text{prot\_pts}_k = \max(\text{round\_to\_tick}(0.50 \times EMA\_TR_k),\; 4 \cdot \text{tick\_size})$.
1. **From `FLAT`:** Inside §2.2 Active Entry Window, if `Impulse_Qualifies[k]`: set `dir = d_cand[k]`, seed `wave_origin_adj`, `imp_peak_adj`, `pb_ext_adj`, `pb_ext_raw_px`, `pb_ext_mnq_px`, `pb_reached_25pct = False`, `imp_bars = 0`, `pb_bars = 0`, and set `state = PULLBACK_ARMED`.
2. **While `PULLBACK_ARMED`:**
   - Let `bar_ext_adj = H_adj[k]` (`+1`) / `L_adj[k]` (`-1`).
     - **If `not pb_reached_25pct` and `dir * (bar_ext_adj - imp_peak_adj) > 0`:** update `imp_peak_adj = bar_ext_adj`, re-seed `pb_ext_adj` (`L_adj[k]` for `+1`, `H_adj[k]` for `-1`), `pb_ext_raw_px`, and `pb_ext_mnq_px` to bar $k$, **reset `pb_bars = 0`**, increment `imp_bars += 1`, and **abort to `FLAT` if `imp_bars > 30`**.
     - **Else:** update `pb_ext_adj` (`min L_adj[k]` for `+1`, `max H_adj[k]` for `-1`), `pb_ext_raw_px`, `pb_ext_mnq_px`, and increment `pb_bars += 1`.
   - **Unconditionally on every bar $k$ in `PULLBACK_ARMED`** *(catching same-bar outside-reversal crashes and preventing `UnboundLocalError`)*: compute `imp_range = max(dir * (imp_peak_adj - wave_origin_adj), tick_size)`, `retrace_frac = dir * (imp_peak_adj - pb_ext_adj) / imp_range`, and update `pb_reached_25pct |= (retrace_frac >= 0.25)`.
   - **Abort immediately to `FLAT`** if `retrace_frac > 0.50` or `dir * (C_adj[k] - wave_origin_adj) <= 0` or `pb_bars > max_pb_bars` or past Active Entry Window end.
   - **2-Bar Micro-Structure Reclaim (`PENDING_ENTRY` Trigger):** Let $\text{Ext}_{2, k-1} = \max_{j=1..L_{\text{reclaim}}} H_{\text{adj}, k-j}$ (`+1`) / $\min_{j=1..L_{\text{reclaim}}} L_{\text{adj}, k-j}$ (`-1`). **Strictly when `pb_reached_25pct == True` and `pb_bars >= 2` and $\text{dir} \cdot (C_{\text{adj}, k} - \text{Ext}_{2, k-1}) > 0$ and `Flow_Aligned_dir[k]`** (evaluating `MNQ` staleness/spread/basis gates only on this reclaim bar):
     - If `Common_Entry_Gates[k]` holds, compute `pb_stop_ref = pb_ext_mnq_px` (coupled/live) or `pb_ext_raw_px` (`NQ`-only) and `pb_stop_px = round_tick_down/up(pb_stop_ref - dir * 0.50 * EMA_TR[k])`.
     - **Floor Stop at `4.0 * cost_rt_pts` (Never Aborting Tight Early Turns) & Cap Outlier / Late-Chase Risk:** Set `stop_ref_px = min(pb_stop_px, round_tick_down(entry_ref_px - 4.0 * cost_rt_pts))` for `+1` (`max(pb_stop_px, round_tick_up(entry_ref_px + 4.0 * cost_rt_pts))` for `-1`), and `init_risk_pts = dir * (entry_ref_px - stop_ref_px)` (strictly `>= 4.0 * cost_rt_pts`). Abort to `FLAT` **only** if `init_risk_pts > 0.35 * ATR_{track, 20d, d}` **or** `dir * (C_adj[k] - imp_peak_adj) > 1.0 * EMA_TR[k]` (late Wave-2 runaway chase); otherwise cache `entry_*` (`entry_due_us = obs_ts_us[k] + delta_lat_us`, `entry_init_risk_pts = init_risk_pts`) and enter `PENDING_ENTRY` (else abort failed reclaim to `FLAT`).
3. **While `IN_POSITION` (Monotonic Chandelier Runner Trail — No Premature Climax Caps):**
   - Let `mfe_R = dir * (pos_ext_px - fill_px) / R_pts` and `cur_eff_stop = max(stop_px, pending_stop_px)` (`+1`, if `ratchet_due_us > 0` else `stop_px`; symmetric `min` for `-1`).
   - If `mfe_R >= 4.0`, set `trail_mult = 2.0`; else if `mfe_R >= 1.5`, set `trail_mult = 3.0`; else leave initial `stop_px` untouched.
   - When `mfe_R >= 1.5`, compute `cand_stop = round_tick_down/up(pos_ext_px - dir * trail_mult * EMA_TR[k])`; if `dir * (cand_stop - cur_eff_stop) > 0`, schedule monotonic ratchet `pending_stop_px = cand_stop` at `ratchet_due_us = obs_ts_us[k] + delta_lat_us`.

### 7.3 Strictly Convex Walk-Forward Training & Nested Causal Tuning
- **Zero Cross-Session Leakage & 20-Session Refit Cadence:** Because both $T_t$ and $y_i$ terminate strictly inside session $d$, 100% of labels through session $s - 1$ are realized before `17:00` ET. Designate the first **`B_ml = 120` qualifying sessions** per track as `ML_BURN_IN_ONLY` (requiring $\ge 200$ historical candidates before activating; else `FLAT`), refitting M-A and M-B every **20 qualifying sessions** over trailing `[max(0, s - 360) .. s - 1]` inside the `17:00–18:00` ET EOD job.
- **Unique-Optimum Convex Solver & Nested CV:** Solve via `scipy.optimize.minimize(..., method='L-BFGS-B', tol=1e-10)` over $w \in [0, \infty)^8, b \in \mathbb{R}$:
  $$\min_{w \ge 0,\; b \in \mathbb{R}} \frac{1}{N_{\text{train}}} \sum_{i=1}^{N_{\text{train}}} \ell_{\text{Huber}, \delta=1.0}\left(y_i - (w^\top x_i + b)\right) + \lambda \|w\|_2^2$$
  selecting $\lambda \in \{10^{-3}, 10^{-2}, 10^{-1}, 1.0, 10.0\}$ via 3-fold expanding chronological CV strictly inside `[0 .. s - 1]` to maximize mean OOF $y_i$ on top-30% scored predictions (ties broken toward larger $\lambda$).
- **Causal Entry Rule:** Compute $\tau_d = Q_{\text{step}, q_{\text{sel}}}(\{w^\top x_j + b\})$ ($q_{\text{sel}} = 0.70$) over candidates in the trailing $\min(60, \text{available})$ qualifying sessions (`<= s - 1`, scored under current $(w, b)$). Trigger `PENDING_ENTRY` on reclaim bar $k$ when `Common_Entry_Gates[k]` (including `hat_T[k] <= theta_tox[d]`) holds, $\|w\|_\infty \ge 10^{-6}$, and $\hat{y}_k = w^\top x_k + b > \max(+0.05,\; \tau_d)$ (else reset `PULLBACK_ARMED -> FLAT`).
