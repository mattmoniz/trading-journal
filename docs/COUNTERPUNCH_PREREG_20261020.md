# Pre-registration: the counterpunch after a big drop (same day or within a few days) — 2026-10-20

Written and committed BEFORE any outcome is computed. Origin: user 2026-10-20: "whenever there is a large move down there is almost always a counterpunch that day or within a few days". Distinct from the 4h "balance then thrust" test (null) and the 1-minute sweep test (null). Same data, 4h bars, episode machine and conventions as `POST_SELLOFF_THRUST_PREREG_20261020.md` (anchor 18:00 ET, roll weeks excluded, ATR20 units, selloff = rolling p90 of the 12-bar drop, episode low = lowest low since trigger, `--since=1` form).

## Part A — describe the observation (hindsight, no trade claim)
For every episode (trigger = DROP at or above the rolling p50, p70 or p90 of its prior 1500 bars; band = highest of the three reached at trigger, bands reported separately: [p50,p70) ordinary, [p70,p90) medium, >=p90 big), take the hindsight episode low L (final lowest low in the episode) and measure the rebound from it over the next 6, 18 and 30 four-hour bars (1, 3, 5 days): rebound = (max high after the low - L)/ATR20 and as a fraction of the preceding drop (window high before the trigger - L). Report, per band: N, median rebound, share with rebound >= 0.5 of the drop within 18 bars, share with rebound >= 1 ATR20 within 18 bars. The claim "almost always" is checked as a frequency AND as "bigger for bigger drops" (a rebound that is the same size after ordinary drops is a generic bounce, not a counterpunch). Hindsight lows mechanically have some rebound after them, so no verdict from Part A alone; it only tells us whether Part B is worth reading.

## Part B — can it be caught point-in-time (the test with a verdict)
- Signal: inside an active p90 episode with clock c in 0-17, a bullish 4h reversal bar: low below the previous bar's low AND close above the previous bar's high. Entry = that bar's close (known when it closes). First such bar per episode is the primary event; later ones inside the episode are reported separately.
- Control: the same reversal bar anywhere NOT inside an episode (CONTROL), and all bars.
- Outcome from the entry close over the next 18 bars (3 days): R18 = (close[+18] - entry)/ATR20, U = max up excursion, D = max down excursion, and the race: first to +1 ATR20 vs -1 ATR20 within 18 bars (win/loss/neither).
- Primary statistic: mean R18 in episode reversal events minus mean R18 in control reversal bars; episode-clustered bootstrap CI (2000), shifted-episode placebo (200 draws moving each episode by 20-120 sessions, re-finding its first reversal bar), explore 2023-03-06..2025-05-31 / hold-out 2025-06-01..2026-10-16.
- SUPPORTED only if (i) CI lower bound > 0, (ii) beats placebo 95th percentile, (iii) both halves positive with hold-out >= half explore, (iv) positive without the best episode. Race win-rate minus control reported as a secondary, with the gambler's-ruin caveat (symmetric +-1 ATR so no asymmetric-barrier artifact). Sensitivity: horizon 6 and 30 bars, p85/p95 episodes. No trade rule; a SUPPORTED result licenses only a pre-registered trade-level test with realistic fills.

## Addendum C (pre-registered 2026-10-20 before it was run; added after the user pointed out that fixed cutoffs are myopic because the timing and size of a counterpunch vary)
Parts A/B above used a fixed 3-day horizon, a fixed 1 ATR20 race and a fixed percentile; Part B's primary failed at 18 bars while the 6-bar sensitivity passed, which is exactly the dependence on a chosen horizon the user warned about. Part C removes the fixed horizon and the fixed size.
- Events: same as Part B (first bullish 4h reversal bar per p90 episode with c 0-17; control = the same bar outside any episode). Entry = that bar's close E. Hwin = highest high of the trailing 12 four-hour bars up to that bar. Skip events with E >= Hwin.
- Size is each event's OWN: target(q) = E + q*(Hwin - E), q = 0.25, 0.5 (primary), 0.75, 1.0 (a full retrace of the drop). No ATR constant.
- Timing is the whole curve: T_q = first bar k = 1..30 whose high reaches target(q) (censored at 30 = 5 days). CDF_ep(h) and CDF_ctrl(h) for h = 1..30.
- Primary statistic (q = 0.5): maxD = max over h of [CDF_ep(h) - CDF_ctrl(h)] (a one-sided KS distance; no horizon is picked). Null: 200 shifted-episode placebo draws (each episode's first reversal re-found 20-120 sessions away in non-episode bars), same statistic. Also the episode-clustered bootstrap CI of D(h) at every h, and the median time to reach q in each group.
- SUPPORTED only if (i) real maxD exceeds the placebo 95th percentile; (ii) D(h) > 0 for at least 70 percent of h in BOTH halves (explore 2023-03-06..2025-05-31 / hold-out 2025-06-01..2026-10-16); (iii) maxD stays positive after dropping the best episode; (iv) the other q values (0.25, 0.75, 1.0) all have positive maxD. Otherwise NOT SUPPORTED.
- The sweep test (1-minute) had the same myopia (depth floor 0.03 ATR20 from a median, fixed 15-minute reclaim, fixed forward windows); a scale-free redo (depth as a percentile of the level's own trailing penetration distribution, whole forward path) is queued after this.
