# Event-snapshot study: what does the data around a tell look like just before price continues or pivots? (written 2026-11-02 BEFORE any outcome is read; user idea: "take snapshots of the data at the points where there is a high likelihood of pivoting or continuing, study those exact moments a little before and a little after, look at the depth, the information I can't see as a human, and string a story together"; user go: "Yea")

## 0. What this is and is not
A HYPOTHESIS-GENERATION study with a built-in independent check, not a trade rule. Earlier direction tests each fixed ONE definition (a delta flip, a break side, an entry); none looked broadly at the whole picture around each event and none used the order book. The risk of a broad look is storytelling: with many features and few events something always looks convincing. So the rules below fix, in advance, what counts as a finding and where it must be confirmed. Nothing here changes anything live.
Honest prior: about 5-10 percent that a candidate survives its confirmation. Order-book information tends to decay in seconds to minutes while the horizon here is 15-60 minutes, and the depth sample is small.

## 1. Events (one family, all from existing validated code)
- Data: 500-contract bars (bid and ask volume per bar, trades per bar, volume at price) rebuilt from the tick files with the sidecar's builder (`bootstrap.build_sessions`, validated against the database RTH volume), baselines chained in memory (`scripts/event_snapshots/build_days.py`).
- DEPTH-ERA set (discovery): every NQ session from 2026-05-01 with a validated rebuild; events from the first session with a 20-session baseline; depth coverage is the 104 sessions 2026-06-15 .. 2026-10-30. TELL events = the V1 research simulation's signal bars (`trade.simulate`), t0 = the signal bar's end. CONTROL events = decision-grid bars (every 10th bar index, 10:00-14:30) with price moving and no tell in either direction, drawn at random (seeded) at 4 per tell event, matched to the tell's entry-time bin.
- EXPLORE set (independent confirmation for the bar-level features only): the 516 explore sessions to 2025-05-16 (`scripts/amt_vbars` extracts): 1,760 V1 tell fires plus its grid controls. The depth features exist only in the depth era, so they can only be confirmed FORWARD (section 5).

## 2. The outcome ("pivot or continue"), price only
Pre-event direction p = sign of the price change over the last 10 bars ending at t0 (the tell's own motion window); events with p = 0 are dropped and counted. Continuation score y = p x (close of the last bar completed within 30 minutes of t0 minus the close at t0) / ATR20 (the 30-minute horizon is primary; 15 and 60 minutes are reported, uncounted). y > 0 means price continued the pre-event move, y < 0 means it pivoted. Also reported: the raw signed move (up or down) for the user's direction question.

## 3. Snapshot features (all computed from bars completed at or before t0; nothing after t0)
Bar-level (available in both eras): signed delta share of the last 1, 3 and 10 bars in the direction of p ((ask - bid) / volume x p); delta-price divergence over 10 and 20 bars (price made a new extreme in the direction of p while the cumulative aligned delta did not); absorption (volume per bar over range per bar, last 3 bars, relative to the session so far); pace (seconds per bar against the hourly baseline, A); trades per bar and contracts per trade (a large-lot proxy); share of the last 10 bars' volume traded at the extreme two ticks of the move (rejection or absorption at the edge); location (close in the day's range so far; distance to the overnight high and low in ATR units); count of the four non-ladder tell components met in the pre-event direction (0-4) and whether the node ladder exists (ladder length at least 2; the code returns presence, not length; amended 2026-11-02 before any outcome was read). 15 features, fixed now (the first draft said 17; the list has 15: delta share at 1, 3, 10 bars; delta-price divergence at 10 and 20 bars; absorption; pace; trades per bar; contracts per trade; edge share; location; distance ahead to the overnight extreme and distance behind to the opposite one; components met; ladder presence; corrected before any outcome was read).
Depth-level (depth era only, built as stage 2 from `scripts/depth_absorption/depth_reader.py` book reconstruction): at t0 and over the 60 seconds before it: total resting size within 5, 10 and 20 ticks on each side, the bid-over-ask imbalance aligned with p, its change over 60 seconds, depth thinning on the side of the move (liquidity pull) and stacking on the far side, resting size consumed versus size executed (absorption by resting orders). Defined in the stage-2 script header BEFORE it is run on outcomes.
Event-time paths (the "story" view, descriptive): for continuation versus pivot groups, the mean aligned delta, volume and range paths from 20 bars before to 20 bars after t0, and, for depth events, the mean aligned imbalance path from 120 seconds before to 120 seconds after.

## 4. Counted tests and rules
- DISCOVERY (depth era, TELL events only, then the same on CONTROL events as the comparison): for each bar-level feature the Spearman correlation with y at 30 minutes, day-blocked bootstrap CI (3000), circular day-shift placebo p (1000); BH q = 0.10 across the 15 bar-level features (family `event_snapshot_20261102`, ledger tests = 15). The depth features are a second counted family run after stage 2 (count fixed when its script is committed).
- A feature is a CANDIDATE only if it is a BH survivor in the depth-era TELL events AND has the same sign in the first and second halves of those events. The tell-versus-control comparison (is the relationship different for tells than for non-tell moments?) is reported, not used to filter.
- CONFIRMATION of a bar-level candidate: its sign and size on the EXPLORE set (1,760 tell fires), one look, same statistic, CI excluding zero with the SAME sign, BH over the number of candidates. Only then is it a lead. Depth-level candidates: confirmed only by FORWARD data (the nightly recording keeps adding events) with a pre-written rule: at least 150 new tell events, same sign, CI excluding zero, one look.
- Resolution, stated now: about 350 depth-era tell events on about 100 days resolve a correlation of roughly +/-0.11 to 0.13 (day-clustering included), far above the 0.03-0.04 that would pay after costs at the 30-minute horizon (claim `horizon_economics_power_vs_cost_20261102`). So this study can only flag LARGE relationships; everything smaller is invisible. The explore confirmation set (1,760 events) resolves about +/-0.05.
- No feature, horizon or subgroup is promoted after seeing outcomes; the "story" paths are descriptive and generate hypotheses for the NEXT pre-registration, they never count as a result.

## 5. Forward accrual (what makes depth findings testable at all)
The nightly job already rebuilds bars for tagging; adding a stage that snapshots each day's tell events with the same feature code (and the depth features while depth is recorded) would grow the sample by about 3-4 events per session. Not built until a candidate exists and the user agrees; the depth files are about 1 GB per day and are not archived by this repo, so a decision on keeping them is needed if depth turns out to matter.

## 6. Reading rules
No BH survivor, or no survivor with a stable sign: the broad look found nothing a human cannot already see; recorded with the resolution; the depth stage still runs for its descriptive paths. Survivors that fail the explore confirmation: not shown (a depth-era artifact). Survivors that confirm: a lead for a mechanical, pre-registered direction rule on the explore bars, with realistic fills, in a new document. Under no outcome is anything wired or any order placed.

## Part B (added 2026-11-02 BEFORE any outcome of either part was read; user: "test it in all situations"): the ALL-MOMENTS direction screen with depth
Why: the tell events are only about 350 moments, and the tell is a volatility marker; the information worth testing (flow, absorption, resting depth) exists at EVERY minute. So Part B drops the tell gate and asks whether the data at any moment predicts the SIGNED forward move.
Population: every minute on a 60-second grid from 10:00 to 15:30 ET (RTH) on each of the 95 sessions with a per-second order-book summary (`scratch/depth_persec_20261021/`, produced by `scripts/depth_persec_20261021.mjs`, columns: second of ET day, best bid, best ask, size at best bid, size at best ask, bid depth over the 10 ticks below and over 40, ask depth over the 10 ticks above and over 40; 2026-06-15 .. 2026-10-20) that also has a validated rebuilt bar day (stage 1). The price path is the book's mid price at each second; forward move over H = 1, 5, 15 and 30 minutes = mid(t + H) - mid(t), in ATR20 units (also reported in points). Points whose t + H falls after 15:55 or whose book is missing at t or t + H are dropped and counted.
Features (11, all SIGNED so they can predict direction, all from information at or before t; the bar features use the last complete 500-volume bar with end <= t): imb_best = (size at best bid - size at best ask) / (sum); imb10 and imb40 = (bid depth - ask depth) / (sum) over 10 and 40 ticks; d_imb10_60 = imb10(t) minus imb10(t - 60 s); d_imb40_300 = imb40(t) minus imb40(t - 300 s); chg_diff = (bid depth10(t) / bid depth10(t - 60 s) - 1) minus the same for the ask side (a liquidity pull or stack on one side); delta3 and delta10 = (ask - bid volume) / volume over the last 3 and 10 bars; mom10 = (close - close 10 bars ago) / ATR; loc_s = 2 x (close - RTH low) / (RTH high - RTH low) - 1 over the RTH so far; mom60m = (close of the last complete bar at t minus the close of the last complete bar one hour earlier) / ATR (taken from the bars, because the book summary starts at 09:30 and an hour-back mid would not exist before 10:30; corrected before any outcome was read).
Counted tests (family `event_snapshot_allmoments_20261102`, ledger tests = 48): (i) 11 features x 4 horizons = 44 Spearman correlations of the feature with the signed forward move (day-blocked bootstrap CI 3000 on 95 days; circular-shift placebo p 1000 on the time-ordered series; BH q = 0.10 over the 44); (ii) 4 walk-forward ridge models (one per horizon: all 11 features plus 30-minute clock dummies; trained on earlier days only, refit every 10 days after the first 30; metric = out-of-sample Spearman of the prediction with the signed forward move; PASS needs the day-blocked CI above zero, the circular-shift p below 0.05 and the same sign in both halves). A feature or model is a CANDIDATE if it passes with the same sign in both chronological halves of the 95 days.
Economic reading printed beside every candidate (not a counted test): gross dollars per trade of trading the sign of the feature's top-decile moments = mean signed move in points x $2, versus a cost of 1.25 points ($2.50) per trade; a candidate whose top-decile gross is below cost is "statistically visible, not tradable at this horizon". Correlation needed to pay at each horizon is from claim `horizon_economics_power_vs_cost_20261102`: about 0.09 at 5 minutes, 0.05 at 15, 0.037 at 30 (1 minute: not computed, larger still).
Resolution, stated now: about 95 days with roughly 330 grid minutes each; independent windows at the 15-minute horizon are about 2,000, so a correlation of roughly +/-0.05 to 0.06 (day-clustered) is resolvable at 15 minutes, +/-0.04 at 5 minutes; smaller effects are invisible. Depth features are RTH only: the Globex analogue needs a Globex per-second depth summary that does not exist (hr-002: recorded as an UNTESTED GAP, not as not-applicable); a Globex bar-feature-only arm is run from the stage-1 bars as an uncounted descriptive screen.
Reading rules: no candidate: the book and flow at any moment do not carry a resolvable signed signal at these horizons (with the resolution quoted). A candidate that is tradable by the economic reading: next step is a forward pre-registered shadow test (depth keeps recording), never a live rule. A statistically visible but untradable candidate at 1-5 minutes: recorded, and the question becomes whether a resting-order (maker) execution could keep part of it, a separate pre-registration. Confirmation of any depth finding is FORWARD only.
