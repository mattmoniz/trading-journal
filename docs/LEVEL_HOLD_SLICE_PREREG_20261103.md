## Frozen definitions (all numbers fixed now; the feasibility counts that informed the scale are in section "Calibration disclosure")
- Data: per-second RTH arrays `scratch/persec_px_20261021` (buy and sell aggressor volume, low, high, last); stored levels `level_prices` (causal by trade date; OR and IB levels exist only after they form). MAJOR families only (9: OR_HL, MIDS, IB_HL, OVN, PRIOR_DAY, VALUE_AREA, WM_PIVOTS, OPENS_VWAP, PERIOD_HLP); FLOOR and CAMARILLA (user MINOR classes) excluded. Library `scripts/tape_companion/lib.py`, 8 hand-traced synthetic tests pass (`test_lib.py`).
- Scale unit: median range of non-overlapping 5-minute blocks, median over the previous 20 sessions (causal). About 20 points.
- Cluster: stored levels within 0.5 scale of each other merge (single link) into ONE event; the event lists its member levels and families.
- Tolerance 0.5 scale on each side of the cluster band. Re-arm: price must be at least 1.0 scale (plus the band half-width) from the cluster centre since the last contact. Approach side = side of the previous second's last price (+1 = came from above, the level acts as support; -1 = from below). First contact = the first second whose high-low range touches the band while armed. Contacts 09:35:00 to 15:30:00 ET only.
- x(t) = side x (last - cluster centre): positive on the approach/bounce side. DECISION MOMENT t0 = contact + 60 seconds (clock seconds, not bars). Gate: the event is evaluable only if at t0 x >= -tol, price never traded 1.0 scale beyond the level during the dwell (else THROUGH) and never bounced 1.0 scale during the dwell (else ALREADY_HELD). THROUGH and ALREADY_HELD are counted and reported by stratum, not scored.
- LABEL after t0 (cap 900 seconds, per-second high/low): HOLD = x reaches +1.0 scale before -1.0 scale; SLICE = x reaches -1.0 scale first; neither within the cap, or both in the same second = NEITHER (reported, excluded from the binary score).
- Stale levels: a date is dropped if the stored PD_HIGH or PD_LOW differs by more than 3 points from the previous session's RTH extremes in the per-second file (9 of 514 dates in the feasibility run).
- Features at t0 (seconds <= t0 only; all flow aligned so positive = toward the bounce side). B0 structural: the 9 family flags, n_levels in the cluster, log scale, side, clock-hour dummies (9-15), signed distance from the open in scales, log1p(touch number of the day at that cluster). B1 = B0 + price behaviour during the dwell: approach speed (5 minutes into the contact, in scales), dwell fraction inside the band, deepest penetration, bounce so far. F = B1 + TAPE: log volume rate vs the same clock hour (median of the previous 20 sessions), aligned delta share over the last 30 s and 120 s, aligned cumulative delta share since 09:30, tussle (share of sign flips among consecutive 10-second net-delta buckets over the last 120 s; missing imputed by the training median).
- Model: L2 logistic regression, C = 0.1, features standardised on the training rows, median imputation, no tuning. Walk-forward by DATE, expanding: the first 100 evaluable dates train only, refit every 20 dates, predict the next 20 dates' events (labels never cross days).

## The ONE counted test (C1)
C1 = TAPE increment over price behaviour: mean per-event out-of-sample log-loss of B1 minus that of F (positive = the tape helps), over HOLD and SLICE events. Day-blocked bootstrap 95% CI (3000 resamples of dates, seed 20261103) and a within-date label-permutation p (100 permutations of the whole walk-forward, seed 20261103). SUPPORTED (statistically) only if the CI lower bound > 0 AND permutation p < 0.05 AND the sign is positive in both chronological halves of the evaluated dates.
Economic reading, fixed now: a probability gain of about 13 points (log-loss gain of about 0.034) is the order needed for a 1-MNQ taker at roughly 1.25 to 2 points of cost per round trip to benefit from the read (DeepSeek round G power note; Delta-p x captured move > cost). Verdicts: CI half-width > 0.034 -> UNINFORMATIVE; CI includes 0 and half-width <= 0.034 -> NOT SHOWN (an informative null at the economic scale); CI > 0 but the point estimate < 0.034 -> STATISTICAL ONLY, economically dead on arrival; point estimate >= 0.034 with the support rule -> LEAD (explore only, goes to the forward tag, never a rule).

## Reported, uncounted
B1 over B0 (does price behaviour at the dwell add anything); base HOLD share by side, hour, family; NEITHER, THROUGH and ALREADY_HELD shares by stratum; AUC and a decile calibration table for F; the same pipeline on PSEUDO-levels (each real cluster shifted by +/- 2.0 scale, rejected if within 1.0 scale of a real cluster or within 2.5 points of a multiple of 50) to see whether any increment is specific to real levels; 15-minute scale sensitivity; increment by clock-hour block (09:35-11:30, 11:30-14:00, 14:00-15:30); leave-the-10-largest-dates-out; the increment from each tape feature alone is NOT run (multiplicity).

## Calibration disclosure (counts only, no label was computed)
The first run used a 1-minute scale (about 8 points): 100,583 touch episodes (about 200 per day), 61% ALREADY_HELD, so thresholds sat inside ordinary wiggle. 5-minute scale: 30,425 episodes, 14,169 evaluable (47%), 499 dates, top 5 dates 2.8% of events. 15-minute scale: 10,792 episodes, 5,667 evaluable, 471 dates. The 5-minute scale was chosen before any label; 15-minute is the stated sensitivity. 9 dates dropped as stale.
