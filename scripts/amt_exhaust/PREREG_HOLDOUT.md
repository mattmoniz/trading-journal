# ONE frozen look at the sealed hold-out: exhaustion at a real market-generated level (frozen 2026-10-28)

Committed BEFORE the hold-out is touched. User approved ("Yes go ahead and do #3"). Hold-out = the eligible RTH sessions after 2025-05-16 (about 340, May 2025 to Oct 2026), never used for this question. The look is single: `holdout_exhaust.lock` makes a second run refuse.

## Why this one hypothesis
On the explore sessions the exhaustion events whose extreme sat very near a real level did much better than the same events at sham levels, in both the strict and the widened push (`amt_exhaustion_widened_push_not_shown_20261028`). It is one of about 25 uncounted looks and the two versions overlap, so it is a lead generated on explore; this look is its only confirmation.

## Definition (all frozen; nothing here may change after the run)
- Events: exactly the widened-push definition of `PREREG.md` + `PREREG_WIDE.md` (push = trailing 80th percentile move and 60th percentile efficiency; new extreme; exhaustion = leading-side aggressive volume over the last 3 bars at or below the 25th percentile of the earlier push; E2 adds the sustained counter-side response within 5 bars). The trailing 20-session windows use all earlier sessions (explore sessions feed the first hold-out days; no lookahead).
- Levels (known before the event): pdVAH, pdVAL, pdPOC, prior-day high, prior-day low, ONH, ONL, and IBH/IBL only when the push bar is at or after 10:30 (bar index 59).
- AT-LEVEL: the push extreme lies within **0.030821 ATR20** (about 9 points) of the nearest level. This is the 25th percentile of the distance over the EXPLORE wide-push E1 events (outcome-blind), computed before this prereg. SHAM-LEVEL: within **0.038765 ATR20** of the nearest level after every level is shifted up by 0.5 ATR20 (the explore 25th percentile of that distance).
- Two counted cells, one-sided alpha 0.025 each (so the 97.5% lower bound): **E1-at-level** (enter the opposite direction at the open of the bar after the exhaustion bar) and **E2-at-level** (enter after the counter-side response). Statistic = gross mean of the 15-bar forward return F_15 (points; entry open + 1 tick adverse; same definition as the explore runs), day-blocked bootstrap (3000 iterations, seed 20261028).
- LEAD (per cell) iff (1) the gross F_15 97.5% lower bound > 0, (2) the cell's mean beats the random-entry placebo p95 (100 trials: a random bar within +/- 30 bars of the entry in the same session, random direction), and (3) the at-level minus sham-level difference-in-differences (same events; near-real quartile vs rest minus near-sham vs rest, using the frozen cuts) has a day-blocked 95% CI lower bound > 0. Anything else is "not confirmed" (never "no effect").
- Expected size (GUESS from explore counts scaled by the session ratio): E1-at-level about 90 to 100 events, E2-at-level about 35 to 45. E2-at-level is likely UNDERPOWERED (its CI half-width on explore with N=58 was about 9 points); that is stated now and a miss there says little. A cell with fewer than 30 events is reported as NOT EVALUATED.
- Also reported, descriptive and not counted: E1 and E2 overall on the hold-out, the C2 and NR controls, long vs short, segments, and the geometry stage (stop 1 tick + 0.5 x ATR14 beyond the extreme, target 50% retracement, 30-bar time stop, flatten 15:55, costs as before) for the at-level events with its own random-entry placebo. No level-by-level mining on the hold-out (that analysis is explore-only; the hold-out is used up by this look).
