## 1. Definitions (all known at 09:29 ET, causal)
OVERNIGHT = 18:00 ET of the previous calendar day through 09:29. Source: the tick-built per-minute up/down-tick volume (`scratch/updown_1m_20261104/`, Sierra's trade-price rule, reconciled to the DB minute by minute) and 1-minute RTH bars. INVENTORY share I = (up-tick volume - down-tick volume) / (up + down) over the overnight session; s = sign(I) = the side that carried the net overnight position (long if positive). COST BASIS = the overnight volume-weighted average price = sum(minute close x volume) / sum(volume) over the overnight minutes (minute close as the price proxy; the error is small against a points-scale distance). OPEN = the 09:30 bar open. ADR = the median RTH range of the prior 20 sessions. STRESS S = s x (cost basis - open) / ADR: positive = the open is on the WRONG side of the basis for the inventory (long inventory with the open below its average price, or short with the open above): the position is underwater and 'RTH has not validated the move'; negative = the open validates it.
## 2. Outcome: the early-session rotation against the inventory
R = -s x (close of the 10:29 bar - open) / ADR, in ADR units (positive = price moved against the overnight inventory in the first hour, the 'sharp early-session rotation'). First 30 minutes (close of the 09:59 bar) is reported as sensitivity.
## 3. The two counted tests (explore, one observation per session)
T1 (the vulnerability claim): mean R of the sessions with S > 0 MINUS mean R of the sessions with S < 0, all sessions with I not zero. T2 (the extreme-inventory claim): the same contrast restricted to sessions with |I| at or above the expanding 67th percentile of the |I| of EARLIER sessions only (at least 60 earlier sessions). Each: week-block bootstrap CI at 97.5 percent two-sided (Bonferroni 2; 4,000 draws, seed 20261104), both chronological halves, and a PLACEBO: s permuted across sessions 1,000 times (S and R recomputed with the permuted s, the day-block label permutation of cv-007), the real contrast must lie outside the 97.5th percentile of |permuted contrast|. A test is a LEAD only if the CI excludes 0, the sign agrees in both halves and the placebo is beaten; otherwise NOT SHOWN. The detectable contrast (2.8 x SE) is printed BEFORE the verdicts.
## 4. Controls and what is printed, not counted
The same-side control (cv-094): S compares a long and a short inventory at the same stress; the placebo answers whether the contrast is only 'price below the overnight average tends to bounce'. Mean R by stress quintile; R for I alone (the sign of the inventory) and for the stress sign alone with the inventory ignored; first 30 minutes; gap-fill descriptives (share of sessions whose first hour reaches the prior RTH close, by gap direction aligned or against the inventory and by S sign), no verdict. Sessions with fewer than 20 prior sessions, missing ticks or missing bars are excluded and counted.
## 5. Limits
Explore only; the rotation is a price move in ADR units, not a trade (no stop, target or dollars); inventory is inferred from signed volume (aggressor or tick rule), not from real positions, and a net-buying night can be buying from shorts covering; the cost basis is a single average, not the cost of each participant; the overnight edge in other studies appears mainly in 2026 and the explore window ends 2025-05; hr-070 (RTH volume inflated in places) is avoided by using tick volume only. A LEAD is a candidate for ONE sealed look and a display, never a gate.

## 7. RESULT (2026-11-04 ~17:10 ET; ledger look 166; script `scripts/tell_context/research_20261103/overnight_inventory.py`; output `scratch/overnight_inventory_20261104_output.txt`; claim `overnight_inventory_not_shown_20261104`)
501 explore sessions (20 early, 1 without ticks excluded): 98 had the open on the WRONG side of the overnight basis for the inventory (S > 0, underwater), 403 validated (S < 0); 257 long and 244 short inventory. T1 (S>0 minus S<0, first-hour rotation against the inventory, ADR units): contrast -0.013, CI [-0.119, +0.093], halves +0.026 / -0.053: NOT SHOWN; the detectable contrast is 0.131 ADR (about 48 points), so anything under that is unresolvable. T2 (extreme inventory): NOT TESTABLE as designed: only 6 of the 150 extreme-inventory sessions open underwater. Mean R by stress quintile -0.006, +0.011, -0.022, -0.018, -0.030 (flat); first 30 minutes -0.036; inventory sign alone ~0 (long +0.001, short -0.027); the first hour moves toward the overnight VWAP by -0.004 ADR on average (nil).
DEFECTS DISCLOSED (found after the run, before any claim): (1) the sign-permutation placebo promised in section 3 is INVALID by construction (permuting s flips S and R together so the permuted contrast is almost constant, -(m1+m2)); its '97.5th percentile 0.011' is not a null and is ignored; the verdicts rest on the week-block CIs, which include 0. T1 as defined also confounds two opposite mechanisms (inventory vulnerability predicts R > 0 for S > 0, a pull toward the overnight VWAP predicts R < 0); the VWAP pull is itself about zero (-0.004 ADR), so no confound is hiding an effect. (2) The gap-fill descriptive (aligned gaps: 61 percent filled when S>0 vs 35 percent when S<0) is a GAP-SIZE artifact: the S>0 gaps are smaller (median 0.17 vs 0.42 ADR); within gap-size tertiles the fill rates are 72 vs 72 (small), 44 vs 37 (mid, N=16), 25 vs 6 (large, N=4). NOT a signal.
STRUCTURAL FINDING (descriptive, not counted): the share of sessions that open underwater falls with how one-sided the inventory was: 36.5 percent in the lowest tercile of |I|, 18 percent in the middle, 4.2 percent in the highest. Extreme one-sided overnight flow almost always moves price so the RTH open validates it; the scenario 'extreme inventory and RTH fails to validate' is rare (6 of 150 here), so it cannot be studied with explore-sized data.
VERDICT: no evidence that overnight inventory, defined as net up/down-tick volume against its overnight average price, predicts a first-hour rotation. NOT tested: other definitions of inventory (e.g. position of the overnight range or value against the open, the profile shape of the overnight session as the user reads it), 2026 (the overnight work shows an edge mainly there, the sealed era), other hours, a trade. A LEAD would have been a candidate for one sealed look; none exists.
