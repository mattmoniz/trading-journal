# Scope: does the tape at a major level tell hold from slice? And the tape companion (2026-11-03)

STATUS: SCOPE ONLY. Nothing built, nothing run, no outcome read. To be critiqued by DeepSeek, then pre-registered (frozen before any outcome), then run on EXPLORE data only (sessions through 2025-05-16; the later window stays sealed, spend it only through `spendSealedLook()`).

## 1. Why (user's words, 2026-11-03)
"When I look at a setup and it's at a certain level, I look to see that it actually stops and there is a tussle there and maybe a push in my direction for added confirmation. I want to make sure that it doesn't get just sliced through." Frozen charts hide the velocity and intensity of that moment. They want a companion that watches the ticks and cumulative momentum/delta, uses what we have, learns, is probabilistic, and eventually acts live. They think the meta-labeler may have had the wrong inputs.

What the repo says about that: the meta-labeler saw 1-minute-bar level distances, delta and time only; the pace claim states "tape pace needs a tick feed and is not tested"; its population (candidate fires of a roster that earns about nothing) and its extended-reach label are likely bigger limits than its inputs. Mechanical tape proxies tested so far (absorption marks, bursts, one-bar confirm, depth snapshots) were null on outcomes, but none asked this question: hold or slice at a MAJOR level, from the tape state at the moment of the check, over every touch rather than only our fires.

## 2. The question (one, falsifiable)
At a first-contact touch of a major level, once price has been at the level for a short, fixed dwell, does tape state (approach speed, tape rate, two-sided tussle, short-window and cumulative delta) predict whether the level HOLDS or is SLICED, beyond what the level class, time of day, volatility and distance already predict?

## 3. Data (all exist)
- 500-contract volume bars, 536 sessions 2023-03-07..2025-05-16, full session from the 18:00 ET open (Globex and RTH): `scratch/volbars500_full_20261028/*.bars.tsv` (start/end seconds since 18:00, OHLC, volume, bid and ask volume per bar) plus per-session volume-at-price. Finer per-second extraction exists for tick work (`scripts/tick_microstructure/extract_persec_*`, `.scid` ticks on C:).
- Levels: `level_prices` stored families, causal by trade date. MAJOR = OR_HL, MIDS, IB_HL, OVN, PRIOR_DAY, VALUE_AREA, WM_PIVOTS, OPENS_VWAP, PERIOD_HLP; MINOR = FLOOR and CAMARILLA (user classes, claim `tell_level_classes_not_shown_20261102`). Known caveats to handle, not ignore: 3M/PM/PW period lines differ from Sierra's fixed blocks (decision three_month_poc_vah_differ_from_sierra_20261026); bar-derived level tables go stale after bar repairs (hr-054); NQ roll weeks (basis); NQ bar gaps in 2024-2025.
- Depth (Jun-Oct 2026 only, ~104 sessions) is OUT of the first study: too few days for training; used later as a display input only.

## 4. Event and label design (to be frozen in the pre-registration)
- TOUCH EPISODE: first contact of a level, in either direction, within a tolerance derived from the data (a fraction of the local median 500-bar range), recording every episode in a session (cv-005), with a data-derived re-arm rule (price must leave by a multiple of that range). Levels within one tolerance of each other collapse to ONE event with the cluster's family list (cv-025), because many stored levels sit within a few points.
- DECISION MOMENT (where lookahead is easiest to introduce): the close of the K-th completed 500-bar after first contact (K fixed, e.g. 2 and 4 as two pre-declared variants), counted only if price is still on the approach side or within tolerance (the user's "it stops"); if price has already gone through, the event is labelled ALREADY_THROUGH and excluded from the hold/slice question but counted and reported.
- LABEL after the decision moment, scale-free in the same median-range units: HOLD = price retraces at least m from the level toward the origin side before trading s beyond it; SLICE = price trades s beyond the level first; neither within a time cap = NEITHER (reported, not forced into a class). m, s and the cap fixed in advance from the data's own resolution (hr-044), one primary definition plus two sensitivity definitions, none selected after seeing outcomes.
- BOTH SESSIONS: RTH and Globex are separate strata with the same code (hr-002); Globex is not assumed to mirror RTH (hr-039).

## 5. Features at the decision moment (causal, tick-derived, direction-aligned to the approach)
Approach speed (points per minute over the last 5 and 15 minutes in median-range units); tape rate (seconds per 500-bar over the baseline for the same clock hour, existing activity ratio A); tussle (share of the last N bar-delta pairs that flip sign, existing Q; share of two-sided volume at the level's price bins); delta over the last 30 seconds / 2 minutes / since the touch, aligned; cumulative delta since the 18:00 open, aligned; dwell (bars and seconds within tolerance); volume at the level's price vs the day profile (the existing volume-at-price). Baseline features (no tape): level class and family count, time of day, session, volatility tercile, distance from the open, day-so-far move, touch number of the day at that level.

## 6. Controls and tests (confounds that have burned us before)
- Same-DISTANCE placebo: shifted pseudo-levels at +/- fixed offsets (equal count, same distance from price at the same times) run through the identical pipeline, so a "level" effect is not a volatility or round-number marker (cv-094).
- Same-SIDE control and random-timestamp placebo (cv-029 style) for any directional claim.
- Baseline model first (level class, time, session, volatility), then baseline + tape: the question is the INCREMENT. Day-block walk-forward (expanding, purge), a regularised logistic model as primary (the pasted review's point is right: a linear baseline must be beaten before any tree is allowed), shallow boosted trees only as a secondary with monotone constraints and a label-permutation null (day-block label permutation, cv-007). Metrics: out-of-sample log-loss / Brier improvement over baseline with a day-blocked CI, calibration table by predicted-probability decile, AUC reported but not the criterion.
- ONE counted primary test (the increment on the primary label at K=2, RTH); everything else reported uncounted with its multiplicity priced. Reading rules fixed in advance: CI includes zero, or sign differs between chronological halves, or the effect sits in fewer than N days -> NOT SHOWN with the resolution stated (smallest increment the sample could have seen).
- No dollars in this study. A trade needs its own pre-registered simulation (entry on confirmation, stop behind the level, realistic fills and the 1.25-point cost) only if the increment is real.

## 7. Companion (parallel, informational, no claim)
Own service and route, never `acd.js` (feedback-isolate-non-trading-features): for the level price is approaching, a live strip with approach speed, tape rate vs normal, 30 s / 2 min / cumulative delta aligned, dwell, and a HELD / TUSSLE / SLICING status computed from the same frozen definitions; plus a snapshot of the tape state saved at each My Calls entry (the user's read plus the tape numbers). Probabilities are shown only if section 6 passes, always with an interval and a "not enough data" state. Reuse the live tick tailer / sidecar plumbing (`scripts/amt_tell_live`), read-only.

## 8. Staging toward live action (each stage needs the user's go)
S0 display only -> S1 shadow decisions logged with probabilities, fixed-schedule retrain, champion/challenger, forward scorecard, no daily peeking -> S2 sim-account orders through the EXISTING eligibility, daily-loss and kill-switch path only after a pre-registered forward gate (the tell Stage 2 precedent) -> real money is a separate decision. No new order path (cv-078). A probability that has not passed section 6 never gates anything.

## 9. Known risks and unknowns
Label ambiguity (hold/slice thresholds) is the biggest design risk; touch overlap and level clustering inflate N (day-blocking and cluster collapse are mandatory); explore data has had many looks in this family of work (new ledger family with its own budget); the decision-moment rule can leak the outcome if the "still at the level" gate uses later bars; bar-level (500-contract) timing may be too coarse for the 30-second features (fallback: per-second tick extraction for touch windows only); effective independent days are about 536, not thousands of touches. Honest prior that tape adds a usable increment beyond level class, time and volatility: modest. If section 6 is NOT SHOWN the companion stays a display and we say so.

## 10. DeepSeek round G critique and audit (2026-11-03; full text `scratch/review_20261103/response_G.md`, request `request_G.md`)
ACCEPTED (to be built into the pre-registration before any outcome):
- The "still at the level" gate conditions on survival, so a fast slice becomes ALREADY_THROUGH and leaves the base rate. Keep the gate (the user would also see it already gone) but report the ALREADY_THROUGH share per stratum, and note the model is conditional on surviving to the decision moment. The gate also depends on tape rate, so it can manufacture an increment: freeze dwell in CLOCK SECONDS from ticks, not in bars.
- 30-second features and dwell-in-seconds cannot come from 500-contract bars; per-second tick extraction for touch windows is MANDATORY, not a fallback. First-contact time inside a bar is unknowable from bars.
- Freeze numbers, not "a fraction": tolerance, re-arm, m, s, cap (cap from the clock-hour seconds per bar), and the two sensitivities written down in advance; ONE primary K (drop "2 and 4").
- Controls: add a same-side control for the hold/slice base rate itself (cv-094), reject pseudo-levels within tolerance of a real level or a round number, test round-number proximity alone, collapse clusters identically on real and pseudo events before the bootstrap. Strata: level class, session (RTH/Globex separate), clock hour, ATR20 tercile, distance from the open, approach side, touch number, day type.
- Power, stated before any outcome: with ~516 evaluable days the detectable increment is roughly 5-10 probability points (Brier ~0.002-0.006); an economically material one for a 1-MNQ taker is several times larger (a 5-point capture needs about +25 points of probability, 10 points about +13). The pre-registration must print this and declare "UNINFORMATIVE" (not "null") if the achieved CI half-width exceeds the economic threshold. No trees in the first pass.
- Evaluable days are ~516 (20-session baseline), not 536. Stale `level_prices` rows (hr-054) must be re-derived or flagged, not just acknowledged.
- DeepSeek prior: tape increment low, tradable edge very low; run only time-boxed, do not gate the companion on it; the first thing worth doing is the free S0: record tape snapshots at the user's own My Calls entries.
REJECTED / CORRECTED after audit:
- "Spend the sealed look on the at-level exhaustion lead": that look was ALREADY SPENT on 2026-10-28 (claim amt_exhaustion_at_level_holdout_not_confirmed_20261028, 109 events, -10.34 points, sign reversed, 96% of placebo trials at or above). Nothing unspent remains there.
- "Restrict to 10:00-14:30 because the live system only trades then": that is the TELL's window. Level fades first touch at 9:35 and run to 16:00, with a 9:30-9:35 dead zone and a Globex no-trade window 18:00-18:30 (cv-057, cv-058, cv-056, cv-067). Use 09:35-16:00 RTH and Globex after 18:30 as separate strata.
- Cost: the 1.25-point figure is commission $2 plus slippage; the repo's own simulated-fill claims use about $4 per trade, so the economic threshold is a range, not a constant.
- Not verified by DeepSeek or me: touch counts, dwell-survival rate, per-second extraction coverage across all explore sessions (measure these first, free, before the pre-registration).

## 11. Result (2026-11-03, run while the user was away)
Pre-registration `docs/LEVEL_HOLD_SLICE_PREREG_20261103.md`, claim `level_hold_slice_tape_statistical_only_20261103`, outputs `scripts/tape_companion/run_hold_slice_output.txt` and `run_hold_slice_sens15_output.txt`. Verdict STATISTICAL ONLY: the tape (volume rate, aligned delta, tussle, cumulative delta) adds a real but small increment (+0.0068 log-loss, about 5 probability points, CI [+0.0043, +0.0091], permutation p 0.01, both halves) over price behaviour at the dwell, and the same increment appears at PSEUDO-levels, so it is generic short-term flow persistence, not a level-specific hold/slice read. The level class itself adds nothing. Economically dead by the pre-registered bar (needs about 13+ points). Step 1 built: `scripts/tape_companion/snapshot_at.py` rebuilds the tape state at any logged call timestamp from the tick files (no display); parity test with the study 60/60.
