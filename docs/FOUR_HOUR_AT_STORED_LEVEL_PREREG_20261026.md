# Pre-registration: a four-hour defended level sitting ON a stored period level — does the combination pay? — 2026-10-26

Written BEFORE running anything. Origin: the user's annotated four-hour chart (2026-10-26): arrows at turns on the 3-month VAH/VAL/POC and other period lines after multi-day moves, with the question "how can't these be validated". The four-hour level study (`docs/FOUR_HOUR_DEFENDED_LEVEL_PREREG_20261026.md`: 0 of 4 cells) built levels from price action alone and ignored the stored period lines; the moonshot exit study (`docs/FOUR_HOUR_MOONSHOT_EXIT_PREREG_20261026.md`: 0 of 2) did the same. Earlier stored-level studies used fire-time one-minute entries. So "three or more four-hour bars ending at the same price, AND that price is a stored period level" is untested. The user asked to start with this test (2026-10-26). The user's linear-regression-average idea (price stretched from it) is NOT in this test: the line's settings were not supplied, and trying several lengths would be a forking path; it can be added later as a second metric with a length fixed in advance.

## Population and labels
The same first-`K>=3` four-hour events as the two studies above (floors long, tops short; `scripts/lib/fourHourLevels.mjs`; NQ 1-minute bars 2023-03-06 .. last complete date minus 3, pinned sampler; roll weeks, 2025-09-28..11-19 and bars < 60 minutes excluded; the pre-registered strict exit bar 1,439 minutes after entry must exist, so n = 429: 154 floors, 275 tops). An event is AT-LEVEL if its frozen level price lies within one band (0.25 x H4, about 34 points) of any stored level on the event's trade date from `level_prices`: `{3M, PM, PW, PY} x {VAL, POC, VAH}` plus `PM_HIGH, PM_LOW, PW_HIGH, PW_LOW`; otherwise ELSEWHERE. (Already counted before this document: 239 of 615 events, on 218 dates, are AT-LEVEL; the count of the 429 strict events is printed.) Descriptive splits: orientation-matched (floor at VAL/LOW/POC, top at VAH/HIGH/POC) versus other; by family (3M, PM, PW, PY).

## Outcomes (the same two as before; one contract, realistic fills, 1.5 points cost)
(a) exit-free move over 1,440 minutes from the next open, in D20 units, minus the clock-matched blind same-direction entry (30 draws, same partition); (b) the moonshot exit M (stop 0.5 x D20, no target, 1,440-minute hold, gap-through fills at the open), in points and dollars, minus the same exit on the blind entries. One metric only on top of level proximity: volume vs normal (relative volume at the defended bars, as before): HEAVY = above the median of explore AT-LEVEL events, LIGHT otherwise; descriptive.

## Controls (the combination is credited only if both hold)
1. The clock-matched blind entry (drift and stop/target geometry).
2. **Defense adds nothing beyond the stored level itself?** EXCESS-K1AT = the event's outcome minus the mean outcome of up to 30 fresh K=1 birth events of the same side at the SAME clock, whose level also lies within a band of a stored level (a fresh low at a period line, not yet defended), same exit, same partition. If a stored level alone does as well, the defense is not what matters.
3. The ELSEWHERE events are the within-population contrast for "does being at a stored level matter": AT-LEVEL minus ELSEWHERE, date-blocked two-sample CI, descriptive.

## Counted cells, screens, verdict rules
Counted cells = 4: FLOOR-AT-LEVEL and TOP-AT-LEVEL x {outcome (a), outcome (b)}, each as EXCESS over blind entries. Explore = earlier 60 percent of event dates (cut fixed by the strict-event dates, as in the moonshot study); explore events whose exit window crosses the cut are dropped from both screens; controls come only from the event's own partition. CANDIDATE = explore date-blocked CI at 98.75 percent (Bonferroni over 4) lower bound above 0; SCREEN-SURVIVOR = candidate AND hold-out point above 0 AND hold-out 95 percent CI lower bound above 0 AND explore point above 0 AND both halves stay above 0 after removing their single largest date AND EXCESS-K1AT point above 0 in both halves (else labelled "stored-level artifact: the level alone does as well"); outcome (b) additionally "usable lead" only if the absolute net mean per trade is above zero in both halves. UNDERPOWERED if fewer than 30 events or fewer than 20 distinct dates in a half; printed per cell: n, dates, mean and median, share stopped, top-5 trade and date shares, smallest detectable effect (CI half-width), expected-by-chance (cells x 0.0125, a floor), overlap share.

## Forward collection (set up now, nothing scheduled)
`FORWARD_FROM=<date>` makes the study script analyse only events whose trade date is after that date, with no screening. The pre-registered forward window starts **2026-10-28** (the first trade date after this document is committed and the first-look result is in); the forward look is run ONCE, on or after 2026-12-15 or when 20 forward AT-LEVEL events exist per side, whichever is later, reading only forward dates, and its result is reported whether positive or not. Not wired anywhere live.

## Limits stated in advance
This is the fifth four-hour study on the same history after one explicit pre-registered null each time; a lead here is a lead. The AT-LEVEL definition (one band, 14 level names, any orientation) comes from the user's chart, not tuned; 3M VAL alone has only 8 events, 3M VAH 40, PW_HIGH 63, so family-level verdicts are impossible; the user's drawn lines are the same stored levels as far as can be told (the chart's exact level set is not known). The strict exit rule drops 30 percent of events (maintenance gap, weekend; a lenient-exit sensitivity is printed). A null means the combination could not be shown at about 35 points (70 dollars) of resolution per trade, not that your turns are not real.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-26, before the run) — DeepSeek review (`reports/deepseek_at_stored_level_prereg_review_2026-10-26.md`)
1. **Confounding of AT-LEVEL vs ELSEWHERE (finding 1).** The counted cells compare each group with blind clock-matched entries and fresh K=1 births, not with each other, so this confound does not enter a verdict; it does enter the descriptive AT-LEVEL minus ELSEWHERE contrast. The script now prints a balance table for the two groups (mean D20, mean band, share per clock slot, share per year, mean DROP into the level) and the contrast is labelled descriptive only. No matching is added.
2. **K=1-at-stored-level control (finding 2).** The number of K=1 controls found per event is printed (median, share with at least 10). If the median is below 10 the EXCESS-K1AT condition is declared uninformative and a survivor is labelled "K1AT control uninformative" instead of passing or failing it. Matching on distance travelled is not added (the control is already clock-, side-, partition- and stored-level-matched; more matching leaves almost no events).
3. **Power (finding 3).** Printed per cell: the realised CI half-width (the smallest detectable effect). No family-level verdicts or splits are reported as evidence.
4. **Family-wise error (finding 4).** Written rule: only the four counted cells and the single forward look may be cited as evidence. Orientation, family, volume, AT-minus-ELSEWHERE and sensitivity outputs are for reading, not for citing; nobody (including me) may report one as a finding.
5. **Breadth of the net (finding 5).** Taking the diagnosis, not the proposed remedy: the primary definition stays the broad one fixed above (it was fixed before any outcome of this test was seen, and the orientation-matched definition is printed beside it as the second reading), but the script now prints how selective the net is: the share of randomly sampled bars (the blind-control sample) whose close lies within one band of a stored level, next to the 39 percent of events that do. If those shares are close, AT-LEVEL is not special and the result says nothing about stored levels.
6. **Strict-exit drop (finding 6).** The AT-LEVEL share among dropped (no exact exit bar) versus kept events is printed.
7. **Forward look fixed (finding 7).** The forward look is run ONCE on **2026-12-15**, using the forward window 2026-10-28 .. 2026-12-14 as it stands that day, whatever the counts are (an underpowered look is reported as underpowered, no deferral). The earlier "20 per side" trigger is dropped.
