## 1. The stop and the rule (all frozen)
s = the node-stop distance of the entry (V1). The position ALWAYS holds a stop: at entry it is at 1.0 x s from the entry. After each COMPLETED bar j (j >= the entry bar) the displayed watcher state of that bar (`watcher.run`, causal, UNKNOWN before 60 minutes of RTH) may re-set the stop for bar j+1 onward:
- BALANCE_CONFIRMED -> the stop moves to 2.0 x s from the entry (WIDEN), at most once, and never after a tighten.
- a DRIVE (or DRIVE_*_BUILDING) AGAINST the position (long: DRIVE_DOWN / DRIVE_DOWN_BUILDING; short: DRIVE_UP / DRIVE_UP_BUILDING) -> the stop moves to 0.5 x s from the entry (TIGHTEN), at most once; a tighten is permanent. If the market is already beyond the new stop the position leaves at the next bar's open with the usual 1-tick slippage.
- every other state (UNKNOWN, BALANCE_FORMING, a drive in the position's direction): no change.
The multipliers 0.5 / 2.0 are conventions taken from the ladder sweep grid, NOT tuned.
## 2. Arms (same entries, the entry-price control is built in) and controls
A0 = V1 static (must reproduce the file's net dollars exactly). A1 = widen only. A2 = tighten only. A3 = both. PLACEBO A3p = the identical rule driven by the watcher state sequence of a DIFFERENT, randomly drawn session read at the same clock time (8 draws per entry, mean taken): the policy of changing the stop at some times without the day's real information. Static references (printed, from the ladder run): m = 0.5 +0.30, 1.0 +1.93, 2.0 +2.64 dollars/trade.
## 3. Counted tests (4) and the lead rule
T1 A1 minus A0, T2 A2 minus A0, T3 A3 minus A0, T4 A3 minus A3p (the real state versus another day's state): mean dollars per trade per entry, day-block 95 percent bootstrap CI (2000 draws). A LEAD needs T3 with the CI lower bound above 0 AND T4 with the CI lower bound above 0; only then is the hold-out spent (once) for the confirmation. Printed, uncounted: win rate, average winner / loser, the largest single loss, the number of entries whose stop was widened / tightened, the dollars at risk (sum of the stop distances), by final day shape and by the clock time of the entry.
## 4. Limits
Widening raises the loss per stopped trade: the largest loss and the average loser are reported beside the mean, and a higher mean bought with a bigger tail is stated as such. The tell's entries before 10:30 see UNKNOWN (nothing to act on). The watcher conventions (hysteresis, FIGHT_MIN, DEFENDED) are its own and untuned here. Explore window with many looks, so a lead needs the hold-out. The ladder sweep that motivated the multipliers read these same trades, so the explore result is optimistic by construction.
