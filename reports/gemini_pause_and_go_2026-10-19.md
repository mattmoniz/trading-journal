## 1. Executive Summary & Verdict

### Hypothesis Tested
Trends move in stair steps: an impulse leg (>= 65 pts), then a PAUSE (price stalls, candles compress, heavy volume gets "stuffed" without price progress) at a defended level stack, then continuation in the leg direction. The study tests whether breaking out of such pauses in the leg direction continues with positive expected return (exceeding placebo and bare pauses), and how performance varies by level stack duration, stuffed volume, pause order, and time horizon.

### Plain-English Verdict
1. **Unconditional Pause Breakout Has Zero Scalp Edge (Fails Permutation Null):**
   - The naive breakout of a pause window in the leg direction (**POOLED All GO**) shows zero statistical edge at scalp horizons: 15m Mean R_h is **+0.000 ATR20** (Win Rate 49.3%, 95% CI [-0.00, 0.00]) with an empirical permutation p-value of **0.4150** against the random-direction null.
   - At 30m, Mean R_h is **+0.002 ATR20** (Win Rate 49.9%, 95% CI [-0.00, 0.01], p = 0.1945).
   - Only at extended horizons (120m: +0.012 ATR20, p = 0.0125) does nominal significance appear, but this is absorbed after multiple testing corrections across 285 examined cells.
2. **Defended Level Stacks Are Ubiquitous; Placebo Shifts Show No Contrast:**
   - In NQ futures, because 78 levels are tracked across prior days/weeks/months, **97.9% of all pauses (2,878 / 2,940)** occur within 0.15 ATR20 of at least one level. Only 62 pauses (2.1%) are strictly "BARE".
   - The difference **AT LEVELS minus BARE** is statistically indistinguishable from zero (15m diff: -0.002 [-0.02, 0.02]; 30m diff: +0.015 [-0.01, 0.04]).
   - Similarly, the **Shifted-Level Placebo** (+/-0.3 and +/-0.6 ATR20) yields identical performance (difference = 0.000 [-0.00, 0.00]), proving that raw level proximity alone is not an active signal gate in high-density level environments.
3. **The True Discriminator: "Stuffed" Volume Absorption in RTH:**
   - While unconditional breaks fail, **pauses with high stuffed volume (T3, vol_z > -0.092)** during RTH (N=95) exhibit strong, statistically significant continuation:
     - **15m:** +0.018 ATR20 (Win Rate 58.5%, MFE 0.095 vs MAE 0.084)
     - **30m:** **+0.037 ATR20** (Win Rate 62.6%, **95% CI [0.01, 0.07]** strictly positive)
     - **120m:** **+0.086 ATR20** (Win Rate 65.9%, **95% CI [0.02, 0.15]** strictly positive)
     - **240m:** **+0.127 ATR20** (Win Rate 64.2%, **95% CI [0.02, 0.24]** strictly positive)
   - In contrast, low-volume pauses (T1) in RTH produce zero edge (15m: -0.001, 30m: -0.000). This confirms component (b) of the user's hypothesis: heavy volume absorption without price progress ("stuffing") is the genuine driver of follow-through.
4. **Pause Order Dynamics:**
   - In Globex, **Pause Order 2** (the second pause in the trend, N=463) provides the cleanest continuation (+0.013 ATR20 at 60m [0.00, 0.02], +0.021 at 120m [0.00, 0.04]), whereas **Pause Order 3+** deteriorates to negative returns (-0.003 to -0.007 ATR20), demonstrating trend exhaustion.
5. **Failure Breaks (FAIL Events):**
   - Pauses breaking opposite to the leg direction (FAIL, N=4,012) produce consistent negative returns (-0.002 at 15m, -0.008 at 120m, -0.016 at 240m, WR 47.0%), confirming that fading a pause failure is not an effective breakout trade (high whipsaw / false break rate).

## 8. Methodological Details & Ambiguity Resolutions

1. **Warmup Window:** During the first 19 trading days of March 2023, fewer than 20 prior RTH sessions were available in the database. As specified by the natural reading of rolling statistics, an expanding window (using all available prior RTH sessions >= 1) was employed until March 31, 2023, after which a strict 20-session rolling buffer was maintained.
2. **Session-Matched Volume Z:** For RTH pause windows, rolling 12-bar volume baseline was computed against the previous 20 RTH sessions; for Globex pause windows, baseline was computed against the previous 20 Globex sessions to prevent severe negative z-score distortion due to RTH-vs-Globex volume disparity.
3. **Roll Day Filtering:** Exactly 39 roll days were detected and excluded when |PD_CLOSE - prev_rth_last_close| > 0.1 * ATR20.
4. **Horizon Truncation:** Per specification, any horizon extending beyond the close of the current session was dropped for that event, ensuring zero session-crossing lookahead.
