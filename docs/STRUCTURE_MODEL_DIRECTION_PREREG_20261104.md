## 1. Label (signed, scale-free, no trade rule)
y_dir = (the close of minute T+59 - the close of minute T-1) / ADR_t: the next-hour close-to-close move in ADR units (positive = up). The rank-based Spearman correlation is the primary measure (the label is heavy tailed).
## 2. Features: signed, known at the decision minute; the user's three groups plus order flow
PRICE ACTION: return since the open, the last 60, 30 and 5 minutes (ADR units), the close's position in the day's range and in the last-30-minute range, the signed initial-balance extension (above minus below), the clock dummies. SUPPORT AND RESISTANCE: the signed distance (ADR) from the close to the NEAREST key level above (resistance) and the nearest below (support) among the prior-day high and low, the overnight high and low (open + / - the table's overnight extents), the initial-balance high and low; the number of key levels within one IB range of the close; whether the close is inside the prior-day range. OVERNIGHT: the overnight net move, the overnight delta share, the gap, the overnight up-minus-down extent, the maximum overnight 60-minute drive, the open's position in the prior value area. ORDER FLOW: the signed up/down-tick delta share of the last 30 minutes and since the open (`scratch/updown_1m_20261104/`, tick-derived). All from the same files as Step A.
## 3. Models and the three counted tests (expanding walk-forward by day, refit every 5 days, warm-up 120 days, ridge with the penalty chosen by time-ordered inner CV; week-block bootstrap CI at 98.33 percent (Bonferroni 3, 4,000 draws, seed 20261104); both chronological halves; detectable correlation printed first)
FULL = clock + price action + support/resistance + overnight + order flow. MOMENTUM = clock + the three price-action returns only. T1: Spearman rho(FULL prediction, y_dir) on the scored rows. T2: the squared-error improvement of FULL over MOMENTUM on y_dir (do support / resistance, overnight and order flow add anything beyond what the price returns say). T3 (the regime idea): the same rho(FULL prediction, y_dir) restricted to the rows whose Step-A predicted RANGE (the walk-forward M2 prediction) is above the EXPANDING 67th percentile of the earlier scored predictions (the regime picks the moment, the model picks the side; at least 60 earlier rows). PLACEBO for T1 and T2: the added columns circularly shifted by a whole number of days (at least 10, 200 draws, the real run's penalties reused); the gate = the 97.5th percentile of |rho| (T1) or of the zero-centred improvement (T2). A test is a LEAD only if its CI excludes 0, both halves share the sign and the placebo gate is beaten.
## 4. Reported, not counted
The sign hit rate overall and in the top fifth of |prediction|; the dollars of the plain rule 'long if the prediction is positive, else short, for the next hour' per decision row after the $2 round trip and one tick of slippage each way (1 MNQ, close to close), against always long, the momentum sign (the 30-minute return) and the mean of long and short (random direction), with week-block CIs; the gradient-boosted challenger's rho; per decision time; the standardized ridge weights per group (what it found, never used to select).
## 5. What a result means
A LEAD (T1 and T2 pass) would be the first intraday directional signal found here: it would go to ONE sealed look (needs tick data for the later era, an extraction of 2025-05 to 2026-10) and a forward shadow arm, never a gate. T1 null with T3 positive would mean 'direction only in expanding regimes' (the overnight pattern) and is a lead for its own pre-registration. Both null: the model stays a volatility forecaster, and support / resistance reaction is tested next as a LEVEL-TOUCH reaction question (reverse versus break at a touch), the right unit for support and resistance. Limits: explore only; 1,424 scored rows on 356 days (clustered within days, the day is the unit); a close-to-close hour, not a trade with a stop; the sample era is 2023-03 to 2025-05.
