# Spike exhaustion, marks only (definition frozen 2026-10-28 BEFORE any detector output)

Origin: the user reviewed the explore windows of 2023-05-18..05-24 and circled (blue) the exhaustions they see: sharp spikes to an extreme, a high or a V-low, after which price "comes right back" ("exhaustions happen quick because the opposing side steps away at the end and price comes right back"). They are NOT the slow efficient 30-bar pushes the E2 rule (PREREG.md) requires, which is why E2 misses them. This stage is MARKS ONLY (same workflow as the absorption marks in scripts/amt_absorb): no trades, no P&L, no outcome statistics. Trailing the trade is a separate later question (the repo record for trail / breakeven exits under realistic fills is poor, so it would be tested only after the detection is right).

## Definition (1-minute RTH bars; every size threshold is a quantile of the prior 20 sessions' same quantity at the same clock bar +/- 15 minutes)
Spike HIGH at bar t (the mirror is a spike LOW):
1. THRUST: over the last M = 5 bars ending at t, bar t is the highest high of the window, the window's lowest low came BEFORE it (an upward thrust), and thrust size = highest high minus that lowest low >= the trailing 90th percentile of thrust size (pooled up and down).
2. NEW EXTREME: that high exceeds the highest high of the K = 30 bars before the thrust window.
3. RIGHT BACK: within the next 5 bars price closes back through the thrust's midpoint (WEAK) and STRONG if it closes back at or below the thrust's base (the whole thrust erased). The mark is placed when the midpoint is first closed through; one mark per spike; a 15-bar refractory period per direction.
No level, order-flow or time-of-day condition is required in this stage; the user judges by eye. Direction of the (unscored) idea: against the spike. Sensitivity (counts only): M = 3 and 8; quantile 80 and 95.
Fidelity check: the 12 spikes circled by the user, with times read off their screenshots (about +/- 5 minutes), are looked up after the run as a hit list; the thresholds are NOT adjusted to hit them.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-28, marks stage, after the first census and a gate diagnosis on the user's circles; no outcome exists)
First run (M=5, Q=90): 2.11 marks per session, 5 of the 12 circles hit. Gate check on the misses: 5 had a thrust of only 0.56-0.88 x the 90th-percentile size bar while retracing 79-214% of the thrust within 5 bars; 1 (2023-05-19 09:43) is before the detector's first bar (it needed 35 bars of history); 1 (2023-05-19 12:22) is not a new 30-bar extreme. All twelve circles retraced at least 57% within 5 bars (ten at least 75%), so the user's concept ("price comes right back") is carried by the return, not by the size. The 90th-percentile size gate came from the E2 design, not from the user's description. Changes, written before the re-run: (1) size gate = trailing 75th percentile (was 90th); (2) STRONG = closes back through at least 75% of the thrust within 5 bars (was: the whole thrust), WEAK = through the midpoint (unchanged); (3) the 30-bar new-extreme lookback uses the bars available since the open when fewer than 30 exist (minimum 8 bars before the thrust window), so the first hour is covered. The 12:22 case (not a new extreme) is left as a known miss. Counts per session and the circle hit list are reported again; thresholds are not tuned further to the circles. Sensitivity counts: quantile 65 and 85.

## Amendment 2 (2026-10-29, ERRATUM: lookahead in the mark, found by hand-tracing an implausible path-study result; fixed before the corrected re-run)
`spike.detect` kept scanning the bars AFTER the confirmation bar u (up to t+5): (1) it discarded the event if a later bar made a higher high (spike high) / lower low than the extreme, and (2) it set STRONG from closes after u. Both use information that arrives after the entry bar, so an event survived only if the structural stop was not hit in the next few bars and STRONG selected events whose return kept going. That inflated every outcome statistic computed on these marks (the first path-study run showed strong spike marks at +25 dollars per trade against a -9 placebo; DISCARDED, see scripts/amt_marks_path/PREREG.md erratum). Fix: the mark is decided at u from information up to and including bar u only: the extension check runs on bars t+1..u (including u), and STRONG = the close of bar u itself is back through 75 percent of the thrust (WEAK = through the midpoint only). Nothing else changes. The marks drawn on the published chart before this fix are the leaky version; they differ from the corrected marks only where the spike extended after u (now marked) and in the strong/weak split (strong is now rarer).
