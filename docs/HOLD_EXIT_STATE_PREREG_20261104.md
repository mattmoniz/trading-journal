# Pre-registration: does the day's state change the value of HOLDING an open position versus flattening it? (written 2026-11-04 BEFORE any hold-value was computed)

STATUS: FROZEN. User (2026-11-04): the project is about guiding DECISIONS (entries, holds, exits), assume balance until the watcher / forecast says otherwise, a stop is always there. Test bed = the tell's V1 entries (explore 1,760 and, only if a lead appears, the hold-out 1,486 once): the question is entry-agnostic ("a position is open at a checkpoint; what does the state say about holding"), and the tell's entries are the only large set with exact path simulation (the steadier shadow setups have about 80 days). Nothing is live. Ledger family `hold_exit_state_20261104`, counted tests 4.
## 1. Positions and arms (same entries, same stop; arms differ only in what happens after the checkpoint)
Checkpoints t_c in {11:30, 12:30, 13:30, 14:30} ET. A position is OPEN at t_c if its V1 exit (stop, timeout, tell-fade, flatten) is at or after the first bar that completes after t_c. HOLD = the V1 result unchanged (the node stop stays in force: there is always a stop). FLAT = leave at the open of that bar with the usual 1-tick slippage, net = ((exit - entry) x direction) x 2 - 2 dollars. HOLD-VALUE = HOLD net - FLAT net (positive = holding was better), per position per checkpoint.
## 2. States known at t_c (causal: the last bar completed by t_c)
IB state (`watcher_stop_lib.ib_state_at`: NORMAL when neither side of the first-hour range is extended beyond 0.1 IBR, else not NORMAL); watcher state (`watcher.run` displayed state: BALANCE_CONFIRMED / drive in the position's favour / drive against / other); open P&L sign (close of the last completed bar vs the entry).
## 3. Counted tests (4), explore, day-block 95 percent CIs (2000 draws), positions at 12:30 unless stated
T1 hold-value for IB-NORMAL positions minus not-NORMAL positions. T2 hold-value with the watcher BALANCE_CONFIRMED minus all other watcher states. T3 hold-value for positions in profit at t_c minus positions in loss. T4 the T1 contrast pooled over all four checkpoints (a position counts at each checkpoint it is open). LEAD rule: T1 or T2 with the CI excluding 0 AND the same sign in both halves of the window AND the same sign at 11:30 and 13:30 in the printed checkpoint table; T3 is a reading of 'let winners run', not part of the lead rule. Only a lead spends the hold-out (once, same four arms, family `hold_exit_state_20261104`).
## 4. Printed, uncounted
N, mean HOLD, mean FLAT and hold-value by state and checkpoint; the same by final day shape (hindsight, for comparison only); the share of positions open at each checkpoint.
## 5. Limits
The tell's entries are flat-to-negative as a strategy (the hold-out replay averaged -6.64 per entry), so HOLD and FLAT are both measured on a weak base; the contrast between states is what matters, not the level; a position open at 12:30 is a survivor (it has not been stopped), so hold-value is conditional on survival; explore has had many looks; conventions are untuned.
