## Arms (events, both from four-hour bars aligned to the Globex open, `scripts/lib/fourHourLevels.mjs`, bars through the last complete date minus 3, roll weeks and 2025-09-28..2025-11-19 excluded as before)
- **SEQ (the user's picture, primary):** `scripts/lib/sequentialRuns.mjs`, band `0.25 x H4`, minimum run 4. FLOOR run = 4+ consecutive four-hour bars whose lows sit within the band of each other, every close above the run's lowest low, the bar before the run coming from above; TOP is the mirror. The event fires at the close of the bar where the run first reaches 4 bars ("several defenses"). Floors go long, tops short. One event per run.
- **APPR (my earlier reading, secondary):** events of `scripts/build_defended_level_events_20261026.mjs` (levels = tick-volume period lines, defenses counted over 130 trade days since the last break, hold = first close 0.5 x H4 away within 6 bars) with at least one non-prior-day line and 3 or more separate approaches. Tested only because the user asked to see what it does; it is not their definition.

## Trade (identical for both arms; every number derived from the data, none tuned on outcomes)
Entry = open of the first one-minute bar after the firing bar closes. Level `L` = the run's extreme (floor: lowest low; top: highest high); for APPR `L` = the line price minus/plus its band. **Stop** = `0.5 x H4` beyond `L` on the defended side ("a large stop": beyond the whole defended zone, so a break of the zone is the invalidation). **Target** = `3 x` the stop distance from entry (a far target, scale-free, defined for every event). **Time exit** = the close of the bar 2,879 minutes after entry (about two sessions, the user's "today or tomorrow"; the last bar before that minute when a maintenance gap or weekend makes it missing, `lenient`). Fills: a stop already beyond the market fills at the bar's open (`walk()` in `scripts/lib/floorEngine.mjs`), stop checked before target within a bar, cost 1.5 points. One MNQ contract, $2/point.

## Controls and counted cells
EXCESS = event net points minus the mean of up to 30 clock-matched blind same-direction entries (same session clock within 30 minutes, other dates, the event's own partition, sampler pinned to the end date) run through the identical stop/target distances in points and the identical time exit. EXCESS-FRESH = minus up to 30 fresh-extreme birth events of the same side at the same clock (`k1Events`, no sequential defense), same exit: "price at a fresh extreme" must not be credited to defense. A mirrored-direction placebo (the same events traded the other way with the same exit) is printed.
**Counted cells = 4:** SEQ floor, SEQ top, APPR floor, APPR top, each at the primary trade above, on EXCESS. Explore = earlier 60 percent of event dates, hold-out = later 40 percent (a SCREEN only: this history has been used all day); an explore event whose exit window crosses the cut is dropped from both screens. CANDIDATE = explore date-blocked CI at 98.75 percent (Bonferroni over 4) with lower bound above 0; SCREEN-SURVIVOR = candidate AND hold-out point above 0 AND hold-out one-sided 95 percent lower bound above 0 AND explore point above 0 AND both stay above 0 after dropping their largest-contribution date; "level-location artifact" if EXCESS-FRESH is not above 0 in both halves; "usable lead" additionally needs absolute net mean above 0 in both halves. UNDERPOWERED if fewer than 30 events or 20 distinct dates in a partition. Printed: n, distinct dates, mean and median net points and dollars, share stopped / target / time-exit, worst trade, top-5-trades and top-5-dates share of total, smallest detectable effect (CI half-width), overlap share, expected-by-chance (cells x 0.0125).

## Descriptive only (never a verdict)
Sensitivity: stop 1.0 x H4; target 2 x and 4 x risk; the entry after a hold (first close 0.5 x H4 away); band 0.5 x H4; the far target being the nearest period line at least 1.5 x risk away (subset, with the count that had one); HEAVY vs LIGHT volume vs normal (cut = explore median of the run bars' relative volume); runs that sit within one band of a tick-volume period line versus runs at no named line; floors against tops; run length 4 versus 5+.

## Stated limits
About 150 floor and 210 top runs over 3.5 years before the split, so each explore cell has roughly 90-120 events: the smallest detectable effect will be large (tens of points); a null means "not shown at this size". Tick-based period lines are missing 2025-05..2026-04 (the context and APPR arm have no events there). The history is spent (four-hour studies earlier today, the unified backtests), so the explore/hold-out split is a screen; the only clean test is a forward look: events dated 2026-10-27 or later with this definition frozen, one look when at least 30 SEQ events exist or on 2027-04-01, whichever is first (decision to be flagged). The user's band, hold and "obviously defended" are judged by eye; `0.25 x H4` is my choice (it reproduces this morning's floor at 29,024.25 and is stricter than 0.35/0.5); the user's checks of 13 charts (the Sequential Run Check page) are recorded before the run.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-26, before any outcome) — DeepSeek design review (`reports/deepseek_sequential_run_prereg_review_2026-10-26_raw.md`) audited against the code
1. **Detector bug confirmed and fixed** (`scripts/lib/sequentialRuns.mjs`; tests `node scripts/test_sequential_runs_20261026.mjs`, 5 cases): a bar closing exactly at the run low made the scan stop, discarding longer valid runs whose earlier, lower low would have made every close clear the run low. And "came from above" was not actually enforced (the scan recorded a run whether or not the bar before it sat more than one band higher). Both fixed: a run is valid only if the bar before it is more than one band above the run low (so a run from index 0, or one longer than the 40-bar lookback, is rejected). **Effect: events at band 0.25 x H4 fell from 355 to 149 (floors 40, tops 109), at 0.35 from 626 to 271, at 0.5 from 1,021 to 460.** The earlier published check cards were regenerated (only 4 of the first 13 survived); no ratings had been entered on the old ones.
2. **Band decision rule, fixed now, applied after the user's check page** (https://claude.ai/artifact/6PtzgU4x32DfEEEEFrmxB6, 15 cards: this morning's floor, 6 found at band 0.25, 8 found only at 0.35): if the user marks at least half of the "0.35-only" cards as Looks defended, the primary band is 0.35 x H4; otherwise 0.25. If the check shows the definition is wrong, the definition is changed and this document amended before any run. With 40 floor events at 0.25 the explore floor cell would be UNDERPOWERED by construction (fewer than 30 in explore); that is stated, not hidden.
3. **EXCESS-FRESH becomes a hard gate (finding 3):** a CANDIDATE needs the explore EXCESS-FRESH point estimate above 0 as well; "level-location artifact" is then a label only for survivors failing it in the hold-out. The APPR arm (mostly non-sequential defenses) is the same-selection-minus-sequential contrast; SEQ minus APPR is printed descriptively. Stop and target distances are printed in H4 units, with the share of events whose stop sits within 0.5 H4 of entry.
4. **Overlap and effective n (finding 4):** also printed: a greedy non-overlapping subset (events at least 2,880 minutes apart per side), intra-date correlation, effective n. Expected-by-chance and the near-certainty of a null at this size are stated up front. **Forward look pre-committed:** the same CANDIDATE/SURVIVOR gates applied to forward events alone (dated 2026-10-27 or later, the definition and band frozen from this run), one look at 30 SEQ events or 2027-04-01, whichever is first.
5. **Co-primary tier for the user's named features (finding 5), own Bonferroni budget of 4:** SEQ with the target at the nearest tick-volume period line at least 1.5 x risk away (floors, tops; events with no such line are counted and excluded) and SEQ HEAVY minus LIGHT volume vs normal on the primary trade (floors, tops; cut = explore median of the run bars' relative volume, fixed before the hold-out is read). These do not change the 4 primary cells. **SEQ is a proxy** for "obviously defended" (it ignores the 3-month value areas as context, ES, the zoomed-out read for other support); a null bounds the proxy, not the thesis.
