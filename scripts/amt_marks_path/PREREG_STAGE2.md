# Stage 2: the spike signature (end of a large move vs a stop-pop that continues), frozen 2026-10-29 BEFORE any outcome

Origin: the user's reviews of 8 weeks of the marks chart, and two statements: (1) "sometimes the spike can be the end of a large move or the beginning of a large move if there wasn't one"; (2) "if there is high velocity, minor exhaustion is just stops getting popped and more continuation (so we might need to figure out this signature)". Also: many exhaustive bursts are shorts being stopped out who must buy back "with some intensity". Stage 1 (PREREG.md, corrected) found: fading the corrected spike marks with a structural stop beats a same-stop random-entry placebo by about 12.6 dollars per trade (CI 7.6..17.6) yet loses 1.9 dollars per trade after costs; spikes WITH the day drift or at a new session extreme did better (+18.0, +21.8) than counter-trend pops (+1.5, CI -7.7..+11.6) - the first numeric support for the two-case idea. Everything stays EXPLORE (sessions to 2025-05-16), marks tuned by eye on the same sessions, RTH only, nothing out of sample.

## Variables, all known at the confirmation bar u (no lookahead)
- PRIOR_RUN: signed move in the SPIKE direction over the 60 bars before the thrust window, in ATR20 units (large = the spike ends a large move; small or negative = the spike may start one).
- VELOCITY: thrust size divided by the trailing 75th-percentile thrust size at the same clock bar (how much bigger than a typical big thrust; scale-free).
- LEVEL_PIERCED: the spike extreme went beyond a named level (ONH/ONL, pdH/pdL, pdVAH/pdVAL, IBH/IBL from 10:30) that the thrust started on the other side of (stops rest beyond levels).
- INTENSITY: aggressive volume of the spike side summed over the thrust bars (ask for a spike high, bid for a spike low) >= the trailing 75th percentile of the same quantity at the same clock bar over the prior 20 sessions.
- DRIFT: spike with or against the day drift (close[t] vs the session open), as in stage 1.
Cut points for tertiles (PRIOR_RUN, VELOCITY) are taken from the pooled explore spike marks, outcome-blind.

## Two arms, both with the stage-1 machinery (structural stop, no target, no stop moving, flatten 15:55, realistic fills, 2 dollars per round trip, placebo = 50 random entry bars with the same stop distance and direction, day-blocked bootstrap of real minus placebo, PRE < 2025-01-01 / POST)
- FADE (stage 1): direction D = the way price returned; stop at the spike extreme.
- CONTINUATION: direction C = the original spike direction (the move continues after the pop); entry at the open of u+1 (+1 tick adverse), stop = the pullback extreme (lowest low of bars t+1..u for a spike high; highest high for a spike low) minus/plus one tick; events whose entry price is beyond that stop are excluded and counted.

## Pre-specified hypotheses (the only two counted; everything else is descriptive)
H1 (end of a large move): FADE real-minus-placebo is larger for the TOP tertile of PRIOR_RUN than for the BOTTOM tertile; counted as shown if top-minus-bottom > 0 with a day-blocked CI excluding zero and the top tertile itself beats its placebo (CI excludes zero), N >= 100 per tertile, same sign in PRE and POST.
H2 (stop-pop continuation): among HIGH-VELOCITY (top tertile) spikes that are AGAINST the day drift, CONTINUATION real-minus-placebo > 0 with a day-blocked CI excluding zero, N >= 100, same sign in PRE and POST, top-5 days < 50 percent of a positive total.
Descriptive tables, no pass/fail: both arms by every variable (tertiles or yes/no), by side, and the intensity and level tags. About 20 descriptive looks: any pattern there is a lead only. A null states the CI half-width, what could not be shown, and that Globex is untested.

## Not in this stage
Globex; trailing and two-lot exits; the 500-volume-bar tell components as a hold gate (separate prereg); absorption marks as a variable (their confirmation can come after the spike entry and leaks, so they are used only as an annotation).

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-29, user idea, written BEFORE any stage-2 outcome): score the move in standard deviations and calibrate what it predicts
User: "maybe absorption spikes need to be assessed based on a backtested standard deviation that accurately predicts its impact with that move? Ie turning point or move stop or continuation."
- Z-SCORES, not typed cutoffs: for each event, SIZE_Z = (thrust size - mean) / std of all 5-bar thrust sizes at the same clock bar +/- 15 minutes over the prior 20 sessions; PRIOR_RUN_Z and INTENSITY_Z likewise (the 60-bar prior move in the spike direction; the thrust-bar aggressive volume of the spike side). For absorption marks: EFFORT_Z = (the pressing side's window volume - mean) / std over the same trailing set. Buckets of each z are terciles of the pooled explore events (outcome-blind).
- THREE OUTCOME CLASSES after the entry bar u+1, with barriers in volatility units, no typed point values: sigma_H = the std of H-bar price changes at the same clock bar over the prior 20 sessions, H = 60 bars (120 as sensitivity). TURN = price travels +1 sigma_H in the RETURN direction (the fade direction) before -1 sigma_H; CONTINUE = it travels 1 sigma_H in the original spike direction first (the stop-pop continuation); STALL = neither barrier by H bars or by the 15:55 flatten (move stops). Barrier 1 sigma_H is a data-resolution choice, sensitivity 0.5 and 1.5 reported, never selected.
- CALIBRATION (the "accurately predicts" test, counted as H3): walk-forward in date order; for each event, class probabilities per bucket of SIZE_Z are estimated ONLY from earlier events (expanding window, at least 100 prior events, else the unconditional prior-rate model). Score = multiclass Brier score of the bucket-calibrated predictor versus the unconditional class rates, improvement per event with a day-blocked 95 percent CI. H3 is shown only if the CI excludes zero, the sign holds in PRE and POST, N >= 300, and the same holds when the buckets come from PRIOR_RUN_Z + SIZE_Z combined (reported, not selected). A reliability table (predicted vs observed class rates per bucket) is printed. Absorption marks get the same treatment with EFFORT_Z.
- Reported with the class shares per bucket against the same-direction random-entry placebo class shares (what the barriers do at random bars), so a class that is common everywhere is not mistaken for a signal. Counted hypotheses are now H1, H2, H3; everything else descriptive, about 30 looks, lead only.

## Amendment 2 (2026-10-29, after the DeepSeek critique and the stage-1 Amendment 1 controls; written BEFORE stage 2 is run)
Stage-1 Amendment 1 result (path_output_amend1.txt): the TIME-MATCHED placebo is even worse than the uniform one (-20.7 vs -14.9) so time of day does not explain the stage-1 edge, BUT the DIRECTION-FLIP control (the opposite trade at the same bar with the same risk) does about as well as the fade (flip -0.90 vs fade -1.92 per trade; real minus flip -1.02, CI -11.1..+8.8): the spike marks time the entry (more volatile, trending conditions) but the fade DIRECTION carries no information overall. The earlier tag reading ("with the day drift +18.0 beats counter-trend +1.5") was a placebo artifact and REVERSES against the flip control: spikes AGAINST the day drift: fade minus flip +26.6 (CI +1.3..+58.4, n=704, exploratory); spikes WITH the drift: fade minus flip -14.5 (CI -31.0..+1.0). Changes to stage 2 (adopting DeepSeek's critique where it holds): (1) every cell is judged against the flip control, the time-matched placebo and the uniform placebo, not the uniform placebo alone; (2) H1/H2/H3 are DEMOTED to descriptive refinements (they were operationalised after seeing stage-1 tags, no out-of-sample content); (3) ONE primary hypothesis for a single sealed look on post-2025-05-16 sessions (code and thresholds frozen first): among spikes AGAINST the day drift, the fade beats the direction-flip control at the same bar and risk (real minus flip > 0, day-blocked CI excluding zero, N >= 100, same sign PRE/POST); (4) H3 is reformulated as a single binary, not three classes (DeepSeek: STALL dominates the multiclass Brier): among events where a barrier is hit within H bars, does the RETURN direction (+1 sigma_H) win before the spike direction, scored by log-loss against the same-direction placebo base rate, with the STALL share reported separately; (5) the CONTINUATION arm is kept only as the flip-style counter-arm with the same risk distance (DeepSeek: a pullback-extreme stop makes it a different strategy with a tighter stop, so it is not a mirror of the fade). Multiplicity: one primary; every other look is a lead requiring a fresh pre-registration on untouched data.
