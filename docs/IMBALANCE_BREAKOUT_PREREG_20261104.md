# Pre-registration (explore): do diagonal footprint imbalances, read over a multi-bar window and located against structure, tell acceptance from rejection of a breakout? (register row 15; written 2026-11-04 BEFORE any imbalance feature or outcome was computed)

STATUS: FROZEN. Origin: the user's idea (2026-11-04: 'imbalances in a wider lens as opposed to microsecond ... tracking the frequency, clustering, and location of imbalances (especially diagonal footprint imbalances at 3:1 or 4:1) acts as a confirmation tool for structural shifts ... they prove whether a breakout out of a balance area is met with institutional acceptance (heavy initiative stacked imbalances) or rejection / absorption (high volume but zero directional imbalances, a failed sweep) ... context over flags: where the imbalances stack relative to key structural levels'). User instruction: 'dont just shoot down if theres something there': this design gives the idea a fair test (descriptive structure first, the user's own initiative-versus-absorption contrast as a counted test, pre-registered splits by level type so a pooled null cannot hide a pocket, the ruled-out and kept-alive lists stated at the end). Research only, nothing live, explore only (522 sessions), hold-out UNSPENT. Ledger family `imbalance_breakout_20261104`, counted tests 3.
## 1. Footprints (data: the tick-built 500-contract bar files, `scratch/volbars500_full_20261028/DATE.bars.tsv` and `.vap.tsv`: per bar per price the bid volume (sellers hitting the bid) and ask volume; cell volume = bid + ask on every cell, checked)
A window footprint = the sum of the per-bar cells of a run of consecutive 500-contract bars (the volume clock: scale-free across 2023-2026 volume levels). Diagonal imbalances at ratio R: BUY imbalance at price P if ask(P) >= R x bid(P - 1 tick); SELL imbalance at P if bid(P) >= R x ask(P + 1 tick); an imbalance also needs own-side volume >= MINVOL = the median nonzero own-side cell of the earlier NON-OVERLAPPING 20-bar windows of the same session (expanding, causal; at least 5 earlier windows), so thin cells do not count. R in {3, 4} (the user's conventions, kept as two pre-registered variants, not tuned). STACK = 3 or more consecutive prices with the same-side imbalance.
## 2. Events: breakouts of structural levels (RTH, causal)
Levels, all from the same bar files: overnight high / low (ONH, ONL: bars ending by 09:30), initial balance high / low (IBH, IBL: RTH 09:30-10:30; usable from 10:30), prior-day RTH high / low (PDH, PDL). A BREAKOUT = the first 500-bar after the level is known (RTH only) whose CLOSE is beyond the level by at least one tick; direction d = up or down. Events of the same direction within 10 minutes of an earlier event the same day are collapsed to the first (cv-025: siblings). The DECISION bar = the breakout bar + 5 bars (2,500 contracts of acceptance, volume clock). The FEATURE WINDOW = the 20 bars ending at the decision bar. Events with a decision after 14:30 (horizon) or fewer than 25 bars of history are excluded and counted.
## 3. Features (aligned with d: a buy imbalance is aligned for an up breakout, a sell imbalance for a down breakout)
STACK_A = number of aligned stacks in the window; STACK_O = opposed stacks; NET_STACK = STACK_A - STACK_O (ratio 3 and ratio 4 versions); LOC = the share of aligned imbalance prices that lie BEYOND the broken level (initiative outside the balance), undefined if none; IMB_RATE = (aligned - opposed imbalance prices) / occupied prices; window volume ratio VOLR = the window's contracts / the median of the earlier non-overlapping 20-bar windows of the session (volume 'now vs lately'); window range / s. HIGH VOLUME = VOLR at or above the expanding median of earlier events' VOLR (cut from earlier events only).
## 4. Outcome (continuous, scale-free, no trade rule)
y = (maximum favourable minus maximum adverse excursion over the next 60 minutes, measured from the decision bar's close, favourable = further in the breakout direction) / s, s = the median high-low of the 20 bars ending at the decision bar. Secondary (not counted): FAIL = the price closes back on the inside of the broken level within the 60 minutes.
## 5. Counted tests (3): day-block bootstrap CI at 98.33 percent (Bonferroni 3, 4,000 draws, seed 20261104), both chronological halves, placebo
T1: Spearman rho(NET_STACK ratio 3, y). T2: Spearman rho(NET_STACK ratio 4, y). T3 (the user's own contrast): among HIGH-VOLUME events, mean y with at least one aligned stack MINUS mean y with none (ratio 3); predicted positive (initiative continues, absorption fails). PLACEBO: the feature vector of each event replaced by that of a random event of ANOTHER date with the same level type and direction (seeded, 1,000 draws for the contrast; the real statistic must exceed the 97.5th percentile of |placebo|). LEAD only if the CI excludes 0, both halves have the sign of the statistic, and the placebo is beaten; else NOT SHOWN. The detectable effect (2.8 x SE) is printed BEFORE the verdicts.
## 6. Printed first, uncounted (the descriptive 'what do imbalances look like' the user asked about)
How many events by level type and direction; imbalance prices and stacks per window (ratio 3 and 4): share of windows with at least one stack, run lengths, the share of aligned stacks located beyond the level vs inside; mean y and FAIL rate by tercile of NET_STACK, of VOLR, and by the initiative / absorption cell; and the same split by LEVEL TYPE (IB, ON, PD) and by direction (the pre-registered strata: a pooled verdict is a question, cv-033). Control (cv-094): rho(VOLR, y) and rho(window range / s, y) are printed next to T1, because stack counts grow with activity; the within-VOLR-tercile rho of NET_STACK is printed too.
## 7. Limits and what is not done
Explore only. Globex breakouts (the same levels broken overnight) are NOT tested here: hr-002 gap recorded, a second pre-registered pass if RTH shows a lead. The 3:1 / 4:1 ratios are the user's conventions (static by definition of the footprint), not derived: ratio sensitivity is the two variants only. A 500-contract bar's cell sizes are small, hence the 20-bar aggregation; the true 1-minute or range-bar footprint the user watches differs (a range bar clock is a possible later variant). Imbalance location uses only the 3 level families named. A LEAD would be a candidate for ONE sealed look and a display, never a gate. If nothing is shown, the result is stated as exactly what was tested and what could not be shown, with the strata that look best listed as leads needing their own pre-registration, not as findings.

## 9. A1. Amendment (2026-11-04, after building the event table and printing its FEATURE distributions, BEFORE any feature-outcome relation or test was computed; disclosed: the table's outcome columns were computed and I printed their overall mean y +0.19 and overall FAIL rate 91 percent, nothing split by a feature)
Two design faults found from the features alone. (1) VOLR as written (window contracts / earlier windows' contracts) is constant by construction: a 20-bar window of 500-contract bars is always 10,000 contracts. On a volume clock 'more activity' is TIME: VOLR is redefined as the median DURATION (seconds from the window's first bar start to its last bar end) of the earlier non-overlapping 20-bar windows of the session divided by this window's duration (above 1 = the same 10,000 contracts traded faster, the repo's 'activity A'); HIGH VOLUME = VOLR at or above the expanding median of earlier events' VOLR. (2) The user's stacks are RARE at these settings: an aligned stack (3+ consecutive imbalance prices) appears in only 7 percent of windows at 3:1 and 3 percent at 4:1 (opposed: 2 percent), so counted tests on stack counts would be under-powered by construction and a null would say nothing. The counted measures therefore use the CONTINUOUS frequency of imbalances, which the user also named ('frequency, clustering, and location'): T1 = Spearman rho(RATE3, y), T2 = Spearman rho(RATE4, y), with RATE = (aligned imbalance prices - opposed imbalance prices) / occupied prices in the window; T3 (the user's contrast) = among HIGH-VOLUME events, mean y with RATE3 above the expanding median of earlier events' RATE3 (INITIATIVE) MINUS mean y with RATE3 at or below it (ABSORPTION: fast tape without directional imbalances). STACKS stay in the design as DESCRIPTIVE and as an uncounted sensitivity: the same T3 contrast with 'at least one aligned stack at 3:1' as the initiative cell, its N printed. Everything else in sections 1-8 is unchanged (events, decision bar, window, outcome, placebo, halves, Bonferroni over 3 counted tests, strata, controls). FAIL (91 percent of events close back inside the level at some bar of the next hour) is too loose to be informative and is reported only, not used.

THE PER-BAR DESIGN (counted tests T4-T6).
