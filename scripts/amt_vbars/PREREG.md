## Definitions (frozen)
- **Day profile:** volume at price (0.25 bins) from the 18:00 open through the last completed bar (the user's 1-day profile including Globex). The 18:00 reset is assumed to be the chart's session start.
- **Node:** a contiguous price range of the day profile around a Sierra-style volume peak: a bin whose volume exceeds each of the 3 bins above and below it (session extremes excluded), extended up and down to the first bin whose volume is at or below the median volume of the profile's non-empty bins; nodes narrower than 3 bins are dropped, overlapping nodes merge. Node time = the volume-weighted median bar index of the volume inside the node. Nodes are re-read from the profile as it stands at each decision.
- **Left behind (direction d, +1 up):** a node whose upper edge (d=+1; lower edge for d=-1) is on the near side of the current close. **Ladder length L** = the longest chain of left-behind nodes, ordered by price in direction d, whose node times strictly increase (each new node formed after the one below it), counted 0, 1, 2, 3+.
- **Moving:** close of the current bar minus the close 10 bars earlier is > 0 in direction d.
- **Flow over the last 10 completed bars:** ACTIVITY A = baseline seconds-per-bar / current mean seconds-per-bar, where the baseline is the mean seconds-per-bar of the 500-contract bars of the same clock hour over the 20 prior sessions (no lookahead; A > 1 means faster than usual); BACK-AND-FORTH Q = the share of the 9 consecutive bar pairs whose bar deltas (ask minus bid volume) have opposite signs (0.5 = random). HIGH FLOW = A >= 1 AND Q >= the median Q pooled over all decision points (outcome-free cutoff, printed before any outcome).
- **Decision points:** every 10th completed bar from the first RTH bar ending at or after 10:00 through the last ending at or before 14:30 ET (thinned to cut overlap); only points where price is still inside the combined prior range in direction d: the extreme of the overnight range (18:00-09:29 high/low) and, once the IB is complete (bars through 10:29), the IB extreme, whichever is further out; i.e. no completed bar so far has closed beyond it in direction d.
- **Outcomes:** (a) signed 60-minute move d x (close of the last bar ending within 60 minutes of the decision - decision close) in ATR20 units (ATR20 = mean RTH high-low range of the 20 prior RTH sessions); (b) BREAK = any completed bar closing beyond that combined-range extreme in direction d within the next 60 minutes. No slippage (signal screen).
- **Distance stratum:** distance to the boundary in ATR20 units, terciles pooled over all decision points (outcome-free); all comparisons are within strata, weighted by the number of events of the arm being tested.

## Arms and the one primary statistic
TELL = moving AND L >= 2 AND HIGH FLOW. Controls (same strata): MOVING-ONLY (moving, any nodes, any flow), LADDER-ONLY (moving AND L >= 2, flow not high), FLOW-ONLY (moving AND HIGH FLOW AND L < 2). **Primary (one counted cell):** signed 60-minute move, TELL minus MOVING-ONLY (price drift alone), strata-weighted, day-blocked bootstrap (resample sessions, 3000, seed 20261028), per-arm means plain sums over N, one-sided 97.5 percent lower bound must exceed 0. Reported alongside, not counted: BREAK rate difference TELL minus MOVING-ONLY, TELL minus LADDER-ONLY and minus FLOW-ONLY (does each half add?), by direction, arm sizes (printed FIRST) per stratum and per decision time, the share of decision points per L.

## Reading (frozen)
A lead only if the primary lower bound > 0 AND TELL beats LADDER-ONLY and FLOW-ONLY on the signed move (point estimates). Otherwise "not shown", with the resolution reached (never "no effect"). Under 150 TELL events or 60 dates = underpowered. A lead earns a new frozen trade-machinery prereg (entry at the tell, stop behind the last node) and one sealed hold-out look only with the user's approval.
