# Initiative Ignition & Retest (revised "Setup B") (frozen 2026-10-28)

Committed BEFORE any outcome is read. The rules below are the user's spec, verbatim in numbers; section "Resolutions" lists only what the spec leaves open, chosen before any result. EXPLORE only: the same 529 eligible sessions to 2025-05-16 used by the other AMT studies. The hold-out (after 2025-05-16) and every earlier-sealed hold-out stay SEALED and are not loaded into any computation. The explore sessions have been reused across several studies, so a lead here is a hypothesis for one frozen hold-out look, never a result. No ACSIL code until an edge over the placebo is verified. No parameter changes after the first run.

## The user's spec
- Window: ignition bar open between 09:35:00 and 15:00:00 ET.
- Breakout Line: LONG: if pdVAH is within 20 points of IBH or ONH, the line is the MAXIMUM of the clustered prices. SHORT: if pdVAL is within 20 points of IBL or ONL, the line is the MINIMUM of the clustered prices.
- Ignition bar Bar[t] (1-minute): range >= 15.0 points; Close >= Line + 5.0 (long) / <= Line - 5.0 (short); close in the top 25% of the range (long, (C-L)/(H-L) >= 0.75) / bottom 25% (short, <= 0.25); volume >= 1.5 x SMA(volume, 20); bar delta (ask minus bid volume) > 0 long / < 0 short.
- Execution: at the open of Bar[t+1] place a resting limit at exactly the Line price; cancel if not filled by the close of Bar[t+5]; a fill requires price to trade THROUGH the limit by at least 1 tick (0.25 points); zero slippage on limit fills.
- Management after the fill: hard stop 12.0 points beyond the Line (long: Line - 12; short: Line + 12). Structural stop: any subsequent 1-minute bar closing more than 4 points back through the line (long: Close < Line - 4; short: Close > Line + 4) flattens at market on the next bar open. Target: limit 24.0 points from the Line (2:1).

## Resolutions of what the spec leaves open (chosen before any result)
1. **Money:** 1 MNQ ($2/point, $2 round-trip commission, repo ground truth). Market exits (structural stop, end of day) pay 1 tick adverse slippage. The hard stop fills at the stop price minus 1 tick; a stop that takes effect when the market is already beyond it fills at that bar's open. The target limit fills only when the bar trades 1 tick THROUGH it, at the target price. Stop is checked before target on the same bar, including the fill bar. Flatten at the 15:55 open. One position or resting order at a time.
2. **Levels:** pdVAH/pdVAL = prior full RTH session 70% value area (asserted equal to the loader's). ONH/ONL = high/low of the 1-minute bars 18:00 the previous calendar day to 09:29 (>= 500 bars, else missing that day). IBH/IBL = high/low of the 09:30-10:29 bars and only usable for ignition bars from the 10:30 bar on (formation gate); before that a cluster can only use ON levels.
3. **Clustered prices:** the cluster is pdVAH plus every of IBH/ONH within 20.0 points of pdVAH (pdVAL with IBL/ONL for the short side). If neither is within 20 points there is no line that side. The line is the maximum (min for short) of the cluster prices.
4. **"Breaks the line":** the ignition bar must cross the line: Low[t] <= Line (long) / High[t] >= Line (short), and meet the clearance rule above. A bar entirely above the line never counts. After a signal for a direction (filled or not) that direction is disarmed until a bar closes at or back through the line (long: Close <= Line; short: Close >= Line), so one run above the line gives one signal.
5. **Volume SMA(20):** the mean volume of the 20 bars BEFORE Bar[t] in the same RTH session (Bar[t] excluded). This means the first possible ignition bar is the 09:50 bar (index 20) even though the window opens 09:35; stated here, not tuned.
6. **Limit bar window:** bars t+1 .. t+5 inclusive. If a bar opens already through the limit it fills at the Line price (the limit price, conservative: a real order would fill at the better open price). Placing the order needs the bar t+1 to exist (open in the window of orders); no new order placed after a position is open.
7. **Structural stop timing:** the close of the fill bar counts (it happens after the fill); the exit is at the next bar's open.
8. **Per-trade geometry:** the entry price equals the Line, so the risk is exactly 12.0 points and the reward 24.0 points before slippage; a zero-drift trade wins about 1/3 of the time on stop/target alone, fewer once the 4-point structural stop (which exits earlier and smaller) is in play.

## What is reported (explore)
Per variant: ignition signals, orders filled (fill rate), trades, win rate, profit factor (after friction), mean per trade (dollars and points), max drawdown, average winner / loser, day-blocked 95% CI of mean dollars (3000 iterations, seed 20261028), trades per session, exit mix, share of sessions with at least one line. Descriptive only, not part of the reading: of the UNFILLED orders, the share whose price reached Line + 24 (long) / Line - 24 (short) within 30 bars after the order was placed (breakouts that ran without a retest).

## Controls (frozen)
- **Random-entry placebo:** for each real trade a random entry bar in the same session inside 09:35-15:00 with a random direction, entering at that bar's open (no slippage, deliberately giving the placebo the same entry advantage the limit fill has), with the SAME stop and target distances (12 / 24), no structural stop, flatten at 15:55; 100 trials. Reported: real-stripped mean (the real trades re-simulated on this stripped geometry from the same entry bar, so real and placebo use one geometry) vs placebo p5 / p50 / p95 and the share of trials at or above the real mean.
- **Same-selection control ("plain break", the baseline):** the same line, the same crossing bar with Close >= Line + 5 (short mirror) and the same limit retest and management, but the bar FAILS at least one of the initiative conditions (displacement, rejection, volume surge, delta agreement). If the full ignition does not beat this plain break, the initiative conditions add nothing.
- **Market-at-next-open version (diagnostic, not counted):** the same ignition signals entered at the open of Bar[t+1] at market with the same 12 / 24 stop and target measured from that entry, to show what the limit retest does to the result.

## Reading (frozen)
A lead only if the ignition-and-retest explore mean per trade after friction has a day-blocked CI lower bound > 0 AND beats the placebo p95 AND beats the plain-break control's mean. Otherwise "not shown" (never "no effect"). A lead earns a single sealed hold-out look only with the user's approval; a miss ends this branch's explore stage. Not shown on a thin N (under 150 trades) is reported as underpowered.
