# Pre-registration: the tell as context for roster setups, with an expansion-forecast model (written 2026-11-02 BEFORE any outcome was read)

Scope and reasons: `docs/SCOPE_ROSTER_CONTEXT_EXPANSION_FORECAST_20261102.md`. Population decision (resolved 2026-11-02, user delegated): explore on the simulated BACKFILL rows through 2025-05-16, exploration only; forecast trained on the explore decision points; NO read of the sealed window (sessions after 2025-05-16 stay unextracted); confirmation only on forward live tags. Any hold-out spend needs the user.
Nothing below was computed from outcomes. Only counts were read: `decision_points_explore.csv` columns `date,t,dir` (41,272 rows, 516 dates, 2023-04-04..2025-05-16, by clock hour 10:14,854 11:10,143 12:7,067 13:6,166 14:3,042) and `active_setups` row counts. 536 explore sessions load; 516 have the 20-session baseline the research code requires.

## Facts that bound the study (counts, no outcomes)
- BACKFILL rows through 2025-05-16 fired RTH 10:00-14:30 ET: 1,787 rows, 120 setup types, and ALL of them are level-fade or magnet types (name contains `_FADE_` or `MAGNET`). There is no breakout or trend family in the simulated history, so a fade-versus-trend split is NOT testable here; it can only come from forward live tags.
- Per type the average is about 15 rows, so type-level conclusions are impossible; the pooled fade family is the only roster-level unit (cv-033: pooling is a question, so the family result is a lead, not a verdict).
- Real roster rows begin 2026-07-09 and are not used (sealed window). Globex is not tested (recorded gap, hr-002; `PREREG_GLOBEX.md` is a draft). Primary window: fire or decision time 10:00-14:30 ET; state lookbacks are truncated at 09:30 ET (`minutes_since_rth_open` is reported).

## Frozen definitions
- Tell state: `scripts/amt_context/tell_state.py` (`state_at`), which calls the research functions in `scripts/amt_vbars/trade.py` literally. `tell_on` at a completed bar = the research TELL5 (pace A>=1, back-and-forth Q>=QCUT, oscillation R>=RCUT, strides, ladder L>=2) in either direction while price is inside the boundary (`inside_range`). Lookbacks 5/15/30/60 minutes. State uses only bars with end <= the time, floored to the minute. Causality is a TEST: `parity_state.py` (prefix-invariance plus two mutation tests) must pass before any outcome is read.
- Target: E_W = (highest high minus lowest low of the completed bars with end in (ts, ts+W]) divided by ATR20, direction-free, W in {15, 30, 60} minutes. PRIMARY horizon W = 30. 15 and 60 are reported, not counted. ATR20 = mean RTH range of the prior 20 sessions (research definition).
- Decision grid for the forecast: the research grid, every 10th completed bar ending 10:00-14:30 ET, one row per bar (not per side), recomputed from the session files, not taken from the CSV.
- Feature set (frozen): everything `state_at` returns except `i0`, `bar_end_sec`, `age_of_last_bar_s`; plus clock minute and day of week. No other feature may be added after seeing a result.
- Baseline forecast: mean E_W of the training dates in the same 30-minute clock bucket (shrunk to the global mean when a bucket has fewer than 50 training rows).
- Model: ridge regression on log E_W, regularisation chosen by time-ordered cross-validation inside the training dates only (grid 0.1, 1, 10, 100, 1000, fixed now). A gradient-boosted model is allowed ONLY if the ridge result passes H2, and then as a labelled secondary.
- Walk-forward: expanding by date, first 120 dates train only, refit every 20 dates.
- Statistics: day-blocked bootstrap CI (3000 resamples, seed 20261102) for every interval; label-permutation null by permuting E vectors across days (blocks), 1000 draws; chronological halves by median date; Benjamini-Hochberg q=0.10 across the counted hypotheses.

## Hypotheses (counted family H2, H3, H4; H1 is descriptive context, not counted)
- H1 (P1): E_30 is higher when `tell_any_30m` is true than when false, within clock-hour strata; report the stratum-weighted difference with CI. Descriptive; uses full-sample strata.
- H2 (P2, PRIMARY): the ridge forecast beats the baseline forecast out of sample for E_30, measured as the difference in Spearman rank correlation (model minus baseline). PASS = CI lower bound > 0 AND permutation p < 0.05 AND the difference > 0 in both chronological halves.
- H3 (P2): the ridge forecast beats a model that has only the clock bucket plus the five `tell_*` flags, same metric and pass rule (does anything beyond "tell yes or no" matter).
- H4 (P3, only if H2 passes): on the 1,787 BACKFILL fade rows, skipping rows whose forecast E_30 is in the top third (cutoff from the training dates before the row, expanding) gives a lower total simulated P&L loss than skipping the same number of random rows (matched placebo, 1000 draws). Outcome = sum of `actual_pnl` of the skipped rows, a day-blocked CI of the mean per skipped row, paired against the placebo, reported per day and per trade. PASS = skipped rows have negative mean P&L with CI upper bound < 0 AND beat the placebo at p < 0.05 AND both halves point the same way. Half-size is reported, not counted. BACKFILL is simulated, so a pass is a hypothesis for forward tags, never a dollar claim (hr-006).
- H5 (P4, forward only): the same rule on forward live tags (ACTIVE and SHADOW, `is_cluster_primary`, RTH 10:00-14:30). Not evaluated before 60 trading days of tags and at least 300 qualifying rows; the first look is a counted look; a fade-versus-trend split is only possible here.

## Kill rules
P1: H1 shows no E_30 difference beyond what the earlier expansion study showed -> stop the tell-state line and record it. P2: H2 fails -> the forecast is dropped (no boosted model, no extra features, no new horizons to rescue it); the tell stays an expansion marker. P3: H4 fails -> no roster rule is proposed; P4 stays as a passive log only. A pass anywhere is a lead for the next stage, not a result.

## Ledger and discipline
Every script that reads E_W, `actual_pnl` or `mfe/mae` first logs a look with `scripts/lib/look_ledger.mjs` (family `roster_context_20261102`, window `explore<=2025-05-16`, label counts of what it reads); results via `recordClaim()`. No sealed look is spent. The explore dates have been used by many earlier studies, so positive results here carry a selection caveat and are hypotheses. Decisions and horizons are fixed above; any addition is a written amendment BEFORE it is run, with the rerun disclosed alongside the original.

## Amendment 1 (2026-11-02, written AFTER reading the H1 output and BEFORE any further outcome is read; the H1 run is `run_p1_output.txt`, look id 32)
Why: H1 used `tell_any_30m`, which is true on 63.8 percent of the 30,821 decision points (19,674 on, 11,147 off; at the 10:00 hour 8,420 on versus 2,102 off). That is not a selective tell flag: it largely separates active stretches from quiet ones, and the tell requires fast pace by construction. The primary H1 contrast (E_30 +0.061 ATR20, day-blocked CI +0.047..+0.078, both halves positive, same sign at 15 and 60 minutes) therefore cannot be read as a tell effect. Also the "pace held fixed" check only conditioned on pace at the decision bar (A >= 1). This is a disclosure, not a rescue; H1 stays as run.
Changes (all before any further outcome read):
1. Descriptive additions, post-hoc and labelled as such, uncounted, a lead only if they hold: (a) the contrast for `tell_now` (the research TELL5 at the decision bar itself) and `tell_any_5m`; (b) every tell flag contrasted inside clock-hour x A_now-tercile strata (a pace-matched control), same estimator and CI as H1. Ledger: one look, tests = 4.
2. H3 is split. H3a stays as written (model versus clock bucket + the five tell flags). NEW counted H3b, the decisive tell question: the full ridge forecast versus the same ridge WITHOUT any tell feature (`tell_*`, `c4max_now`, `minutes_since_last_tell`), i.e. activity features only (pace A_now, range so far, overnight range, clock, day of week). Same metric (difference in out-of-sample Spearman for E_30) and the same pass rule. If H3b fails, "the tell adds nothing beyond activity" is the recorded result for E_30.

## Amendment 2 (2026-11-02, written after the P2 result `run_p2_output.txt` (H2, H3a, H3b all PASS as registered) and BEFORE the stricter control was run; look ledger entry follows)

## Amendment 4 (2026-11-02, written after the H4 result `run_p3_output.txt` (NOT PASSED) and BEFORE the check below is run; descriptive, uncounted, a lead only)
Question: H4 tested the SIGN of the effect (does skipping busy-forecast fades save money). It did not test whether the forecast relates to the SIZE of what these trades experience. Check, on exactly the 1,507 evaluated rows of `p3_rows.csv` (no new rows, no new thresholds): by forecast third (the H4 expanding thirds) report the mean of the trade's own excursion `mfe_points + mae_points`, its mean `mfe_points`, mean `mae_points`, and mean absolute `actual_pnl`; plus the Spearman correlation between the forecast and `mfe_points + mae_points` with a day-blocked 95% CI. Reading rule: a positive correlation with a CI above zero and an increasing excursion by third means the forecast tracks the travel these trades see (so H4's null is about sign and costs, not about the forecast); otherwise the link from forecast to these trades is itself not shown. Nothing is tuned; no result of this check changes H4, which stays NOT PASSED. Ledger: one look, tests = 5.

## Amendment 5 (2026-11-02, written after the H4 and Amendment 4 results and BEFORE the horizon is computed or any rerun; user go: "match it, set the forecast horizon"). ONE horizon-matched rerun, not a search.
Why: H4 and the excursion check both failed to connect the 30-minute forecast to the fade trades, and the trades' mean excursion (about 74 points) suggests they are held much longer than 30 minutes. This tests that single untested explanation.
Rule for the horizon, fixed now: H* = the median hold time, in minutes, from `fired_at` to `resolved_at` over the usable BACKFILL rows of H4 (rows with a non-null `resolved_at`), rounded to the nearest 15 minutes, floor 30, cap 240. No other horizon is tried. If more than 30 percent of the training decision points cannot have a complete H* window (the session data end first), the test is declared UNDERPOWERED and reported as such, not altered.
Procedure, identical to H4 except the target: the no-tell model `notell2` is trained to predict log E_H* (E_H* = highest high minus lowest low of completed bars with end in (ts, ts + H*], over ATR20, windows required to be complete), same walk-forward blocks, same alpha rule; each row's forecast is the model trained on earlier dates applied to the features at the fire time; skip the expanding top third; outcomes `actual_pnl`. Pass rule and placebo exactly as H4 (skipped CI upper < 0, placebo p < 0.05, skipped mean below kept mean in both halves). Also reported (uncounted): the Spearman between the forecast and the trade's own `mfe_points + mae_points` and the means by forecast third. A pass is a hypothesis for forward tags only (simulated P&L). A fail closes the simulated-history route: the forecast then goes to the forward tags without a rule from this study. Ledger: one look, tests = 3.

## Amendment 6 (2026-11-02, written BEFORE the script exists or any outcome is read; user-approved item 3 of `docs/HANDOFF_20261102_NEXT_SESSION.md`). ONE incremental test: overnight volume added to the 30-minute forecast.
Why: the overnight-to-RTH range effect was already shown (claims `overnight_rth_range_expansion_control_20261026`, `overnight_rth_context_partA_20261026`: a big overnight range / high overnight volume per bar predicts a wider RTH range, +0.14 to +0.26 ATR) but feeds nothing; the forecast already carries the overnight RANGE, so the open question is whether overnight VOLUME adds anything beyond it (hr-001 consumer; hr-052 prior work found first).
Feature, fixed now: ONV = log( N_on / mean of N_on over the 20 sessions before this one ), where N_on = number of COMPLETE 500-volume bars with end before 09:30 ET in the session (a bar is 500 contracts, so the count is overnight contract volume / 500); the 20-session window is the same one the ATR20 baseline uses (`core.baseline_inputs` / `sessions_and_contexts`), so nothing from the session itself or later enters its own baseline. Causal: every overnight bar is complete by 09:30 and the feature is constant within a session. Sessions whose 20-session baseline lacks a count are dropped and counted.
Models (the P2b/Amendment 3 pipeline unchanged: ridge on log E_30, same grid points, FIRST_TRAIN 120 dates, refit every 20 dates, alpha by the same time-ordered inner CV): BASE = `notell2` (the champion's design); PLUS = BASE + ONV as one more continuous column. Statistic: difference in out-of-sample Spearman(prediction, realized E_30), PLUS minus BASE, over all out-of-sample points. PASS = day-blocked bootstrap 95% CI (3000, seed 20261102) lower bound > 0 AND date-permutation p < 0.05 (1000 draws, the P2b null: realized target permuted across dates, models refit on the cached designs) AND positive in both halves by median date. Counted tests: 1 (ledger tests = 1).
Reported, uncounted: the Spearman of ONV with overnight_range_atr (collinearity), the PLUS coefficient sign in the last refit, out-of-sample Spearman of BASE and PLUS separately, and the four chronological quarters (cv-033).
Reading rule: PASS -> PLUS becomes a CHALLENGER only (docs/FORECAST_SERVICE_20261102.md rules: no champion swap, no wiring; live serving would need the same feature in `forecast_features.py` with a parity test, built only if the user wants it); NOT PASSED -> overnight volume adds nothing measurable beyond overnight range for the 30-minute forecast, recorded with the CI half-width as the resolution, and the earlier day-level volume fact stays a context fact with no consumer. No sealed window, no money claim.
