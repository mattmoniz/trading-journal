## 1. Rows and label

LABEL y = ln((highest high - lowest low of the next 60 minutes) / ADR_t), ADR_t = the median RTH range of the 20 sessions before the day. ## 2. Models (expanding walk-forward by day, refit every 5 days, warm-up 120 days, ridge with the penalty chosen by time-ordered inner CV)
M0 BASELINE: three clock dummies + the HAR-X day regressors (ln of the previous session's range / ADR, its mean over the previous 5 and 22 sessions, ln(overnight range through 09:29 / ADR): all known at 09:30). M1 = M0 + group D. M2 = M1 + group C. M3 = M2 + group S.

GROUP C, now versus lately: ln(range of the last 30 minutes / the median of the same 30-minute window over the previous 20 sessions); ln(tick-derived volume of the last 30 minutes / its 20-session norm for the same window); the absolute share imbalance (|up-tick - down-tick| / total) of the last 30 minutes.
