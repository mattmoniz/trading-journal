## Data and bars

Scale `H4` = mean high-low range of the 4-hour bars over the 20 prior trade days; `D20` = mean full-session range of the 20 prior trade days (as in earlier studies); `A15` as before.

## The level (price action only, no stored level; causal at each 4-hour bar close)
At each completed 4-hour bar `t`, over the last 12 bars (2 trading days): candidate FLOOR level `Lf` = the lowest low (earliest bar achieving it). Band `b = 0.25 x H4` (sensitivity 0.5). A bar is a DEFENDED bar if its low is within `[Lf, Lf + b]` and its close is above `Lf`. `K` = number of defended bars in the window (distinct bars, not necessarily consecutive). The level is BROKEN at the first 4-hour bar that closes below `Lf - b`. TOP is the mirror (highs, closes below `Hf`). An EVENT is emitted at the close of the bar where `K` FIRST reaches 3, 4 and 5+ for that level (one event per level per K, at most one K=5+ event per level); events carry K, direction (floor or top), the level, the touching bars' relative volume, and the session the confirmation bar closed in.
**Relative volume (the one metric):** mean over the defended bars of (bar volume / mean volume of the SAME session-slot 4-hour bar over the 20 prior trade days); HEAVY = above the median of explore-period events, LIGHT otherwise.

## Tests (each reported separately; a pooled verdict is never given)
Entry for all arms = the open of the first 1-minute bar after the confirming 4-hour bar closes. Outcome = direction-signed forward return from that open to the close of the bar 239 / 1,439 / 2,879 minutes later, in `D20` units, MINUS a clock-matched blind same-direction entry on other dates (within 30 minutes of the clock, up to 10 draws) = EXCESS. Primary horizon +1,440 minutes (the level is a 12-20 hour structure, so the hold is a day, not an hour). Costs: exit-free, so none; any later barrier test subtracts 1.5 points.
- **FADE-F (long off a floor) and FADE-T (short off a top):** events as above (K = 3, 4, 5+ pooled for the verdict, K split descriptively).
- **BREAK-F (short after a floor breaks) and BREAK-T (long after a top breaks):** event = the first 4-hour close beyond a level that had `K >= 3` defended bars (the break), entry at the next open; the survivorship-free control is the same event for levels with `K = 1` (a one-bar low, lightly defended).
- **Survivorship control for the fades:** paired within level: the same level's outcome after its K = 3 confirmation versus after its FIRST defended bar (K = 1), same direction (so "a level that survived three tests" is not mistaken for "a level that predicts a bounce").
- **Volume split:** each primary cell is also reported HEAVY and LIGHT; the HEAVY-minus-LIGHT contrast is descriptive.
**Counted cells = 8** (FADE-F, FADE-T, BREAK-F, BREAK-T x {HEAVY, LIGHT}); ALL, K splits, the paired contrasts, the 0.5 band and RTH/Globex splits are descriptive/robustness. Explore = earlier 60 percent of dates, hold-out = later 40 percent (a SCREEN only: this history has been mined all day). CANDIDATE = explore date-blocked 95 percent CI lower bound > 0; SCREEN-SURVIVOR = candidate AND hold-out point > 0 AND hold-out CI lower bound > 0 AND explore point > 0; UNDERPOWERED if < 30 events or < 20 distinct dates. Printed: events per direction and per K, expected-by-chance (tested x 0.025, a floor), smallest detectable effect (CI half-width), and the share of events whose 1,440-minute windows overlap another event of the same direction.

## Limits stated in advance
Levels from 4-hour bars are slow and few (expect roughly 100-300 floor events and a similar number of tops over 3.5 years, much fewer independent levels); the band, window and K definitions come from the user's description, not from the data, with one sensitivity point; overlapping 1,440-minute windows after consecutive K events; the chart the user reads is not exactly these bars (session alignment of the user's 240-minute bars is assumed to start at 18:00); NQ drifts up, hence the clock-matched control and the paired design.
