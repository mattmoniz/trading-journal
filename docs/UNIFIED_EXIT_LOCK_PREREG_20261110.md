## The arms (one setting each, fixed now; sensitivities are descriptive)
- LOCK RULE (used by every arm): once triggered, stop = entry + d x 0.5 x (best favourable excursion through the last COMPLETED bar), only ever moved in the trade's favour, re-ratcheting as the excursion grows, effective from the next bar. 0.5 is Argon's "lock half the open gain": the runner keeps the other half.
- P (profit-gated): trigger = best favourable excursion >= GATE: tell 0.15 x ADR20 of the trade date (about 60 to 90 pts; the user's "100 points" is 0.17 ADR20 in November 2026); vacuum 1.5 x the initial stop distance.
- K (capitulation-gated, tell only): trigger = the first completed bar whose end time is at or after the end time of a same-direction CLIMAX (row 42 definition: live turn-signal S1_SPIKE, extension in the rolling top tercile) that fired after the entry, while the open gain at that bar's close is > 0; then the LOCK RULE.
- S (speed split, tell only; USES THE POST-HOC SPEED LEAD, labelled): P, plus an exit at the next bar open on the first same-direction NOT-fast climax (PACE5 above the rolling tercile cutoff of row 44) while the open gain at its time is > 0 (price-based, so it can be fixed before the path is known); extreme-fast climaxes trigger nothing extra.
- P' (vacuum, RTH and Globex apart): P with the vacuum gate. K / S do not apply to the vacuum (its entries ARE the thrusts).

## Counted family (assert 5), estimator and gate
Cells: T-P, T-K, T-S, V-P-RTH, V-P-GBX. Statistic = mean PAIRED difference (arm minus baseline) per trade (sum/N; day-blocked bootstrap, 3,000 draws, seeds 1700 + k, one-sided: arm > baseline), p = (1 + count)/(draws + 1). Gate: >= 100 trades whose path the arm CHANGED ("acted") on >= 60 days, else the cell is PARKED (K and S are expected to be thin). Holm 0.10 over the cells that pass; pre-declared fallback 0.05 if the null gate exceeds 0.10. Reported with each cell: N, acted, mean difference, CI, MDE in dollars / points, halves, top-5-days share, the exit mix, trades improved vs worsened, and the give-back captured.
Descriptive, never counted: (a) a TIMING PLACEBO for K and S (the same lock at a random completed in-profit bar of the same trade, seeded); (b) sensitivities: gate x0.67 and x1.33, lock fraction 0.25 and 0.75; (c) the arms split RTH vs Globex entries for the tell; (d) the worst-trade and 5th-percentile trade of each arm (left tail); (e) per-year.
