## 1. Watcher (per completed 500-contract bar; same bars the reader already builds)
States (the user's words): UNKNOWN, BALANCE_FORMING, BALANCE_CONFIRMED, DRIVE_UP, DRIVE_DOWN, PING_PONG (after a drive). Inputs only from completed bars through bar t (no lookahead).
- Scale: r20 = median (high-low) of the last 20 bars (the tell's own scale); windows are in bars and reuse the tell's 20-bar stride window (N=20) and its 10-bar flow window.
- UNKNOWN: fewer than 60 RTH minutes (as the context label). Everything below runs after that.
- DRIVE_UP: at least one RTH close above ONH (the overnight high; the user's cue names the overnight levels, not IB) AND at least 2 new RTH highs beyond ONH, the latest within the last 40 bars (A1: 40, not 20). DRIVE_DOWN mirrors with ONL. Leaves the drive when price closes back inside ONH/ONL (or ONL/ONH) for 10 consecutive bars.
- BALANCE_CONFIRMED (user: "tests its low and doesn't revisit it, then makes a high, then defends both"): the RTH low and high of the day are both at least 40 bars old (two stride windows) and neither has been exceeded since, AND at least one DENIED APPROACH occurred (a bar high within 1 r20 of the extreme that did not exceed it, followed by a retreat of at least 2 r20 within 20 bars). A side with at least 2 denied approaches is a DEFENDED edge (user: "second and third attempts denied"); defended edges are recorded in the evidence.
- BALANCE_FORMING: RTH profile past 60 minutes, no drive, balance not yet confirmed.
- PING_PONG: the last drive ended (or a P/b-like thin tail exists) and the last 40 closes show a Kaufman efficiency ratio (|net| / sum |changes|) in the lowest tercile of the explore sessions' own distribution for that clock hour (data-derived, features only) AND the share of the last 9 bar-delta pairs that flip sign is at least the tell's own Q cutoff (0.4444, frozen from the tell). Meaning: drive stopped, two-sided volume.
- Hysteresis: a state change needs the new state's condition true for 3 consecutive completed bars and the old state must have lasted at least 10 bars. One bar never flips a state. Priority when several hold: DRIVE > PING_PONG > BALANCE_CONFIRMED > BALANCE_FORMING.
- Output per bar: state, confidence (the count of satisfied sub-conditions out of the total for that state), evidence jsonb. Persisted only on CHANGE: table `tell_watcher_states` (append-only: trade_date, bar_idx, bar_end_et, state, prev_state, evidence jsonb, code_version). Every tell fire's context row gets the state at its signal bar (`context.watcher`).

## 2. Shadow context books (logged, simulated fills, no orders, same entries as the tell)
Simulated on the same completed bars: entry = the open of the bar after the signal bar plus 1 tick against (as `trade.run_position`), one position at a time per book, exits as the tell's V1 where not stated (120-minute no-break time exit, 15:55 flatten, FADE after a break), a stop gapped through fills at the bar open (cv-030), a bar touching both stop and target counts as the stop first, costs 2 dollars round trip plus 1 dollar slippage. The tell's own V1 row is Book A (taken from `amt_tell_trades`, not re-simulated).
- Book B_k "value-edge stop" (k in {0.5, 1, 2} x r20, three arms): same entry and direction as the tell; stop just beyond the value edge on the adverse side (short: VAH + k r20; long: VAL - k r20); if the entry is already beyond that edge (in the tail) the stop goes beyond the day's extreme so far + k r20. The question: does a thesis-based stop beat the node-ladder stop.
- Book C "flip": when the label is AGAINST the trade goes the OTHER way at the same entry; stop just beyond the value edge it defends (flipped long from the bottom: VAL - 1 r20; flipped short from the top: VAH + 1 r20), exit target the POC, 120-minute time exit, 15:55 flatten. AGREES, NEUTRAL and UNKNOWN fires follow the tell (no flip, no skip; Book C then equals Book A on those fires and only flipped fires are scored as their own trades, with the paired difference against the tell's original trade on the same fire).
- Persistence `tell_shadow_trades` (book, tell_trade_id, direction, entry, stop, target, status, exit fields, net_dollars, mfe/mae, lifecycle OPEN -> CLOSED then frozen, code_version). Written by the reader as bars complete.
- Scorecard (Monitor & Learning, one section): per book N closed, mean dollars per trade (sum/N, never day-weighted), the paired difference against the tell on the same fires, N below 20 is not a result (hr-008), the watcher state distribution at fire, and a plain "NOT VALIDATED, nothing is gated" line.

## 5. Amendment A1 after DeepSeek round L (full text `scratch/deepseek_response_20261103_172038.md`, audited against the files)
ACCEPTED. (1) v1 is the SMALLEST trustworthy version: watcher states UNKNOWN, BALANCE_FORMING, BALANCE_CONFIRMED, DRIVE_UP, DRIVE_DOWN (PING_PONG, the 2-denial "defended edge" label and the stop-size grid k in {0.5, 2} wait for v1.1; PING_PONG chains to the shape letter that is still being settled). (2) SAME EXIT BY CONSTRUCTION (cv-026, hr-042, hr-022): every book runs the tell's REAL `trade.run_position` (V1 exit: stop, STOP_GAP, 120-minute no-break time exit, FADE after the break, 15:55 flatten) on the same bars with only the stop (B) or the direction and stop (C) changed; Book A is RE-SIMULATED through the same function with the tell's own stop on the reader's bars, as the entry-price-only control, and checked for parity against the `amt_tell_trades` row (the DB row is not used as the control). The POC target is an extra v1.1 arm (C_poc), not part of v1 C, so the flip effect is not confounded with an exit change. (3) Stop validity: books inherit the tell's guard (`d*(entry - stop) <= 0` skips); with the context label UNKNOWN (before 60 RTH minutes) books B and C follow A. (4) The scorecard never sums dollars across books; it prints paired differences against A per fire, N in distinct fires AND distinct days, median and mean, the sign-consistency count, a plain N<20 gate and the state-at-fire distribution. (5) Retention: the reader keeps the full bar dicts (same objects as the session's, no extra memory) and builds the tell's own `ctx` with `core.build_ctx`; shadow trades are a STATELESS replay (re-run on the completed bars so far, upsert OPEN, freeze once CLOSED), so a restart recovers by itself and nothing is orphaned; the session's last pass runs with the tell's `final` convention. (6) `code_version` also hashes `watcher.py` and `shadow.py`. (7) DEFINITIONS made codable. Denied approach for the day high H set at bar j: a later bar k with high within 1 r20(k) of H and not above it, DENIED if some close within the next 20 bars is at least 2 r20(k) below that bar's high; an episode ends when price has retreated 2 r20 below the approach high, so one episode counts once (lower extreme mirrors). BALANCE_CONFIRMED needs both extremes at least 40 bars old and at least one denial on either side (per-side counts kept in the evidence; the user's "defended" = 2 or more, recorded as a flag only). DRIVE_UP: an RTH close above the overnight high, at least 2 new RTH highs beyond it, the latest within the last 40 bars (a stalled drive ends), and not all of the last 10 closes back inside; evidence also records the tell's own gate (max(ONH, IBH)) so the two breakout definitions can be compared. Raw state per bar is memoryless; hysteresis is a separate pass (3 consecutive bars to change, 10-bar dwell, the first state after the 60-minute floor is immediate). Priority DRIVE > BALANCE_CONFIRMED > BALANCE_FORMING.
NOT ACCEPTED: cutting the state-change log (the flip-flop rate is the main early gauge of the watcher, the user was promised it); keeping both is cheap (rows only on change). The Globex session stays out of v1 (the tell is RTH only; hr-002 gap recorded, tested from scratch later, hr-039).
