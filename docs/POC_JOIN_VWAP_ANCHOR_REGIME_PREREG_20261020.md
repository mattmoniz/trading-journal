# Pre-registration: POC_ROTATION_JOIN VWAP follow-ups A and B (written 2026-10-20 BEFORE any outcome from these two tests)

Origin: DeepSeek review (scratch/deepseek_response_vwap_review_20261020.md, audited 2026-10-20). Verified there: (1) the first VWAP-slope gate anchored VWAP at 18:00 (a cumulative 23h average) and calibrated its flat cut on whole-session slopes, so it is not "the VWAP a trader looks at in RTH"; its slope class is also strongly tied to price side (price above VWAP in 90 percent of UP joins, 29 percent of DOWN); (2) only 352 of 3,626 joins classified DOWN, so the short side was barely tested; (3) the label-shuffle should be within direction; (4) a concentration criterion must fail on a non-positive total. Those implementation fixes are already applied to the first two tests (permutation p 0.586 and 0.933 after the fixes; conclusions unchanged). The user's own idea: VWAP only as a check of the market's general direction; a FLAT VWAP is balance, where joins may do well fading the outer edges (b/D-shaped profiles). Constraint (CLAUDE.md, repeated by DeepSeek): directional-pressure/position signals validated in RTH tend to reverse in Globex for this roster, so RTH and GLOBEX are judged SEPARATELY and each must pass.

Common: events = real `detectSignalEvents` (65pt, standard, point-in-time THETA), 2023-03-06..2026-10-16, roll weeks excluded, entry = open of the bar after the trigger bar; outcome = realistic-fill live exit E1 (100pt stop, breakeven from forward bar 60, hold to next event or 24h, stop beyond the market fills at the bar open, $2/pt, $2 round trip, forward bars strictly after the entry bar); day-blocked bootstrap 2000, per-trade mean = sum/N; eras by trade date (PRE < 2025-06-01); `computeRunningVwapSeries` for VWAP; RTH = entry bar 09:30-15:59 ET, GLOBEX = entry bar before 09:30 ET (the 18:00-09:29 window). "Previous 20 RTH dates" = the 20 dates before d with an RTH session of >= 300 bars.

## TEST A -- the one-shot anchor check (RTH only; closes the slope-direction thread)
- VWAP anchored at the RTH open: `computeRunningVwapSeries` over the 1-minute RTH bars (09:30-15:59) of trade date d. A join is eligible if its trigger bar is an RTH bar with index t >= 60 counted from 09:30 (so entry 10:30 or later).
- slope = VWAP[t] - VWAP[t-60]. Flat cut c(d) = p33 (floor((n-1)/3) of the sorted pool) of |slope| sampled at every 15th RTH bar index >= 60 of each of the previous 20 RTH dates (RTH-matched, no Globex mixing). Classes UP/DOWN/FLAT, ALIGNED/OPPOSED exactly as the first VWAP gate.
- Rule: fire only ALIGNED. Compare ALIGNED minus ALL (eligible RTH joins), ALIGNED minus OPPOSED, overall and by era. Placebo 1: flipped direction. Placebo 2: shuffle UP/DOWN/FLAT labels within direction x calendar month, 2000 permutations (one-sided p). Tail: P(pnl >= +100); top-5 share of the aligned total (must be a POSITIVE total to pass).
- Pass (ALL): N aligned >= 100 overall and >= 40 in each era; ALIGNED minus ALL > 0 with overall CI excluding zero and > 0 in both eras; ALIGNED minus OPPOSED CI excludes zero; permutation p <= 0.05; beats flipped placebo overall and in both eras; top-5 share < 50 percent of a positive total; P(>=+100) aligned > all.
- If it fails: the "VWAP slope as a direction check" thread is closed permanently (no more anchors/windows). If it passes: SHADOW tag only, forward recheck.

## TEST B -- FLAT regime x outer-band location ("fade the outer edge of balance")
- Session constructions: RTH joins use the RTH-open-anchored VWAP (as in A, entry index t >= 60 from 09:30); GLOBEX joins use the 18:00-anchored VWAP (index t >= 60 from 18:00, entry before 09:30). Flat cut c = p33 of |slope60| from the previous 20 RTH dates, sampled at every 15th bar index >= 60, SAME session type only (RTH samples from the 09:30 series within RTH; Globex samples from the 18:00 series within 18:00-09:29).
- Location: dist = entry bar open - VWAP[t]; sigma(d) = standard deviation of (typical price - running VWAP) sampled at every 15th bar index >= 60 over the previous 20 RTH dates, same session type; z = dist / sigma. OUTER cut q(d) = p67 of |z| in that same pool (rolling, data-derived top third).
- Population: FLAT joins (|slope| <= c). EDGE-ALIGNED = (long join and z <= -q) or (short join and z >= +q), i.e. a join that moves BACK TOWARD VWAP from an outer position of a balanced session. Rule: in a FLAT regime fire only EDGE-ALIGNED joins.
- Comparisons per session type (RTH and GLOBEX separately, each also by era): EDGE-ALIGNED minus ALL FLAT joins, and minus the OTHER flat joins; placebo 1: flipped direction; placebo 2: shuffle the side-of-VWAP label (sign of z) within direction x month among FLAT joins, 2000 permutations; tail metrics as above.
- Pass (ALL, in BOTH RTH and GLOBEX separately): N edge-aligned >= 40 in that session type; EDGE-ALIGNED minus ALL FLAT > 0 with the day-blocked CI excluding zero; positive in PRE and POST; permutation p <= 0.05; beats flipped placebo; top-5 share < 50 percent of a positive total; P(>=+100) edge-aligned > all flat.
- Power warning: FLAT is about a third of joins and EDGE-ALIGNED a third of those; the test can only detect large effects. A failure for want of power is reported as such, not as "no effect".
If either test passes only in one session type the conclusion is "no", per the RTH/GLOBEX constraint. Independent Gemini builds from this file alone; disagreements traced before reporting.
