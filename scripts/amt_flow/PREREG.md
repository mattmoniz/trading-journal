# Order-flow branch: Setup A (sweep + CVD absorption) and Setup B (build-and-go) (frozen 2026-10-28)

Committed BEFORE any outcome is read. User spec: "Context Sweeps & Micro-Volume Acceptance". EXPLORE only (the same 529 eligible sessions to 2025-05-16 used by the 80%-rule study); the option-1 hold-out and every other hold-out stay SEALED and are not touched. No ACSIL code until an edge over the placebo is verified.

## Resolutions of what the spec leaves open (chosen before any result)
1. **Size and money:** 1 MNQ contract ($2/point, $2 round-trip commission, repo ground truth); 1 tick adverse slippage on entries (market), stop fills and market exits; target limits fill at the level with no slippage but only when the bar trades 1 tick THROUGH it; a stop that takes effect when the market is already beyond it fills at that bar's open; stop checked before the target on the same bar. Results are also in points.
2. **Flat time:** the spec gives none; assumed 15:55 (flatten at that bar's open), as in the other studies. One position at a time per setup. The spec gives no daily cap and none is added (clustered trades are handled by day-blocked CIs; trades per session are reported).
3. **Levels:** pdVAH/pdVAL = prior full RTH session 70% value area (`engine.profile`, asserted equal to the loader's). ONH/ONL = highest high / lowest low of the 1-minute bars from 18:00 the previous calendar day to 09:29 (needs >= 500 bars, else the ON levels are missing that day). IBH/IBL = high/low of the 09:30-10:29 bars (complete at the 10:30 open).
4. **Confluence Level = a ZONE:** for each pair (pdVAH or pdVAL) x (ONH, ONL, IBH, IBL) whose two prices are within 20.0 points, the zone is the interval between them (identical zones merged). A bar "touches or penetrates" a zone when its high-low range intersects it. When several zones qualify on one bar the zone whose centre is nearest the trigger bar's close is used.
5. **Micro VPOC:** the POC (first maximum) of the volume profile of the last 15 one-minute bars (each bar's volume spread across its own high-low tick range), recomputed every bar.
6. **Setup A:** trigger bar t closes with both bars t-1 and t touching one zone; LONG if low[t] < low[t-1] and delta[t] (ask minus bid volume) > 0; SHORT if high[t] > high[t-1] and delta[t] < 0. Entry at the open of t+1 (+1 tick adverse), the entry bar's open must be between 10:30 and 15:15. Stop = 6.0 points beyond the lowest low (long) / highest high (short) of bars t-1 and t. Target = 15.0 points from the entry. Structural exit: a bar closing 5.0 points beyond the zone on the adverse side (long: close < zone low - 5; short: close > zone high + 5) exits at the next open (market, 1 tick).
7. **Setup B:** for a zone and direction UP: the last 5 closes (t-4..t) are all above the zone high AND micro VPOC[t] > zone high; the trigger fires on the FIRST bar where this becomes true for that zone and direction and does not fire again until a close at or inside the zone high re-arms it (DOWN mirrored with the zone low). Entry at the next open (+1 tick), entry bar open between 10:30 and 15:00. Stop 10.0 points and target 20.0 points from the entry, both fixed; no other exits.

## What is reported (explore, per setup)
Trades, win rate, profit factor (after friction), mean per trade (dollars and points), max drawdown (trade-sequence equity), average winner / loser, day-blocked 95% CI of mean dollars (3000 iterations, seed 20261028), trades per session, exit mix, share of sessions with at least one confluence zone.

## Controls (frozen)
- **Random-entry placebo (the one the user asked for):** for each real trade, a random entry bar in the same session inside the same entry window and a random direction, with the SAME stop distance and target distance as that real trade, no structural exit, flatten at 15:55 (so real and placebo use one stripped geometry); 100 trials. Reported: real-stripped mean dollars per trade vs the placebo p5/p50/p95 and the share of trials at or above the real mean.
- **Same-selection control (project rule):** Setup A without the order-flow condition (same zone touch and same lower-low / higher-high bar, but delta <= 0 for a long, >= 0 for a short; bars that are both a lower low and a higher high are excluded from the control); Setup B without the acceptance condition (price outside the zone for 5 bars but micro VPOC NOT beyond it). Same engine, same exits. If the signal does not beat its own control, the order-flow condition adds nothing.
- Zero-drift reference: a fixed stop/target geometry with no edge loses about the round-trip cost (about 1.5 points all-in per trade, measured in `scripts/amt_events/exit_placebo_diagnostic.py`); the zero-drift win probability of A is about stop/(stop+target) and of B about 33%.

## Reading (frozen)
A setup is "a lead" only if its explore mean per trade after friction has a day-blocked CI lower bound > 0 AND beats the random-entry placebo p95 AND beats its own same-selection control. Otherwise "not shown" (never "no effect"). A lead earns a single hold-out look only with the user's approval; a miss ends this branch's explore stage. No parameter changes after the first run.
