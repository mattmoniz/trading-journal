# Pre-registration: choose setups by demonstrated edge (walk-forward), and a standing rule for when the ML earns a gate — 2026-10-21

Written and committed BEFORE the walk-forward is run or the scorecard computed. Origin: user (2026-10-21) after the finding that the live ML checkpoint adds no measurable value on unseen trades (`ml_checkpoint_unseen_trades_no_value_20261021`) and the roster averages about -3.3 per trade: "yes lets do those", and include the minute OR mids and the sweeps of the overnight low and overnight high. Research only; nothing live changes.

## Part 1 — walk-forward selection of setups by demonstrated edge
- **Units:** every live-detected setup_type (ACTIVE and SHADOW origin, 2026-07-09 on, 239 types as of 2026-10-21) PLUS two groups the user asked to include explicitly: (a) the minute-OR mids `OR5/OR10/OR15/OR30_MID_FADE_LONG/SHORT` (already setup_types, so they enter as themselves and are printed explicitly); (b) sweep-by-level units for the sweep rules (rows of `STOP_SWEEP_*` and `FAILED_SWEEP_REVERSAL_*`, RTH-fired), one unit per level and direction named `SWEEP_<LEVEL>_<LONG|SHORT>` from the row's `t1_label` (levels include ONL and ONH, the sweeps of the overnight low and high, PDL, PDH, IB low/high and the pivot/POC/OR levels). A sweep row therefore appears both as its own setup_type unit and as its sweep-level unit (the two views are evaluated separately, never summed).
- **Events:** rows with `actual_pnl`, mark-to-market exits INCLUDED (the house filter that drops them is known to bias timeout-exit setups), `NOT_FILLED` excluded, stale/bad-bars flags excluded, rows fired 16:00-18:00 ET excluded (the pre-2026-09-16 post-close flood); one event per confluence-cluster touch (`collapseClusterSiblings`). Dollars per event as recorded (simulated bar-walk fills, no extra slippage).
- **Weeks:** trade_date calendar weeks (Monday start). Evaluation starts at the 5th week of data; each week W uses only events strictly before W (expanding window, no lookahead).
- **Selection at week W (frozen):** a unit is selected if, on its history before W, it has at least 20 collapsed events on at least 10 distinct days, the day-blocked 95 percent interval of its mean lies above zero, AND its mean is above zero in both chronological halves of that history.
- **Forward test:** the events of the selected units that occur in week W. Pooled over all evaluation weeks: mean dollars per event, events per day, total, day-blocked 95 percent interval; the same for ALL units that week (take-all) and for the difference (selected minus take-all, day-blocked).
- **Placebo:** each week, draw random sets of the same number of units from the units that were ELIGIBLE (same event-count and day-count floors but not necessarily positive), 2000 draws; report where the real selected set's pooled forward mean falls in the draws' distribution.
- **Verdict (frozen):** SELECTION HELPS only if (i) the selected forward mean interval is above zero, (ii) selected minus take-all interval is above zero, (iii) the real set ranks at or above the 95th percentile of the placebo draws, and (iv) there are at least 60 forward events on at least 20 distinct days. Otherwise NOT SHOWN, stated as what could not be shown; an underpowered result is reported as underpowered, not as a verdict.
- **Printed whatever the result:** which units were selected in which weeks; the forward results of IB_HIGH_FADE_SHORT, the OR mids, SWEEP_ONL and SWEEP_ONH units each week they had enough history (selected or not); events per day of the selected set (a single contract cannot take overlapping trades, so the realistic count is lower).
- **Secondary arm, no verdict:** selection history = the BACKFILL rows (2023-11 to 2026-07-08, synthetic) plus the live rows before W. The earlier backfill-to-live family study found the history-selected edges failed forward (`setup_family_sim_fills_drag_edge_oot_20261021`), so this arm is reported for comparison only.

## Part 2 — standing rule for when the ML earns a gate (weekly scorecard)
`scripts/ml_unseen_scorecard.mjs` (weekly) recomputes, on trades fired at least 3 days after the live checkpoint was trained and scored by that checkpoint (`ml_verdicts`), the quantities in `scripts/analyze_ml_checkpoint_unseen_20261021.mjs`: take-all, TAKE and VETO means, policy minus random approval of the same share (day-blocked 95 percent interval), and the Spearman correlation between score and P&L. It stores one `performance_audit` row (`signal_type` `ML_UNSEEN_SCORECARD`). The gate rule: the ML may be proposed for any gating role only if, in the same weekly run, (a) at least 20 distinct unseen days, (b) policy minus random approval has its interval above zero, (c) the score-P&L Spearman correlation is above zero with a day-blocked interval above zero, AND this holds in two consecutive weekly runs. When that happens the script flags an `OPEN_DECISION` for the user; it never changes any live behavior itself. Until then the ML stays log-only. The first run (today) is the baseline and is expected to fail (a 16-day window).

## Known limits (stated in advance)
Only about 3.5 months and 78 trading days of live-detected rows exist, so most units reach the 20-event floor late or never and the selected set will be small; the simulated fills are bar-walks, not broker fills; selection among 239+ units has a multiple-testing surface (the half-stability rule and the placebo are the guards); the sweep-level units for ONL/ONH come only from the old StopSweep rule's rows until the merged detector (live after the next restart) produces its own.
