## 1. Construction
The same 1,760 explore entries, V1 management unchanged, the entry unchanged; the stop is CAPPED: stop distance = min(the tell's own stop distance, C) points from the fill (a capped stop is tighter only on trades whose own stop is wider than C; every other trade is exactly V1, parity printed first). Re-simulated with `watcher_stop_lib.run_dyn` (default path reproduces V1; the cap passes the smaller distance). Caps are RISK BUDGETS for one contract, fixed now as display levels spanning the sample's stop distribution (the median tell stop is about 26 points, the 90th percentile about 80): **C = 40, 60, 80, 100, 150, 200 points** (about $80, $120, $160, $200, $300, $400 of price risk per contract, plus the $2 commission). They are not tuned and not candidates for a hypothesis.
No re-entry after a capped stop-out is modelled (the tell's own re-fire is not simulated): stated limitation.

## 2. Reported per cap (the curve)
Share of trades whose stop was capped; mean net dollars per trade and the paired difference to V1 with a day-block bootstrap 95 percent CI (4,000 draws, seed 20261104); the TOTAL profit given up over the whole explore sample (sum of V1 net minus sum of capped net) and per bucket of the tell's own stop (under 50, 50 to 100, over 100 points); win rate; average winner and loser; the worst loss and the 1st and 5th percentile of the trade net; the worst day (sum of net by trade date) and the maximum drawdown of the cumulative net over all explore days (not a prop-account simulation: the explore entries are overlapping tell fires on one account's rules and a bust model would need the real sizing and the $2,000 end-of-day trailing loss; a prop-race replay on the shadow books is a separate study); and the cost per dollar of risk removed: profit given up divided by the reduction in the worst loss and in the 1st-percentile loss. By entry hour (10:00-10:59 vs later).

The curve prices risk in the tell's own history; whether the price is worth paying is the user's decision (register: a user risk choice is open).
