# Pre-registration: a no-direction bracket after the tell, sized by the forecast, in two structures (OPTION 3; user idea 2026-11-02: "what if we didnt pick a direction ... limit orders on either side of price, whichever gets filled, cancel the other ... stops and limit order spots adjusted"; designed before any outcome; scripts to be written and run next)

Background: the earlier side-agnostic STOP bracket (breakout) after the tell was NOT SHOWN (`scripts/amt_vbars/PREREG_BRACKET.md`, `run_bracket_output.txt`: TELL absolute -$2.43/-$1.99/-$1.52 per point at 5/15/30 minutes for k = 1 x median bar range, no edge over controls). Two things are new: (1) the LIMIT-FADE mirror, which wins if the first excursion reverses and loses if the expansion continues; (2) the distances come from the forecast. The tell includes a back-and-forth component, which is why a two-sided fade is plausible. Whipsaw is the main risk and is simulated with a worst-case rule, not assumed away. Explore only (<= 2025-05-16); sealed window not read; nothing live; simulated dollars.

## Structures at a V1 tell signal bar (reference price P = the signal bar's close; both orders live for W = 30 minutes; one position at a time; 1 MNQ, $2 per point, $2 round trip)
- S-STOP (breakout): buy stop at P + k, sell stop at P - k; the first to trade fills (1 tick adverse slip, gap fills at the open); the other trigger is the stop (distance 2k); exit at the stop, at W minutes after the fill, or at the 15:55 open.
- S-LIMIT (fade): buy limit at P - k, sell limit at P + k; the first to FILL cancels the other. A limit fills only if price trades THROUGH it by one tick (no queue-position credit). After a fill at P -/+ k: target = back to P (a limit that fills only on a trade-through of one tick), stop = k beyond the fill (loss k plus one tick slip), time exit W minutes after the fill, 15:55 flatten.
- Distance: k = c x (forecast E_30 in points at the signal), c in {0.25, 0.5}, forecast = the walk-forward no-tell model of Amendment 3 times ATR20 in points. Two widths only; no other value tried. The stop-only tests used a bar-range k; forecast-sized k is the new part.
- Bar-resolution rule (500-volume bars carry no intrabar order): a bar that reaches BOTH sides is AMBIGUOUS and scored as the WORST case (S-LIMIT: filled on one side and stopped on the other; S-STOP: entered on one side and stopped on the other). Ambiguous share reported; a cell with more than a quarter of fills ambiguous is flagged as dominated by bar resolution.

## Arms and statistics (counted cells: 2 structures x 2 widths = 4)
TELL = every V1 signal bar of the explore sessions (the research simulation's fires, first 120 dates excluded for the model); CONTROL_MOVING = decision-grid bars with no tell and price moving in at least one direction, same k rule from their own forecast. Per-point mean net dollars (no fill = 0) and per fill; fill rate; day-blocked bootstrap CIs (3000, seed 20261102); TELL minus CONTROL weighted to the tell's entry-time mix (under 90, 90-180, 180+ minutes since 09:30); halves; the same cell split by forecast HIGH vs LOW (causal terciles) as a descriptive.
A cell "shows a bracket edge" only if ALL hold: >= 200 tell points on >= 100 dates; TELL absolute mean per point > 0 with CI lower bound > 0 (relative skill with negative dollars is NOT a rule); TELL minus CONTROL > 0 with CI excluding zero; same sign in both halves; ambiguous share under a quarter. Four cells tested: a pass is a LEAD for a forward-shadow comparison, not a result; the BH-adjusted p across the four applies.

## Reading rules and what is not tested
Both structures fail -> symmetric tell structures do not pay after costs even with forecast sizing; recorded with resolution. S-LIMIT passes and S-STOP fails -> the tell's expansion reverses at the scale of k (the back-and-forth component), which would be reported with the era split first (cv-033). Not tested: Globex, scale-outs, trails, a different reference price, sub-bar order from ticks (the tick archive would remove the ambiguity and is the natural refinement), live OCO behaviour (Sierra Chart OCO groups and simultaneous fills must be checked in its documentation before any live use; do not guess, hr-027).
