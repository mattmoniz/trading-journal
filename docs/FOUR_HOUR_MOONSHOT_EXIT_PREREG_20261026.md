# Pre-registration: the moonshot exit (large stop, no target, day-long hold) on four-hour defended levels — 2026-10-26

Written BEFORE running anything. Origin: user, 2026-10-26: asks for the system to fire automatically on a defended floor/top with "moonshots (large stop, far target / long hold)" (`docs/MOONSHOT_EXIT_PAIRED_TEST_SCOPE_20261026.md`, "step 2"), and "do number 4" after the four-hour-level study found no edge in the EXIT-FREE move: that study measured the raw forward move, which says nothing about whether a large protective stop plus a long hold turns the same entries into a usable trade. This test is deliberately about the exit, with the entry fixed to the four-hour-level events, and it controls for the two things that make any wide-stop/long-hold simulation look good by itself (NQ drift and the stop/target geometry).

## Population
First `K >= 3` event per four-hour level at band `0.25 x H4`, floors (long) and tops (short), as in `docs/FOUR_HOUR_DEFENDED_LEVEL_PREREG_20261026.md` (same code, `scripts/lib/fourHourLevels.mjs`; NQ 1-minute bars 2023-03-06 .. last complete date minus 3; roll weeks, 2025-09-28..11-19 and bars < 60 minutes excluded; the +1,440-minute exit must exist). Entry = the open of the first 1-minute bar after the confirming four-hour bar closes. One contract. Events can overlap in time within a side (not excluded); the overlap share is printed and the CI is date-blocked.

## Exits (all simulated bar by bar on 1-minute bars with the shared realistic-fill walker `walk()` in `scripts/lib/floorEngine.mjs`)
A stop that is already beyond the market fills at that bar's open, never at the stop price; the stop is checked before the target within a bar (conservative). Cost 1.5 points per trade (commission and slippage, MNQ $2/point so about $3), subtracted from every trade including controls. Stop and target distances in D20 units (mean full-session range of the 20 prior trade days, about 360 points).
- **PRIMARY exit M:** stop `0.5 x D20` (about 180 points, about $360 on one contract), NO target, time exit at the close of the bar 1,439 minutes after entry.
- Sensitivity (printed, never a verdict, never counted): stops `0.25` and `1.0 x D20` at hold 1,440; holds 240 and 2,880 at stop 0.5; a far target `1.0 x D20` with stop 0.5 and hold 2,880; a STRUCTURAL stop at the level's own break line (`L - b` for a floor, `L + b` for a top) with no target, hold 1,440 (risk varies per event, so reported in points and dollars only).

## Controls (the placebo for geometry and drift)
For each event, up to 10 clock-matched blind same-direction entries on other dates (within 30 minutes of the same clock; the same sampler as the four-hour study, seed fixed), each run through the IDENTICAL exit (same stop distance in points, hold, cost). EXCESS = event P&L minus the mean control P&L for that event. A mirrored-direction placebo (the same events traded the other way with the same exit) is also printed: if the real direction does not beat the mirror, there is no directional content, only exit geometry. Also paired within event: the exit-free outcome (hold 1,440, no stop) versus M, so the effect of the protective stop itself is visible.

## Counted cells and screens
Counted cells = 2: FLOOR long and TOP short at the primary exit M, on the EXCESS over blind entries. Explore = earlier 60 percent of dates, hold-out = later 40 percent (a SCREEN only: this history has been used all day). CANDIDATE = explore date-blocked 97.5 percent CI (Bonferroni over 2) lower bound above 0 on the excess; SCREEN-SURVIVOR = candidate AND hold-out point above 0 AND hold-out 95 percent CI lower bound above 0 AND explore point above 0. UNDERPOWERED if fewer than 30 events or fewer than 20 distinct dates. Printed for each cell and both raw and excess: n, distinct dates, mean and MEDIAN P&L per trade in points and dollars, share stopped / time-exited, worst trade, share of total P&L from the top 5 trades and top 5 dates (the heavy-tail check; a moonshot lives on a few big trades), the date-blocked CI, the smallest detectable effect (CI half-width), overlap share of events, expected-by-chance (cells x 0.025, a floor).
Absolute net P&L of M is also reported (a moonshot that beats blind entries but loses money on one contract is not usable); a verdict of "usable" needs the excess CI to clear zero AND the absolute net mean per trade to be above zero.

## Next steps (only if a cell survives)
A SHADOW-only conditional variant or detector firing on a four-hour level with exit M; promotion only through the live-roster rule on forward data. Nothing is wired by this study. If nothing survives, the finding is: the four-hour defended level has no demonstrated moonshot edge in this history; it says nothing about picks selected by eye (that is the companion test).

## Limits stated in advance
Few independent levels (about 150 floors and 275 tops, fewer on distinct dates); the exit (0.5 D20, 24 hours) was chosen by reasoning, not tuned; MNQ cost assumption approximate; 1-minute bars cannot resolve intra-minute order of stop and target; stops that gap at the Sunday open fill at that open (counted); the drift control assumes blind entries are exchangeable with events on clock and direction only; heavy tails make means unstable (median and concentration printed).
