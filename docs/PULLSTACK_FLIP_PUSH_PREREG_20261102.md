# Pre-registration: wait for a pull/stack flip, then for price to push the same way (written 2026-11-02 BEFORE the script exists or any outcome is read; user: "can you wait for a pull/stack flip and for price to push in a certain direction?")
Sierra Chart has a pulling/stacking feature (liquidity added vs withdrawn, computed from the order book, columns of the Numbers Bars Calculated Values study; support board thread 73302 says its documentation gives the mathematics). I could not find the formula and do not guess third-party behaviour (hr-027), so the definition below is MINE, built from the raw depth records (the Stage B per-second heatmaps, `scripts/depth_stageb/extract_heatmap.py`, validated 100 percent against an independent summary), not Sierra's calculation; if the user gives their Sierra settings or the documented formula it replaces this definition in a new pre-registration. RTH only (the heatmaps cover RTH); Globex is an untested gap (hr-002). Depth era 2026-06..2026-10, about 52 evaluated sessions; nothing live.
## Definitions (causal, per session, all from seconds <= t)
Per second: STACK = size ADDED and PULL = size cancelled (not traded; the extractor's trade-vs-cancel attribution), summed over the 5 levels nearest the touch on each side (bids: the best bid and 4 ticks below; asks: the best ask and 4 ticks above). Net pressure numerator N(t) = (bid STACK - bid PULL) - (ask STACK - ask PULL) (positive = bid-side stacking or ask-side pulling = bullish). Denominator D(t) = bid STACK + bid PULL + ask STACK + ask PULL. U(t) = sum of N over the last 30 s divided by the sum of D over the same 30 s, in [-1, 1].
FLIP UP at second t: U(t) >= theta and U(t - 30) <= -theta; FLIP DOWN is the mirror. theta = the median of |U| over all RTH seconds of the 20 PRIOR sessions (rolling, strictly earlier). One flip per 60 s (first qualifying second).
PUSH: the mid-price moves in the flip direction by at least P over the 60 seconds after the flip: dir x (mid(t + 60) - mid(t)) >= P, P = the 75th percentile of |mid(s + 60) - mid(s)| over the prior 20 sessions' RTH seconds. The decision second is s = t + 60 (the push is observed before acting). Entry at s at the mid plus half the spread in the trade direction.
Outcome: y_H = dir x (mid(s + H) - mid(s)) in points, H = 60, 300, 900 seconds (complete windows before 15:55), gross mid-to-mid; the economic reading subtracts half the spread at entry and exit and the 1.25-point round trip (tradable only above about 1.4 points).
## Controls and counted tests (family `pullstack_flip_push_20261102`, ledger tests = 3, BH q = 0.10)
CONTROL = PUSH ONLY: decision seconds where the last 60 s moved the mid by at least P in one direction (direction = that move) and NO flip of either sign occurred in the previous 120 s; first qualifying second per 60 s. The excess isolates what the flip adds beyond price already pushing. Counted: for H = 60, 300, 900 s, FLIP+PUSH minus PUSH-ONLY in mean y (session-blocked bootstrap CI, 3000 draws) and the FLIP+PUSH absolute mean. CANDIDATE = BH survivor, the difference CI excludes zero, the absolute mean above the economic bar (1.4 points) with its CI lower bound above it, same sign in both halves of the sessions, at least 100 events in at least 40 sessions, else UNDERPOWERED.
Uncounted, printed: FLIP without a push (does the flip alone predict), PUSH-ONLY absolute mean, flip up vs down, event counts and sessions, flips per session.
Resolution, stated now: thousands of events in about 50 sessions resolve a 5-minute mean of about +/-1 point and a 15-minute mean of about +/-2 points; an effect above the 1.4-point bar is visible. Reading: no candidate: a pull/stack flip plus a price push does not predict the next move beyond the push itself at this resolution, which closes this depth idea for RTH on this sample; a candidate: a lead for a forward shadow log, never a live rule.
