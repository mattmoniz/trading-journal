# Pre-registration: Bookmap-style Stage B on the RAW order book joined to the trades (written 2026-11-02 BEFORE the extractor exists or any outcome is read; user: resume the direction work; DeepSeek round F design adapted)

## 0. Why Stage B
Stage A (bar-level proxies) and the per-second imbalance screen could not see the things Bookmap users read: whether a large resting order is REAL (stays and trades) or FAKE (pulled), absorption with refills, hidden size (iceberg), pulls and stacks, sweeps that fail. Those need (a) the full depth records and (b) the trade prints, with every size DROP classified as executed (TRADE) or withdrawn (CANCEL). Prior (stated so a null is not whiplash): low, about 5 percent that anything is tradable for a 1-MNQ taker at 5-60 minutes (DeepSeek round F: order-book signals live at seconds to a few minutes; the literature supports a maker edge, not a taker edge). The honest purpose is to measure what the book carries that the summaries could not, on the one sample that exists.

## 1. Data and the heatmap object (RTH first; the Globex analogue is a declared, not-yet-run second pass, hr-002)
- Raw depth: Sierra `.depth` records (24 bytes: microsecond datetime, command, flags, NumOrders, price, quantity) read with `scripts/depth_absorption/depth_reader.py` semantics (CLEAR_BOOK starts a buffered rebuild committed atomically at FLAG_END_OF_BATCH; a rebuild is a RESYNC, never a flow event; any quantity change that coincides with a resync is excluded). Trades: Sierra `.scid` tick records of the same contract (aggressor from the bid/ask volume fields; sub-trades summed as in `scid_reader`), the same microsecond clock (validated by `scripts/depth_absorption/reconstruct_and_validate.py`).
- Sessions: the 2026-06-15 .. 2026-10-30 NQ sessions that have a depth file and the matching contract's tick file (about 100). RTH 09:30-16:00 ET per second.
- The object per session and second t: for each tick level k = -20 .. +20 around the anchor a(t) = the best bid at t (absolute tick index fixed for the second): size on the bid side and ask side at the END of the second, ADDED size, PULLED size (a decrease not explained by trades) and TRADED size (executed volume at that price during the second), plus best bid, best ask, total aggressive buy and sell volume. Attribution rule (fixed now): when the quantity at price p drops by d at time t, the part of d up to the executed volume at p within +/-50 ms of t is a TRADE, the remainder is a CANCEL; a trade with no drop is a refill/iceberg signature. Extraction code: `scripts/depth_stageb/extract_heatmap.py` (numba), validated before use by (i) reproducing the existing per-second summary columns (best bid/ask, size at best, 10- and 40-tick depth) on at least 5 sessions to within rounding, and (ii) the share of aggressive buys at or above the best ask and sells at or below the best bid (a clock-alignment check). A session failing a check is dropped and counted.

## 2. Detectors (thresholds are rolling quantiles of the 20 PRIOR sessions pooled, RTH only; every detector fires per side; at most one event per detector per side per 60 s)
- B1 REAL vs FAKE WALL: a "large order" appears when the size at a level within 10 ticks of the touch is >= q95 of such level sizes AND was < 50% of that size one second earlier. Observe 60 s: FAKE = size falls below 20% of its initial value with traded volume at that price <= 20% of the initial size (pulled, not filled); REAL = size stays >= 50% for the whole 60 s OR traded volume at that price >= 50% of the initial size. Decision time = the end of the 60 s window. Predictions: a REAL bid (ask) wall -> price UP (DOWN) afterwards; a FAKE bid (ask) wall -> price DOWN (UP) afterwards.
- B2 ABSORPTION: over the last 30 s, executed volume at a level at or one tick from the touch >= q90 of the same statistic AND the touch never moved through that level AND the level's displayed size is still >= 50% of its size 30 s ago (refilled). Absorbed bid -> price UP; absorbed ask -> price DOWN.
- B3 ICEBERG PROXY: over the last 30 s, executed volume at a price divided by the maximum displayed size at that price >= q90 of such ratios (and the touch never moved through). Same prediction as B2.
- B4 PULL / STACK: within a 10 s window, CANCELLED (not traded) size within 5 ticks of the touch on one side >= q95 AND cancelled share of the size removed >= 80 percent. A pulled bid -> DOWN; a pulled ask -> UP.
- B5 SWEEP THEN REJECT: within 10 s the aggressive volume in one direction >= q95 AND the touch moves >= 2 ticks in that direction AND the swept side's depth over levels 1-5 falls >= 60 percent; then within the next 30 s price retraces >= 50 percent of the sweep AND that depth recovers >= 80 percent. Decision at the end of the rejection window. Prediction: REVERSAL.
## 3. Outcome, control, statistics
Outcome: the mid-price move in points from the decision second to H = 1, 5 and 15 minutes later (30 min reported, uncounted), signed by the detector's predicted direction; costs: 1.25 points per trade plus half the spread (mid-to-mid is optimistic; the printed gross is compared with 1.25 + half spread). Control: for each detector the same-side seconds that satisfy the detector's FIRST condition but not the rest (B1: all new large orders, regardless of persistence; B2/B3: all high-execution seconds without refill / with the touch moving through; B4: all seconds with any pulled size; B5: all sweeps that are not rejected), so the excess isolates the distinctive part. Statistic: excess = mean signed move of events minus mean signed move of its control; session-blocked bootstrap CI (3000), circular-shift placebo (1000), same sign in both halves of the sessions; at least 100 events in at least 40 sessions else UNDERPOWERED. 5 detectors x 3 horizons = 15 counted tests, BH q = 0.10 (family `bookmap_stage_b_depth_20261102`, ledger tests = 15). A CANDIDATE needs |excess| above 1.25 points + half spread to be called tradable; smaller is "visible, not tradable".
Resolution, stated now: about 100 sessions; if events number a few hundred to a few thousand per detector the mean move resolves about +/-1 to 3 points at 5-15 minutes; a 1-minute effect of 1 point is resolvable only for the most frequent detectors.
