BURST bar j (j = i+1..i+3, the FIRST qualifying bar): |delta share| = |ask - bid| / volume of the bar >= the 90th percentile of that statistic over all bars of the 20 PRIOR sessions (rolling, strictly earlier); direction b = sign of the bar delta (+1 buyers, -1 sellers).
