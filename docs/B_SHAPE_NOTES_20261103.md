## What B is (the user's words and the reference)
- A profile with TWO distributions (two humps) inside one period; "in between the two double humps is probably a strong market level" (user). NinjaTrader's article: two D-shaped profiles in one period, generally trend continuation, one POC dominates (the more dominant hump says whether activity was at the top or bottom). The user: "B and D are hard, this isn't a distinct shape"; B vs D is a continuum; Oct 30 and Nov 2 (a dominant early hump plus two lower humps) were "hard" and are read as P (tails and POC) with several humps.
## What we measured (all descriptive, explore 2023-03-07..2025-05-16, features and raw 500-bar prices only)
- Bimodality score = (1 - trough / smaller peak) x (smaller / larger peak) of the two largest peaks at least 5 of 40 bins apart on the RTH profile; the top third of sessions (cut 0.365, `scripts/tell_context/shape_cuts.json`) is the MULTI_HUMP flag, 35 percent of sessions, too wide to be a distinct shape. Of those, by the tails/POC letter: D 130, P 26, b 20 (of 176): B is mostly a SYMMETRIC value area with two humps.
- Strong B (top decile, N=52) vs strong D (N=90): range 1.52 vs 1.04 percent of the open, |net| 1.03 vs 0.34, trough crossings per hour 0.38 vs 3.92, humps about 4.1 hours apart (1.6 on D); the net move continued toward the second hump on 92 percent of strong B days (63 percent on strong D). The trough is crossed about once, not tested again and again: a thin zone, not a level that gets revisited. Hump COUNT (2 vs 3+) adds no distinction.
- After a drive that built (fight, then push), multi-hump is more common by midday: 49-58 percent vs 34 overall (claim `watcher_fight_then_push_sharpens_p_call_20261103`, `watcher_building_drive_states_ib_or_overnight_20261103`).

## Added later the same night: the user's description of B and a first null (ledger look 115, `scripts/tell_context/research_20261103/b_sequence.py`)
User: "I just know it starts to look like D and then it moves quickly and starts filling in a second area (continuation)". So B is TIME-ORDERED: a D-like body first, then a quick move (a thin zone), then a second body; P and b are the reverse order (the fast move first, then the body); D is the body only.
First attempt to find it in the watcher's state history (balance -> drive -> balance again, before 12:00 / 13:00 / 14:00, 522 explore sessions): the sequence is on about half of days (249 of 522 by 12:00) and those days ended multi-hump 36 percent vs 32 percent without it (strong B, top decile: 10 vs 10 percent); at 14:00 37 vs 27 percent. Almost no lift. Reasons: every session STARTS as a balance state (so "starts like a D" cannot discriminate), the watcher changes state about 7 times a day (many drives come back), and the sequence ignores GEOMETRY. What separates the strong B days (section above: humps about 4.1 hours apart, the trough crossed about once) is the distance and the thin zone between the two areas and the time spent in the second one. A real B-forming flag therefore needs: (1) the first area (a value area or hump that formed first), (2) a second area at a data-derived distance beyond it with a thin zone between, (3) enough bars spent in the second area; to be tested against the final flag before use. Claim `b_sequence_in_watcher_states_no_lift_20261103` recorded. Uncounted, non-citable.

## Formation study result (2026-11-03 night, ledger looks 117 and 119, family `b_d_formation_20261103`, claim `b_d_formation_late_afternoon_visible_20261103`; scripts `research_20261103/b_d_formation.py` + `b_d_formation_report.py`, rows in `scratch/tell_context_20261103/b_d_formation_rows.json`)
Descriptive, hindsight, 522 explore sessions, groups from the FINAL profile (strong B N=53, strong D N=91). Draft prereg `docs/B_D_FORMATION_STUDY_PREREG_20261103.md`.
- B is WIDER (final 1.52 vs 1.05 percent of the open) but not until the afternoon: extent, value-area share and bimodality are the same as D until about 13:00-13:30 (extent at 11:00: 0.74 vs 0.75; at 15:00: 1.47 vs 0.93). The value area of a B spans both humps (70 vs 43 percent of the extent).
- The second area is late: it has 25 percent of its final volume only about 240 minutes after the open (D: 60); first crossing of the thin zone about 177 minutes in (D: 14); a B crosses the thin zone about twice in the day (D about 25 times); the fastest swing between the outer thirds takes 53 minutes (D: 8). So "moves quickly" is not found in minutes: a B is a few slow travels, a D chops inside its body.
- Order: lower area first then upper 66 percent (independent check, session low before session high: 35 of 53), upper first 34 percent; the first-built area is the larger one. The window drifted up, so the bottom-first lean may be drift (look at letter b days before calling it a B property).
- The developing multi-hump flag already shows on 38 percent of eventual flag days at 11:00, 50 percent at 13:00, 70 percent at 15:30 (strong B 43 vs strong D 12 percent at 13:00): the flag becomes readable in the afternoon, not before. Hump count (>= half the biggest) is non-informative.
- NO RULE ADOPTED. Candidate in words only: a B is declared once, after about 12:30, price has travelled from the first area to a second area in the opposite outer third, spent a stretch there, and the developing bimodality is at or above the multi-hump cut; that is nearly the existing flag, only later and with the traversal. It needs the user's eye on example days (B: 2025-03-05, 2024-02-12, 2024-07-02, 2024-06-05, 2023-04-13, 2024-06-17; D: 2024-04-10, 2025-01-29, 2023-08-17, 2025-04-02; between: 2024-02-08, 2023-10-02, 2025-01-15, 2025-04-10) before anything else.
