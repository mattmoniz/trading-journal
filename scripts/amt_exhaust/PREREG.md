# Exhaustion reversal (the trader's own rule), RTH, explore only (frozen 2026-10-28)

Committed BEFORE any outcome is read. The trader's description (verbatim intent): price pushes in one direction (mostly morning or afternoon); the leading side steps away; the other side takes over; get in the OPPOSITE trade at that exhaustion point; "even more so" when it happens at significant market-generated-information (MGI) levels. Design reviewed by Gemini (Flash High) and DeepSeek before freezing (`scratch/antigravity_response_exhaustion_design_20261028.md`, `scratch/deepseek_response_20261028_103640.md`); where they differed this file says which was taken. Explore only: the same 509 explore sessions that have a 20-session trailing history (to 2025-05-16); every hold-out stays sealed; RTH only, the Globex arm is NOT tested here (flagged as an open decision as the repo requires). 1 MNQ contract, $2/point, $2 round trip, 1 tick slippage on market and stop fills, limit targets fill when the bar trades 1 tick through, stop gaps fill at the open, stop before target on the same bar, flatten 15:55.

## Trader clarification adopted before freezing (2026-10-28, user)
"Exhaustion happens and price has to move in the opposite direction for us to jump in with buyers on our side (or vice versa) ... it's not just looking for sellers, the main controller, to step away, it's looking for the countertrade, hopefully at a market generated level, but not always ... continuously step in and move price up. Price needs to respond to the countertrade in order to legitimize establishing a position." So the trade rule is exhaustion PLUS a sustained counter-side response with price actually responding. Levels are a bonus, not a requirement.

## Definitions (all lookahead-free; K_eff(t) = min(30, t) one-minute bars, signals from t >= 15)
- Trailing distributions: every quantity compared to a threshold takes it from the prior 20 sessions' values of the same quantity at the same clock bar +/- 15 bars (no hand-picked numbers other than the quantile levels).
- **PUSH at bar t:** direction D = sign(close[t] - close[t-K_eff]); |net move| >= the trailing 90th percentile of |net move|; efficiency ratio ER = |net move| / sum of |bar-to-bar close changes| over the window >= the trailing 75th percentile (one-sidedness); the sum of bar delta (ask minus bid volume) over the window has the sign D.
- **NEW EXTREME:** high[t] > max(high[t-K_eff .. t-1]) for an up push (low[t] < min(...) for a down push).
- **EXHAUSTION ("the leading side steps away"):** leading-side aggressive volume a_i = ask volume for an up push (bid volume for a down push), ask = (volume + delta)/2, bid = (volume - delta)/2. Tail = mean of a over bars t-2..t; Reference = the 3-bar rolling means of a at j = t-K_eff+2 .. t-3 (the earlier part of the same push, no overlap with the tail; at least 10 values). Exhaustion when Tail <= the 25th percentile of the Reference.
- **COUNTER-SIDE RESPONSE ("the other side steps in and price responds"):** within 5 bars after t, the first bar u (u - t >= 2) such that (a) at least 2 bars in t+1..u have bar delta of the counter sign (it keeps stepping in, not one bar), (b) counter-side aggressive volume summed over t+1..u exceeds leading-side aggressive volume over the same bars (the counter side has taken control), and (c) close[u] is beyond the extreme bar's opposite end (up push: close[u] < low[t]; down push: close[u] > high[t]) (price responded).
- **E2 = THE TRADER'S RULE (the one counted cell):** PUSH and NEW EXTREME and EXHAUSTION at t, then a COUNTER-SIDE RESPONSE at u. Enter the OPPOSITE direction at the open of u+1 (+1 tick adverse), entry bar open between 09:35 and 15:15; a 30-bar refractory period after each base signal (one event per push).
- **E1 (diagnostic, not counted):** PUSH and NEW EXTREME and EXHAUSTION, entered at the open of t+1 with no response required (what a fade of the exhaustion bar alone does).
- **Controls (same-selection-minus-signal, both enter the opposite direction like E2):** **C2 = response without exhaustion** (PUSH and NEW EXTREME, leading side NOT faded: Tail > the median of the Reference, followed by the same COUNTER-SIDE RESPONSE): isolates whether the exhaustion fade adds anything beyond a reversal response. **NR = exhaustion without a response** (E1 signals that never get a response in 5 bars), entered at the open of t + L + 1 with L drawn from the confirmed lags of E2 (outcome-blind): isolates whether the response is what carries the edge, at matched elapsed time.

## Outcome and statistic (ONE counted cell: E2; one-sided alpha 0.025)
Entry price = open of the entry bar + 1 tick adverse. Uncensored forward return F_h = trade direction x (close of the h-th bar counting the entry bar as bar 1 - entry price), in points; PRIMARY h = 15 bars; also h = 5, 30, 60 and mean MFE / MAE over 30 bars (descriptive). Statistic = gross mean F_15 and net of the 1.5-point all-in cost, day-blocked bootstrap (events grouped by trading day, 3000 iterations, seed 20261028). Also reported (descriptive, not counted): by segment (MORNING entries 09:35-11:29, MIDDAY 11:30-13:29, AFTERNOON 13:30-15:15) and long vs short reversals.

## MGI interaction ("even more so at levels"; descriptive, one test)
Levels known before the event: pdVAH, pdVAL, pdPOC, prior-day high and low, ONH, ONL, and IBH/IBL ONLY for events at or after 10:30 (IB lookahead removed); VWAP is dynamic and is reported separately, not in the MGI set. d_real = distance in ATR20 units from the push extreme to the nearest available level. d_sham = the same with every level shifted up by 0.5 x ATR20 (a level-free placebo with the same geometry). AT-MGI = d <= the 25th percentile of d over the explore E1 events (outcome-blind cut). Interaction = [mean F_15(near real) - mean F_15(not near real)] - [mean F_15(near sham) - mean F_15(not near sham)] on the SAME events, day-blocked bootstrap 95% CI. A near-real advantage that the sham reproduces is clustering at push extremes, not an MGI effect.

## Secondary stage: trade geometry (descriptive, not counted)
Stop = the exhaustion extreme beyond by 1 tick + 0.5 x ATR14 of 1-minute true range at t (the reviewers disagreed on the scale; DeepSeek's "1 x ATR20" is the DAILY range, about 293 points, and was rejected; Gemini's 0.1 x ATR14 is about 1 point, which is inside normal noise, so a middle value was fixed before any run). Target = the 50% retracement of the push (from close[t-K_eff] to the extreme). Time stop = bar 30 after entry (exit at the next open), flatten 15:55. Reported for E2, C2 and NR, with a random-entry placebo (random bar within +/- 30 bars of the entry, random direction, same stop distance, target distance and time stop).

## Controls and reading (frozen)
E2 is "a lead" only if (1) its gross mean F_15 has a day-blocked CI lower bound > 0, (2) it beats the random-entry placebo p95 (100 trials: a random bar within +/- 30 bars of the entry, random direction), (3) E2 minus C2 and E2 minus NR each have a day-blocked CI lower bound > 0. Net profitability is judged by net F_15 and by the geometry stage (with its own random-entry placebo). A miss is "not shown", never "no effect". Resolution is stated from the CI. Sensitivities (K = 15 and 60; exhaustion quantile 10th and 40th; response window 3 and 8) are descriptive and never selected. E1 is reported for information only. No ACSIL until a lead survives; a hold-out look needs the user's approval.
