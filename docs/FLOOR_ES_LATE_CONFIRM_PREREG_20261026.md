# Pre-registration: NQ turns first, ES joins later — is the entry at ES's confirmation worthwhile? — 2026-10-26

Written BEFORE running anything. Origin: user, 2026-10-26: "I'm not just looking for ES leading but ES confirming... NQ could be leading but just needs a friend." The parent entry study's E2 reads ES only AT the NQ fire bar (AGREE = ES had already turned by then), so an NQ-leads / ES-joins-a-few-bars-later case is classified DISAGREE there and the user's idea was not tested. Parents: `docs/FLOOR_ENTRY_ES_AGREEMENT_PREREG_20261026.md` (+ Amendment 1), `docs/FLOOR_CONTEXT_NARROWING_PREREG_20261026.md` (+ Amendment 1). Reuses the parent event rows (`scratch/floor_entry_events_20261026_m0.25.json`: fired events, long floors and mirror ceilings separately, pinned end date 2026-10-25) and the same bars, same turn definition (0.25 x A15 for NQ, 0.25 x A15_ES for ES), same 50-minute window W.

## The mechanism being tested ("arm, queue, fire on the friend")
Arm at the floor test; NQ turns (the parent fire bar j); the queue waits for ES to confirm; FIRE at ES's confirmation. ES confirms at the first minute j' > j, no later than W minutes after the episode start, at which ES's close is at least 0.25 x A15_ES above ES's own lowest low since the episode start. That is **LATE-CONFIRMED**. Events where ES had already turned at j are **SAME-OR-EARLIER** (the parent's AGREE); events where ES never confirms inside W are **NEVER**. Entry for LATE = open of the bar after j'.

## Tests
- **L1 (primary): is the late-confirmed entry worthwhile?** For LATE events, ret60 from the ES-confirmation entry minus ret60 of a delay-matched blind entry at the same floor test (same family/arm/year delay pool taken from the LATE events' own ES-confirmation delays, never the focal event's own delay, episode still active at that delay, clock within 30 minutes, up to 10 draws). A15 units. Sequence-blocked and day-blocked bootstrap, 98.75 percent intervals (two primaries in the family: L1 and L2 below, Bonferroni), chronological halves both positive.
- **L2 (does waiting for ES beat firing on NQ alone): ** among events where ES confirms LATE, ret60 from the ES-confirmation entry minus ret60 from the NQ fire-bar entry of the SAME event (paired, same trade, entering later but with ES). Control for the structural cost/benefit of a later entry: the same difference for matched blind later entries (the delay-matched control's ret60 minus the NQ-fire ret60 of the same event). Reported as the difference-in-differences.
- **Descriptive only (not tradable, no verdict):** from the NQ fire bar, ret60 of LATE vs NEVER vs SAME-OR-EARLIER; share of fires in each class and the distribution of the ES join delay (so a reader sees how long the queue waits); RTH/Globex split; the VAH mirror.

## Limits and honest priors
LATE needs ES to move after NQ already did, so the entry is later and higher; the delay-matched control is the guard against crediting ES for an entry-price effect. The parent E1 (NQ turn vs delay-matched blind entry) was NOT worthwhile at long floors (-0.022 A15, tight), so a LATE entry only passes if ES's confirmation adds beyond that. Expected resolution is looser than the parent (LATE is a subset). A cell needs >= 20 distinct days and, for the control draw, >= 30 matched events, else UNDERPOWERED. Nothing here changes live behaviour; a SHOWN only licenses a forward test on the nightly ES refresh with the frozen rule (turn fractions, W) written into the claim.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-26, before the run) — DeepSeek design review (`reports/deepseek_floor_es_late_confirm_prereg_review_2026-10-26.md`)
1. **L2 was algebraically L1** (the NQ-fire baseline cancels in the difference-in-differences). There is now ONE primary, L1. The raw paired "ES-confirm entry minus NQ-fire entry" of the same event is descriptive only.
2. **L1's control is now NQ-conditioned, so ES is the only difference.** For a LATE event A (NQ fired at bar j, ES confirmed `dl` minutes later), the control entries are made at the same elapsed time `dl` after the NQ fire, on OTHER NQ-fired events of the same family, arm, year and chronological half whose ES had NOT yet confirmed by then (causal: they may still confirm later), whose episode is still active at that time (no escape above the zone, no decisive break, no POC reached) and whose entry clock is within 30 minutes of A's. Up to 10 draws, mean taken; A's own sequence excluded; events with no eligible control are dropped and counted. **L1 = A's ret60 from the ES-confirmation entry minus the mean control ret60 from the matched elapsed-time entry**, A15 units. The earlier blind delay-matched control is also printed as a secondary line.
3. **Controls and delay pool are half-local** (explore events use only explore delays and explore controls, hold-out only hold-out), removing the full-sample leakage.
4. **Wait time is reported as a censored distribution** (LATE delays, plus the share SAME-OR-EARLIER and NEVER within W), with the caption that the class comparisons are survivorship-confounded and not a result.
5. **One primary at 95 percent coverage** (sequence-blocked; day-blocked alongside), both chronological halves positive, >= 20 distinct days, else UNDERPOWERED with the smallest detectable effect printed; a NOT SHOWN is worded "unresolved below the resolution bar", never "ES adds nothing". A long-floor SHOWN that the VAH mirror contradicts is flagged and not treated as forward-worthy.
