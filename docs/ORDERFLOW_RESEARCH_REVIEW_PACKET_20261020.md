## The system and the test bed
- Instrument MNQ/NQ, 1 contract, $2/point. A live level-fade roster (about 250 setup types: fade a touch of PD/PW/OR/IB/VWAP/profile levels, ACTIVE or SHADOW) with stops/targets from a calibrated table (OPTIMAL_STOP). Sierra Chart is the charting/data source.
- The test bed for roster questions is a 3.5-year RTH replay (2023-04..2026-10, 21,480 level-fade trades with touch times, realistic fills: a stop beyond the market fills at the bar open, targets fill at the open when gapped). Splits: explore 2023-03-06..2025-05-31, hold-out 2025-06-01..2026-10. Day-blocked bootstrap CIs, placebo (shuffled labels within month x clock bucket), per-family splits, per-trade means as sum/N, pre-registration committed before outcomes, and a standing rule to record every claim.
- "POC rotation-direction gate" (the "POC gate"): a trade is AGREE if its direction equals the direction of the in-progress 65-point swing leg (rotationDetector, anchored at 18:00, leg confirmed by the fire bar), else DISAGREE. It is live as a force-SHADOW gate on DISAGREE candidates. On the replay it is NOT shown to help (fixed 65pt: +1/trade, CI crosses zero, sign flips across eras); it looks best on middle-range days (+8.9/trade) and worst on expansion-like days (-6.0).
- Tick data: Sierra `.scid` files, 836 RTH sessions through 2026-10-19 (gaps: Jun-Aug 2025 sparse files, some 2024 legacy layout reconciled to the database; archive validated against the database's per-session RTH volume). Big "orders" are reconstructed: consecutive same-side prints at most 1 ms apart merged (matches the user's Delta Dynamics "Big Trades" study: aggregate gap 1 ms, minimum 70 contracts; checked against the user's chart numbers on 2026-10-20: 97, 74, 89, 102, 78, 98, 96, 86, 80, 297, 140, 105, 111 all reproduce). Depth-of-market files (`.depth`) exist on disk for some days but are not used (no reader). No live tick feed is wired into the app; a live read-only watcher was raised and deliberately deferred by the user.

## What the user believes (verbatim ideas, in their words)
- "When the market is driving down, large buy orders are getting stuffed and then large sellers come in. So once you see that... our buying needs to step aside. Usually at the end, only after the market has come down a bit and markets had started to turn up, you see larger buyers come in."
- "Whenever there is a large move down there is almost always a counterpunch that day or within a few days. The timing of the counter punch and the size can vary. So using .03 or xyz is myopic."
- "Bubbles are just visual indicators that you can track behind the scenes; consider pace and aggressiveness." "If you just look at big orders and there is a flood of small orders... it's going to get run over." "Sellers are getting run over" (chart examples: grey sell bubbles of 98 and 96 during the 10-20 morning rally; a 297 sell at 13:01 that flushed 23 points then recovered; a 111 sell at 14:42).
- Wants: (a) avoid bad counter-trades, (b) stay in winners beyond the target when volume backs it, (c) a gate for the POC gate itself (on 2026-10-20 the AGREE roster peaked about +864 at 10:22 and gave back to about -1,100 by 13:20 while price was flat: +175 pts in the run-up, -38 pts in the give-back; DISAGREE gained +1,157 in the same stretch), and a way to monitor it.

## Known limits and traps already found
- Look-ahead at the touch bar close; overlap vs base rate (a "97 percent overlap" that was only 1.2x lift); fixed cut-offs ("using .03 is myopic" — now rank-based/scale-free where possible); month-stratified rather than day-blocked nulls; argmax-window selection; size-confounded controls; the DTC install's `.scid` stores prices at 1/100 (fixed; sizes/sides/times unaffected).
- Population is a roster of level-fades with fixed calibrated stops/targets, 1 contract, RTH only. Order-flow events were tested unconditionally (any time) and as flags on those trades, NOT as inputs to a discretionary-style composite (context at a level + flow + structure + time).
- Tests use RTH ticks from 2023-03..2026-10, NQ only (no ES/other markets, no depth of market, no options/news/session-auction context). Globex not covered by the big-order tests.
- Many tests, several families: this is the 3rd big-order test of one day; multiplicity was handled by pre-registered primaries and by stating sensitivities never rescue a failed primary.
- Pre-registration was written by the same assistant that ran the tests (no independent author); only some scripts have had an outside code review (not the three big-order scripts).
