# Revised Sweep & Absorb (Setup A, user's own trading rules) (frozen 2026-10-28)

Committed BEFORE any outcome is read. User spec (numbers verbatim); "Resolutions" lists only what the spec leaves open, chosen before any result. EXPLORE only: the same 529 eligible sessions to 2025-05-16 used by the other AMT studies; the 340 sessions after it and every earlier-sealed hold-out stay SEALED and unloaded. The explore sessions have been reused across many studies, so a lead is a hypothesis for one frozen hold-out look. No ACSIL until an edge over the placebo is verified. No parameter changes after the first run.

## The user's spec
- Confluence Level: pdVAH or pdVAL within 20 points of an IB or ON extreme.
- Trigger (1-minute): LONG: price sweeps below the lower Confluence Level; Bar[t] makes a lower low than Bar[t-1]; Volume[t] >= 75 percent of the 20-period SMA of volume; bar delta (ask minus bid) on Bar[t] strictly > +50. SHORT: sweeps above the upper level; Bar[t] higher high than Bar[t-1]; same volume floor; delta strictly < -50.
- Execution: evaluate at the Bar[t] close; enter at market on the first tick of Bar[t+1].
- Stop: ATR(14) on 1-minute at Bar[t]; long stop = Low[t] - 2.0 x ATR(14), short stop = High[t] + 2.0 x ATR(14). If the stop is more than 25.0 points from the entry price, abort the trade.
- Riding winners: sell 50 percent at +10.0 points; move the stop on the remainder to entry + 2 ticks; Target 2 (runner) = the current Session VWAP; paradox guard: if the Session VWAP is behind the entry or less than 15.0 points from the entry, Target 2 is a fixed limit +25.0 points from the entry.

## Resolutions of what the spec leaves open (chosen before any result)
1. **Size and money:** 2 MNQ contracts (so a 50 percent scale-out exists), $2 per point per contract, $2 round-trip commission per contract (repo ground truth; the 80 percent rule study used 2 MNQ too). Market entry and market exits (EOD, gap fills) pay 1 tick adverse slippage; a stop pays its price minus 1 tick; a stop that takes effect when the market is already beyond it fills at that bar's open. Limit targets fill only when the bar trades 1 tick THROUGH the level, at the level, no slippage. Stop is checked before targets on the same bar, including the entry bar. Flatten at the 15:55 open. One position at a time. Results are reported per trade (both contracts, dollars) and in points per contract.
2. **Levels and zones:** pdVAH/pdVAL = prior full RTH 70 percent value area (asserted equal to the loader's); ON extremes = high/low of the 1-minute bars 18:00 the previous calendar day to 09:29 (needs >= 500 bars, else the ON levels are missing that day); IB extremes = high/low of the 09:30-10:29 bars (usable only from the entry bar 10:30). A Confluence ZONE is the interval between a value-area edge and an IB or ON extreme within 20 points (identical intervals merged), exactly as in the earlier order-flow Setup A (`scripts/amt_flow/flow.py build_zones`). "The lower Confluence Level" = the zone low (long), "the upper level" = the zone high (short).
3. **Sweep:** LONG needs Low[t] < zone low (strictly below), SHORT needs High[t] > zone high. When several zones qualify the zone whose swept edge is nearest the price (highest zone low for a long, lowest zone high for a short) is used. The spec has no re-arm rule and none is added: repeated qualifying bars during one run below a zone can each produce a signal when flat; trades per session and the distance of the signal bar beyond the zone are reported.
4. **Volume SMA(20):** the mean volume of the 20 bars before Bar[t] in the same session (Bar[t] excluded); the entry-bar window below makes the first bar always have 20 prior bars.
5. **Entry window:** the entry bar open is between 10:30 and 15:15 (same as the earlier Setup A), which also means IB levels are complete.
6. **ATR(14):** mean true range of the 14 one-minute bars ending at Bar[t] (the engine's `atr14`, bar t included, no later bar). The 25-point risk cap compares the stop distance from the actual entry price (open of t+1 plus 1 tick adverse).
7. **Breakeven stop timing:** the stop moves to entry + 2 ticks (+0.5 points long, -0.5 short) effective from the bar AFTER the one that filled Target 1 (a stop armed on a bar's own information acts from the next bar; if that bar already opens beyond it, the gap rule fills at the open). The original stop stays in force on the Target 1 bar.
8. **Session VWAP:** cumulative sum of ((H+L+C)/3 x volume) over cumulative volume from the 09:30 bar. The paradox guard is evaluated ONCE at the entry (VWAP through the Bar[t] close against the entry price): VWAP mode if VWAP is at least 15.0 points beyond the entry in the trade direction, else fixed +25. In VWAP mode the runner limit sits at the session VWAP through the PREVIOUS bar's close (the order is refreshed each bar), and fills when the bar trades 1 tick through that level at the level; in fixed mode it is entry +/- 25.
9. **Both contracts together:** the runner may also fill on the same bar as Target 1 when the bar trades through both levels.

## What is reported (explore)
Trades, win rate (trade net > 0), profit factor (after friction), mean per trade in dollars (both contracts) and in points per contract, max drawdown, average winner / loser, day-blocked 95 percent CI of mean dollars (3000 iterations, seed 20261028), trades per session, exit mix (stop both legs, Target 1 then breakeven stop, Target 1 then runner by VWAP or fixed, EOD), share of runners in VWAP mode, share of signals aborted by the 25-point risk cap, median distance of the signal bar beyond the zone. Entry quality without any exits (descriptive, not the reading): mean 15-bar forward move in the trade direction from the entry price, signal vs control vs random.

## Controls (frozen)
- **Random-entry placebo (same management):** for each real trade a random entry bar in the same session inside the same entry window and a random direction, entered at that bar's open with the same 1-tick slippage, the SAME stop distance as that real trade, the same scale-out, breakeven rule and runner rule (including the paradox guard evaluated at that bar), flatten 15:55; 100 trials. Reported: real mean vs placebo p5/p50/p95 and the share of trials at or above the real mean.
- **Same-selection control (project rule):** same zone sweep, same lower low / higher high bar, same volume floor, same entry and management, but the delta absorption condition is replaced by the opposite flow (long: delta <= 0, short: delta >= 0); a bar that qualifies as both a long and a short candidate is excluded. If the signal does not beat its own control, the absorption condition adds nothing.

## Reading (frozen)
A lead only if the explore mean per trade after friction has a day-blocked CI lower bound > 0 AND beats the placebo p95 AND beats the same-selection control's mean. Otherwise "not shown" (never "no effect"). Under 150 trades = underpowered. A lead earns a single sealed hold-out look only with the user's approval.
