# Pre-registration (descriptive, market behaviour on raw bars): after price reaches one level of a pair, does it rotate to the COUNTER level? (written 2026-11-04 BEFORE any outcome was computed)

STATUS: FROZEN. User (2026-11-04, after the value-area traverse checks): "Value area moves too so you can check overnight high to overnight low and vice versa. But it goes between counter levels of market levels." Raw 500-contract bar history first (hr-057), explore window only (522 sessions), features/prices only; ledger family `counter_level_rotation_20261104`, counted tests 4; the hold-out bars stay unspent. Nothing is live.
## 1. Level pairs (fixed before the touch, no lookahead)
ON = overnight high / low (bars completed by 09:30); PD = prior session's RTH high / low (the previous kept session); IB = initial balance high / low (RTH bars completed by 10:30; usable from 10:30). Upper level U, lower level L, D = U - L.
## 2. Events and outcome (stop ignored: a ceiling of what the market does)
From 10:00 ET (10:30 for IB) to 30 minutes before the close, the FIRST bar per pair and side whose range touches the level (high >= U: a DOWN event, target L; low <= L: an UP event, target U). Decision at that bar's close c: entry = c, target distance D' = distance from c to the counter level (must be > 0). Outcome from the next bar to the RTH close: REACH = the counter level is touched; minutes to reach; the ADVERSE excursion before reaching (how far price goes against the entry first); also whether price instead travelled D' the other way (CONTINUES).
## 3. Control (the thing the test is really about, cv-094 / hr-057)
Distance and clock explain most 'reaches': so for each event, 20 control entries are drawn from OTHER sessions at the same clock time (within +-30 minutes, same eligibility), entry = that bar's close, SAME direction and SAME distance D' to a target that is not a market level; same outcome fields. Per event the paired difference = reach(event) - mean reach(controls); day-block (session) bootstrap 95 percent CI of the mean paired difference, 2000 draws.
## 4. Counted tests (4) and printed context
T1-T3: the paired reach difference for ON, PD and IB (both directions pooled). T4: ON events on strong-D days (final bimodality <= the explore bottom quartile with letter D) vs the other days: reach rates and the paired difference. Printed, uncounted: DOWN and UP separately; reach, minutes and adverse excursion for events vs controls; by the watcher state at the touch (BALANCE_* vs DRIVE_*) and by the final letter. N below 20 is never a result.
## 5. Reading and limits
A pair shows a level effect only if its paired CI excludes 0 (a 'lead' needs its own pre-registered test with the hold-out). Final day type is hindsight; stop ignored (ceiling, not a trade); the 500-contract bars time-stamp by volume so 'minutes' is clock time of bar ends; one regime; the prior session can be a day earlier than the calendar prior day.
