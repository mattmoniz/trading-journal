# Capitulation REPLACES the target: hold with the original stop until the capitulation, by entry location (row 30 amendment A10). PRE-REGISTRATION, FROZEN 2026-11-09 night before any row is scored on the new arm

## Why this exists (correction of A4-A9, stated plainly)
The user's idea (2026-11-09 16:52 and again 21:28): use the capitulation to leave a trade "instead of a physical or numerical target". The forward logger (C1/C2/C3) and every replay A5-A9 instead tested capitulation as an ADDITIONAL early exit: a signal only counts while the setup's own trade is still open, so the exit can only come EARLIER than the setup's own target, stop or expiry, never later. In the origin episode (Globex 11-08/09) the two shorts entered at the Friday zone (MS1_FADE_SHORT_OVERNIGHT 27,289.75 and GLOBEX_VWAP_MAGNET_SHORT 27,315.75; both tagged at_boundary and, for the second, pivot_live) hit their targets at 22:59 and 21:37, hours before the 02:45:30 capitulation signal, so the logger scored them NO_SIGNAL: the gain (about +454 / +480 points held against +90 / +45 at the targets, hindsight, one session, `scripts/capitulation_exit_illustration_20261109.mjs`) came from NOT taking the target. That question has never been tested. The negative A5-A9 results stand for what they tested (early exit) and say nothing about this.

## Arms (per real setup row, same entry; dollars, cost $2 = 1.0 pt)
- **A (own exit)**: the row's stored `actual_pnl`.
- **C0 (no-target control)**: hold from the row's entry with its ORIGINAL stop (`stop_level`, gap-aware fill: a stop already breached at the bar's open fills at the open), NO target, until the row's own expiry (`expires_at`; rows without one: the 15:55 ET flat for RTH-fired and 09:29:50 ET for Globex-fired), exit at that time's bar open (mark to market).
- **H (hold to capitulation)**: C0, except the position also exits at the NEXT 500V bar's open after the first same-direction EXTENDED vacuum thrust (same definition as the logger: extension in the top tercile of earlier same-track events, not in the 18:00-18:30 ET blackout) occurring after the entry; whichever comes first of stop, thrust, expiry.
- Primary contrast **d = H - C0** (does the capitulation timing add anything beyond merely removing the target; the hr-042 entry-only-control logic); secondary **H - A** (the user's whole proposal against what the setup does today). Both reported for every stratum.

## Population and strata (frozen)
The 5,191 real ACTIVE/SHADOW cluster-primary rows of A6.2 (2026-06-15..2026-10-09; entry = `COALESCE(price_at_detection, entry_zone_high)`; direction from the name suffix else sign(t1 - entry)); the origin episode (2026-11-08/09) and the other watched sessions are OUTSIDE this window and are not part of the evidence. Tags at the row's fire, computed by the logger's own functions (`capitulation_exit_shadow.mjs`: `at_boundary` = entry within 0.05 ADR20 of the prior RTH high/low/VAH/VAL or the overnight high/low; `pivot_live` = a live confirmed swing pivot of the fade side holds the entry, `scripts/lib/pivotContext.mjs`), backfilled over the window by the same code (extract the tag functions to a shared module first, cv-079; the stored retro/forward rows must reproduce).
Strata: `at_defended_pivot` (pivot_live), `at_boundary`, and each tag's complement (the user's hypothesis is that location decides, so tagged rows should do better than untagged).

## Phase 1 (outcome-blind, FIRST; decides feasibility)
Counts only (no P&L, no resolution price): rows per stratum, independent thrust-exit events per stratum (the number of rows where H differs from C0), days, RTH vs Globex. Floor: 20 independent events on 10 days per counted test; a stratum below it is reported as "no verdict" and the counted test is dropped (hr-008), not pooled into a bigger bucket.

## Counted tests (Holm 0.10 one-sided; hr-002 RTH and Globex separately inside each)
- **R1**: mean d = H - C0 over `at_defended_pivot` rows. **R2**: mean d = H - C0 over `at_boundary` rows. (Family of 2; the control strata are descriptive.)
- Statistic and gates as A6.2: signalled-stratum events collapsed by shared thrust (cv-025), day-blocked bootstrap (4,000), winsorized > 0, drop-top-2 > 0, leave-one-day-out sign, calendar halves same sign, risk-set placebo (a control row of the same stratum, other day, open under arm C0 at the same elapsed seconds, with no earlier extended thrust, exits there instead: the thrust must beat a matched random moment), tagged minus untagged must be > 0 (A2-style). MDE printed in points and ADR20 against the 0.015 ADR20 co-025 bar; above it = PARKED, never retired.

## What this test cannot do (stated before data)
The window has no origin-episode data; the stop is the setup's ORIGINAL stop and many setups' stops are wide, so C0/H can lose far more than the target would have won (the illustration's other four shorts were stopped out); SHADOW fills are theoretical; the two tags are rare (the forward logger tagged 5 of 48 rows at a boundary and 4 at a pivot), so Phase 1 may leave R1 or R2 below the floor, in which case the forward logger is the only route and needs a new column (H and C0 virtual P&L; see Forward below).

## Forward arm (build after Phase 1, user's go)
The logger's signal window must stop ending at `resolved_at`: add per row the C0 and H virtual P&L (held with the original stop until the thrust or the expiry), computed from `price_bars_primary` minute bars plus `vacuum_live_events`, stored beside `delta_pnl` (early-exit arm kept). The Monitor card then shows early-exit and replace-target progress separately.

## Next-step tree (co-022)

Not shown or below floor: PARKED with the printed counts / MDE; forward arm continues. ## Amendment A10.2 (2026-11-09 night, after Argon's review of the A10 lead; written BEFORE any of the analyses below was run; the counted tests R1_RTH / R2_RTH / R2_GBX and their verdicts are UNCHANGED; all new items are DESCRIPTIVE, look tests = 0, no new p-value counts toward any family)
Argon's main points (audited in `docs/ARGON_REVIEW_AUDIT_A10_20261109.md`): (1) H - C0 is measured only on the rows where an extended thrust occurred before the stop or expiry (25.5 percent of rows), so it says nothing about what removing the target costs on the other 74.5 percent; the secondary H - A on that signalled subset is selected on a favourable path (on the 66 untagged Globex events C0 - A = +$113 while H - C0 = -$26). (2) The risk-set placebo (exit at a matched elapsed time) compares an exit after a large favourable move with an exit near flat, so it does not separate order flow from price excursion. (3) H - C0 is minus the C0 holder's future P&L after the thrust, i.e. it measures post-thrust mean reversion; whether the flow signal or just the price excursion causes it is untested. (4) The 240-minute extension window includes pre-entry movement.
New descriptive analyses on the SAME 5,177 rows, window and arms (script `scripts/vacuum_sim/capitulation_replay_a10_extras.py`, ledger look tests 0):
- **ITT (unconditional) table**, all rows of a stratum including those where no thrust fired (H equals C0 there): mean A, mean C0, mean H, mean H - A, mean H - C0, with day-blocked 90 percent CIs, for R2_RTH, R2_GBX, R1_RTH (pivot_live), the untagged rows by track, and all rows. This is the number that says whether replacing the target pays on the whole stratum; it becomes a REQUIRED condition for any promotion (A11.1), the conditional d stays the lead statistic.
- **Matched static target control T**: for each R2_RTH row, hold with the original stop and exit at a fixed price target = the row entry plus (direction x the walk-forward median excursion, in ADR20 units, of the H exits of STRICTLY EARLIER days' R2_RTH events, at least 10), to expiry otherwise. Compared unconditionally and on the signalled rows: if T captures most of what H captures, the capitulation is a noisy proxy for a price target. (cv-022: the target is a statistic of the same population's excursions, so it is used only as a control, walk-forward, never as a result.)
- **Thrust type**: on R2_RTH, H_non (exit at the first NON-extended same-direction thrust) and H_any (first of either) minus C0.
- **Timing and extension split** of the R2_RTH signalled events: by elapsed minutes from entry to the thrust (terciles), by the pre-entry 240-minute displacement in the trade direction (ADR20 units, terciles) and by the extension E at the thrust (terciles): H - C0 per tercile with N.
- **Globex diagnostics (counts only)**: Globex onsets by ET hour (all 513 + 68 scored sessions) and the percentile of the 11-09 02:45 event's extension (0.84 ADR20) among Globex extension values, to check Argon's claims that Globex onsets cluster at the London / pre-market opens and that the origin episode is a tail event.
Not built and not run (registered ideas, need the user's decision): the PROTECTED arm P (when the original target price is first reached, move the stop to breakeven and hold to the capitulation or expiry; trigger = the row's own `t1_level`, no new constant) and HP; a replication of the A10 design on the 2023-25 BACKFILL level-fade rows (outcome-blind Phase 1 first); a Globex variant (time-of-day baseline or a faster bar), which Argon argues the 500V ROC10 mis-specifies overnight.

## Amendment A10.4 (2026-11-09 night; a DATA-INTEGRITY correction found AFTER the A10, A10.2 and A10.3 outcomes were read, so it is post-outcome and is reported as such; the tests, statistics and gates are unchanged)
Found while checking an implausible Globex number in the replication (holding with the original stop returned about +$440 per Globex row). The minute-bar series is an UNADJUSTED front-month stitch: at each quarterly contract roll a 00:00 bar opens 150-300 points away from the previous close (+176.5 on 2023-06-12, +210.0 on 2023-12-11, +247.5 on 2024-03-11, +236.5 on 2024-09-16, +300.8 on 2024-12-17, +208.5 on 2025-03-18, +296.2 on 2026-09-14), so any arm that HOLDS across a roll prints a fictitious $400-600 gain or loss. The repo's own studies exclude NQ roll weeks (`isInsideNqRollWeek` / `getNqRollWeekDates`, `server/services/acdShared.js`); the capitulation replays A5-A10 did not apply it to the minute-bar arms. Rule (frozen now, applied to every replay that simulates C0 / H on minute bars): a row is EXCLUDED when any date between its fire date and its C0 exit date is a skip date = the repo's roll-week dates UNION the dates within -5 / +1 days of a data-detected stitching jump (a 00:00 bar opening more than 150 points from the previous close; this catches 2023-12 and 2024-03, which the repo list misses). `scripts/vacuum_sim/capitulation_replay_lib.py` `roll_skip_dates` / `build_hold_rows(skip=...)`; every script has `--include-roll` to reproduce the first run (verified byte-identical for A10). The first-run numbers stay in the recorded claims and logs and are reported beside the corrected ones. Other replays that use minute bars (A5/A6.2 use 500V bars for their exits and are not affected; A8 flush excludes roll weeks by its own prereg; A9 defended detectors skip roll dates internally but its OWN-exit simulation held across 00:00 is not covered: to be re-checked).
