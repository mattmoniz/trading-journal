### Core framework: Sierra Chart 500-Volume bars, NQ/MNQ
Subgraph 1, `Divide/Ratio` (the speedometer, unsigned vacuum metric): Ratio_t = EMA10(True Range_t) / EMA10(Bid + Ask Depth_t). Price displacement per unit of RESTING order book liquidity. Unsigned: spikes UP on both crashes and rallies.
- Spike above the green upper Bollinger band (Z > +1.5): liquidity vacuum (air pocket); resting limit orders pulled, market orders slice through empty space.
- Drop below the yellow lower band (Z < -1.5): concrete absorption wall; massive resting limit orders stacked on the DOM, price cannot move despite heavy volume; breakouts here have zero EV.
- Cooldown to the purple middle band (Z about 0): liquidity repopulating / normal resting book density.
(Bands: 50-period SMA +- 1.5 sigma.)

Subgraph 2, `Bar Time Duration` (the awake / velocity gate): dt_t = close timestamp - open timestamp.
- RTH benchmark: dt <= 15.0 seconds confirms fast tape / aggressive institutional sweeps (33+ contracts per second).
- Visual clamping warning: the Sierra setting `Limit Bar Time Duration to Maximum = Yes, 00:00:15` only clips the display into a flat green block; in code, evaluate dt <= 15.0 numerically, leave the chart unclamped with a dashed 15 s reference line.
- Globex pace adjustment: overnight 500V bars naturally take 3-5 minutes. At the 18:00 open, acceleration is relative: dt_t <= 0.65 x SMA50(dt_t) AND dt_t <= 120.0 s (duration dropping by half confirms urgency).

Subgraph 3, `Rate Of Change - Points` (the compass and direction): ROC10_t = Close_t - Close_{t-10}. Bands: 50-period SMA +- 2.0 sigma.
- Breakout through the green top band: bullish velocity thrust. Breakdown through the yellow bottom band: bearish waterfall.
- Purple middle band and the ZERO line are the continuation anchor: in a healthy trend, pullbacks hold on the trend side of zero and bounce off the purple middle line. Crossing 0.00 deactivates the trend and triggers a Priority 2 Structural Exit.
