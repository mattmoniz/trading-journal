# Scope: trade-watcher marks and labels (research tooling first, nothing live) (written 2026-11-04; NOT BUILT; RETIRED 2026-11-04 by the user after steps 0 and 0b were NOT SHOWN, see section 8)

Origin: the user's exit design thread of 2026-11-04 (register rows 12 and 13, `docs/IMPROVEMENT_REGISTER_20261104.md`). Every exit test run today (fixed trails, nearest-zone targets, structure exits, a leg-profile stop) was underpowered or traded profit for safety; the one piece of information the data cannot give is WHICH stalls are digestion before another leg and which are absorption before a reversal. The user can tell by eye. Their labels are the missing data. Principle (user, 13:10 ET): every reading must be NOW versus LATELY, relative to its own recent past in the same session, never an absolute level; no fixed thresholds (CLAUDE.md hr-003, cv-028). Memory: `feedback-watcher-now-vs-lately-20261104`.

## 1. What gets built (in this order, each step usable alone)
1. **A per-minute feature table (research only, no server).** For every RTH minute of the explore dates and of today: volume against the median of the previous W minutes; selling share (bid volume share) against its mean over the previous W minutes; range per contract against its median over W; minutes since the last new favourable/adverse extreme of the current leg; dwell (minutes within one typical bar of the current price); the rotation state (leg direction, side of the rotational VWAP, lost-VWAP flag: `server/services/rotationGuide.js`); distance to the nearest overnight thin zone and market level. Each reading also carries its PERCENTILE among the minutes of the same session so far (causal). W is a DISPLAY parameter chosen from the data's own persistence (the shortest window beyond which the feature's autocorrelation falls to its floor, per feature, computed on explore data) and stated before use; not hand-set. Reuses the real functions: `rotationDetector.js`, `structure_lib.py`, the tell's bars.
2. **Marks on charts (static images, scratch only).** 1-minute bars, volume, the three relative readings as panels, the rotation legs and VWAP, the thin zones and levels; a mark where two or more readings are simultaneously extreme for their own session (percentile cut derived from the day's distribution, shown, not tuned). Output PNGs in `scratch/watcher_marks_20261104/` for a chosen set of trades.
3. **A blind labelling protocol.** For each chosen event the user sees the chart TRUNCATED at the event minute (no future) and answers: digestion (the move continues), absorption (reverses), or neither/unclear, optionally with a few words. The chart is then revealed and the outcome measured (the favourable and adverse excursion over the next 60 minutes in units of the typical bar: a higher-powered outcome than net dollars, step 1 lesson). Labels are stored in a small versioned JSON next to the images, never typed into docs as statistics (hr-019).
4. **Only then a written definition** that tries to reproduce the user's labels on a held-out half (agreement by Cohen's kappa and by the excursion outcome each label predicts); if no definition agrees, the watcher stays a display and the idea is retired with an autopsy (co-022). A pre-registered test on the explore tell trades (excursion profile after a mark, day-block CIs, detectable effect printed first) follows ONLY if the labels show the user's reads separate continuation from reversal.
5. **Display (last, and only if 4 passes).** A section of the single Monitor & Learning page (CLAUDE.md cv-110), display only, then a forward shadow record like the stop books; nothing gates, sizes or exits until the user decides.

## 2. The event set to label (small on purpose)
About 30 events: today's tell trades (10:03, 10:05, 10:11, 10:20, 10:24 short, 12:28 long) at their stalls and the two lows (11:05-11:06 and 11:25-11:28); plus 20 explore tell trades chosen without looking at the outcome: 10 with a large favourable move and 10 that reversed, each shown at a stall minute chosen by a fixed causal rule (the first minute after the entry at which price has not made a new extreme for as many minutes as the feature table says is typical). A second, independent batch of about 30 is the hold-out of the labelling itself (used once, to score the written definition).

## 3. Data needed and checks
`price_bars_primary` NQ 1-minute bars with `bid_volume` and `ask_volume` (columns exist; verify coverage by date before use and exclude dates where the bid plus ask does not equal the volume within a tolerance, hr-070: RTH volume is about 2.7 times inflated on some mid-2025 dates, so readings are RATIOS within a day, never absolute volume across days); the explore trades file; the rotation legs export (`export_legs_20261104.mjs`). Both time bounds on every query (cv-009). Delta stays OUT of any rule until the user says how they read it (13:10 ET and 12:12 ET), the bid share is only a display reading.

## 4. Risks and limits (stated now)
User time: 30 events is about 15 minutes; more is the user's choice. Small N: the labels measure the user's reading skill and whether a definition can copy it; they are not a trading edge by themselves. Explore only; Globex untested; the 65-point leg is a small swing on a day like 2026-11-04 (legs flip every few minutes), so a leg-based reading may be noisy; the thin-zone and level sets exclude the weekly open and TPO levels. A relative reading in a quiet session can look extreme while being small in dollars: the percentile and the raw numbers are always shown together.

## 7. A1. Amendment after DeepSeek design critique round T (2026-11-04 13:30 ET; `scratch/deepseek_response_20261104_132949.md`; written before any outcome was read)
Diagnosis taken, remedies applied as follows (hr-035: deviations stated).
1. **Step 0 is new and comes first: a free kill test on data already on disk** (`docs/TELL_STALL_FEATURES_KILL_TEST_PREREG_20261104.md`): do the NOW-versus-LATELY features, measured at the stall minute of the 1,760 explore tell entries, separate the next 60 minutes' continuation from reversal at all? It costs no user time and has far more power than 30 labels. Steps 1-5 below run only if it shows a lead OR the user chooses to label anyway after seeing a null (their time, their call); a null is reported as 'features alone do not carry it', not as 'the user's read is wrong'.
2. **Event set (section 2) replaced.** The 20 explore events are drawn uniformly at random (seeded) from the explore entries that have a stall minute, stratified only by entry hour (10:00-10:59 vs later), never by outcome (the earlier text said both 'without looking at the outcome' and '10 large favourable, 10 reversed'; that was inconsistent and set the label base rate by sampling). A 10/10 outcome-split set is kept only as a separate diagnostic batch. Today's 8 events are outcome-known and were seen by the user: PILOT only, to test the chart and truncation and whether the user's blind read matches their live read; never scored.
3. **Blinding (step 3):** price axis relative to the event-minute close; levels shown as offsets in typical bars; no date, no filename date, no entry or stop price; time shown as ELAPSED minutes since the session open, not clock time. If the user wants clock time, the scored batch is blind with elapsed time and a declared non-blind pass with clock time follows only after scoring.
4. **Step 4 plan:** before any definition, a TEST-RETEST gate: the user relabels 10 shuffled events a day later; below kappa 0.6 (binary 0.7) there is no stable ground truth and the idea stops there. Primary verdict is Cohen's kappa on the 3 classes (unclear is a real class); the excursion outcome is a descriptive cross-check only (it must not define the classes, cv-022). Precision stated now: with 30 events kappa has SE about 0.13, so 30 labels can tell chance from clearly good (kappa 0.4 or more), not a weak real agreement; ONE definition, ONE hold-out spend, no refit after the hold-out kappa (cv-006, hr-023 `spendSealedLook()`). 'Typical bar' = median 1-minute range of the session up to the event minute (never the full day, hr-020).
5. **Features:** one window grid pre-registered for all features (W = 20 minutes primary, 10 and 30 as sensitivity; NOW = the last 5 minutes), replacing the per-feature 'autocorrelation floor' choice. Added: VOLUME PER POINT OF NET MOVE (effort), now versus lately. Dropped as duplicates: dwell (same stall as minutes since the last extreme). A session percentile is shown only after 60 completed RTH minutes (the existing UNKNOWN convention); before that only the ratios are shown, so the 10:03-10:24 pilot events carry ratios, not percentiles. The 'two readings extreme' mark is dropped from the kill test (volume, range and bid share co-move in any fast leg); it can return only as a conjunction tested against its own parts.
6. **Retire rule (co-022):** features show no lead in step 0 AND the user declines to label, or user test-retest kappa under the floor, or the definition's hold-out kappa is 0.26 or lower with no excursion separation: the watcher stays a DISPLAY of the ratios (volume per point, bid share, range per contract) on the Monitor page; cheapest next test is then a hold-or-slice tape study with tick data. Salvage in every branch: the per-minute feature table and the effort ratio.
