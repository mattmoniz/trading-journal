# Pre-registration: does the flip (live override) help before 12:00 ET and hurt from 12:00 ET? ONE sealed look (written 2026-11-03 night BEFORE any hold-out trade was simulated or read)

STATUS: FROZEN. Ledger family `flip_time_of_day_20261103`; the sealed look is spent via `spendSealedLook()` window `sealed_2025-05-19_2026-10-19` by `flip_time_sealed.mjs`, only after phase 1 prints the counts and the detectable effect. Nothing here changes the live system; the user (2026-11-03) said to leave the flip in and asked for this test.
Origin: a POST-HOC lead found on the explore window while reading an uncounted split (claim `flip_helps_morning_hurts_afternoon_posthoc_20261103`, ledger look 126): on AGAINST fires (N=402) flip minus tell = +13.9 dollars/trade before 12:00 (N=226, CI -7.3..+34.1) and -44.4 from 12:00 (N=176, CI -70.1..-18.8); early minus late +58.3 (CI +26.1..+90.7); the late loss was -5.2 in the first half of the window and -76.3 in the second. The 12:00 cut was picked AFTER seeing those numbers, so the explore result is no evidence; only this sealed look counts.
## 1. What is run (no new trading logic)
The tell's research simulation `trade.simulate(ctx, 'V1')` (unchanged; it reproduces all 1,760 explore trades of the 2026-10-28 file exactly, checked) on the hold-out sessions (500-contract bars built for them, `scratch/volbars500_holdout_20261103`; the first sessions' prior-20 statistics come from the end of the explore window), then for each fire the live context label (`context.context_at_fire`) and the shadow books A (the tell) and C (the flip of AGAINST fires; other fires follow the tell) from `shadow.simulate_fire`, exactly as in `tell_replay_lib.py` (`window='sealed'`). Fires of every session with at least 20 prior sessions. Fill, stop and exit conventions are the tell's (2 dollars/point, 2 dollars commission). A fire is EARLY if its signal bar completed before 12:00 ET, LATE from 12:00 ET.
## 2. Primary test (one) and the rule
Among AGAINST fires: GAP = book C net minus book A net per fire. CONFIRMED needs ALL of: (1) mean GAP early minus mean GAP late > 0 with a day-block bootstrap 95 percent CI (2000 draws) excluding 0; (2) mean GAP late < 0 with the CI upper bound < 0; (3) mean GAP early > 0 (point estimate); (4) early minus late > 0 in both halves of the sealed window (split at the median fire date). Otherwise NOT CONFIRMED, reported as exactly which condition failed. Phase 1 prints BEFORE any outcome: sessions, fires, AGAINST fires early / late, and the detectable early-minus-late difference (2.8 x sd x sqrt(1/n_early + 1/n_late) with sd = 166.7 dollars, the explore per-fire spread of GAP); if it exceeds 40 dollars (the explore estimate is 58.3) the run ends INCONCLUSIVE IN ADVANCE and the sealed look is NOT spent.
## 3. Secondary (counted, tests = 3 in the ledger with the primary; cannot change the verdict)
(a) overall AGAINST GAP (flip minus tell, all day) with its CI; (b) control: the tell's own mean on NON-AGAINST fires early vs late (does the clock alone move tell results?). Printed, uncounted: the hourly table of tell / flip means and N; the tell and flip means separately early and late.
## 4. Regression check before the sealed look
`node scripts/tell_context/research_20261103/flip_time_sealed.mjs explore-check` re-runs the identical code on the explore window (simulation path `explore_sim`) and must reproduce the explore numbers above (early +13.9, late -44.4); logged as an explore look with 0 new tests.
## 5. Meaning
CONFIRMED = on unseen sessions the flip behaves differently before and after noon in the way the explore lead said; it supports a one-line rule (no flips from 12:00) which still needs the user's decision; NOT CONFIRMED = the lead was a selected look and the flip is judged by the live checkpoint. Either way the sealed window is spent for this family. Limits: replay conventions, one instrument, the tell's V1 book only, hindsight about nothing (all measures are at or after the fire); the hold-out base rates and the AGAINST share may differ from explore (printed).

## 6. AMENDMENT A1 (2026-11-03 night, APPROVED BY THE USER, written after the hold-out COUNTS were seen and before any hold-out outcome was read)
