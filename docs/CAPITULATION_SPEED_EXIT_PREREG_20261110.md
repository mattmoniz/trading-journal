## Definitions (causal at the climax bar's close; real functions)

- EXTREME-FAST = PACE5 <= the 33rd percentile of PACE5 over EARLIER climaxes of the track (rolling, last 600, needs >= 60; earlier events are burn-in and excluded); descriptive: the fastest 10 percent.
- A position in the direction s of the climax (long into a buying climax, short into a selling one) is what the signal protects. The value of the EXIT at the climax = what STAYING IN would have lost over the next 30 bars: v = -fwd30(with s), entry Open[a+1], `shadow.calc_outcome`, hard flats as live; pre-cost (the exit costs nothing extra for a position that is being closed anyway); the net variant subtracts the 2.0 round trip for the opposite-direction (BOUNCE) trade.

## Counted family (assert 6; RTH and Globex separate): per track
T1 EXIT-VALUE on EXTREME-FAST climaxes: mean v > 0 (staying in loses). T2 BOUNCE net on EXTREME-FAST climaxes: mean(v) - 2.0 > 0 (the opposite trade pays after cost). T3 INFORMATION: mean v on EXTREME-FAST minus mean v on the others > 0 (speed adds information). One-sided, estimator sum/N (hr-062), day-blocked bootstrap 3000 draws, seeds 1500 + k (k = 0..5), p = (1 + count)/(draws + 1); gate >= 100 events and >= 60 days per cell else PARKED (Globex fast cell is expected to be below it: forward-only); Holm 0.10 with the pre-declared 0.05 fallback if the null gate exceeds 0.10.
