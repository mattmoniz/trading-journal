# Pre-registration: session x time-block PROMOTION arm (partial pooling) against the pooled live roster (written 2026-11-08 BEFORE any bucket arm outcome was computed; Opus architecture review item 8, user: 'gate promotion for setups to live based on when they perform best. Globex vs rth vs time of day. Maybe a model helps with that')

STATUS: FROZEN. SHADOW only: nothing gates, sizes or vetoes (the live roster stays `scripts/calibrate_live_roster.mjs`). Candidate Q4-1 in `docs/CANDIDATE_QUEUE.md`. Ledger family `bucket_roster_20261108`, 2 counted tests on the retrospective walk-forward (a LEAD at most: the data window overlaps the data that suggested the idea); the confirming look is FORWARD (section 6).

## 1. Buckets (fixed now, ET clock time of `fired_at`, split by the clock not by the setup name, cv-004)
RTH_OPEN 09:35-10:30; RTH_MID 10:30-14:00; RTH_LATE 14:00-16:00; GLOBEX_EVE 18:30-24:00; GLOBEX_NIGHT 00:00-09:30. Fires in 09:30-09:35, 16:00-18:30 are hard-excluded BEFORE the model (the existing dead zones, `isInRthOpenDeadZone`, the no-new-entries 16:00-18:00 rule and the first 30 minutes of Globex); no bucket is estimated for them. Finer buckets (hour, day of week, month) are not estimable at 13-22 trades per type and are NOT used.
## 2. The estimator (no hand-picked shrinkage, hr-003)
Observations = cell-days: for each (setup_type, bucket, calendar day) the mean P&L of the cluster-collapsed events (`collapseClusterSiblings`, same population and basis filters as `calibrate_live_roster.mjs`: ACTIVE+SHADOW since 2026-07-09, no *_basis rows, no 16:00-18:00 rows, no NOT_FILLED, no POC_ROTATION_JOIN). Two-stage empirical Bayes: (A) the setup effect and the bucket effect are each shrunk toward the grand mean with a DerSimonian-Laird variance component estimated from the data; the cell prior mean is the additive combination m = mu_type* + mu_bucket* - mu; (B) the cell mean is shrunk toward m with its own estimated between-cell variance; posterior sd adds the uncertainty of the stage-A estimates. A cell with no data of its own inherits m and a wide sd; a type with no evidence collapses to the grand mean (usually below 0), so it is never promoted. **Promotion rule: posterior mean minus 0.8416 x posterior sd above 0** (the one-sided 80 percent lower bound, fixed now, not tuned), on data strictly BEFORE the day scored (events with `resolved_at` before the day's 00:00). The model code is `server/services/bucketRoster.js` (pure, unit-tested before any outcome is read).
**A1 (2026-11-08, written after the unit tests and BEFORE any outcome was computed):** the unit test showed that a cell with no days of its own would inherit its type's evidence from other buckets and could be promoted in a part of the day where the type never fired (a Globex fire promoted on RTH evidence: the assumption hr-002 / hr-039 warn against). Amended: a (type, bucket) cell with NO data of its own is never promoted; it stays SHADOW until it has its own days (the inherited estimate is logged for display as `wouldPass`). The sentence above that says a cell with no data 'inherits m' describes the estimate, not the decision.
## 3. Arms (scored per calendar day on DECLUSTERED fires, `is_cluster_primary`, hr-007; per-day dollars and per-trade sum/N, hr-062)
POOLED = the real roster rule as of that day (`evaluateType` + `buildRoster`, hysteresis carried day to day, first day seeded without it as the live job does); BUCKET = the model above; ALL = every declustered fire. First scored day = the first calendar day with at least 25 distinct prior trade days. Fires of a type are taken when their type (POOLED) or their (type, bucket) cell (BUCKET) is on the roster.
## 4. Counted tests (2), day-block bootstrap (4,000 draws, seed 20261108), 97.5 percent CI (Bonferroni 2), detectable effect (2.8 x SE) printed first
**T1:** mean daily dollars, BUCKET minus POOLED (paired by calendar day, days where either arm takes a trade). **T2:** mean dollars PER TRADE, BUCKET minus POOLED (sum/N per arm, bootstrap over days). LEAD = T1 CI lower bound above 0 AND both chronological halves positive; T2 is reported against it (a rule that wins per day only by taking more trades is flagged).
## 5. Reported, not counted
Per-arm N trades, days with trades, win rate, average win / loss, worst day; BUCKET vs ALL; by bucket (which buckets add or remove trades); the cells promoted by BUCKET but not POOLED and the reverse (list); the grand mean, variance components and the share of cells with their own data; the same walk-forward with the posterior bound at 90 percent and 70 percent as sensitivity (not counted, not tuned).
## 6. After the arm starts (forward)
The nightly job `scripts/calibrate_bucket_roster.mjs` (a step of `run_daily_calibration.sh` after the live roster) writes `performance_audit` `LIVE_ROSTER_BUCKET`/`CURRENT` each night, never read by any gate. The same walk-forward script re-run on days after 2026-11-08 is the confirmation: PASS = BUCKET minus POOLED lower bound above 0 and both halves positive at 60 distinct forward days, plus a pass-race comparison once the simulator exists; the look cadence is the queue's (60 / 120 / 250). Kill rule: no better than POOLED at 60 AND 120 forward days.
## 7. Honest limits
93 days and 262 types since 2026-07-09; most types have a handful of days, so most cell decisions are inherited from the pooled evidence by design; the window includes the first two live weeks of the roster itself (the pooled arm and the live roster overlap); Globex buckets are thinner than RTH; buckets were chosen from the repo's own findings (dead zones, session reversals), not from this walk-forward; fills and P&L are those of `active_setups` (shadow fills included). Retrospective only = LEAD.

## 8. RESULT (2026-11-08; script `scripts/backtest_bucket_roster.mjs`, ledger look 181, claim `bucket_roster_not_shown_20261108`; model `server/services/bucketRoster.js`, unit tests `scripts/test_bucket_roster.mjs` passed before the run)
79 scored calendar days (2026-08-07..2026-11-06), 10,265 rows on 104 days, 262 setup types; 43 days where either arm took a trade. Detectable effect printed first: T1 $105.5 per day.
- **T1** BUCKET minus POOLED, mean daily dollars: **-64.47 [-156.78, +11.18]**; halves -94.90 / -35.41. **T2** dollars per trade: **-4.22 [-25.23, +14.01]**. **Verdict: NOT SHOWN** (T1 lower bound not above 0, halves both negative).
- Arms (declustered fires): POOLED 109 trades, -8.14 per trade, win rate 31 percent; BUCKET 296 trades, -12.36 per trade, win rate 41 percent, average loss -80 (against -50 for POOLED); ALL 6,490 trades, -2.51 per trade. By bucket the BUCKET arm lost in RTH_MID (165 trades, -1,723), RTH_LATE (-1,052) and GLOBEX_EVE (-868) and made +140 in GLOBEX_NIGHT.
- **Exploratory, not counted (outside the 2 registered tests, one look):** POOLED minus ALL = **-5.62 per trade [-19.89, +8.23]** (inconclusive), BUCKET minus ALL = **-9.85 per trade [-21.47, -1.89]** (the bucket arm did worse than taking every fire). Reading: gating promotion on a rolling few weeks of evidence did not pick next-day winners in this window; the pooled live rule is not shown to beat take-all either (consistent with Opus's winner's-curse warning about the 5-day roster rule).
- READING: partial pooling over session and time-of-day did NOT improve promotion on 93 days. The 80 percent bound is lax by design and promoted 3 times as many fires as the pooled rule at a worse average loss. NOT tested: a stricter bound (sensitivity, not tuned), only the two-bucket RTH / Globex split, a longer window (data starts 2026-07-09), the Sierra tick era. The idea 'when a setup performs best matters' is NOT refuted: this model, this window and this bound are what failed.
- Forward confirmation = re-run `node scripts/backtest_bucket_roster.mjs --first-forward 2026-11-09` at 60 and 120 forward days (rosters are rebuilt as of each day, so no nightly job is needed for the evaluation); a nightly shadow writer was NOT built because the retrospective arm is worse than both the pooled rule and take-all. Candidate Q4-1: kill rule stands (no better than pooled at 60 AND 120 forward days).
