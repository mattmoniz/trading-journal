# "Live means fired": every live row must reach the broker -- design A (2026-10-19, DESIGN ONLY, nothing built)

Status: for independent design review (DeepSeek) BEFORE any code, per the higher-stakes-work rule. Related decisions: `live_means_fired_every_live_row_must_reach_broker_20261019`, `major_pivot_live_rows_always_miss_3min_entry_window_20261019`.

## User directive
"All the trades that are in live need to fire at the broker. Live means fired." Chosen design: **A = make them fire** (retry a still-valid row instead of dropping it; stop applying the flat 3-minute rule where it does not belong; shorten detector lag). The "insert as SHADOW if it cannot be ordered" backstop (B) was NOT chosen for now.

## Facts (measured 2026-10-19)
- 75 `origin_status='ACTIVE'` rows today; 14 (19%) got no entry order. By recorded cause (`gated_candidates` `SIERRA_ENTRY_SKIP`): 6 `kill switch not armed` (rows written in the disarmed window after a restart, around 10:31), 6 `signal older than the 3-minute entry window`, 2 daily-loss-limit "trade without broker P&L" (morning gate bug, already fixed). Earlier days far worse (10-09: 45 of 45 live rows without an order; 10-16: 17 of 21) because of the outages.
- The 3-minute number is `ENTRY_LOOKBACK_MINUTES = 3` (`server/services/sierraChart/orderSweep.js:35`) and is used in THREE places that must move together: (1) `sweepEntries()`'s SELECT (`fired_at > NOW() - 3 min`), (2) the "too old" skip note in the same function, (3) `autoResolveMissingOrders()` (`server/services/missingOrderAutoResolve.js`, `LIVE_AUTO_RESOLVE_MISSING_ORDER_BUFFER_MIN = 3`) which, on every 15 s poll, sets `status='EXPIRED', resolution='NOT_FILLED', resolution_method='PRIMARY_UNFILLED'` for any ACTIVE-origin row older than 3 minutes with no ENTRY order -- so even a sweep that retried would find the row already killed. The age is measured from `fired_at` (the signal's bar time), not from when the row was written.
- Row creation lags the signal time systematically. Last 6 days, `created_at - fired_at` by family (seconds): CAM_S3 p50 132 / p90 149 / max 154; OR30_LOW 131/142/145; VWAP_MAGNET 123/129/140; C_PAIRED 111/156/162 (4 of 22 above 150 s); A_UP_STRONG 110/154/158; IB_HIGH 102/122; POC_ROTATION_JOIN 89 / **439** / **1829**. Row written -> entry order submitted (non-escalated entries, n=63): p50 12 s, p90 37 s. So a 180 s window leaves ~20-80 s of slack for normal rows. MAJOR_PIVOT_DEFENDED_BREAK: detector result cached 4 minutes (`CACHE_TTL_MS`), rows written 2:37-2:51 after the signal; both live rows today missed (0 of 2 ever ordered).
- What the window was FOR (`orderSweep.js:35` comment + `missingOrderAutoResolve.js` header): "don't backfill an entry for an old row a deploy/restart happens to see fresh" -- a restart or outage must not enter hours-old signals. It was never about normal detector lag.
- Order types: `wantsMarketEntry()` (`orderSweep.js`) sends price-triggered families at MARKET (`MARKET_ENTRY_PREFIXES`: POC_ROTATION_JOIN_, MAJOR_PIVOT_, STALL_, MINOR_, MOMENTUM_CHASE_, A_UP_/A_DOWN_/C_PAIRED_/TRT_, ...); level fades (PD_/CAM_/FLOOR_/OR*/IB_/PW_/WS/MS/_OVERNIGHT) go in as LIMIT at `entry_zone_low`. A MARKET entry stale-fills at the current price while `stop_level`/`t1_level` stay fixed; the gap guard in `reconciliation.js` (~line 919) re-places the stop at fill +/- the calibrated risk distance if the fill landed past the planned stop. LIMIT entries that rest unfilled are escalated to MARKET after `ENTRY_FILL_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`.
- `isOrderEligible()` (`orderEligibility.js`): `kill switch not armed` returns ineligible and the skip is terminal today (one `SIERRA_ENTRY_SKIP` note, then the auto-resolver kills the row).
- Same-day finding that fits: setups.js/`quick-check` show these rows as "live" with `no order`; the displayed P&L for them is the model number.

## Proposal A (for critique)
1. **Two clocks instead of one.** Replace the flat "3 min since fired_at" with (a) a LATENESS-AT-CREATION check: how late the row was written versus that detector family's own normal lateness, derived from its own history (e.g. a percentile of its past `created_at - fired_at`, with a floor -- no hand-picked minutes), which is what distinguishes "this detector is always ~2.7 min behind" from "a restart delayed this by 9 minutes"; and (b) a SWEEP-FRESHNESS check measured from `created_at` (the sweep must act within N minutes of the row existing), to keep restart protection.
2. **LIMIT vs MARKET.** Apply a strict freshness rule only to MARKET entries (chase risk). A LIMIT at the level stays valid for as long as the row is `status='ACTIVE'` and not structurally invalidated (price has not crossed `stop_level`), up to the row's own `expires_at`. Question: is a late LIMIT truly as safe as it looks (price already through the level fills immediately at market)?
3. **Retryable skips.** `kill switch not armed` and `daily loss limit ... cannot confirm` become NON-terminal: record the skip once (visibility), keep the row eligible, and place the order on the first sweep after the gate clears, subject to the same validity/freshness rules. `autoResolveMissingOrders()` must use the same (new) deadline as the sweep, never a separate constant.
4. **Detector lag.** Shorten MAJOR_PIVOT's 4-minute cache (and check the other 5-minute-bar detectors) so the row is written promptly after the bar closes.
5. **Do not restart during RTH** (each restart disarms Sierra and costs live signals); operational rule, not code.
6. **Visibility.** Whatever still cannot be ordered gets a plain reason on the row (already: `broker_note`), and quick-check's Live view should not count it as a fired trade (separate display decision, user already approved "real shows real, shadow shows shadow").

## Questions for the reviewer
1. Is replacing the flat window with (lateness-vs-family-norm) + (sweep freshness from created_at) sound, or does it reopen the restart/outage back-fill hole? Propose the exact rule and what each input is derived from. Where is it dangerous (a detector whose normal lateness is itself huge, e.g. POC_ROTATION_JOIN p90 439 s / max 1829 s)?
2. LIMIT vs MARKET: is dropping the age rule for LIMIT entries safe? What does a limit placed after price has already traded through the level do, and what does the gap guard/escalation do after that? Should a late LIMIT be re-validated against current price first?
3. For a late MARKET entry, how should risk be treated (stop/target fixed vs shifted) -- is "keep the calibrated risk distance from the real fill" (existing gap guard) enough, and should the target also move?
4. Making kill-switch/daily-limit skips retryable: failure modes (a deliberately-disarmed switch must never be bypassed; a row that waits 20 minutes then fires at market into a different regime; daily-limit gate flapping; double entry; interaction with `idx_order_placements_one_entry_per_setup`, the position-slot cap of 10, `escalateUnfilledEntriesToMarket`, `autoResolveMissingOrders`, `structurallyInvalidateSetups`, `closeOnRotationFlip`).
7. Every place that reads `ENTRY_LOOKBACK_MINUTES` or an equivalent age: grep and list them; which others besides the three named must change together?
8. What tests and rollout order would you require, and how should this be flag-gated and rolled back? What should be verified on the next live session?
