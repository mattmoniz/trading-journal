# Pre-registration: does the busy-or-quiet forecast at the tell's entry predict the trade's FAVORABLE excursion, separately from its adverse one? (written 2026-11-02 BEFORE any outcome was read)

Why: the live tell (Stage 2) has no profit target. Earlier tests showed it detects busy markets with a coin-flip side. This asks whether a forecast of travel, available at the tell's entry, tells us how far the move goes in the TELL'S direction, which is what a data-derived target needs. Context: `docs/FORECAST_SERVICE_20261102.md`, `PREREG_ROSTER_CONTEXT.md` (claims `roster_context_*`). Explore only (<= 2025-05-16); the sealed window is not read; Stage 2 is not touched.

## Population and definitions (frozen)
- Trades: the research V1 tell trades, exactly as Stage 2 trades them: `trade.simulate(ctx, 'V1')` on every explore session with a usable baseline (the code the live sidecar replays literally). Dates before the 121st usable date are dropped (no model yet) and counted. Entry bar `ei`, direction d (the tell's own side), entry = open of `ei` + d x slippage (the research convention). Decision time = end of the signal bar `ei - 1`.
- Forecast: the no-tell activity model of Amendment 3 (`notell2`: pace, range so far, overnight range, minutes since open, rr15/30/60, clock bucket, day of week; ridge on log E_30, alpha by time-ordered inner CV), trained walk-forward on explore decision points dated strictly before the trade's block (blocks of 20 dates, first 120 dates train-only), applied to the features at the decision time. Baseline forecast: mean E_30 of the same 30-minute clock bucket over the training dates (shrunk to the global mean below 50 rows).
- Outcomes, exit-independent: over completed bars j >= ei with end <= end[ei-1] + 1,800 s, in ATR20 units: MFE30 = favorable excursion from the entry price in direction d (highest high above entry for a long, lowest low below for a short, floored at 0); MAE30 = adverse excursion likewise. Trades whose 30-minute window is not complete are dropped and counted.

## Tests (counted family T1, T2; BH q=0.10 over the two; day-blocked bootstrap 3000, seed 20261102; label-permutation null by permuting the forecast vectors across dates, 1000 draws; chronological halves by median date)
- T1 (favorable reach): the forecast's out-of-sample rank correlation with MFE30 exceeds the baseline's. Statistic: Spearman(forecast, MFE30) minus Spearman(baseline, MFE30). PASS = CI lower bound > 0, permutation p < 0.05, difference > 0 in both halves.
- T2 (side advantage): Spearman(forecast, MFE30 minus MAE30). A positive value means a busier forecast goes with MORE favorable than adverse travel for the tell's side, which is what stretching a target without widening the risk would need. PASS = CI lower bound > 0, permutation p < 0.05, positive in both halves.
- Reported, uncounted: Spearman(forecast, MAE30); mean MFE30, MAE30 and the V1 trade's own `net` dollars by forecast third (terciles of all evaluated trades, descriptive); trade count, dates, and resolution (smallest correlation the sample could have shown).

## Reading rules (fixed now)
T1 fails -> no forecast-based target for the tell; thread closes with a recorded claim. T1 passes and T2 fails -> the forecast predicts travel in BOTH directions (mostly mechanical); a target stretch would have to be paired with a stop stretch, which is untested; recorded as such, and a target simulation is a separate pre-registered step only if the user asks. T1 and T2 pass -> a target simulation on the V1 trades is pre-registered next (the target rule and the stop rule written before any simulated dollar is read); NOT run in this step. No dollar claim is made anywhere in this test (the descriptive `net` by third is reported only, uncounted). Ledger: one look, tests = 4. Any addition is a written amendment before it is run.
