## 1. Held fixed
The same 1,760 explore entries, 500-contract bars, V1 management unchanged except where an arm says otherwise (120-minute no-break exit, tell-fade after the break, 15:55 flatten, stop fills at its price minus a tick, a gapped stop fills at the open). Every arm only changes the STOP or ADDS an exit. Parity of the default path with the file must be 0.0000. Scale s = median range of the 20 completed bars before the signal (step 1).

## 2. Distance floors (walk-forward, no lookahead)
For a trade on date D the floors come ONLY from V1 winners (net above 0) on strictly EARLIER dates (expanding window, at least 50 such winners, else the arm follows V1 and the entry is counted as 'no floor yet'): **F_move** = the median of (V1 MFE / s) times this trade's s (about 9.8 s in the full sample); **F_pb** = the 90th percentile of (pre-peak pullback / s) times this trade's s (about 9.6 s). Both use step 1's per-trade values (`scratch/tell_context_20261103/tell_hold_value_trail_rows.csv`: v1_mfe, dd_pre, scale, csv_net, date).

## 3. The leg profile
The current confirmed 65-point leg IN THE TRADE'S DIRECTION (an UP leg for a long, a DOWN leg for a short), from the REAL detector (`server/services/rotationDetector.js` `detectRotationLegs`, run over the RTH 1-minute bars from 09:30; exported with each leg's pivot minute and confirmation minute by `export_legs_20261104.mjs`): the most recent such leg confirmed before the signal minute; its pivot is the start of the profile. The LEG PROFILE at bar j = the tell's own tick volume-at-price (`T.Prof`) accumulated from the first 500-contract bar that ends after the pivot minute through bar j. 'Two LVNs back' = the tell's own rule (`T.stop_price(..., which=2)`: the lowest-volume bin behind the second left-behind node, one tick beyond it) applied to the LEG profile instead of the whole-session profile. No trade-direction leg before the signal: the leg arms follow V1.

## 4. The five arms (the five counted tests)
- **R0 initial stop on the leg profile:** the stop at entry is the leg-profile LVN behind the second node (when it exists and is on the protective side of the fill), everything else V1; no ratchet. Tests whether the structural initial stop beats the tell's whole-session stop; the change in risk (median stop distance, one-contract dollars, worst loss) is reported.
- **R1 leg-profile ratchet:** V1's initial stop; after each completed bar j the leg-profile LVN behind the second node is recomputed; it becomes the new stop (effective from bar j+1) ONLY IF it is at least F_pb behind the best favourable price since entry AND tighter than the current stop; the stop never loosens. Once the candidate is beyond the entry in the profit direction the stop locks a profit and a pullback to it is the exit.
- **T1 target beyond the normal run:** the target is the near edge of the first overnight thin zone ahead whose distance from the entry is at least F_move (zones, definition and limit-fill rule as step 2: runs of at least 5 one-point bins below the 25th percentile of the night's occupied bins, overnight 18:00 to 09:29); none: V1.
- **A3F flip at structure after the run:** step 2's A3 (first rotation flip against the position at a thin zone or a market level, proximity 2 s), taken only if its minute is at or after the first completed bar at which the favourable excursion reached F_move; earlier flips are ignored.
- **A4F value-area cue after the run:** step 2's A4 (out of the developing value area, a flip against the position since, then a close back inside), exit only at or after the same gate.
- **Counted outcome (each arm):** paired difference arm minus V1 in CAPTURE = exit points / max(V1 MFE, s), day-block bootstrap CI at 99 percent (Bonferroni 0.05 / 5, 4,000 draws, seed 20261104), two-sided. KEEPS MORE = capture CI lower bound above 0 AND the net-dollar difference's lower bound above minus (2.8 x its SE) AND both chronological halves positive; capture up with the net-dollar CI entirely below 0 = KEEPS MORE, COSTS RUNNERS; positive capture with a CI including 0 = LEAD. For R0 (a stop at entry, not an exit) the same measures are reported but a lower RISK with no net-dollar loss is also noted as the finding the user cares about (a risk statement, not a statistic).
- An arm that acts on fewer than 150 entries is UNDERPOWERED and not scored.
