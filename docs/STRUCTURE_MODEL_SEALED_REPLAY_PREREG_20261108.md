## 2. Which days qualify (fixed now, counts printed before any score)
(a) in `scratch/contract_by_date_20261020.json` (the contract calendar); (b) at least 385 RTH one-minute bars and 22 prior sessions in `daily_rth_stats`; (c) the tick extraction validated the day: tick RTH volume within 3 percent of the database RTH volume, OR (hr-070: the database RTH volume is inflated about 2.7x on some mid-2025 dates, the ticks are right) the tick RTH high and low equal the database bars' within two ticks; (d) at least 15 of the 20 prior sessions have validated ticks (the live rule). Days failing a rule are DROPPED, never imputed; the count and the reason per rule are printed in phase 1. Windows with fewer than 150 qualifying sessions are called INCONCLUSIVE IN ADVANCE and the sealed look is not spent.
## 3. Counted tests (2): loss = mean squared error of ln(next-hour range / ADR), day-block bootstrap (resample dates, 4,000 draws, seed 20261108), 97.5 percent CI (Bonferroni 2)
- **T1 (primary):** improvement of the model over the HARD baseline (clock + HAR-X + last-30-minute range + range so far) = (loss_hard - loss_model) / loss_hard.
- **T2:** improvement of the model over HAR-X + clock (the registered baseline).
- **LEAD** (the strongest word this window allows; it is not clean, section 5): T1 CI lower bound above 0 AND T1 positive in both chronological halves of the qualifying days. T2 is reported against the original claim (+24.9 percent explore replay).
## 4. Reported, not counted
By decision time; by half-year; mean absolute error in points for model / hard baseline / HAR-X + clock; correlation of prediction and realized; the calibration slope of realized on predicted; the share of days each model wins; ADR (median RTH range) in the sealed era against the explore era; the improvement at ranks of predicted range. Phase 1 prints the DETECTABLE effect (2.8 x SE) scaled from the explore walk-forward paired differences by sqrt(explore days / qualifying days), the counts and the drop reasons, and NO outcome.
## 5. Honest limits, stated now
The window `2025-05-19..2026-10-19` has been spent by other FAMILIES (flip time, tell hold-out and others), so it is not an information-free hold-out for the system as a whole; it IS untouched for THIS model: no feature, hyper-parameter or baseline was chosen on it, and the frozen model above never saw it. ## 7. RESULT (2026-11-08; ONE sealed look spent, ledger id 184; wrapper `scripts/structure_model/sealed_replay.mjs`, output `scratch/sealed_replay_20261108_output.txt`, rows `scratch/structure_model_sealed_replay_20261108/rows_sealed.csv`; claim `structure_model_sealed_replay_20261108`)

The regression check on 40 explore sessions passed first (live-path features equal the research rows to 7e-15; T2 +24.8 percent in-sample).
- **T1 model vs the HARD persistence baseline: +4.33 percent, CI97.5 [-0.13, +8.26]**; halves +4.21 / +4.46. **T2 model vs HAR-X + clock: +26.82 percent [+21.30, +31.83]** (the explore claim was +24.9 percent).

- Reported, not counted: positive against the hard baseline in all four half-years (2025H1 +7.75, 2025H2 +2.19, 2026H1 +7.72, 2026H2 +1.59 percent) and at every decision time (10:30 +9.59, 11:30 +7.61, 13:00 +2.38, 14:30 +0.60 percent: the gain is concentrated early in the day, as persistence terms are least informative then); mean absolute error 37.0 points (model), 37.8 (hard baseline), 43.0 (HAR-X + clock); correlation with realized 0.662 / 0.638 / 0.459; calibration slope 1.076; the model beats the hard baseline on 57.3 percent of days; median ADR 298 points (explore era 208), so the model transfers to a much more volatile regime without refit.
READING: (1) the original headline (+24.9 percent over HAR-X + clock) REPLICATES on 347 unseen sessions (+26.8); (2) the honest edge over plain persistence is about +4 percent, consistent in sign with the explore estimate (+6.5) and in every half-year, but not significant on this window.
