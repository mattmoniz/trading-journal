## 1. Data (causal) and what is held fixed
The same 1,760 explore entries, 500-contract bars, V1 management unchanged (stop, 120-minute no-break exit, tell-fade after the break, 15:55 flatten, stop fills at its price minus a tick, a gapped stop fills at the open). Every arm only ADDS an exit, so it can never hold longer than V1 (the V1 stop stays in force); an arm with no structure ahead or no event follows V1 (coverage reported). Everything used by an arm is known at the time it acts: the overnight session (18:00 previous day to 09:29 ET), the prior RTH session, the RTH minute bars completed so far, and the real 65-point leg detector (`server/services/rotationDetector.js` `detectRotationLegs`, run from 09:30; its state at a minute is the state after that minute is complete). Raw bars come from `scripts/tell_context/research_20261103/export_structure_20261104.mjs` (price_bars_primary NQ, both time bounds).
- **Scale s** = the median range of the 20 completed 500-contract bars before the signal (step 1). **Proximity** = 2 x s ('at' a level).
- **Value area** = the standard 70 percent area expanded from the POC over a profile with each 1-minute bar's volume spread evenly over its high-low range in 1-point bins (hr-021); developing = recomputed from all RTH minutes completed so far.
- **Overnight thin zone** = a maximal run of at least 5 consecutive 1-point bins of the overnight profile, each below the 25th percentile of that night's bin volumes (the percentile is the only parameter, stated now, not tuned).
- **Market levels (the coincidence set for arms 2 and 3):** prior RTH VAL, VAH, POC, high, low; overnight VAL, VAH, POC, high, low; the opening range (09:30-10:00) high, low and mid; the IB high and low once 10:30 has passed (running before); today's developing value area low and high at the moment. Not available from bars in this export and therefore NOT used: the weekly open, the monthly levels, the TPO poor highs and lows (stated limitation: the user named the weekly open as a pivot).

## 2. The four arms (the four counted tests)
- **A1 zone target:** the target is the near edge of the first overnight thin zone ahead of the entry in the trade direction (known at the open); the trade exits at the target by limit (a touch beyond it by 1 tick required, the stop checked first on a bar touching both); if no zone is ahead, follows V1.
- **A2 zone target with a coinciding level:** the same, restricted to zones in which at least one market level lies within the zone or within the proximity of its near edge; the target stays the zone's near edge; otherwise follows V1.
- **A3 flip at structure:** the exit is a market exit at the open of the first 500-contract bar that starts after the first ROTATION FLIP AGAINST the position (a newly confirmed 65-point leg in the opposite direction of the trade) after entry, taken ONLY if at the confirming minute's close price is within the proximity of an overnight thin zone or a market level; a flip elsewhere is ignored and the next one is considered; the first qualifying flip counts.
- **A4 value-area cue:** (for a short; mirrored for a long) after entry, price has traded below the developing value area low, a rotation flip against the position has been confirmed since that first excursion, and a completed minute closes back inside the developing value area: market exit at the open of the next 500-contract bar. The minutes between the first excursion outside value and the exit are the 'time without reward' and are reported, not used as a parameter.
- **Counted outcome (each arm):** paired difference arm minus V1 in CAPTURE = exit points / max(V1 MFE, s), day-block bootstrap CI at 98.75 percent (Bonferroni 0.05 / 4, 4,000 draws, seed 20261104), two-sided. **KEEPS MORE** = capture CI lower bound above 0 AND the net-dollar difference's lower bound above minus (2.8 x its SE) AND both chronological halves positive in capture. Capture up with the net-dollar difference's CI entirely below 0 = **KEEPS MORE, COSTS RUNNERS**. Positive capture with a CI including 0 = LEAD for a forward shadow only. Otherwise NOT SHOWN.
