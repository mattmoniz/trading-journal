# Pre-registration: one joined sweep setup — does a stop with room to breathe help, and does the "force, offers vanish, bids lift" sequence select the winners — 2026-10-21

Written and committed BEFORE the joined replay is built or any outcome computed. Origin: user (2026-10-21): "the levels can all be joined; a reversal sweep and a stop sweep are the same; the stop sweep does not have enough room to breathe as currently built; price drives into a known level with a lot of force, then all the offers disappear, the bids come in and lift it (vice versa when price comes down)." Research only; nothing live changes; the paused/suppressed statuses stay.
This is stage 1 and 2 of three. Stage 3 (order-book depth around the real StopSweep fires, using the Sierra Chart `.depth` files that exist for about 96 sessions 2026-07-10 to 2026-10-13 and about 21 more to 2026-10-20) needs the file format from Sierra's documentation and gets its own pre-registration.

## Stage 1 — joined levels, room to breathe
- **Events:** NQ 1-minute RTH bars 2023-03-06 to 2026-10-20, the same dropped sessions as before. Sweep trigger = the FAILED_SWEEP_REVERSAL form (the looser one, so the user's "same thing" is one rule): some bar in the last 10 traded more than 3 points beyond the level and the close is back on the original side. Levels = the union of both setups' levels, in this priority order: ONL, ONH, PD_LOW, PD_HIGH, IB_LOW, IB_HIGH (from 10:31), PD_POC, PD_VAH, PD_VAL, FLOOR_PIVOT, FLOOR_R1, FLOOR_S1, OR5_HIGH, OR5_LOW (ONL/ONH/PD/IB from bars as the live StopSweep defines them; the rest from `level_prices`). From the 30th RTH bar to 15:55, edge-triggered per level and direction, at most one event per bar, no confluence gate, no extension cap. NEW versus the earlier replays: events are not blocked while another is open (outcomes then depend on the exit, so blocking would change the event set per exit); trades can overlap; the day-blocked bootstrap handles the correlation.
- **Entry** = the event bar close. **Extreme** = lowest low (long) or highest high (short) of the 10 bars.
- **Room unit u:** the median, over the previous 60 sessions, of the high-low range of the 10 bars at the same clock position (data-derived, same frequency as the sweep); at least 40 sessions needed.
- **Exit grid (stop = extreme beyond by b x u):** b in {live 5 points, 0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0}; per b: hold to the 15:58 close with the stop (dollars = points x 2 - 2), and R-race (first to +kR or -1R, k in 1, 2, 3, 5, unresolved marked at the close in R, R = entry to stop). Flipped-direction placebo at every cell (same entry, mirrored distances).
- **Test R1 (room helps):** hold dollars at b = 1.0 minus at the live 5-point stop, paired by event, day-blocked 95 percent interval, explore (to 2025-05-31) and hold-out (2025-06-01 on). ROOM HELPS only if the interval is above zero in both periods AND the surface is non-decreasing in dollars from b = 0 through 1.0 (so the result is a plateau, not one lucky cell) AND real minus flipped at b = 1.0 is above zero in the hold-out. Otherwise NOT SHOWN. The whole surface is printed; no cell is chosen after the fact. (If R1 is not shown, stage 2 runs at b = 1.0 anyway as the pre-declared "room" arm.)

## Stage 2 — the sequence (per-second archive `scratch/persec_px_20261021`, 813 valid sessions)
Features on the same events, point-in-time at the event minute close, using the drive direction (down for a long, up for a short), the extreme second (lowest low or highest high second in the last 600 seconds) and the 15 seconds either side of it only up to the event time:
- **Force F:** aggressor volume IN the drive direction (sell-aggressor for a down drive) in the 60 seconds ending at the extreme second, z-scored against the same clock window over the previous 60 sessions (at least 40 needed).
- **Vanish V:** drive-direction aggressor volume in the (up to) 15 seconds after the extreme second divided by that volume in the 15 seconds up to and including it; small = the sellers (offers hit) disappeared.
- **Lift L:** counter-direction aggression share, (counter minus drive) / (counter plus drive), over the seconds after the extreme second to the event time.
- Cutoffs = explore medians (F and L tight at or above, V tight at or below), frozen for hold-out. Hypotheses: H1 F, H2 V, H3 L, H4 the sequence (all three tight). Outcome: hold dollars at b = 1.0 and the k = 3 R-race at b = 1.0. Rule as in the earlier tests: KEPT (strong) = explore and hold-out intervals of tight minus loose both above zero; KEPT (weak) = explore above zero and hold-out point estimate positive; else NOT SHOWN; MECHANISM SUPPORTED only if H4 (or a kept hypothesis) holds and the tight subset beats its flipped version in the hold-out with the interval above zero, with at least 20 hold-out events on 20 distinct days.
- Descriptive: the share of events tight on the sequence, their mean outcome by level family (the old StopSweep levels vs the pivot/POC/OR levels), and by entry time (10:00-10:30 vs later).

## Counting
Stage 1: 5 contrasts of the surface plus R1 (2 periods) plus the placebo; stage 2: 4 hypotheses x 2 outcomes x 2 periods = 16 intervals, so about one spurious favourable interval is expected; the plateau rule and the placebo are the guards. A cell with fewer than 20 events or 20 days is underpowered.

## Known limits (stated in advance)
Replay, not the live poller (earlier fidelity 13 and 42 percent); prints cannot show resting offers, so "the offers disappear" is only visible as the absence of drive-direction aggression (stage 3 measures the book); events may overlap in time; the unit u scales with typical 10-minute range, so b = 1.0 means very different point distances in quiet and wild sessions (that is the intent); the surface selects nothing for live use.
