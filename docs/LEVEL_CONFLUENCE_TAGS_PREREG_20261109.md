## What is built (outside server/, no restart)

One APPEND-ONLY row per fire, written at first sight and never revised: AMT tell (`amt_tell_trades`, book V1, bar_rule split; the book that orders), every vacuum onset (`vacuum_live_events`, taken or not), every turn-signal ignition (`turn_signal_events`, S2_IGNITION, qualified or not). Fire price = tell entry price / vacuum or turn `entry_open` (else signal close).
Tier-1 levels (frozen): PD_HIGH, PD_LOW, PD_VAH, PD_VAL, PW_HIGH, PW_LOW (`level_prices`, known before the open); ONH, ONL (computed causally from minute bars, only for fires 02:00-17:00 ET, as the capitulation logger); IB_HIGH, IB_LOW (only for fires 10:30-16:00 ET, when the IB is final). A fire whose trade date has no `level_prices` rows yet (a new Globex session) is DEFERRED to the next run, never stored empty.
Stored per fire (continuous, no threshold): nearest level AHEAD in the trade direction (name, price, distance in ADR20, defended = a live confirmed swing pivot of the matching type at that level's price, via `scripts/lib/pivotContext.mjs`); nearest level BEHIND (name, price, distance, minutes since a minute bar closed on its far side within 30 minutes = a recent break of it); nearest level either side with its defended flag; whether the fire price itself sits in a live pivot band (HIGH, LOW). ADR20 is the capitulation logger's (`scripts/lib/levelContext.mjs`, shared).
