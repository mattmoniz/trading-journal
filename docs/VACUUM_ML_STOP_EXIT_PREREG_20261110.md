# ML for the VACUUM's stop distance and exit (pre-registered 2026-11-10 night, BEFORE any model is fit or any outcome of these tests is read)
User (2026-11-10 night): "start testing ML as the way this exits the trade, and ML for the stop distance"; then: "I only want it to study vacuum trades stops and exits ... not every setup we have yet." SCOPE: the live vacuum rule ONLY (ROC10 expansion onset |Z| > 2, direction = sign, entry at the next bar open, 30-bar hold, stop 2.5 x EMA10(TR), no target, one position per track). Not the tell, not any level-fade setup. RESEARCH / SHADOW ONLY: nothing is wired to orders (user has not yet trusted ML for real decisions).
Why the vacuum: about 9,100 explore trades (against 1,750 for the tell), so a model has something to learn from. Why a gate: the row 45 profile showed the vacuum has little give-back to protect, and its explore RTH mean is about -$60 per day at about 14 trades a day with a 2.0-point all-in cost, so a learned stop / exit must turn a negative mean around, not just reshuffle it; the pass race is not the yardstick (the vacuum alone passes with probability about 0.002), the mean per trade and per day is.
DATA: the explore vacuum trades 2023-03-07..2025-05-16 built exactly as `scripts/vacuum_sim/unified_exit_lock.py` (`vacuum_pairs`, parity with `calc_outcome` net_stop25 asserted to its rounding), RTH and Globex as SEPARATE tracks (RTH validated signals can reverse in Globex, hr-039). Sealed (2025-05-17..2026-06-14, spent for the speed family only; other families keep it), the depth era, and the forward window (>= 2026-10-12) are NOT used here. Counted tests: 4 (V1 and V2 on each track), Holm 0.10; each one-sided alpha 0.05; day-blocked bootstrap 3,000 draws; power gate >= 100 test trades on >= 60 days per test.
- ML-V1 STOP DISTANCE: candidate multipliers m in {1.5, 2.0, 2.5 (live), 3.5} x EMA10(TR) at the signal bar; counterfactual net per trade and multiplier by the same simulator (stop effective from the entry bar, gap fills at the open, cost 2.0 pt). Features, ALL known at the signal bar (the entry order is placed after it): |ROC10 Z|, ratio Z, pace percentile of the signal bar, bar-duration acceleration, close location in the bar, 3-bar signed delta in the trade direction, EMA10(TR) / ADR20 (volatility regime), ET hour bin, day of week. Model: LightGBM (depth <= 3, <= 100 trees, learning rate 0.05, min 100 rows per leaf; NO tuning on test folds), one regression per multiplier; policy = the multiplier with the highest predicted net. Walk-forward by calendar quarter (expanding window, at least 1,000 training trades, 1-day embargo); test quarters = every quarter with a training window. COUNTED: mean(policy net - the best CONSTANT multiplier chosen on the same training window) per test trade, H1 > 0. Descriptive: policy vs the live m = 2.5, policy vs each constant, the share of trades where the policy leaves 2.5, and a label-permutation null (50 day-block permutations of the outcome rows) for the selection noise.
- ML-V2 EXIT: at every completed bar j after entry while the trade is open (j < the 30-bar hold end), features from bars up to j only: excursion in stop units, best excursion, give-back share of the best excursion, bars since entry, ROC10 Z now and its change since entry, bar-duration ratio vs the rolling mean, signed delta over 1 and 3 bars in the trade direction, close location, distance to the stop in stop units. Label = (net the baseline would finally realise from here) - (net if exited at the next bar open): what holding earns. Same model family and walk-forward as ML-V1. PREDICTABILITY GATE first: out-of-sample Spearman rank correlation of predicted vs realised hold value per test quarter against a baseline model that sees only (excursion, bars since entry); the gate passes only if the full model beats the baseline by a day-blocked interval above 0. Gate fails -> NO INFORMATION, no policy scored. Gate passes -> sequential policy: exit at the next bar open at the first bar where the predicted hold value <= 0 (a natural zero, no tuned cutoff). COUNTED: mean(policy net - baseline net) per trade, H1 > 0. A policy that raises the win rate but lowers the mean is a FAIL (the lock pattern). The baseline event stream is held fixed (earlier exits do not free the track for extra trades; stated limitation).
- NEXT-STEP TREE: ML-V1 passes -> a shadow arm logging the learned multiplier beside the real stop on every live vacuum fire, judged at a forward floor, no orders; fails -> the 2.5 x EMA10(TR) stop is as good as a learned one on these features. ML-V2 gate fails -> the vacuum's exit has no learnable information in these features; passes and the policy lifts the mean -> a shadow exit arm only. Either way record which feature groups carried any out-of-sample signal (failure autopsy).
- WHAT THIS CANNOT FIX: live execution slippage, regimes the 2023-25 training years did not contain, and the vacuum's cost drag if its raw edge is near zero.
