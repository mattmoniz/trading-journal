## What the code review found before any run (verified in `scripts/backtest_flush_patterns.mjs` and `server/services/rthFlushDetector.js`)
1. **Same mechanism, shared code:** both use `flushMechanics.js` `computeBalanceAndResolution` and the same trigger (first of IB break from 10:30 or ONH/ONL close break from 9:30), structural stop = opposite balance edge, entry = the resolution bar's close.
2. **The backtest target is self-referential (CLAUDE.md "a target set to a statistic of the SAME population being tested is a tautology").** `rthTarget = percentile(rthSetups.map(s => s.mfe), 0.5)` over the whole history, then every setup is scored against it; the building-tier target is the p75 MFE of the building subset, with the median splits also taken from the full sample. The reported WR/EV are therefore in-sample by construction.
3. **Ideal fills:** `simulateExit` exits at `stopPrice`/`targetPrice` exactly (no gap fill), entry at the resolution bar's close (not a tradable price, the bar is already closed), exits at the session close.
4. **The live gate** (`getLiveStatus`) needs real N >= 20 and EV >= -5 per direction; the backtest numbers never feed it.

## Arms (all on the same RTH events, 2023-03-06 .. last complete RTH date, sparse window 2025-09-28..2025-11-19 and NQ roll weeks excluded; counts printed)
- **A, reproduction:** the backtest's own procedure (full-sample p50/p75 MFE targets, ideal fills). Must reproduce the stored N/WR/EV on the same bars (regression check, tolerance: N within 5%, EV within 3 dollars); if not, STOP and report why.
- **B, walk-forward target, ideal fills:** the target for an event is the p50 MFE (or p75 for "building") of PRIOR events only (expanding window, at least 30 prior events; the building medians of avgVolZ and volZTrend also from prior events only). Events before the 30th are dropped from B/C/D and reported.
- **C, walk-forward target, realistic fills (primary):** entry at the OPEN of the bar after the resolution bar; stop fills at `min(stop, open)` (long) / `max(stop, open)` (short) on a gap; target at `max(target, open)` / `min(target, open)` only if the bar opens through it, else at the target; same-bar stop-first; exit at the 15:59 close. Target distance measured from the realistic entry (the walk-forward point target distance is applied from the real entry).
- **D, direction-mirrored placebo of C:** same entries, stop distance and target distance, direction flipped (stop and target mirrored about the entry), same fill rules. A driftless walk with a near target favours the near barrier, so the real edge is C minus D.

## Reporting and rule (frozen)
Per direction and pooled: N, win rate, EV per trade (sum/N), day-blocked 95% bootstrap CI, by calendar year and chronological halves, top-5-day share. Delta `C − D` per trade with a day-blocked delta CI (events paired by date). **Verdict:** SURVIVES only if arm C pooled EV has a CI lower bound above 0 AND `C − D` has a CI lower bound above 0 AND both chronological halves of C are positive. NOT SHOWN otherwise, with what could not be shown. Pooled verdicts are questions: per-direction and per-year results are always printed.
**Live reconciliation** over the common window 2026-07-09..last live date: for every date, does the re-derived event (A procedure) exist, same direction, same entry/stop within 1 point; list every mismatch with its category (missing live row, extra live row, direction differs, price differs, timing differs). Compare the live rows' realized P&L with arm C's simulated P&L for the SAME dates and report both with N.

## Limits
28 live fires cannot judge the setup; the reconciliation is about whether live and backtest are the same strategy, not about live profitability. One year of intraday history of the tick-rebuilt contracts (the first six months NQM23/NQU23 are tick-rebuild-only) is reported by year. The walk-forward drops the first 30 events. The volume baseline (90-bar per-minute rolling) is the backtest's own, not the live `getVolumeBaseline`; any difference between the two is itself a reconciliation finding to report, not to fix here.
