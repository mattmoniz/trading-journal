## What the logger never does
Nothing here places an order or changes any setup's exit (cv-078: a real early close would only ever be a RESOLVED/EXPIRED mark handled by `sweepExits()`, and that is a later, separate decision).

## The overlay (shadow only)
For every `active_setups` row that is OPEN (SHORT, and the mirrored LONG), watched from its fire until its own resolution:
- CAPITULATION SIGNAL (the exit trigger): a ROC10 expansion onset in the SAME direction as the position (a sell thrust for a short), logged by the live vacuum daemon (`vacuum_live_events`, all onsets, taken or not), whose extension E_t is in the top tercile of earlier events of its track. E_t and the tercile are exactly the definitions of `docs/VACUUM_CONTEXT_EXIT_PREREG_20261109.md` (240 trading minutes, ADR20 units, expanding window, at least 30 earlier events; no fixed cutoff, hr-003). The exit price is the NEXT bar's open after the signal (as an order would fill).
- Virtual result: P&L if the row had been closed at that price, minus 2.0 pt cost per the repo cost model (`server/config/instruments.js` ground truth, hr-005), against the row's actual outcome (`actual_pnl`, its own stop/target/timeout). Rows with no signal during their life contribute 0 to the paired difference (the overlay does nothing to them).
- Blackout: signals 18:00-18:30 ET are logged but flagged `reopen` and excluded from the judged population (cv-056).

## Tags written at the row's FIRE (known then, never revised; both logged, neither tuned)
- `at_boundary` (mechanical, Argon): the row's entry is within 0.05 x ADR20 of the prior RTH high, low, VAH or VAL, or of the overnight high or low (after 02:00 ET), and the setup fades back inside the range. Source levels: the repo's own `level_prices`; tolerance is a scale (0.05 ADR20), not a tuned threshold.
- `at_defended_pivot` (the user's definition, 14:05 ET answer): the entry is at a previously DEFENDED level, i.e. a genuine swing pivot where price turned around before ("PW_LOW was not last week's low last week but may be the PW low this week"). Reuse, not reimplement (hr-022): `scripts/lib/pivotContext.mjs` (`pivotTimeline`, `pivotAt`, causal minute-scale pivot timeline), `server/services/majorPivotDefendedBreakDetector.js` (DEFENDED = tested and denied at least once), `minorDefendedLevelDetector.js`, `scripts/lib/fourHourLevels.mjs`. The exact function and its tolerance are fixed at build time from those modules' own parameters and written into the logger header BEFORE the first row is logged; they are not chosen by looking at outcomes.
- Also stored: `bet_class` (VALUE_FADE, GLOBEX_LEVEL, CONTINUATION_LEGACY, ...), `setup_type`, side, `origin_status`, `is_cluster_primary`, track (RTH / Globex).

## Judged population (forward only; hr-006, hr-007)
Rows with `origin_status` ACTIVE or SHADOW (real-forward; never BACKFILL) and `is_cluster_primary = true`, fired after the logger start date. Retro-computing the overlay on BACKFILL or pre-start rows is allowed only as a clearly labelled descriptive look and never counts toward the floor.

## Counted tests (2; Holm, family alpha 0.10, one-sided; day-blocked bootstrap with correlated cluster siblings collapsed first, cv-025)
- C1: among rows with `at_boundary` = true, mean of d = virtual exit P&L - actual P&L over rows that received a signal.
- C2: the same among rows with `at_defended_pivot` = true.
Control strata (descriptive, not counted): the same d among rows NOT at a boundary / pivot (the user's hypothesis is that location decides which positions can be held to a capitulation, so these should be worse or no different); by `bet_class` and by side; by track. Per-setup-type results are descriptive and never gate anything (cv-033: split by the structural variable first).
Floor per test: at least 20 SIGNALLED rows (hr-008) on at least 10 distinct days.

## Judgement (no peeking before the floor; cv-006)
CONFIRM (for a next step, not for a live change): lower 90 percent bound of d > 0 AND the 25 percent trimmed mean > 0 AND the result survives dropping the best day. FAIL: upper 90 percent bound < 0 for both tests at the floor. CONTINUE: otherwise, re-look at 40 signalled rows per test. A FAIL parks the idea (co-025) and records an autopsy; it does not retire it.

## Alarms (not verdicts)
Logger lag (hr-013: fired_at vs wall clock), `vacuum_live_events` heartbeat gap during an open row's life, a signal logged on a row already resolved, any row with `actual_pnl` NULL (cv-069), a tag source returning null for more than 20 percent of fires.

## Known limits (stated before data)
Capitulation episodes are rare (the 68-session real-depth set logged about 21 expansions a session but few are extended); signalled rows may take a quarter or more to reach the floor. The two tags overlap with the system's existing level-fade population, which the user is moving away from (item I); demoting those families to SHADOW does not lose this data (SHADOW rows are judged). Overlapping open rows share one capitulation signal, hence the cluster collapse and day-blocking.
