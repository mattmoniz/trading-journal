# Pre-registration: where do institutions get involved? Pace x vacuum x ROC expansions on 500V bars (candidate Q4-5, the user's ORIGINAL idea)

Written 2026-11-08 BEFORE any script exists and before any outcome of these events is read. Source of the idea: `docs/ORIGINAL_NOTES_500V_LIQUIDITY_VACUUM_20261108.md`, exact indicator settings `docs/USER_SIERRA_INDICATOR_SETTINGS_20261108.md`, formal v0 `docs/ARGON_V0_PRE_AUDIT_SPEC_20261108.md`, audit bug list `docs/ARGON_V0_AUDIT_FINDINGS_CONDENSED_20261108.md`. User directives: thirds are NOT a gate (reported as information only); no idea is discarded on an estimate (co-025); the objective is "to figure out where institutionals are getting involved ... by judging the pace that 500V bars are finishing" (pace is graded, partial involvement counts, 15 s is a reference line not a switch).

## 1. Questions (each answered by its own counted test)
Q-A. Expansion: after a vacuum thrust (Ratio expansion AND ROC10 expansion, same bar), does price continue in the ROC direction beyond a price-only control?
Q-B. Pace (the institutional-involvement hypothesis): WITHIN vacuum thrusts, does faster bar completion (graded) predict a larger continuation? (dose-response, not a gate)
Q-C. Wave 2: after a thrust, a pullback that holds the trend side of the ROC middle band and a re-expansion: does the second expansion continue? (arm M3; FSM built after Q-A/Q-B)

## 2. Data
`scratch/volbars500_full_20261028/*.bars.tsv`, Explore dates through 2025-05-16, 500-contract split bars; the files cover the FULL session (18:00 ET to 17:00 ET), so RTH and Globex are both testable at the user's own 500V (no VB=100). Never the sealed-era directories. Bars: start/end seconds, OHLC, volume, bid/ask aggressor volume, ntrades. Tracks reported separately: RTH 09:35-15:50, Globex 18:30-09:25 (never pooled, hr-002/hr-039: Globex is tested from scratch, not assumed to mirror RTH).

## 3. Definitions (exactly the user's Sierra studies; all causal at bar close)
- ROC10_t = Close_t - Close_{t-10} (Sierra "Rate Of Change - Points", Last, length 10) computed within the session (no gap across the 18:00 reset). Bands: SMA50 +- 2 SD (population SD) of ROC10; Z_ROC_t = (ROC10 - SMA50) / SD50. ROC expansion: |Z_ROC| > 2 in the direction of ROC.
- True Range = Sierra TR (first bar of the session: H-L). EMA10 of TR (alpha 2/11).
- Depth term, two versions, ALWAYS labelled: (D1) PROXY = EMA10 of ntrades x ln(max(dur,0.05)+1) (v0), available on all history; (D2) REAL = EMA10 of the Sierra Bid & Ask Depth Bars Close (mode Sum) rebuilt from the NQ `.depth` files, only since mid-June 2026 and only once the user gives the number of levels (NOT in this first run).
- Ratio_t = EMA10(TR) / EMA10(depth). Bands: SMA50 +- 1.5 SD (population SD) (the screenshot of these bands is pending; notes say 1.5); Z_Ratio. Ratio expansion: Z_Ratio > 1.5 (unsigned).
- Pace (graded): dt_t = end_sec - start_sec of the bar (floor 1e-3 s). P_hour = the bar's percentile rank of dt among bars of the SAME ET clock hour in the prior 20 completed sessions of the same track (lower dt = faster = higher involvement); the user's 15.0 s RTH reference and the Globex rule dt <= 0.65 x SMA50(dt) and <= 120 s are REPORTED as descriptive flags only. Pace buckets for the dose-response: terciles of P_hour (fast / middle / slow), prior-session derived, no static seconds.
- Flow aligned (v0): CLV in the top 40 percent (long) / bottom 40 percent (short) of the prior-200-bar distribution and the 3-bar delta of the same sign.
- Warm-up: the first 60 bars of a session are not scored (50-bar bands need a current-session window); RTH track needs its own 60 bars after 09:30 (the audit's C4: no pre-market bars inside the 09:35 windows); the first 20 qualifying sessions are priors only; burn-in uses the available data from 2023-02-24.

## 4. Event families (signals at bar close t; no overlap: 30-bar per-direction cooldown from t)
E0 MOVING_ONLY (control): |Z_ROC| > 2 only. E1 RATIO_EXP: Z_Ratio > 1.5 only (direction = sign of ROC10 or the bar). E2 THRUST: E0 AND Ratio expansion (the v0 M1 core, without the pace and flow gates). E3 THRUST_FAST: E2 AND pace in the fast tercile. E4 M1_FULL: E2 AND fast tape (the user's rule at the graded definition: fast tercile) AND flow aligned.

## 5. Outcome (read only after this file is frozen)
Direction-signed forward change of the close from bar t to bar min(t+h, last bar at or before 15:55 ET RTH / 09:25 ET Globex), h = 30 bars PRIMARY (10 and 60 descriptive), in points and normalized by the prior-20-session mean session range. Cost context reported, not subtracted: about 2.0 pt all-in per MNQ round trip (1.00 commission + 0.25 x slippage ticks each way).

## 6. Statistics (counted tests; Holm 0.10 across the counted family; day-blocked bootstrap, 3000 draws, seeded)
Counted tests (RTH and Globex are separate tests): T1 E2 minus E0; T2 E4 minus E0; T3 E3 minus E2 (is fast better than all thrusts); T4 pace dose-response inside E2 (fast tercile minus slow tercile); T5 E1 minus E0. = 5 tests x 2 tracks = 10. Each reports N, active days, mean difference with day-blocked 95 percent CI, the raw event mean, and a placebo (random bars matched on session/clock hour and ROC sign). Detectable effect is computed from the real event counts BEFORE any outcome is read (a power note, never a reason to retire). THIRDS: printed as information only, never a gate. THIN_N below 20 events is labelled as such. No result closes the idea: any outcome is recorded as shown / not shown with the next test and a salvage line (co-022, co-025).
