## 2. The question (one, falsifiable)
Is the tell's outcome different when its direction AGREES with the migration of value from the Globex profile to the RTH profile than when it OPPOSES it, beyond what the same alignment does to ordinary moving bars with no tell?

## 3. Definitions (all causal at the fire; every scale is data-derived, none hand-set)
- Population: the 1,760 research V1 tell fires (`scripts/amt_vbars/trades_V1_explore.csv`, RTH 10:00-14:30, 516 evaluable sessions with a 20-session baseline). Sessions are excluded by the same rules `run_trade.py` uses (no baseline, fewer than 30 overnight bars) plus any session in the data-sanity audit's bad-bars list; the excluded count is printed.
- w (scale unit) = median (high - low) of the 500-contract bars of the 20 PRIOR sessions. Profiles use bins of one w-independent tick (0.25) for volume, summed into bins of width w for POC and value area, so the state does not depend on a hand-picked point size.
- G = the Globex profile: bars ending before 09:30 ET, final at 09:30. R_t = the RTH profile from 09:30 through the last completed bar before the fire (the tell's own decision bar, no later bar).
- POC, VAL, VAH of each: the volume POC (highest summed bin; ties to the bin nearest the profile's median) and the standard 70 percent value area grown outward from the POC by the larger neighbour pair (a convention, not tuned).
- PRIMARY STATE M = (POC of R_t minus POC of G) / w, signed. ALIGNED = sign(M) equals the tell direction d; OPPOSED = it differs; M = 0 is excluded and counted.
- Secondary (uncounted) states: SEPARATED_UP (R VAL >= G VAH), SEPARATED_DOWN (R VAH <= G VAL), OVERLAP otherwise; |M| terciles from the explore events' own |M| distribution (features only, no outcome used); distance of the entry to the nearest of G VAH, G VAL, R VAL, R VAH in w units ("at the junction" per the user's example).
- THIN-APPROACH flag T = mean of the day profile's per-bin volume over the price span of the last 10 bars before the fire, divided by the median occupied bin of that profile (today: 11:18 = 0.94, 11:32 = 3.05). THIN = lowest tercile of T over the explore fires (cut from the feature distribution only). Used to STRATIFY, never to select.
- Outcome (primary): signed move in the tell direction from the entry (next bar open plus one tick) to the close of the last bar completed 60 minutes later, in w units. Chosen over V1 net points because the V1 profit sits in the 18-22 percent that break the range (heavy tail, SD about 126 dollars per trade (the CSV measures 126.5; the earlier '170' was wrong)), which would swamp an alignment difference; V1 net points per trade, the break rate and the 30-minute move are printed uncounted.

## 4. Control, test and reading rules
- CONTROL population: decision-grid bars (every 10th completed bar, 10:00-14:30, price moving per the tell's own MOVE_N rule, no tell), direction d = sign of the last 10-bar move, entry and outcome exactly as for the tell, the same state M computed at the same bar. This asks whether ALIGNMENT predicts the next 60 minutes for ANY moving bar (a trend-day or value-migration drift effect), which would be a market fact unrelated to the tell (cv-094 same-side control; cv-026 checklist: the two arms differ only in the hypothesis variable).
- ONE counted primary: difference-in-differences DiD = (mean outcome ALIGNED minus OPPOSED among tell fires) minus (the same among control bars), day-blocked bootstrap CI (collapse same-day arm/re-arm siblings first, cv-025), RTH arm. A second counted test, the Globex arm (hr-002, hr-039): the same construction one step earlier, with the running Globex profile (from 18:00) as R_t and the PRIOR RTH profile as G, events from the causal Globex tell variant (`docs/TELL_PULLBACK_AND_LEVEL_DIRECTION_PREREG_20261102.md` Part C); Globex is tested from scratch, not assumed to mirror RTH. Counted tests = 2, Benjamini-Hochberg q = 0.10, family `tell_profile_state_20261103`.
- Placebo: day-block LABEL permutation (cv-007): reassign each day's state to another day at the same clock hour, 2,000 draws, same pipeline; the observed DiD must sit outside the 95 percent band. Also random-direction placebo for the control population (cv-029).
- Minimums: at least 100 events and 60 dates in each of ALIGNED and OPPOSED (tell arm) or the cell is UNDERPOWERED, not negative. Chronological halves (explore split at the median date) must agree in sign.
- Power, printed before any verdict: the achieved CI half-width of the DiD is compared with the repo's standing cost allowance (commission in the model P&L plus 1 dollar slippage, converted to w units at the median w); if the half-width exceeds that, the verdict is UNINFORMATIVE, not null. The tell's own V1 mean is +1.93 with CI -3.44..+7.62, so a conditioning effect of the size that would matter is expected to be hard to resolve.
- SHOWN needs all of: lower 95 percent bound above 0 on the counted DiD, sign-consistent halves, effect present in at least as many days as the minimum, permutation p below 0.05 after BH. Otherwise NOT SHOWN with the resolution stated (smallest DiD the sample could have seen).
- Never decisive below N = 20 (hr-008). No dollars are claimed from a SHOWN verdict: a trade rule needs its own pre-registered simulation with realistic fills.
