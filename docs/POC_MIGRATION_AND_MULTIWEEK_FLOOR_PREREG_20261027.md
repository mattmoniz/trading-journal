## Honest limits (stated before the run)
The history is spent, so both tests are explore screens; only dates after 2026-10-27 can confirm. The definitions are mine, from the user's words and Sierra's per-bar profile convention (one point of control per bar), not the user's own settings (which profile, which bar size, which lookback are unknown). Chosen, not derived, and frozen with no tuning: the migration window of 3 completed four-hour bars, the percentile cut of 75 for "migrated", the minimum of 3 distinct approaches on 3 distinct trade dates for Test F, and the level-break distance of `0.5 x H4`. A null says these definitions show nothing, not that the user's discretion (which migrations look obvious, which floors matter) has no edge.

## Common frame
NQ, trade-date convention, roll weeks, the sparse window 2025-09-28 to 2025-11-19 and half-days excluded; data pinned to `STUDY_END=2026-10-23` in every script of this thread. H4 = mean four-hour bar range of the 20 prior trade days. Four-hour bars are aligned to the 18:00 ET session open (`getBarProfiles(..., barMinutes=240)` for per-bar profiles; the 1-minute bars of `price_bars_primary` for price). A bar's data are usable only after it has completed. Explore = earlier 60 percent of event dates, hold-out = later 40 percent (a screen). Per-trade means as sum over N, day-blocking only for intervals. Globex and RTH are separate arms: an event belongs to the arm of the session its decision bar ended in (decision bars ending 22:00, 02:00, 06:00, 10:00 = Globex; ending 14:00 = RTH; the 14:00 to 17:00 bar is dropped, entry would fall into the maintenance gap).

## Test E: the point of control migrates with price (hold or engage with the move)
- **Series:** the per-bar point of control of each completed four-hour bar, from real tick volume cells (`getBarProfiles`, 240-minute bars; bars with no cells dropped).
- **Event:** at the completion of a four-hour bar, the net change in point of control over the last 3 completed bars, in H4 units, is at or above the trailing-60-session 75th percentile of that same signed quantity in the same direction (up-migration; down-migration mirrored), AND the bar's close is on the migration side of its own point of control. One event per bar. Decision at the bar's completion; entry at the next 1-minute open; signed forward move in H4 units over the next four-hour bar (240 minutes, primary) and over 720 minutes (sensitivity, the hold question). No stop, target or trade in this screen.
- **Key control (the thing the test is really about):** price momentum itself. For each event, the same-selection-minus-the-signal arm = bars where PRICE moved the same net amount in the same direction over the same 3 bars (matched within one tercile of H4 distance) but the point of control did NOT migrate (below the cut). Migration only matters if it adds to price momentum.
- **Primary statistic (one):** migrated arm minus the matched no-migration arm, mean signed forward 240-minute move in the migration direction, day-blocked bootstrap; the one-sided 97.5 percent lower bound must exceed 0 (counted cells: Globex, RTH; Bonferroni over 2). Secondary (not counted): excess over blind same-clock same-direction entries (30 draws from other dates); mirrored direction; up versus down separately; the 720-minute horizon.
- **NOT SHOWN:** lower bound not above 0 in a counted cell, stated with the resolution reached; an arm under 30 events or 20 dates is UNDERPOWERED, not negative.

## Test F: the Nth approach to a multi-week floor (or top)
- **Level (memory is the new part):** a four-hour pivot low (fresh 6-bar four-hour low, as in `scripts/lib/fourHourLevels.mjs`) is frozen as a floor and LIVES until a four-hour bar closes beyond it by `0.5 x H4`, not for a fixed 12 bars; tops mirrored. The zone band is the level plus or minus `0.25 x` the mean four-hour bar range of the 20 prior days (the earlier test's band).
- **Approach and event:** an approach = a completed four-hour bar whose low reaches the band after at least one bar entirely above it (separate approaches only). The event is the approach that is at least the 3rd, on at least 3 distinct trade dates since the level was born. One event per level per approach number up to the 5th; decision at that approach bar's completion; entry at the next 1-minute open, long for floors and short for tops.
- **Outcome:** signed forward move in H4 units over the next 1,440 minutes (primary) and 4,320 (sensitivity). No stop in this screen.
- **Controls:** blind same-clock same-direction entries on other dates; fresh-extreme births at the same clock (the existing `k1Events` population, no defense history) so that level location is not credited to approach count; the same level's 1st-to-2nd-approach events (an approach-count gradient: does the 4th and 5th do better than the 3rd, descriptive).
- **Primary statistic (one):** EXCESS over fresh-extreme births, mean signed 1,440-minute move, date-blocked 97.5 percent one-sided lower bound above 0 (counted cells: floors, tops).
- **NOT SHOWN:** as above. The earlier four-hour tests found 0 of 4 cells; a null here would mean the multi-week memory did not rescue them, not that the user's case is not real.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-27 15:25 ET, before any event is built) — DeepSeek design review (`reports/deepseek_poc_migration_multiweek_review_2026-10-27_raw.md`), each point checked against the text above
**Accepted and changed**
- **Test E object (1a).** The user reads the composite profile beside the candles, so the primary object is the COMPOSITE point of control, not a single four-hour bar's. Composite = all completed 30-minute cells of the last 5 trade dates plus the current trade date's completed cells (`getPeriodProfile`, all-session; 5 days is a chosen, frozen length). Updated at each completed four-hour bar. The per-bar point of control becomes descriptive only.
- **Control (1b).** A single bar's point of control sits near its own close in a trend, so "migrated" would mean "trended" and the matched arm would be nearly empty. The composite point of control accumulates over days and is not tied to the last close, so both arms exist; the matched arm stays (same net price change over the same window, within one tercile of H4 distance, point of control below the cut). **First look before any outcome:** the distribution of the migration measure, the share of events in the migrated arm, and the matched arm's balance on price change and its size (stop and report if either arm has fewer than 30 events).
- **Window and cut (1c).** Migration = change in composite POC over the last 3 completed four-hour bars, in H4 units, at or above the trailing-60-session 75th percentile of the same signed quantity (the frozen primary). A descriptive sweep over composite length (3, 5, 10 days) and cut (50, 75, 90) is printed and never used to choose the primary.
- **Close-side condition (1d)** dropped.
- **Overlap (1e).** One event per episode: no event within 3 completed bars (12 hours) of the previous one in the same direction; the overlap share and a non-overlapping subset are printed.
- **The user's own case (1b, second fix).** Descriptive stratum, not counted: composite point of control migrating while price is within the floor band.
- **Test F control (2a, 2c).** The counted control becomes events of the SAME level age (bars since birth, within one tercile) that are the 1st or 2nd approach, so approach count is isolated from "the level has survived". Fresh-extreme births become descriptive.
- **Approach separation (2b):** a separating bar must have its low above the band top by at least `0.25 x H4` (chosen, frozen); the gap distribution is printed first.
- **One event per level (2d):** the primary is the 3rd approach only; the 4th and 5th are a descriptive within-level gradient, counted as levels, not events.
- **One effect counted twice (3).** E and F are two zooms of one idea (price turns at a floor and the profile follows). They are pre-registered as ONE family of 4 counted cells (E: Globex, RTH; F: floors, tops), Bonferroni over 4 (one-sided 98.75 percent bound). If both pass, report one corroborated effect, not two.

**Not accepted as written (stated)**
- **Survivorship as a mechanical bias (2a).** DeepSeek says a level that has survived 3 approaches has an upward-biased outcome window. Survival BEFORE the decision is known at decision time, so it does not mechanically bias the forward move; the real problem is comparability with fresh births (different regime and age). I accept the control change for that reason but not the claim of mechanical bias.
- **Session arms (1f).** DeepSeek proposed pooling with a session covariate. Kept as two counted cells defined by the session the DECISION and entry fall in (that is what a trader acts on, and the project's both-windows rule asks for both); the share of each event's migration window that lies in Globex minutes is printed as a descriptive covariate.
- **Dropping fresh-extreme births entirely:** kept as a descriptive reference.

**Power (all guesses, to be replaced by the printed counts):** DeepSeek expects E to have a few hundred migrated bars and a thin matched arm, and F fewer than 30 levels with 3 or more approaches on 3 or more dates; either arm under 30 events or 20 dates is called UNDERPOWERED, not negative. The earlier four-hour tests found 0 of 4 cells and the sequential study 39 floors, which is why F is the one expected to be thin.
