## Definitions (causal)
Population TELL = the V1 simulated signal bars (1,760, 10:00-14:30). CONTROL = decision-grid bars (every 10th index, 10:00-14:30) with price moving and no tell in either direction. At signal bar i the RANGE is the high and low of bars i-9..i (the tell's own 10-bar window, known at i). Window: bars after i whose end is within 15 minutes of bar i's end, before the 15:55 flatten. 
Variant S (sneak): the first bar j in the window whose range trades through the range high + 1 tick (long) or the range low - 1 tick (short); a bar that trades through BOTH is ambiguous (dropped and counted); entry price = max(level, open of j) + slip for a long, min(level, open) - slip for a short, filled intrabar (as a stop order). Variant C (confirmed): the first bar j whose CLOSE is beyond the range high (long) or low (short); entry at the open of bar j+1 plus slip.
Outcome: y_H = direction x (close of the last bar completed within H minutes of the entry bar's end for S, of bar j+1's start for C - the entry price) in points, H = 15, 30, 60, complete windows only; gross (cost 1.25 points on top, slip already included).
