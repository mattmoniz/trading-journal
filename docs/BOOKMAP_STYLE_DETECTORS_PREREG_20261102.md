# Pre-registration: Bookmap-style order-flow detectors from the PUBLIC definitions (written 2026-11-02 BEFORE the script exists or any outcome is read; user: "I would trade it like users with Bookmap" + "Yes use the public definitions")

## 0. What the public material gives (fetched 2026-11-02, hr-027) and what it does not
Bookmap's learning center and its step-by-step guide (`bookmap.com/learning-center`, `bookmap.com/blog/trade-via-bookmap-detailed-step-step-guide`) NAME the concepts: real versus fake liquidity (does a large resting order persist and trade, or vanish as price approaches), absorption and exhaustion, icebergs and large lots, volume bubbles (red = aggressive selling, blue = aggressive buying), order book imbalance, cumulative volume delta, spoof tests / pack hunting, layering, "flip of the book". They give NO entry trigger, stop, target or numeric threshold. So the detectors below are MY operational definitions of the named concepts (every threshold a rolling quantile of earlier sessions, hr-003); they are not Bookmap's rules and the user's own setups, when given, replace or add to them.
Honest prior: low (about 5 percent that any detector is tradable). The aim is a clear read of whether each named concept carries a resolvable, tradable signal on a large sample.

## 1. Stage A (this look): the BAR-LEVEL versions, on all 516 explore sessions, RTH and Globex separately
Data: the 500-contract bars of the explore sessions (`scratch/volbars500_full_20261028`: bid and ask volume per bar, trades per bar, volume at price), all hours; RTH = 10:00-15:30 ET, GLOBEX = 18:30-09:30 ET (the first 30 minutes after the open are excluded, cv-056; hr-002 requires both). 500-contract bars cannot see resting orders, so the concepts that NEED the book (real vs fake liquidity, pulls, refills, large fills against displayed size) are Stage B, not tested here.
Thresholds: for session d every "high" or "low" cutoff is the stated quantile of that feature over the 20 sessions BEFORE d, pooled (all hours of those sessions), never from d itself.
Common definitions at bar i (bars completed at or before i only): ad3 = (ask - bid volume over the last 3 bars) / volume (the aggressive-flow imbalance, the CVD slope); prog3 = close[i] - close[i-3]; lot3 = volume / trades over the last 3 bars (contracts per trade, the large-lot proxy); bar delta share = (ask - bid) / volume of bar i; the push direction p = sign(ad3). Cooldown: after an event of a detector, the next 10 bars cannot start another event of the same detector.
Detectors (each fires for p = +1 and p = -1; each tested on its pre-registered sign):
- D1 ABSORPTION (effort without result): |ad3| >= its q80 AND |prog3| <= its q20 AND bar i is at a 20-bar extreme in the direction p (the high of bar i is the 20-bar high for p = +1, the low the 20-bar low for p = -1). Aggressive flow pushed into an extreme and did not move price. Predicts a PIVOT: y = -p x (move after t0).
- D2 DELTA DIVERGENCE (CVD against price): bar i makes a new 20-bar extreme in direction p AND the aggressive flow over the last 10 bars, p x (ask - bid) / volume, is <= its q20 (price extends while flow does not confirm). Predicts a PIVOT: y = -p x move.
- D3 VOLUME BUBBLE (large aggressive burst): |bar delta share| of bar i >= its q90; p = sign(bar delta). Two-sided: y = p x move (CONTINUATION positive).
- D4 LARGE-LOT PRINTS: lot3 >= its q95 AND the aggressive flow over the same 3 bars has a sign (p = sign(ad3)). Two-sided: y = p x move.
- D5 STOP RUN (sweep then reject): bar i's high is above the maximum high of the previous 60 bars (for p = +1; mirror for p = -1) AND its close is back BELOW that previous maximum AND |bar delta share| >= its q75 in the sweep direction (an aggressive run that failed to hold). Predicts a REVERSAL: y = -p x move.
Outcome: the move from the close of bar i to the close of the last bar completed within H minutes of bar i's end, H in {5, 15, 30}, in POINTS (a point is $2; round-trip cost 1.25 points), only when the window is complete.

## 2. Counted tests, control and rules
- 5 detectors x 3 horizons x 2 segments = 30 counted tests (ledger family `bookmap_style_bar_detectors_20261102`, tests = 30; BH q = 0.10 over the 30).
- Statistic: EXCESS = mean(y | event) - mean(y over ALL bars of the same session segment with the same push direction p, y signed the same way); the sign-matched control removes drift and the "any bar moving that way" effect (the conditional-minus-unconditional lesson of Part A). Session-blocked bootstrap CI (3000 draws; the unit is the session); two-sided circular-shift placebo (1000 shifts of the event flags over the time-ordered bar sequence); same sign in both chronological halves of the 516 sessions; at least 100 events and 60 sessions per cell, else UNDERPOWERED.
- Economic reading printed with every cell: the mean y of the event in points against the 1.25-point cost, and the same by calendar year (era flips are real here, cv-033).
- CANDIDATE = BH survivor AND CI excludes zero AND same sign in both halves AND |excess| > 1.25 points (tradable in principle). Anything statistically visible but below cost is recorded as "visible, not tradable".
- Resolution: with a few thousand events per detector the mean move resolves about +/-2 points at 30 minutes and about +/-0.7 points at 5 minutes, so an effect above cost IS resolvable here, unlike the depth-era samples.
