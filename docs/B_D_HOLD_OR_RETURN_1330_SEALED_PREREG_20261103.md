## 1. Hypothesis and rules (all copied from the explore script, same code: `hold_return_lib.py` classify/rd/mh/boot/perm)
At T = 13:30 ET: FIRST AREA = the 70 percent value area of the profile of bars completed by 12:30 (frozen); MARGIN = median bar range up to 12:30; DEPARTED = a close in (12:30, 13:30] beyond VAH/VAL by at least the margin; HELD = departed and every close in the last 30 minutes before 13:30 beyond the same edge; RETURNED = departed and not held. Target = the FINAL RTH profile is multi-hump (bimodality at or above 0.365, the cut FROZEN from the explore window; strong-B cut 0.61 and strong-D cut 0.09 also frozen from explore).
## 2. Data (built for this test, bars only, no outcome labels read)
500-contract bars + volume-at-price from the NQ tick files for every session after 2025-05-16 up to 2026-10-19 (`extract_volbars_20261028.py --holdout`, directory `scratch/volbars500_holdout_20261103`). VALIDATION CHANGED from the explore build: the database RTH VOLUME is inflated about 2.7 times on some mid-2025 dates (2025-06-10 DB 871,770 vs ticks 317,545 vs a constant ratio; exact match on 2025-12-01 and 2026-06-01), so the volume check cannot be used; instead a date is kept only if the tick RTH high and low equal the database RTH high and low within 2 points (`scratch/db_rth_hilo_holdout_20261103.json` from `scripts/tick_microstructure/db_rth_hilo_holdout_20261103.mjs`); prices matched exactly on all 4 dates tested, and a wrong contract would be off by the roll basis. ELIGIBLE sessions: at least 100 RTH bars and the last RTH bar completing at or after 15:00 ET (drops early closes, where 13:30 is after the close); the counts of extracted, price-mismatched and excluded dates are printed in phase 1.
## 3. Primary test (one) and the SHOWN rule
Among sessions DEPARTED at 13:30: risk difference P(final multi-hump | HELD) minus P(... | RETURNED). CONFIRMED requires ALL of: (1) difference >= 0.15; (2) week-block bootstrap 95 percent CI (2000 draws) excludes 0; (3) within-month permutation p < 0.05; (4) the Mantel-Haenszel difference stratified by the developing multi-hump flag at 13:30 x tercile of the excursion (furthest close beyond the frozen edge, in value-area widths) >= +0.05 with a CI excluding 0; (5) the sign is positive on both departure sides (UP and DOWN). Otherwise NOT CONFIRMED, reported as exactly which condition failed. Phase 1 prints, before any target is read: eligible sessions, HELD / RETURNED / STAYED counts, and the detectable difference (about 2.8 x sqrt(p(1-p)(1/nH + 1/nR)) at p = 0.34); if it exceeds 0.25 the run ends INCONCLUSIVE IN ADVANCE and the sealed look is NOT spent.
## 4. Secondary (counted in the ledger, tests = 4 with the primary; cannot change the verdict)
(a) the same difference at 12:30, (b) at 11:30, (c) whether the difference grows through the clock (13:30 > 12:30 > 11:30), (d) share HELD among strong-B vs strong-D days. They are printed so the user can see the time pattern; a CONFIRMED primary does not make them findings.

## 6. What a result would and would not mean
CONFIRMED = on 340-odd unseen sessions, an afternoon departure that holds outside the earlier value area predicts a two-hump day beyond what the developing profile and the size of the excursion already say; it is a description of a late-day shape, readable only from about 13:30, and says nothing about trading value. NOT CONFIRMED = the explore 13:30 result was a selected look. Either way the window is spent for this family; a later re-test needs the forward window (live days from 2026-11-04) or a new family with a written user decision.
