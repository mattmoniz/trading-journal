## 1. Origin (user, 2026-11-03)
"A large backtest to see how the preceding overnight played out and what came out as the shape of the following RTH day." The overnight session is complete and fixed at 09:30 ET, so anything it predicts is a causal pre-open read. Related work and priors: the open versus the overnight range or the prior value area showed NO directional edge for the market itself (`BAR_CONTEXT_STUDIES_PREREG_A_20261026`), so the prior for DIRECTION is low; shape and range are different (volatility clusters), prior moderate; the day's shape base rates are strong (D 69 percent, P 18, b 12 by the value-area-tails rule; multi-hump 34 percent), so a signal has to beat them, not merely be non-zero. A developing second-area score from volume, time (TPO) or both found no early information for B at 11:00-13:00 (AUC 0.44-0.56, ledger look 116), so B is an OUTCOME here, not a predictor.
## 2. Overnight features (all known at 09:30; one value per session; scales data-derived from the PRIOR 20 sessions, never the current day)
- Range of the session 18:00-09:29 divided by the median RTH range of the prior 20 sessions; net move (09:29 close minus 18:00 open) in the same units; the maximum drive (largest 60-minute excursion) in the same units.
- Shape of the Globex profile by the user's method (value-area midpoint in thirds = tails and POC: D / P / b) and its multi-hump flag (bimodality in the explore top third, `scripts/tell_context/shape_cuts.json`); the TIME-at-price (TPO, 30-minute brackets) POC position and its spread from the volume POC (time and volume agree or not).
- Timing: the block in which the overnight high and low were set (18:00-00:00, 00:00-03:00, 03:00-08:00, 08:00-09:30 ET); whether the overnight high and low were both set in the first or the second half of the session; ordering of the two (which first).
- Location of the 09:30 open: against the overnight range (above, inside, below), against the prior RTH value area (above VAH, inside, below VAL), the gap from the prior close in range units.
- Overnight volume (contracts) relative to the median of the prior 20 overnight sessions.
- ROTATION SIZES AND ROTATION NUMBERS (user, 2026-11-03; the chart shows 'Avg Rotation' about 95.7 and 'Avg Daily Range' 419.50): the NUMBER of rotations in the overnight session and their SIZES (mean, median, largest), each divided by the prior 20 sessions' average rotation so the scale is the system's own. DEFINITION (user, 2026-11-03: 'I'm using 65 pt rotations but it might be different in Globex'): the repo's canonical `detectRotationLegs(bars, R)` in `server/services/rotationDetector.js` (reversal confirmed when price moves R points off the running extreme, real bar highs and lows; the same R=65 that gates POC_ROTATION_JOIN), reused, never re-implemented (hr-022). RTH: R = 65. Overnight: R_ON = 65 x (median overnight 15-minute range / median RTH 15-minute range of the PRIOR 20 sessions) so the size is data-derived and causal; R = 65 unscaled is also reported. Calibration against the chart (2026-11-03, last 20 RTH days, 1-minute bars): the leg size to the confirmation point averages 76.1 points (17.5 rotations a day), the pivot-to-pivot swing 122.3; the chart's Avg Rotation 95.7 is between the two; the user said to IGNORE it (2026-11-03), so the repo's detector at R=65 is the definition; the chart's Avg Daily Range 419.5 matches the mean RTH day range of the last 20 days (418.8). Overnight at R=65 the last 20 nights give 12.3 rotations a night (mean leg 72.1, pivot swing 129.7) vs 17.5 in RTH; at R=45 27.6 a night (mean leg 52.3). Features: rotation COUNT, mean, median and largest leg (leg = the detector's size) and the mean pivot-to-pivot swing, each divided by the prior 20 sessions' value of the same quantity. (Earlier candidates not used: a bracket rotation = the high-low range of each 30-minute bracket, last 20 RTH days mean 100.7 vs the chart's 95.7.) The same two rotation features are computed for the RTH first hour as a secondary (uncounted) outcome of how the day began.
- Baselines (no information about the overnight): the prior session's RTH letter, the day of week, the prior day's range ratio.
## 3. Outcomes (RTH 09:30-16:00 of the same session; hindsight, they are the labels)
Primary: the final RTH letter (D / P / b). Secondary, uncounted and printed: the multi-hump flag (B); RTH range in range units; the signed net move (open to close) and its sign; whether the overnight range was broken and when (the tell's own gate boundary); the watcher state at 11:00 (drive / building / balance, `scripts/tell_context/watcher.py`).
## 4. Test (ONE counted primary)
Does the set of overnight features improve the out-of-sample multinomial log-loss for the RTH letter over the baselines? Expanding-window walk-forward over the sessions (first 120 sessions warm-up, excluded; the cut points of any tercile come only from sessions strictly before the day), a regularised multinomial logistic model as the only model (no trees in the first pass), baseline model = prior letter + day of week + prior range ratio, candidate = baseline + the overnight features. Statistic: the mean per-session log-loss improvement of the candidate over the baseline, day-block bootstrap CI (week blocks, sessions in a week are correlated), 2,000 draws. Control: the same pipeline with the overnight feature rows permuted across days within the same month (a label-permutation placebo, cv-007). Resolution stated before any outcome: the script prints the smallest improvement the sample could have detected; UNINFORMATIVE (not null) if that exceeds one tenth of the baseline log-loss.
## 5. Reading rules (fixed in advance)
SHOWN needs all of: the lower 95 percent bound of the improvement above 0, the same sign in both chronological halves, the effect present in at least half of the calendar quarters, and the permutation placebo outside its 95 percent band. Otherwise NOT SHOWN with the resolution. Secondary outcomes are uncounted, non-citable (hypotheses for the pre-registration of a second test), each logged in `research_looks` first. Per-feature importance is printed but never used to select a feature after seeing results. N below 20 per cell is never called a result (hr-008).
## 6. Data hazards to handle, not ignore
NQ roll weeks and the contract basis (one contract per session from the bar files); NQ bar gaps in 2024-2025 and sessions flagged by the data-sanity audit (excluded, count printed); thin early-overnight bars; holidays and half sessions (excluded, count printed); the prior session's value area is rebuilt from the same bar files (bar-derived tables go stale after repairs, hr-054, so nothing here reads `level_prices`).
## 7. What a result would mean
A SHOWN result gives the watcher and the overlay a pre-open prior (an expected shape before the first bar), nothing more: it does not trade, size or gate anything and any use needs its own pre-registered test and the user's go. A NOT SHOWN or UNINFORMATIVE result is reported with what could not be resolved, not as proof that the overnight is irrelevant.
