## 1. What is held fixed (the entry-price-only control is built in: hr-042, cv-026)
The same 1,760 explore entries of `scripts/amt_vbars/trades_V1_explore.csv`, the same entry price and bar, and V1 management unchanged (120-minute no-break exit, tell-fade exit after the break, 15:55 flatten, stop fills at its price minus 1 tick, a stop already beyond the market fills at the open). ## 2. The arms (each re-simulated with the repo's own `shadow._run`, hr-022; the stop is fixed at entry from information known at the signal bar)
- **K3**: the lowest-volume bin behind the THIRD left-behind node (nodes nearest-first from price, the V1 definition of "behind node k"), one tick beyond it. If fewer than 3 nodes were left behind the arm uses the V1 stop (the share with a defined arm is printed).
- **K4**: the same behind the FOURTH node; V1 stop where fewer than 4 nodes.
- **EXT**: one tick beyond the RTH extreme so far (09:30 up to the signal bar: the running high for a short, the running low for a long), but never closer than the V1 stop (the farther of the two). This is the "above the IB high / day high" stop; the IB itself is only formed at 10:30, so the running RTH extreme is the causal version.
- **SIE**: the V1 rule (LVN behind the second node) on a profile aggregated into FOUR-tick (1 point) bins before the node search, so a thin bump counts the way it does on the user's Sierra chart. One bin size, stated, not tuned (a single sensitivity of the node definition, not a sweep). V1 stop where fewer than 2 coarse nodes or where the stop would fall on the wrong side of the entry.

## 3. Counted tests (4) and the reading rule (fixed now)
For each arm: the paired difference (arm net minus V1 net, same entry) in mean dollars per trade, with a day-block bootstrap CI (resampling trade dates, 4,000 draws, seed 20261104). The family has 4 counted tests, so the CI used for the verdict is 98.75 percent (Bonferroni, 0.05 / 4); the 95 percent CI is printed as well.
- **PAYS** = the lower bound of the 98.75 percent CI above 0 AND both chronological halves of the dates positive AND the arm's mean above V1's in each of the three chronological thirds' sign majority (at least 2 of 3). Anything else is NOT SHOWN; an arm whose paired mean is positive with a CI that includes 0 is recorded as a LEAD for the forward shadow arm only, never as evidence.
- **Detectable effect first**: the day-blocked standard error of each paired difference is printed before the verdict; the smallest effect the data could show is 2.8 times that standard error. A difference below it is "not shown" in the sense of "could not have been shown", and the report says so.
- **Not counted, descriptive only**: share of entries whose stop changed; median and 90th percentile stop distance and dollar risk per arm (the one-contract risk, relevant to the daily loss limit); win rate, average win, average loss, worst loss, exit mix; the local-maximum check for the node arms (each arm's net against the same arm at 0.75 and 1.25 times its own distance: a node effect only if the arm beats both neighbours, as in the ladder test); splits by final day shape (hindsight, strong D, strong B, letter D, P, b, multi-hump) and by entry hour (10:00 to 10:59 vs later), because the user's case is the first hour.

## 4. What this cannot show (stated before the run)
Explore window only (RTH 10:00 to 14:30 decisions); Globex is untested (hr-002: a recorded gap, the tell has no Globex data in this CSV). Wider stops change the payoff shape (fewer stop-outs, larger losses) as well as the placement; the distance sweep of the ladder test already showed means of +0.30 to +2.88 per trade, all CIs including 0, so the node placement as such is unlikely to matter beyond distance; the arms here test the user's specific placements, not distance in general. 2026-11-04's four fires are an observation (forward data), not part of this window. A positive result is a candidate for a forward shadow book scored on live fires (floor 150 trades and 60 dates like the tell), never a change to the live stop.

- **Reported with any verdict:** win rate, average win and loss, worst loss, median and 90th percentile one-contract stop dollars (the daily-loss limit applies before any live use), by label and by entry hour (first hour vs later).
