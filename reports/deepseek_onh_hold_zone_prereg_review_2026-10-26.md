# Design review — ONH-HOLD + ZONE-DEFENDED prereg (2026-10-26)

1. **ZONE-DEFENDED is defined by the day's own low (lookahead).** L = the RTH low of d, and "approaches" are any prior 90-day day whose full-session low sits within 0.15×D20 of L. The zone's location is therefore only knowable after day d closes; a trader's pre-drawn multi-week zone does not move to match today's low. The "4th approach" is circular — any day whose low lands near prior lows qualifies. Fix: fix the zone ex ante from data before d only (the cluster/mode of prior 90-day lows, or the level in force before d), and count approaches against that fixed level; never use d's own low.

2. **Entry near the high → up days by construction, and the control does not remove it.** ONH-HELD requires the 15:55 close above the overnight high with no break below ONH−0.02×D20 all day, so d is already a strong up day and the 15:56 entry sits at the high. (The 15:55→15:56 timing is causal; the problem is selection on d's own path, not a time leak.) The weekday-matched control subtracts only unconditional same-weekday drift, not the day's realized up-ness, so EXCESS is contaminated by continuation of an already-up day rather than the zone/hold picture. Fix: match on d's return-to-15:55 and distance from the high, or use ONH-HELD-only days as the baseline.

3. **Wrong control for "the picture."** The pre-registered primary comparison is against the unconditional weekday mean; the decisive test — does the zone add to the hold — is relegated to a "no verdict" descriptive aside. Fix: make CONJUNCTION vs ONH-HELD-only (and vs ZONE-only) the primary, pre-registered contrasts with their own date-blocked CIs.

4. **Overlapping consecutive days.** The 24h exit window makes consecutive events share outcomes (Monday's exit is Tuesday's close; a Friday event spans the weekend), inflating effective n and breaking the date-blocked bootstrap. The overlap count is printed but never corrected. Fix: require ≥1 clear day between events (non-overlapping sample) or cluster the bootstrap on the outcome horizon; report effective n.

5. **Rare conjunction / relaxation is data-dependent peeking.** Reaching n=30 by dropping to ≥2 approaches then widening the band — even "from counts only" — is post-hoc tuning on the full sample and changes the hypothesis. Fix: freeze definitions before any count; pre-commit to a minimum detectable effect (MDE) instead of n=30; leave <30 cells UNDERPOWERED without relaxation.

6. **Multiplicity across the 6th study, sensitivities, and forward looks.** Bonferroni-over-3 covers only the three cells, not the six studies on this history, the band/count sensitivities, the relaxation ladder, or the 12-15 look. Fix: report a study-wide (and history-wide) adjusted threshold, or demote everything except the single pre-registered forward look to exploratory.

7. **Approach "held" and the 3M_POC extra.** The ≥0.5×D20 three-day bounce is a realized-outcome filter on approaches (fine only if fully before d), and the stored 3M_POC (29,512.75 vs the ~29,800 line) is known-wrong yet drives a descriptive outcome. Fix: verify or drop the 3M_POC level; document the bounce rule's in-sample nature.
