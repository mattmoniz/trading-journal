# Trade test of the 500-volume-bar tell: enter before the break, stop two nodes back, hold the extension (frozen 2026-10-28)

Committed BEFORE any trade outcome is computed. Follows `PREREG.md` + Amendment 1 (the signal screen: TELL5 breaks the overnight/IB range 20.4 percent of the time vs 14.4 percent for price moving alone, counted primary narrowly missed). User, 2026-10-28: "you were already in the trade prior to the breakout and then we can capitalize on the tell for extension", "put the stop two nodes back. Preferably on the second 'lvn' preceding that first node." The stop rule is the user's (my reading of it below); the entry, exits, constants and money rules are mine. EXPLORE only (536 sessions to 2025-05-16 with clean bars, the same ones used for the screen, so every number is a hypothesis); the 340 hold-out sessions are NOT extracted and stay sealed; a lead earns one frozen look only with the user's approval.

## Frozen inputs from the signal screen (not retuned)
Tell components, nodes, ladder, flow definitions exactly as in `PREREG.md` and Amendment 1, with the cutoffs it printed: Q cutoff 0.444, R cutoff 4 (reversals), ACTIVITY A >= 1, STRIDES (20/40 bars), ladder L >= 2; ATR20 and the hourly seconds-per-bar baseline from the 20 prior sessions. TELL5 = all five components.

## Trade rules
- **Evaluation:** at every completed bar from the first RTH bar ending at or after 10:00 through bars ending at or before 14:30 ET (the screen thinned to every 10th bar; the trade test checks every bar), while flat. Direction d is the one whose TELL5 holds and whose price is still inside the combined overnight/IB range (no completed RTH bar has closed beyond the range extreme in d). If both directions qualify on one bar, skip it. One position at a time. After an exit the same direction is re-armed only after at least one completed bar where TELL5 is false.
- **Entry:** market at the open of the next bar (+1 tick adverse).
- **Stop (the user's rule):** nodes left behind (profile of the day so far, at the decision bar) sorted nearest-first from price: n1 (the first node), n2, n3... LVN behind node k = the lowest-volume occupied price bin strictly between node k's far edge and node k+1's near edge (first minimum nearest the node on ties). **The second LVN preceding the first node = the LVN behind n2** (two nodes back; the LVN behind n1 is the first). If a node k+1 does not exist, the LVN behind node k is the lowest-volume occupied bin between node k's far edge and the day's extreme occupied bin in that direction, excluding the extreme bin itself; if node n2 does not exist at all (fewer than two nodes left behind), the trade is skipped (counted in a funnel). The stop sits 1 tick beyond that bin's price (long: bin price - 0.25). Stop distance is printed before any outcome; there is NO risk cap in the primary (the user gave none), trades per risk bucket are reported.
- **Management (primary V1):** (1) the stop; (2) before a break: exit at market 120 minutes after entry if still no break (chosen: twice the 60-minute screen horizon); (3) a BREAK = a completed bar closes beyond the combined-range extreme in d; after the break, hold while the tell is alive and exit at the open of the bar after the first completed bar where the number of tell components met falls to 2 or fewer (the tell has faded); (4) flatten at the 15:55 open. A stop that takes effect when the market is already beyond it fills at that bar's open; the stop fills at its price minus 1 tick; stop checked before any other exit on the same bar; market exits pay 1 tick; the entry bar's own range can stop the trade.
- **Money:** 1 MNQ, $2 per point, $2 round-trip commission.
- **Variants (reported, not the primary):** V2 = same but the stop at the FIRST LVN (behind n1); V3 = stop as V1 but after the break hold to the 15:55 flatten with no tell-fade exit (isolates whether using the tell for the extension helps).

## Controls (frozen)
- **Random-entry placebo:** for each real V1 trade a random bar in the same session inside the same evaluation window and a random direction, market entry next open (+1 tick), the SAME stop distance as that trade, a 120-minute time exit, flatten 15:55, no tell-fade exit (the placebo cannot read the tell); 100 trials. The real trades are also re-simulated on this stripped geometry from the same entry bar (like-for-like) and compared to the placebo p5/p50/p95.
- **Moving-only control:** entries at bars where price is moving (10-bar) in d but TELL5 is false and the price is inside the range, one position at a time, the stop distance drawn at random from the tell trades' own stop distances, same stripped geometry; mean per trade vs the tell's stripped result. If the tell does not beat plain momentum entries on this geometry, the nodes and flow add nothing as an entry.
- **Funnel printed FIRST:** tell fires, skipped for fewer than two nodes, trades, share using the no-next-node fallback, stop distance quantiles (points and dollars), trades per session.

## Reported
Trades, win rate, profit factor (after friction), mean per trade in dollars and points, max drawdown, average winner/loser, day-blocked 95 percent CI of mean dollars (3000, seed 20261028), exit mix (stop / no-break time-out / tell-fade / EOD), share of trades that broke the range and their mean result vs the rest, results by direction and by stop-distance tercile (descriptive).

## Reading (frozen)
A lead only if V1 mean per trade after friction has a day-blocked CI lower bound > 0 AND beats the random-entry placebo p95 on the like-for-like stripped geometry AND the tell's stripped result beats the moving-only control. Otherwise "not shown" (never "no effect"). Under 150 trades = underpowered. V2/V3 are reported but cannot make a lead.
