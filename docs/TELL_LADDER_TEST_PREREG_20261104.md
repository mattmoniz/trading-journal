## 1. Stop-placement sweep (does the NODE location matter, or only the distance?)
For each trade with node-stop distance s (points) the V1 management is re-run unchanged with the stop at m x s from the entry, m in {0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0} (m = 1 must reproduce the file's net dollars exactly: parity). Mean dollars per trade (sum / N), win rate, exit mix, day-block 95 percent CI; paired difference of each m against m = 1. READING: the ladder stop is 'special' only if m = 1 is a LOCAL MAXIMUM: mean(1.0) above both neighbours 0.75 and 1.25 with the paired CI of at least one of the two differences excluding 0 (tests T1, T2); otherwise the result is a dose-response in distance, not a node effect. Counted: T1 (1.0 vs 0.75), T2 (1.0 vs 1.25).
## 2. Ladder length (the signal's ladder component, L >= 2 nodes left behind)
Mean dollars per trade by L = 2, 3, 4 or more (from `core.decision_details`), day-block CIs; counted T3: L >= 3 minus L = 2 paired by day blocks (difference of means with a day-block CI).
## 3. Node TARGET (does a target at the next node AHEAD help?)
Target = the near edge of the nearest node AHEAD of the entry in the trade direction in the day's developing profile (limit fill at the target, a touch beyond it by 1 tick required; the stop is checked first on a bar that touches both; otherwise the V1 exit). The funnel is printed first (share of entries with a node ahead, median target distance vs the stop distance). Counted T4: mean paired difference (target arm minus V1 net) on the entries that HAVE a node ahead, day-block CI. If fewer than 150 entries have a node ahead the test is UNDERPOWERED and not scored.
## 4. By day shape (descriptive and ONE counted contrast)
All of the above split by final shape (strong D vs strong B, letter D / P / b, multi-hump); counted T5: the m = 2.0 minus m = 1.0 paired difference on strong-D days vs on the other days (does a wider stop pay on balance days where the rotation is long, the user's idea), difference of the two paired means with a day-block CI.
## 5. Limits
Descriptive; one window with many looks (a lead needs its own test on the hold-out bars); the sweep changes distance and node placement together, which is why 'local maximum' is the criterion; the day shape is read at the close; node-ahead targets exist only inside the day's developing profile (the extension past the range has no profile), so T4 may be underpowered by design.
