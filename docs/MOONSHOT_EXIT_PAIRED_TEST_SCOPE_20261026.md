## Step 2 (the thing to be built and run): paired exit test
Population: our own long setup fires (`active_setups`, primary rows, wide-stop >= 70 points, ~3,337 evaluated) where the fire-time picture held (DROP: previous-48h high minus prior close in the top explore tercile of D20 units; FLOOR: the 18h low is >= 3 hours old and has been retested once after price left it by 2 A15): 177 rows = 80 distinct floors; one trade per floor (the first) so one position at a time. For each, two exits from the SAME entry price and time: (A) the setup's own recorded exit (`actual_pnl`); (B) the moonshot exit: protective stop 0.5 x D20 below entry (about 180 points, about $360), NO target, time exit at the close 1,440 minutes later, stop gap-through fills at the open, 1.5 points cost per trade. Primary = (B - A) in the picture minus (B - A) for all other fires, stratified by setup type, date-blocked CI; plus B absolute net P&L with CI; explore/hold-out screen; >= 20 distinct dates; 6 sensitivity cells printed (stops 0.25/1.0 D20, holds 240/2,880 minutes) with no verdict. Control for "a long hold always beats tight exits / NQ drifts up": the same B - A on every other fire.
