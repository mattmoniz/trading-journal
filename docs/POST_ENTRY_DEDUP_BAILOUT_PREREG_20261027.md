## Trade set and split (identical to the t-1 study)
`scripts/lib/vbpTradeSet.mjs` (selection code copied from the t-1 study): 5,324 BACKFILL RTH level fades (a strategy SIMULATION, not an account record), 336 dates, 2023-11-16 to 2026-07-21. Train = earliest 60 percent of dates (through 2026-01-02), Hold-Out = the later 40 percent. The set reproduced 5,324 / 336 / 2026-01-02 exactly. Bucket A = Net_PnL > 0; B = STOP_HIT with MFE >= 0.5 x stop distance; C = STOP_HIT with MFE < 0.5 x stop distance; time-expired $0 trades are outside A/B/C (kept in totals). Stored MFE (`mfe_points`) is the realized MFE to the trade's exit.

## Accounting facts established by the outcome-free integrity probe
Stored `actual_pnl` equals exactly `2 x (exit - entry) x dir - 1` for every stop and target exit (residual 0 on all 4,852): the baseline fee is **$1 per trade, no extra slippage**, and every exit fills at the exact stop or target price. All 1,669 stop and 3,183 target resolution bars contain the stop or target price. `t_exit` = the stored resolution minute; the 242 time-expired rows without one use `expires_at`; every `t_exit` is capped at the session's last RTH bar (15:59). Of the trades, 73 exit on the entry bar and 1,201 within 1-5 bars (those are locked in for the bar-k tests).

## Global rules (as specified) and implementation choices
1. Sort: `(session_date, entry_bar_timestamp, |entry_price - level_price| ASC, setup_id ASC)`; `t_entry` = the fired minute bar. State rules use a prior trade's outcome only for trades with `t_entry_new > t_exit_prior`.
2. Post-entry excursions use completed bars `[t_entry+1 .. t_entry+k]` only. A trade is eligible for a bar-k modification only if `t_exit > t_entry + k` and `t_entry + k` is within the session.
3. Modified exits and flips pay the same $1 as the baseline: a bailout exit at `Close[t_entry+k]` pays `dir x (close - entry) x 2 - 1`; a flip pays `dir_flip x (exit - entry) x 2 - 1`.
4. Max drawdown is the TRUE realized curve: trades ordered by `(t_exit, sort-key order)`, cumulative P&L, peak to trough.

## Part 1 — deduplication
Mode 1: within a (session_date, Trade_Dir), a trade is vetoed if `t_entry <= t_exit` of a currently ACCEPTED same-direction trade (only accepted trades make the book). Mode 2: a trade is vetoed if any ACCEPTED same-direction trade of that day has `t_entry` within the previous 5 minutes (`t_entry_new - t_entry_prev <= 5`) and `|level_new - level_prev| <= 0.5 x ATR_14` of the new trade (the session ATR at t-1 from the earlier study's feature code). Mode 0 raw. Dedup is deterministic; it is evaluated by drawdown and by whether the removed trades are below the all-trade average, with a random-removal placebo (same count, 2,000 draws) for both. **Mode 1 is the book for Parts 2-4; every later table is also run on Mode 0 and printed in a second block.**

## Part 2 — realized-stop circuit breaker
Walk accepted trades in sort order. A Realized Steamroll Event = an accepted trade in direction d that is a Bucket C loss with `t_exit` known. A new trade in direction d on the same date is vetoed if some such event has `t_exit < t_entry_new <= t_exit + W`. A vetoed trade is not accepted and cannot create an event. Sweep W in {15, 30, 45, 60, 390} on Train; `W*` = the W with the highest Train remaining-book net P&L; frozen and applied to Hold-Out. Table 2 prints every W on both splits (Hold-Out for W != W* is descriptive only). Placebo: 2,000 random same-count subsets of the split's book, 5th-95th percentile of their mean P&L. Intervals: date-blocked bootstrap (3,000 draws).
**Pass rule (fixed now):** on Hold-Out at `W*`, vetoed mean minus book mean is negative with the one-sided 97.5 percent date-blocked upper bound below 0, AND the vetoed mean is below the placebo 5th percentile, AND the remaining book's net P&L is above the book's.

## Part 3 — absorption bailout
Eligibility, `MFE_k` and `Unrealized_R_k` exactly as specified (R in units of the stop distance). Table 3A at k = 1, 2, 3, 5 for open trades: quartiles of both by eventual bucket A vs C, the conditional win rate and mean eventual P&L for `MFE_k < 0.15 and Unrealized_R_k < -0.20`, beside the same statistics for all open trades. Rule: exit at `Close[t_entry+k]` if `MFE_k <= X` and `Unrealized_R_k <= -Y`. Grid k in {2,3,4} x X in {.05,.10,.15,.25} x Y in {.10,.20,.35,.50} (48 cells) on Train; objective = sum over modified trades of (bailout P&L - original P&L); freeze the best cell; evaluate on Hold-Out. Placebos: (a) random bailout of the same count among the split's trades open at `k*`, 2,000 draws; (b) a selection null on Train: for every grid cell draw a random same-size set from that cell's `k` eligible-open trades, take the maximum improvement over the 48 cells, 500 repetitions, to say how large the best-of-48 improvement is by chance. The dedup book is kept as accepted under the original exits (an earlier exit would free a slot and could admit a vetoed trade; not recomputed, disclosed).
**Pass rule:** Hold-Out net improvement per modified trade has its one-sided 97.5 percent date-blocked lower bound above 0, AND the improvement exceeds the random-bailout placebo's 95th percentile, AND the Train maximum exceeds the 95th percentile of the selection null.

## Part 4 — post-entry flip
Variant A: trades that trigger the frozen Part 3 bailout are flipped at `Close[t_entry+k*]` with stop = the original stop distance and targets 1.0R and 1.5R, time exit at the 15:59 close. Variant B: an accepted Bucket C stop-out flips at the original `Stop_Price` at `t_exit` (same stop distance, targets 1.0R and 1.5R); a supplementary row flips all stop-outs (B and C). Fills: a stop gapped through fills at that bar's open; stop before target within a bar; the flip path starts at the bar after the entry bar (A) or after `t_exit` (B), and the entry bar's extreme is checked against the flip's STOP only (conservative; its target is never credited). Placebo: the same count of random trades drawn within the same entry-hour strata (09, 10, 11-12, 13-15), flipped by the same mechanism (A: random trades open at `k*`, bailed and flipped; B: random STOP_HIT trades, B and C pooled), 1,000 draws, 5th-95th percentile of the draw means. Combined book = the book after the part's modifications (A: bailed trades exit early; B: unmodified) plus the flips, realized-curve order.
**Pass rule:** Hold-Out flip mean P&L has its one-sided 97.5 percent lower bound above 0 AND exceeds the placebo 95th percentile.
