Earlier tests lacked the memory (four-hour levels expired after 2 days) and tested parts alone or at a single scale; this test builds the multi-week memory and the all-day hold and asks about the COMBINATION and each part. The user's other chart elements (3-month value area migration, per-bar POC, the 500-volume chart, MGI) are not reproducible from the database and are NOT in this test; their definitions of the 3-month POC are unresolved (our stored 3M_POC 29,512.75 vs a blue line near 29,800 on their chart), so the stored 3-month lines are used only descriptively.

## Data (fixed)
NQ 1-minute `price_bars_primary` 2023-03-06 .. last complete date minus 3 (pinned), loaded with the shared `floorEngine` loaders (trade-date convention: bars from 18:00 belong to the next trade date); roll weeks and 2025-09-28..11-19 excluded. D20 = mean full-session range of the 20 prior trade days (`prep`/`d20Of`). Units D20.

## Day-level definitions (all causal at 15:55 ET of trade date d)
- **ONH** = highest high from 18:00 of the prior calendar day to 09:29 ET of d (computed from the bars; not the stored level).
- **ONH-HELD** = (a) the first RTH 5-minute bar (starting 09:35 or later) whose CLOSE is above the ONH starts at or before 12:00 ET, and (b) no later 5-minute close up to the bar ending 15:55 is below `ONH - 0.02 x D20`, and (c) the 15:55 close is above the ONH.
- **ZONE-DEFENDED** (the "fourth approach"): L = the RTH low of d. A prior APPROACH day p is a trade date among the previous 90 trade days, at least 4 trade days before d, whose full-session low lies within `0.15 x D20(d)` of L AND that held (the highest high over the following 3 trade days is at least `0.5 x D20` above that low). Approach days are counted greedily with at least 2 trade days between counted approaches. ZONE-DEFENDED = at least 3 prior approaches (today is the 4th). Sensitivities (descriptive): band 0.10 and 0.25 x D20, at least 2 prior approaches.
- **CONJUNCTION** = ONH-HELD and ZONE-DEFENDED. Band, count and the 90-day window are fixed here from the user's description; if the conjunction has fewer than 30 days, the relaxations below are applied in this order and the first one reaching 30 days is used, decided from COUNTS ONLY before any outcome is printed: (1) at least 2 prior approaches, (2) band 0.25 x D20. If neither reaches 30 the cell is declared UNDERPOWERED and reported descriptively.

## Outcome and controls
Entry = open of the 15:56 ET bar of d; exit = close of the 15:55 ET bar of the NEXT trade date (so tonight's Globex plus tomorrow's RTH; a Friday event spans the weekend). r = (exit - entry) / D20(d), long. EXCESS = r minus the mean r of all valid days with the same weekday (the unconditional matched control; drift and the weekend structure removed). Descriptive extras: maximum favourable move to the next close, whether the next session's high reached the stored 3M_POC when it was above the entry.

## Counted cells and screens
Counted cells = 3: ONH-HELD, ZONE-DEFENDED, CONJUNCTION, each as EXCESS. Explore = earlier 60 percent of dates, hold-out = later 40 percent (a screen only). Date-blocked bootstrap (4,000), explore CI at 98.33 percent (Bonferroni over 3). CANDIDATE = explore CI lower bound above 0; SCREEN-SURVIVOR = candidate AND hold-out point above 0 and hold-out one-sided 95 percent lower bound above 0 AND the explore point above 0 after removing its largest date. UNDERPOWERED = fewer than 30 days or fewer than 20 distinct months-weeks (distinct dates) in a half. Printed: n, mean excess, CI half-width (smallest detectable effect), number of days overlapping on consecutive dates, expected by chance (3 x 0.0083). The question "does the combination add to its parts" is answered descriptively: CONJUNCTION vs ONH-HELD-only days and vs ZONE-only days (date-blocked two-sample CI), no verdict.
