## 1. Definitions (all use only bars that have completed by the decision time T; no lookahead)
- Bars: the 500-contract bars of the RTH session (09:30-16:00 ET) as in `b_d_formation.py`; profile = `profile_shape.features` (40 bins, 70 percent value area from the POC).
- Decision time T (primary 12:30 ET; secondary 11:30 and 13:30). Reference time T0 = T minus 60 minutes.
- FIRST AREA = the value area [VAL, VAH] (in price) of the profile built from the bars completed by T0, FROZEN from then on.
- MARGIN = the median high-low range of the session's bars completed by T0 (a data-derived, scale-free "one typical bar").
- DEPARTED = at least one bar completing in (T0, T] has a close beyond VAH by at least MARGIN (side UP) or below VAL by at least MARGIN (side DOWN). The side is the side of the first such bar.
- HELD = DEPARTED and every bar close completing in the last 30 minutes before T is beyond the same frozen edge on the same side (outside now and staying out). RETURNED = DEPARTED and not HELD (it came back, or crossed to the other side). STAYED = not DEPARTED.
- Also stored per session (continuous): the share of volume traded in (T0, T] outside the frozen value area on the departure side; minutes from the first departure to the first close back inside (or none).
## 2. Targets (final RTH profile, hindsight, defined before: `scripts/tell_context/shape_cuts.json` and `b_d_formation_report.py`)
Primary: FINAL MULTI-HUMP flag (bimodality at or above 0.365, base rate 34 percent). Secondary: STRONG B (top decile) vs STRONG D (bottom quartile of bimodality with letter D) among the sessions in either group; Spearman rank correlation of the final bimodality with the continuous outside-volume share.
## 3. Primary test (one)
Among sessions DEPARTED at T=12:30: risk difference P(final multi-hump | HELD) minus P(final multi-hump | RETURNED), 95 percent CI from a week-block bootstrap (resample calendar weeks, 2000 draws), plus a permutation p-value (final flag shuffled across sessions within calendar month, 2000 draws). The script PRINTS BEFORE ANY OUTCOME: the counts of HELD / RETURNED / STAYED / not-evaluable (fewer than 20 bars by T0) and the smallest risk difference the counts could detect (about 2.8 x sqrt(p(1-p)(1/nH + 1/nR)) at p = 0.34); if that detectable difference exceeds 0.25 the result is declared INCONCLUSIVE in advance and no verdict is drawn.
SHOWN requires ALL of: (1) risk difference >= 0.15; (2) bootstrap CI excludes 0; (3) permutation p < 0.05; (4) the same sign and a CI excluding 0 after stratifying by the developing multi-hump flag AT T (Mantel-Haenszel style weighted average of the within-stratum differences, week-block bootstrap), i.e. hold adds information beyond the flag itself; (5) the same sign of the risk difference at T=11:30 and T=13:30 and on both departure sides (UP and DOWN; the window drifted up). Otherwise NOT SHOWN, reported as exactly which condition failed and what could not be shown (not "the idea is wrong").
## 4. Secondary tests (counted, tests = 5 in the ledger: 1 primary + 4 below)
(a) strong B vs strong D P(HELD | departed) at T=12:30; (b) Spearman of the outside-volume share with final bimodality; (c) primary risk difference at T=11:30; (d) at T=13:30. The family is judged against its total tests; secondary results never rescue a failed primary.
## 5. Robustness grid (reported, NOT tests, cannot change the verdict)
Look-back 45 / 60 / 90 minutes, hold window 20 / 30 / 45 minutes, margin 0.5x / 1x / 2x the median bar range; the primary cell must not be the grid's best by a wide margin (hr-045).
## 6. Limits
Groups and targets come from the final profile (partly definitional: a B day must have travelled); the explore window has had many looks on this family tree (looks 108-119); about 50 strong B days; the up-drift of 2023-03..2025-05 may lean DEPARTED UP; a SHOWN result says nothing about trading value and no override or gate is built on it; timing: HELD is known only at T, so it is a late-morning/afternoon read, not an opening one.

## 7. Amendment A1 (2026-11-03 20:50 ET, written after my own review of the script, BEFORE any outcome of this test was read; DeepSeek unavailable, see STATUS)
Hazard found: holding outside the first area can be mechanical on a volatile or trending day (a big excursion both holds and ends wide), so HELD vs RETURNED could just measure how far price went. Added control, computable at T: EXCURSION = the furthest bar close beyond the frozen value-area edge (departure side) in the window, in value-area widths. Primary condition (4) is now: the Mantel-Haenszel difference stratified by developing flag AT T crossed with the TERCILE of the excursion (terciles over the departed sample, no outcome used) is >= +0.05 and its week-block CI excludes 0. The flag-only and excursion-only stratified differences are printed as context. Nothing else changed: SHOWN still needs conditions 1-5, the verdict is still a CANDIDATE needing the sealed confirmation, and the detectable-effect line is still printed first.
