## "Larger-trade" definition (outcome-blind, scale-free, hr-003)
The user's phrase is "setups that tend to have larger historical P&L trades". Selecting on realised P&L would select on outcomes, so the proxy is the row's own bracket at fire: WIDE = target distance |t1_level - price_at_detection| / ADR20 at or above the 2/3 quantile of an expanding window of EARLIER rows (by `fired_at`, all setup types pooled, at least 200 earlier rows as seed; earlier rows are descriptive only). Whether wide rows really are the large-P&L rows is checked DESCRIPTIVELY after the tests (rank correlation of target distance with |actual_pnl|); that check cannot change the counted tests.

## Signal and exit (same as A5; reused, hr-022)
Signal = first same-direction EXTENDED vacuum thrust (ROC10 onset, extension E_t in the top tercile of earlier same-track events, `capitulation_replay_phase1.py` / `context_exit_phase1.py` events) strictly inside (fired_at, resolved_at), not in the 18:00-18:30 ET blackout.
