# Pre-registration: the user's live call log — does the eye pick winners at defended levels? — 2026-10-26

Written BEFORE any call exists. Origin: after a run of mechanical nulls (four-hour defended levels, at stored period levels, moonshot exit, stuffed big orders at defended pivots) the user's chart reads (floors and tops at defended lines) cannot be validated from after-the-fact screenshots. This is the forward, blind version: the user logs a call in the artifact "My Calls" (https://claude.ai/artifact/DwiHd2fbCwS4aCVynHS5AJ) BEFORE knowing the outcome; each call is scored later by a fixed script. Scope doc it supersedes in part: `docs/MY_CALL_LOG_SCOPE_20261026.md`.

## What a call is (fixed here)
Direction (LONG/SHORT), entry mode (NOW at market, or AT THE LEVEL as a limit), the level price (required only for a limit entry), a one-line reason OR a screenshot (either is enough, both allowed), optional own stop and target. A screenshot is uploaded to the artifact's own asset store and only its id and link are kept in the call; it is context for reading the call later and plays no part in the scoring. The page stamps the time itself (America/New_York wall clock and UTC) at the moment of the tap; there is no editable time field and no edit or delete control (the store itself is not tamper-proof, and a call written after the move was seen is invalid by the user's own rule printed on the page). Outcomes are never shown on the page.

## Scoring (fixed here, identical for every call; script `scripts/score_my_calls_20261026.mjs`)
- **Entry.** NOW: the open of the first 1-minute NQ bar after the minute of the call. AT THE LEVEL: a resting limit at the level price, filled by the first 1-minute bar after the call whose range reaches it (fill price = the level, or the bar's open if it opens through the level), valid 4 hours; not filled by then = NOT_FILLED (counted and reported, no outcome).
- **Outcomes.** (a) exit-free move to 15, 60 and 240 minutes after the entry minute, direction-signed, in D20 units (mean full-session range of the 20 prior trade days); EXCESS = minus the mean of up to 30 clock-matched blind same-direction entries on other dates (the same sampler as the four-hour studies). (b) the moonshot exit M (stop 0.5 x D20, no target, 1,440 minutes, gap-through fills at the open, 1.5 points cost) in points, with the same blind-entry excess. (c) descriptive: the user's own stop and target when given, simulated bar by bar with the same realistic fills.
- **What the system did at that moment (descriptive):** the SHADOW/ACTIVE fires of the same direction within 30 minutes of the call, to see whether the user and the system agreed.

## The look (one, pre-registered)
No look at outcomes before it. The single look happens on the first date on or after **2026-11-30** on which at least **40 filled calls** on at least **15 distinct dates** exist; if that is not reached by 2026-12-31 the look is run on what exists and called UNDERPOWERED. It is run ONCE and reported whether positive or not (a second look needs a new pre-registration). One counted cell: all filled calls pooled, direction-signed, outcome (a) at 60 minutes, excess over blind entries, date-blocked 95 percent bootstrap CI. Everything else (15/240 minutes, outcome b, by direction, by mode, by reason keywords hand-coded blind to outcome, the user's own stops) is descriptive and not citable.

## Limits stated in advance
40 calls resolve only large effects (expect an interval of roughly +-0.1 D20, about 35 points); the user's calls are not independent of the day's regime; 'defended level' is whatever the user means at the time, and the reason text is the only label; a user who sees recent results may change behaviour, so outcomes stay hidden until the look; an eye that wins would still need rules a machine can copy, and the reason text and levels logged here are the labelled examples for that.
