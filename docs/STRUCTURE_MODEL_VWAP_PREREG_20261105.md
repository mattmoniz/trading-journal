## 1. Group V (all causal at the decision minute)
RTH VWAP = the volume-weighted average of the typical price ((high + low + close) / 3) of the minutes from 09:30 up to the decision minute, volume from the TICK-derived per-minute files (`scratch/updown_1m_20261104/`, hr-070). GLOBEX VWAP = the volume-weighted average of the minute closes from 18:00 of the previous calendar day to 09:29 (the same definition as the overnight inventory study). Features: the close's signed distance to the RTH VWAP and to the Globex VWAP (ADR units); the absolute distances; the spread RTH VWAP minus Globex VWAP (ADR); the RTH distance in sigma (the standard deviation of the minute closes since 09:30, at least 30 minutes of data).
