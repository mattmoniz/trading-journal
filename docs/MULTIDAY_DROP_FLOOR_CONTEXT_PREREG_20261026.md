## Context at the fire time (NQ 1-minute bars strictly BEFORE `fired_at`; scale = D20, the mean full-session (18:00-17:00) high-low range of the 20 prior trade days; A15 as in the floor studies)
- **DROP:** (highest high of the previous 48 hours of bars - close at the fire minute) / D20 is in the top tercile of that quantity over explore-period events.
- **FLOOR:** the lowest low of the previous 18 hours was set at least 3 hours before the fire, no bar since has broken it by more than 1 A15, and price has returned within 1 A15 of it at least once after first leaving (>= 2 A15 away): a base with at least one retest.
- **CONTEXT = DROP and FLOOR** (the user's "dropped a lot then put in a distinct floor"). DROP-only and FLOOR-only are descriptive.

## Outcomes and controls
(1) The setup's OWN result: `actual_pnl` in dollars, CONTEXT minus OTHER, stratified by `setup_type` (so the setup mix cannot drive it), strata weighted by the OTHER count (the `strat()` form used in the floor studies). (2) Exit-free "moonshot horizon": direction-long forward return from the setup's own entry price (`entry_zone_low`) to the close 240 and 1,440 minutes after the fire minute, in D20 units, minus a clock-matched blind control (other days, within 30 minutes of the same clock minute, up to 10 draws). Day-blocked bootstrap CIs. Explore = earlier 60 percent of dates, hold-out later 40 percent (a SCREEN only: the same history has been mined by every study today). **4 counted cells** = {own P&L, ret1440 excess} x {all wide-stop longs, moonshot-like}. CANDIDATE = explore CI lower bound > 0; SCREEN-SURVIVOR = candidate AND hold-out point > 0 AND hold-out CI lower bound > 0; UNDERPOWERED if < 20 distinct trade dates in a cell.
