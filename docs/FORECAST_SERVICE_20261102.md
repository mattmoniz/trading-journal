# Live busy-or-quiet forecast service (2026-11-02)

User objective (2026-11-02): a LIVE forecast that keeps learning, serving base hits and home runs. Memory: `project-live-learning-forecast-objective-20261102`. Evidence behind it: `scripts/amt_context/PREREG_ROSTER_CONTEXT.md` and the claims `roster_context_p2_forecast_beats_baseline_20261102` (the forecast works: out-of-sample rank correlation about 0.65 vs 0.26 for the clock baseline), `roster_context_tell_adds_nothing_beyond_range_20261102` (the tell adds nothing once recent range is known), `roster_context_h4_skip_busy_fades_not_shown_20261102` / `roster_context_h4c_horizon_matched_not_shown_20261102` / `roster_context_forecast_vs_fade_excursion_null_20261102` (no link to the simulated fades). So: the forecast is validated as a forecast of travel; its usefulness for trades is UNPROVEN and is what the forward data is for.

## What it is
A model that predicts, at any minute from 09:30 ET, how far NQ will travel (highest high minus lowest low, in ATR20 units) over the next H minutes (H = 30 for v1). Ridge on log travel, activity features only (pace of the 500-volume bars, range so far, overnight range, recent realized range over 15/30/60 minutes, clock bucket, day of week). No tell feature. Definitions: `scripts/amt_context/forecast_features.py` (serving) = `run_p2b.py`/`run_p3.py` (study); equality proven by `test_forecast_features.py`.

## What is built (all shadow: no order, no live-path change, no restart needed)
- Tables (`scripts/migrate_create_forecast_tables_20261102.mjs`): `forecast_models` (versioned registry, CHAMPION/CHALLENGER/RETIRED, coefficients stored so every forecast can be re-derived), `forecast_points` (forward decision-grid bars with features, every model's forecast and the realized travel once the window completed; price data only), `forecast_tags` (the forecast beside every RTH roster fire (ACTIVE and SHADOW primaries) and every V1 tell trade; outcome-blind, no P&L column).
- `scripts/amt_context/forecast_train.py HORIZON` trains and registers a version from the explore decision grid (through 2025-05-16) plus, later, completed forward points.
- `scripts/amt_context/forecast_tag_day.py DATE`: rebuilds the day's 500-volume bars with the sidecar's validated builder (like `tag_flips_day.py`), writes points and tags. Nightly via `forecast_tag_nightly.sh`, a step of `scripts/run_daily_calibration.sh` (8:20 PM ET Mon-Fri), forward start 2026-11-02, today and two previous weekdays, idempotent, a failed day never fails the job.
- `scripts/amt_context/forecast_status.mjs`: COUNTS only, no skill statistic, so nightly runs cannot cause optional stopping.

## The learning loop (BUILT 2026-11-02, replaces the earlier draft rules 1-5; `scripts/amt_context/forecast_loop.py`, weekly step of `run_weekly_backtests.sh`, tests `test_forecast_loop.py`)
Why the draft was replaced: DeepSeek round D found the loop was dead code (the trainer never read forward points) and that weekly overlapping evaluations gave no real protection against optional stopping. What is now true and tested:
1. **Data path.** `forecast_train.py` trains on the explore grid (cached, immutable) plus, with `--through DATE`, every COMPLETE and VALIDATED forward point on or before DATE; every registered model carries `trained_through`; retraining on explore data alone reproduces the registered champion exactly (test 6).
2. **A non-overlapping calendar.** Look i (0, 1, 2, ...) needs 20 x (i + 2) complete forward trading days; its held-out span is forward days [20 x (i + 1), 20 x (i + 2)); a challenger is trained at look time ONLY on explore points plus forward days before the span (so it never saw the span); no two looks share a day. Look 0 is at day 40 and is also F1/F1b below. The weekly job prints COUNTS only before that.
3. **Who is compared** (all out-of-sample on the span, recomputed from stored coefficients, never from stored predictions): the champion, the new challenger, PERSIST (ridge on rr15/rr30/rr60 + clock) and a clock-bucket baseline; Spearman with E_30, day-blocked bootstrap CI (3000) and a date-permutation p (1000) for challenger minus champion and champion minus PERSIST. A point on or before a model's trained_through is never evaluated (guard + mutation test).
4. **Promotion** only if challenger minus champion has CI lower bound > 0 AND p < 0.05 AND at least 28 days since the last promotion; the retired champion stays in the registry; one research_looks row per look (family `forecast_learning_loop`, kind FORWARD); the result is flagged once as an OPEN_DECISION (`forecast_loop_look_<i>_result`) to read.
5. **Decay alarm** (data-derived, evaluated only at looks): the champion's span Spearman below the 5th percentile of the explore rolling-20-date Spearman (stored in the champion's notes as `decay_floor`, currently +0.474) raises HIGH decision `forecast_decay_alarm_look_<i>`.
6. No feature, horizon or model class is added to rescue a weak result; a new one is a pre-registered amendment with its own look.
DEVIATION RECORDED 2026-11-02 (smoke test): to prove the look path runs end to end, a DRY look was executed once on the 3 real forward days with the calendar patched to train through day 1 and evaluate days 2-3 (150 points; nothing written except the one-off `decay_floor` note computed from explore data). It computed skill numbers on 2 forward days (champion +0.553, clock +0.628, PERSIST +0.502, challenger equal to champion). No decision was taken from it and it does not change any rule; it is disclosed because the service's own rule is that no forward skill statistic is looked at before the pre-set look, and these 2 days belong to the first 40.

## Forward confirmation of the champion itself (rule fixed 2026-11-02, before the first forward skill look)
F1 is the first and only skill look on the forward points until the learning loop starts. WHEN: the first time 40 forward trading days of completed 30-minute windows exist (the nightly tagger started 2026-11-02, plus the loaded live days 10-28..10-30; expected around the end of December 2026). WHAT: out-of-sample Spearman(champion forecast, realized E_30) minus Spearman(clock-bucket baseline, realized E_30) on the forward decision points, day-blocked 95% CI (3000, seed 20261102), halves by median date. PASS ("the forecast works on unseen data") = CI lower bound > 0 AND point estimate >= +0.17 (half of the explore value, +0.34) AND positive in both halves. FAIL = the explore skill did not carry forward; the champion is flagged, the cause is investigated (regime, data, bars) before any retrain, and no consumer is evaluated. One ledger look (family `forecast_forward_confirmation`). Nightly status stays count-only until then. Two later looks (80 and 120 days) are allowed on the same rule and counted.

**Amendment to F1 (2026-11-02, written before any forward skill look; reason: Amendment 7, claim `roster_context_persistence_baseline_20261102`).** On explore data a persistence model (ridge on rr15/rr30/rr60 + clock bucket + day of week, called PERSIST) already recovers 95% of the champion's lift over the clock baseline (clock +0.263, PERSIST +0.628, champion +0.647; the raw trailing 30-minute range alone +0.592), so F1's clock-bucket bar is easy and a pass says "persistence plus clock carries forward", NOT "the champion's extra features add skill". F1 stays as written for continuity. Added F1b, computed on the same forward points in the same single look: champion minus PERSIST Spearman, day-blocked CI, REPORT-ONLY (40 forward days cannot resolve the explore increment of +0.019, so no pass gate). Decision rule fixed now: if the forward champion minus PERSIST point estimate is below +0.01, the simpler PERSIST model becomes the forecast the service describes and serves (nothing was ever wired to the champion, so nothing is lost); a challenger must beat PERSIST, not only the clock, to be promoted.

## Consumers (anything that ACTS on the forecast): all SHADOW until the user says otherwise
Candidates, each a separate pre-registered test on the forward tags, logging what it WOULD have done and never changing an order: skip a fade when busy; size stops and targets from expected travel (candidate for the tell, which has no target); switch setup family (quiet favors fades, busy favors continuation setups; needs the trend families that only the forward data contains); stand down when one bad trade could use the daily-loss limit. The user's standing position: no ML gate on real decisions without being asked ([[project-ml-silo-trust-unresolved-20260929]]); Stage 2 stays unchanged until its own review.

## Forward-only hypothesis from the stop-scale null (pre-registered 2026-11-02, before any forward outcome)
F2 (read ONCE, at the Stage 2 review when at least 150 forward V1 tell trades have forecast tags; ledger family `forecast_forward_tell_third`, one look): the mean simulated net of tell trades whose champion forecast is in the TOP third is lower than that of the other two thirds. Third cutoffs are the 1/3 and 2/3 quantiles of the champion's forecasts on the explore V1 trades (computed by script before the look, stored in `forecast_models.notes`). PASS = difference (top third minus the rest) below 0 with a day-blocked 95% CI upper bound below 0 and the same sign in both halves. Descriptive explore numbers behind it (uncounted, from the stop-scale run): +$7.16 / +$8.44 / -$2.48 by third. This is a hypothesis for a SHADOW skip rule only; Stage 2 is not changed, and no rule is evaluated before the floor.
