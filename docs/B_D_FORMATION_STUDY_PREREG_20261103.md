# Pre-registration (DRAFT): how a B and a D FORM through the RTH day, bar by bar (written 2026-11-03 BEFORE any outcome of this study is read)

STATUS: DRAFT, DESCRIPTIVE, nothing built, nothing run. Priority one for the next session (user, 2026-11-03: "the B formation study first, and it might be a D formation study too in order to compare; I have a sense that B shape might be wider too"). Purpose: find, in the data, HOW and WHEN a B becomes a B and how that differs from a D, to propose a RULE for B as a fourth letter (docs/B_SHAPE_NOTES_20261103.md). Features and raw 500-bar prices and times only, no tell or setup outcomes; explore sessions only (522); ledger look FIRST (family `b_d_formation_20261103`). Hypotheses only: nothing here is a finding or a gate; a candidate rule gets its own pre-registration and a one-time sealed confirmation.
## 1. Groups (frozen before reading anything)
STRONG B = top decile of the final bimodality score (N=52, cut 0.61); STRONG D = bottom quartile of bimodality with the final value-area midpoint in the middle third (N=90); MULTI-HUMP (the flag, bimodality at or above 0.365, N=176) and ALL sessions as context; P and b shown as reference groups (the drive-first shapes). Letters and scores from the shared `scripts/tell_context/profile_shape.py`.
## 2. Per-session bar-by-bar tracking (the developing RTH profile after every completed 500-contract bar from 09:30; checkpoints printed every 30 minutes and at 10:30, 11:00 ... 15:30)
- WIDTH (the user's hypothesis that B is wider): the price extent of the profile so far and at the close (points, % of the open, and in units of the prior 20 sessions' average daily range); the value-area width (points and share of the extent); the distance between the two largest humps; all as curves through the day.
- Humps: number of humps at least half the biggest, their positions (fraction of the extent), the time (median bar time of the volume in each hump) each began to build, which hump is the larger, and the first moment a second hump of at least half the first appears.
- Where each area builds: which side first (the area in the upper or the lower half of the final extent) and in which clock block (open to 11:00, 11:00-13:00, 13:00-close); the order of the two areas by time: morning top then afternoon bottom, morning bottom then afternoon top, or both in the same block.
- Movement between areas: the sequence of visits of price across the three zones (thirds of the extent so far and of the final extent), the number of crossings of the trough (thin zone) between the humps and how fast (minutes per crossing), the share of time and of volume in the middle third vs the outer thirds, and whether price swings top to bottom skipping the middle or lingers there.
- TIME and VOLUME (TPO): the time-at-price profile (one mark per price row per 30-minute bracket from the bar highs and lows) beside the volume profile; the first bracket in which a second area has both time and volume; time and volume agree or not.
- The tell's own watcher state history (the files' `watcher.py`) at each checkpoint as context.
## 3. Outputs (all descriptive, printed with N)
Median and interquartile curves per group for each measure through the day, with the first clock hour at which B and D separate (medians outside each other's IQR); the order-and-side table (the four orders above, share of B and of D); a crossing-and-skipping table; width at the close B vs D with day-block CI (uncounted); a few EXAMPLE days per group (dates, with their profiles) for the user's eye. A candidate B rule (a few measures with cutoffs read off these curves) is written down, NOT adopted, and goes to the user with example days it calls B, D and in between.
## 4. Reading rules and limits
Everything is hindsight on finished days, so 'when a B becomes a B' is when it can be seen in the developing profile, not a forecast; earlier work found no early information from a developing volume, time or combined second-area score (AUC 0.44-0.56 at 11:00-13:00, ledger look 116), so a B rule built here may only become visible late in the day; groups are defined by the final profile (partly definitional: a B day must have travelled); about 50 strong B days, so curves are medians with IQR, never a test; the explore window has had nine looks on this family (looks 108-116) so any candidate rule is only a candidate until confirmed once on the sealed window (`spendSealedLook()`); ledger look first; the script name and the group counts are printed before any curve is read.
