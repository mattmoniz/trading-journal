## 1. The delta definition and its reproduction check (done before this prereg)
Sierra's 'Cumulative Delta Bars - Up/Down Tick Volume' (sierrachart.com StudiesReference ID=323, fetched 2026-11-04): uptick volume = volume at a HIGHER trade price than the previous trade, downtick = at a LOWER price, an unchanged price takes the previous non-unchanged direction; classification is by trade price, NOT bid/ask; the cumulative sum resets at the start of the trading day. Implemented in `scripts/tick_microstructure/updown_tick_delta.py` (today, from `NQZ26.scid`) and `extract_updown_minutes_20261104.py` (explore dates). ## 2. Population, stall and outcome (identical to the first test, `stall_lib.py`)
The 1,760 explore tell entries (date, dir, ehr only); stall = no new favourable extreme for L minutes with L = 11 (the p90 of the gaps; PRIMARY because the 3-minute stall of the first test is not a visible stall); outcome y = (max favourable - max adverse excursion over the next 60 minutes) / s, s = median 1-minute range of the session so far; entries with no stall within 120 minutes, within 60 minutes of 15:55, or on dates without extracted ticks are excluded and counted.

## 3. Features (causal at the stall minute t*; Cd = direction x cumulative up/down delta since 18:00; e = the minute of the last new favourable extreme; em = the entry minute; scale5 = median absolute 5-minute net delta over the RTH minutes of the session up to t*)
1. DSHARE: in-direction delta share (up - down) / (up + down) over the last 5 minutes MINUS the same over the 20 minutes before that.
2. SINCE_EXT: (Cd(t*) - Cd(e)) / scale5: did flow keep going with the move or turn against it since the extreme.
3. DIV: (Cd(e) - max of Cd over [em, e]) / scale5, at most 0: 0 = delta confirmed the best price, negative = the extreme was not confirmed by delta (the divergence the user reads).

## 4. Counted tests and verdict
Spearman rho of each feature with y; day-block bootstrap CI at 98.33 percent (Bonferroni 0.05 / 3, 4,000 draws, seed 20261104); placebo = the same feature computed on the same minute positions (em, e, t*) from a random other date, seeded; a feature is a LEAD only if the CI excludes 0, both chronological halves have the same sign, and |rho| exceeds the placebo |rho| with a placebo CI that includes 0; else NOT SHOWN. Detectable |rho| (2.8 x SE) printed BEFORE the verdicts. Reported, not counted: L = 3; rho by entry hour; W = 10 and 30 for DSHARE; mean y by quintile.
