# Pre-registration: trade geometry and sweep-depth calibration on the Mode 1 deduplicated book — 2026-10-27

Written BEFORE any Part 1-4 number was computed. Origin: the user's request after the post-entry study (`docs/POST_ENTRY_DEDUP_BAILOUT_PREREG_20261027.md`). The user's working premise: Bucket C losses are premature entries / stop-outs at the apex of liquidity sweeps, not runaway trend days. **That premise is the hypothesis under test, not an established fact.** The earlier Variant B result (flipping at the stop loses) shows only that price does not continue one further stop-distance quickly; it does not by itself show reversion, and reversal after a fresh extreme also happens generically. Part 1 measures reversion directly against a random-time placebo.

## Facts established by an outcome-free probe before writing this (no performance numbers looked at)
- Same trade set as before: 5,324 simulated RTH level fades, 336 dates, 60/40 split through 2026-01-02. Mode 1 (no overlapping open same-direction trade, ordering `(date, entry bar, |entry-level|, id)`) is rebuilt here and must reproduce 1,723 trades with Hold-Out max drawdown 2,357 dollars (printed as a check).
- Geometry in the trade frame: the entry sits on the PRE-level side in 82.7 percent of trades, median 9.5 points before the stored level (10th/90th percentile -4.5 / +14.25). The baseline stop sits a median 70 points BEYOND the level and 76 points from entry; the target is a median 41 points from entry. So moving the entry from the trigger to the level shifts it by about 12 percent of the stop distance, and the stop is not near the level.
- An engine with a conservative entry-bar rule (stop checked on the entry bar, target not credited there, gap fills at the open) reproduces the stored resolution on 5,279 of 5,324 trades (99.2 percent).
- Expiry is 16:00 for 99 percent of trades (13:00 for 57), i.e. the session end (capped at the 15:59 bar). The stored baseline scores every TIME_EXPIRED trade at exactly 0 dollars (no fee), a "free scratch at the close".
- Stored P&L = `2 x points - 1` (fee 1 dollar, no slippage, exact stop and target fills).

## Engine (all re-simulated rows, real and placebo, use it)
Bars are 1-minute NQ. Entry: either immediate at the stored trigger price on bar `t` (stop checked on bar `t`, its target never credited) or a LIMIT order valid for bars `t+1 .. t+15` (and not past expiry), filled at the limit price when the bar trades THROUGH it by one tick (buy: Low <= limit - 0.25; sell: High >= limit + 0.25). Bar `t` is excluded from limit fills (the intrabar order of the trigger and the retrace is unknown). On the fill bar only the stop is checked (conservative). After that: per bar, a stop gapped through fills at the open, stop touch fills at the stop, then the same for the target, stop before target in the same bar. Expiry bar = stored `expires_at`, capped at 15:59. **Unresolved at expiry: primary = marked to the close of the expiry bar (realistic); the baseline flat-0 convention is shown beside it.** Fee 1 dollar per filled trade (the baseline), plus a "realistic" column at 4 dollars fee+slippage per filled trade.
Every variant is defined by distances from the stored trigger price `ref` with `d` = trade direction, `SD` = stop distance, `TD` = target distance, `s = max(0, (ref - level) x d)` (the entry's distance before the level; trades whose entry is already at or past the level keep the baseline entry for Variant 1, s = 0):
- Control (re-simulated baseline): entry `ref` immediate, stop `ref - d SD`, target `ref + d TD`.
- V1a at-level limit, target PRICE fixed: limit `ref - d s`, stop `limit - d SD` (stop distance constant), target `ref + d TD`. V1b: target DISTANCE fixed (`limit + d TD`).
- V2 mid-sweep limit at `ref - d 0.5 SD`, stop unchanged `ref - d SD`, target `ref + d TD`.
- V3 second mouse: wait up to 15 bars for price to trade through `Q = ref - d SD` (the baseline stop price), fade `d` from `Q` with stop `Q - d SD` and target `Q + d m SD`, m in {1.0, 1.5}.
**Mirrored-geometry placebo** (the standing rule for asymmetric stop/target structures): the identical distances with `d` replaced by `-d` (same `ref`, `s`, `SD`, `TD`), which keeps the stop/target asymmetry and the fill mechanics but removes the directional claim. It is printed for every variant and for Part 3.

## Part 1 — post-stop path of Bucket C stop-outs (Mode 1 book)
For each Bucket C stop-out with bars after `t_exit` (up to the 15:59 bar): over bars `t_exit+1 .. t_exit+M`, M in {5,15,30}: Post_Stop_Continuation_R = max(0, further adverse travel beyond the stop)/SD; Post_Stop_Reversion_R = max(0, travel back in the fade's favor from the stop)/SD; reached-entry and reached-target flags (High/Low through the entry or target price). Quartiles and mean on Train and Hold-Out. Controls: (i) Bucket B stop-outs; (ii) a random-time placebo: 30 draws per trade at the same clock time (+/- 30 minutes) on other dates, reference = that bar's open (standing in for the stop price), same direction and the same SD in points, entry analog = reference + d SD, same measures. The excess of the actual over its own placebo mean is date-blocked bootstrapped. **Pass for "reverts more than generically":** Hold-Out excess of reached-entry within 30 minutes AND of reversion-minus-continuation have 95 percent lower bounds above 0. Even a pass does not establish premature entry (a fresh-extreme bounce is generic); it would motivate Parts 2-3 only.

## Part 2 — entry-depth variants; Part 3 — stop width x target scale
Part 2 prints, for Control, V1a, V1b, V2, V3 (1.0R, 1.5R) and the stored baseline, on Train and Hold-Out: fill rate (filled / Mode 1 trades in the split), win rate (filled trades with positive P&L), profit factor, mean P&L per filled trade with a date-blocked 95 percent interval, mean at the 4-dollar fee, total net, true realized max drawdown; plus the Control's P&L on the SAME filled trades and on the unfilled ones (a missed-fill accounting), the flat-0 convention mean, and the mirrored placebo. **Pass (per variant, Hold-Out):** mean P&L at the 4-dollar fee has its lower bound above 0 AND exceeds the mirrored-placebo mean AND total net (1-dollar fee) exceeds the Control's total net.
Part 3: stop scale S in {1.25, 1.5, 2.0} x target scale T in {1.0, 1.25, 1.5} on the Control geometry (stop `ref - d S SD`, target `ref + d T TD`; T = 1.0 is fixed target distance), 9 cells plus the Control. Fit on Train: the cell with the highest mean P&L at the 4-dollar fee; freeze; evaluate on Hold-Out. **Pass:** Hold-Out mean at the 4-dollar fee has its lower bound above 0 AND exceeds the Control's Hold-Out mean AND the mirrored placebo mean. All 9 cells are printed on both splits for transparency; only the frozen cell counts. The wider-stop dollar risk per loss grows with S; profit factor and drawdown are printed.
