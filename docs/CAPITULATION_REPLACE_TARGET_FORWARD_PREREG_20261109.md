## Test F1 (one counted test, its own family, one-sided alpha 0.10)
Population: forward rows (`forward` = fired at or after 2026-11-09 14:30 ET), `session_track` RTH, `at_boundary` = true (entry within 0.05 ADR20 of the prior RTH high / low / VAH / VAL or the overnight high / low; the logger's frozen tag), with `h_state` = SIGNAL and `hc0_delta` known (C0 terminal). Statistic: mean of `hc0_delta`, rows sharing one thrust collapsed to one event (`h_sig_event_id`, cv-025), day-blocked bootstrap (the logger's `--report`, a line to be added at the floor). Stated before data: the A10 replay value to compare with is +$72.3 per event (59 events); a forward mean below the replay lead is expected (regression to the mean) and is not a failure by itself.
Floor: 20 independent signalled events on 10 days (hr-008); nothing is judged below it.
- CONFIRM (for a next step, not a live change): lower 90 percent bound of the mean > 0, 25 percent trimmed mean > 0, the result survives dropping the best day, the forward untagged RTH rows' mean H minus C0 is lower than the tagged mean, AND the risk-set placebo (a matched-moment exit of a control row of the same tag and track, exactly as the replay) is beaten: that placebo is computed AT THE FLOOR by pointing `capitulation_replay_a10.py` at the forward rows (a `--forward` mode to be added then; not computable earlier).
- FAIL: upper 90 percent bound < 0 at the floor. CONTINUE otherwise: re-look at 40 events.
- Descriptive, uncounted, same table: Globex boundary rows (the replay had 28 events, +$25.8, not shown), `pivot_live` rows (the replay had 12 events: below the floor), untagged rows, H minus own exit (`ha_delta`), ACTIVE vs SHADOW, by whether C0 ended by the stop or the expiry (holding is stopped out in about 68 percent of rows: where the thrust never comes, C0 is the loser; the question F1 asks is only whether the capitulation timing rescues enough of them), by structural group, never per setup type as a test (cv-033).

## Expected duration and re-look triggers (not retirements, co-025)
The replay found 59 RTH boundary events in 68 sessions (about 0.9 per session), so 20 events would take about 23 sessions (roughly five weeks) at that rate, assuming the boundary-tagged level fades keep logging SHADOW rows (they do: monitor-only still detects them). Re-look triggers: fewer than 5 forward RTH boundary signalled events by 2026-12-21 (check the logger and the tag source first); the floor not reached by 2027-02-01.

## What a confirm would and would not mean; next-step tree (co-022)
A confirm says that, on rows entered at a boundary in RTH, leaving at the capitulation instead of holding to expiry with the original stop helped, on new data. It does NOT say that removing a target is safe: C0 is stopped out in about 68 percent of rows, the account has a $2,000 end-of-day trailing loss, and the rows are mostly level-fade families that are monitor-only. Confirm: a prereg for the combined rule on a named setup set (and the row 33 level tags as the way to apply the location condition to the market setups), then the user's decision; any live exit change is a RESOLVED mark through `sweepExits()` (cv-078), never new order code, and a target removal needs its own decision. Fail: autopsy by tag, session, stop vs expiry, how many H exits cut a winner; park. Continue: the logger runs unchanged.
## Known limits (stated before data)
SHADOW rows are theoretical fills; the forward window is short and shares market regime with the replay window's end; the vacuum thrust events come from the live daemon (`vacuum_live_events`), whose outage would leave rows unscored (alarm: any row with `h_state` OPEN after its C0 ended); a thrust is searched only after the row's fire, so a row fired before the daemon logged anything is scored without signals.

## Amendment A11.1 (2026-11-09 night, after Argon's review and the A10.2 descriptive analyses; written BEFORE any forward row was judged; F1's frozen statistic, floor 20 / 10 and FAIL rule are UNCHANGED)
(1) The UNCONDITIONAL (ITT) contrast is reported beside F1 from the stored columns: for every forward row of a stratum, mean of `h_pnl` minus `actual_pnl` and `h_pnl` minus `c0_pnl` (H equals C0 on rows with no thrust), day-blocked. It is a REQUIRED condition of any CONFIRM or promotion: F1 can only be called confirmed for a next step if the ITT H - A lower 90 percent bound is above zero for the stratum; the conditional H - C0 alone is no longer enough (A10.2: on R2_RTH it was +$72.3 conditional, -$1.0 ITT).
(2) PROMOTION-grade floor: 35 independent signalled events on 20 distinct days (first-look floor stays 20 / 10).
(3) NEW forward test F2 (counted, own family of two with F1, Holm 0.10 one-sided): ITT mean of `h_pnl` minus `actual_pnl` over forward RTH `pivot_live` rows (the user's defended-level case; replay lead +$24.0 [+4.3, +44.4] on 143 rows, post-hoc, 1 of 9 strata), floor 100 rows on 40 days (about 2 such rows a session), no verdict below it. CONFIRM needs the lower 90 percent bound > 0, trimmed mean > 0 and surviving dropping the best day.
(4) A matched static-target arm T (original stop, exit at the walk-forward median excursion of earlier H exits, in ADR20 units) is computed OFFLINE from the stored columns (`entry_px`, `stop_px`, `expires_at`, `adr20`, `h_exit_px`) and minute bars at the floor; a confirm also requires H to beat T unconditionally by a positive lower bound (A10.2: H - T = -$0.6 [-9.5, +10.2]).
(5) Declined (Argon): killing at 20 events on a negative point estimate (co-025: never retire on an estimate; FAIL stays the upper 90 percent bound below zero at the floor). Registered, not built: arm P (protected hold: stop to breakeven when the row's own `t1_level` is first reached, then hold to the capitulation or expiry), arm HP.
