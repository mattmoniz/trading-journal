# Event-snapshot study: what does the data around a tell look like just before price continues or pivots? (written 2026-11-02 BEFORE any outcome is read; user idea: "take snapshots of the data at the points where there is a high likelihood of pivoting or continuing, study those exact moments a little before and a little after, look at the depth, the information I can't see as a human, and string a story together"; user go: "Yea")

## 0. What this is and is not
A HYPOTHESIS-GENERATION study with a built-in independent check, not a trade rule. Earlier direction tests each fixed ONE definition (a delta flip, a break side, an entry); none looked broadly at the whole picture around each event and none used the order book. The risk of a broad look is storytelling: with many features and few events something always looks convincing. So the rules below fix, in advance, what counts as a finding and where it must be confirmed. Nothing here changes anything live.
Honest prior: about 5-10 percent that a candidate survives its confirmation. Order-book information tends to decay in seconds to minutes while the horizon here is 15-60 minutes, and the depth sample is small.

## 1. Events (one family, all from existing validated code)
- Data: 500-contract bars (bid and ask volume per bar, trades per bar, volume at price) rebuilt from the tick files with the sidecar's builder (`bootstrap.build_sessions`, validated against the database RTH volume), baselines chained in memory (`scripts/event_snapshots/build_days.py`).
- DEPTH-ERA set (discovery): every NQ session from 2026-05-01 with a validated rebuild; events from the first session with a 20-session baseline; depth coverage is the 104 sessions 2026-06-15 .. 2026-10-30. TELL events = the V1 research simulation's signal bars (`trade.simulate`), t0 = the signal bar's end. CONTROL events = decision-grid bars (every 10th bar index, 10:00-14:30) with price moving and no tell in either direction, drawn at random (seeded) at 4 per tell event, matched to the tell's entry-time bin.
- EXPLORE set (independent confirmation for the bar-level features only): the 516 explore sessions to 2025-05-16 (`scripts/amt_vbars` extracts): 1,760 V1 tell fires plus its grid controls. The depth features exist only in the depth era, so they can only be confirmed FORWARD (section 5).

## 2. The outcome ("pivot or continue"), price only
Pre-event direction p = sign of the price change over the last 10 bars ending at t0 (the tell's own motion window); events with p = 0 are dropped and counted. Continuation score y = p x (close of the last bar completed within 30 minutes of t0 minus the close at t0) / ATR20 (the 30-minute horizon is primary; 15 and 60 minutes are reported, uncounted). y > 0 means price continued the pre-event move, y < 0 means it pivoted. Also reported: the raw signed move (up or down) for the user's direction question.

## 3. Snapshot features (all computed from bars completed at or before t0; nothing after t0)
Bar-level (available in both eras): signed delta share of the last 1, 3 and 10 bars in the direction of p ((ask - bid) / volume x p); delta-price divergence over 10 and 20 bars (price made a new extreme in the direction of p while the cumulative aligned delta did not); absorption (volume per bar over range per bar, last 3 bars, relative to the session so far); pace (seconds per bar against the hourly baseline, A); trades per bar and contracts per trade (a large-lot proxy); share of the last 10 bars' volume traded at the extreme two ticks of the move (rejection or absorption at the edge); location (close in the day's range so far; distance to the overnight high and low in ATR units); count of tell components met (0-4) and node ladder length. 17 features, fixed now.
Depth-level (depth era only, built as stage 2 from `scripts/depth_absorption/depth_reader.py` book reconstruction): at t0 and over the 60 seconds before it: total resting size within 5, 10 and 20 ticks on each side, the bid-over-ask imbalance aligned with p, its change over 60 seconds, depth thinning on the side of the move (liquidity pull) and stacking on the far side, resting size consumed versus size executed (absorption by resting orders). Defined in the stage-2 script header BEFORE it is run on outcomes.
Event-time paths (the "story" view, descriptive): for continuation versus pivot groups, the mean aligned delta, volume and range paths from 20 bars before to 20 bars after t0, and, for depth events, the mean aligned imbalance path from 120 seconds before to 120 seconds after.

## 4. Counted tests and rules
- DISCOVERY (depth era, TELL events only, then the same on CONTROL events as the comparison): for each bar-level feature the Spearman correlation with y at 30 minutes, day-blocked bootstrap CI (3000), circular day-shift placebo p (1000); BH q = 0.10 across the 17 bar-level features (family `event_snapshot_20261102`, ledger tests = 17). The depth features are a second counted family run after stage 2 (count fixed when its script is committed).
- A feature is a CANDIDATE only if it is a BH survivor in the depth-era TELL events AND has the same sign in the first and second halves of those events. The tell-versus-control comparison (is the relationship different for tells than for non-tell moments?) is reported, not used to filter.
- CONFIRMATION of a bar-level candidate: its sign and size on the EXPLORE set (1,760 tell fires), one look, same statistic, CI excluding zero with the SAME sign, BH over the number of candidates. Only then is it a lead. Depth-level candidates: confirmed only by FORWARD data (the nightly recording keeps adding events) with a pre-written rule: at least 150 new tell events, same sign, CI excluding zero, one look.
- Resolution, stated now: about 350 depth-era tell events on about 100 days resolve a correlation of roughly +/-0.11 to 0.13 (day-clustering included), far above the 0.03-0.04 that would pay after costs at the 30-minute horizon (claim `horizon_economics_power_vs_cost_20261102`). So this study can only flag LARGE relationships; everything smaller is invisible. The explore confirmation set (1,760 events) resolves about +/-0.05.
- No feature, horizon or subgroup is promoted after seeing outcomes; the "story" paths are descriptive and generate hypotheses for the NEXT pre-registration, they never count as a result.

## 5. Forward accrual (what makes depth findings testable at all)
The nightly job already rebuilds bars for tagging; adding a stage that snapshots each day's tell events with the same feature code (and the depth features while depth is recorded) would grow the sample by about 3-4 events per session. Not built until a candidate exists and the user agrees; the depth files are about 1 GB per day and are not archived by this repo, so a decision on keeping them is needed if depth turns out to matter.

## 6. Reading rules
No BH survivor, or no survivor with a stable sign: the broad look found nothing a human cannot already see; recorded with the resolution; the depth stage still runs for its descriptive paths. Survivors that fail the explore confirmation: not shown (a depth-era artifact). Survivors that confirm: a lead for a mechanical, pre-registered direction rule on the explore bars, with realistic fills, in a new document. Under no outcome is anything wired or any order placed.
