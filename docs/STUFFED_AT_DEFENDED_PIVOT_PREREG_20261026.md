## Definitions (fixed here, all causal at the order's reference time)
- **STUFFED** exactly as the 10-20 test, definition A: 60 seconds after the order ends, price is at least 2 ticks back against the order's last print. t_ref = end + 60 s; nothing after t_ref is used for any label.
- **Active pivot at time T.** Pivots are accepted by an INCREMENTAL ZigZag walk (the same logic as `walkZigZagAcceptance()`, with each accepted pivot recording when it was confirmed (its swing index + 5 five-minute bars) and when it was replaced by a more extreme same-type pivot; the final accepted set is asserted equal to the shared function's output). A pivot is ACTIVE at T if confirmed at least 30 minutes before T, not yet replaced, and not BROKEN (a 5-minute close beyond it by more than the band since confirmation). Band `b = 0.02 x D20` (the minor detector's own band, about 8 points).
- **DEFENDED.** At least one completed prior TEST of the active pivot: the 5-minute bars touched the band, and later the price left the band to at least `0.1 x D20` on the approach side, with tests at least 10 minutes apart, all completed before T. (`defended = 0` pivots are the NOT-DEFENDED control.)
- **AT-LEVEL.** The order's LAST print is within the band of an active pivot of the matching orientation: a SELL order at a LOW pivot (the user's picture: sellers stuffed at a defended low), a BUY order at a HIGH pivot (mirror). Nearest pivot wins.

## Tests (RTH big orders with t_ref between 10:00 and 15:30 ET, as before)
Outcome: ATR20-normalised price change from t_ref to 5 / 15 / 30 minutes later, positive = price moves OPPOSITE to the order (up after a stuffed sell, down after a stuffed buy). The contrast is always matched on side x 30-minute clock bucket x size class.
- **Counted cells = 2:** SELL-STUFFED-AT-DEFENDED-LOW and BUY-STUFFED-AT-DEFENDED-HIGH, each judged as the matched excess over ALL OTHER big orders of the same side (control C).
- **Required controls for "the two things together matter" (printed, point estimates must be positive in both halves for a survivor):** (A) FOLLOWED orders (price kept going 2+ ticks with the order) at a defended pivot of the same orientation, same side — is it the level alone?; (B) STUFFED orders NOT at any active pivot — is it stuffing alone? (C) as above. Also printed: AT-LEVEL but NOT yet defended, and the number of controls per event.
- **Screens:** explore 2023-03-06..2025-05-31, hold-out 2025-06-01..2026-10-19. Day-blocked bootstrap (1,500 resamples) at 98.75 percent two-sided (Bonferroni over 2). CANDIDATE = explore CI lower bound above 0 at 15 minutes AND at one other horizon; SCREEN-SURVIVOR = candidate AND hold-out point above 0 and at least half the explore size AND the A and B point estimates above 0 in both halves AND the cell stays positive after removing its largest date. UNDERPOWERED = fewer than 30 events or fewer than 20 distinct dates in a half. Printed: n, dates, mean, the smallest detectable effect (CI half-width), expected-by-chance (2 x 0.0125 x horizons tested), time-of-day split (before 11:00 / later), size class split, share of events sharing a date.
- **One dollar translation (descriptive, no verdict):** for events that pass the screen only, the same orders traded as one MNQ contract with a 2 x ATR-fraction stop and the moonshot hold are NOT part of this study; the rank/dollar step is a separate pre-registration if anything survives.
