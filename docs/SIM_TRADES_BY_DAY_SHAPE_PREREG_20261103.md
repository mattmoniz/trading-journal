# Pre-registration (descriptive): the real sim trades against the shape of the day they happened on (written 2026-11-03 night BEFORE any shape was joined to any trade outcome)

STATUS: FROZEN. Descriptive, hindsight (the day's FINAL profile is known only at the close), tiny effective N (about 16 trading days), uncounted for any gate; ledger family `sim_trades_by_day_shape_20261103`. User request (2026-11-03): "take the really profitable trades and groups of trades we have had in sim and show where each fell in the shape of that day and whether the shape explained how they did." The user explicitly lifted the standing rule against using the trades table as evidence of signal-following (co-002) FOR THIS ANALYSIS ONLY; nothing here says whether signals were followed.
## 1. Trades (frozen before any join)
`active_setups` rows with origin_status = 'ACTIVE', pnl_source = 'BROKER' and broker_pnl not null (a real sim broker fill; 340 rows on 16 days, 2026-09-28..2026-11-03), PRIMARY population additionally is_cluster_primary = true (cluster siblings double count, hr-007; 253 rows) and no *_basis flag true (stale_entry_price_basis, late_fill_past_expiry_basis, bad_bars_basis, ib_window_stale_basis, stale_levels_basis; 3 rows). Sensitivity: all 340. P&L = broker_pnl dollars (1 MNQ; the 2026-09-28/29 rows of about -2,150 and -1,750 are reported separately as outliers, never silently dropped). The flood window of 2026-07-09..09-16 does not intersect. TELL trades = setup_type starting AMT_TELL (the Stage 2 orders). RTH rows (is_rth) and Globex rows are separate groups; a Globex row belongs to the trade_date it was set to and is compared with the NEXT RTH day's shape (hindsight, labelled as such).
## 2. Day shape (frozen)
The FINAL RTH profile of the trade date through `profile_shape.features` (40 bins, value area 70 percent): letter D / P / b from the value-area midpoint, multi-hump flag (bimodality at or above 0.365), strong B (at or above 0.61) and strong D (at or below 0.09 with letter D) with the explore cuts. Profile source: the exact 500-contract bars with volume at price (`scratch/volbars500_holdout_20261103`) where the date is covered (through 2026-10-19), otherwise the 1-minute bars of `price_bars_primary` with each bar's volume spread evenly across its high-low range in 0.25-point steps (hr-021); on dates where both exist the agreement of the letter and the correlation of the midpoint are printed, and the ONE source per date is the exact one when it exists.
Location of the trade in the day: the entry price (the broker entry fill when available, else the row's entry price) in thirds of the final range (lower / middle / upper) and against the final value area (below VAL / inside / above VAH); direction long or short; TOWARD VALUE = a long below the value area midpoint or a short above it, otherwise AWAY.
## 3. Outputs (all descriptive, N and day counts printed, N below 20 never called a result, day-block 95 percent CIs of means with 2000 draws)
O1 per trade table of the biggest winners and losers (top 12 each) with date, setup, direction, time, P&L, the day's letter, multi-hump flag, entry location. O2 mean and total P&L by final letter and by multi-hump, all trades, RTH only, tell only, level fades only (setup_type containing _FADE_), other. O3 enrichment: the share of the top-decile winners (and bottom-decile losers) falling on each letter and on multi-hump days against the share of all trades, with the trades-per-day concentration shown. O4 entry location x direction (toward value / away) mean P&L. O5 the per-day table: day P&L, number of trades, letter, bimodality. O6 the shape-source agreement check. Setup-type breakdown only where a type has N of 20 or more (almost none will: about 70 types, a handful of trades each).
## 4. Reading rules and limits
The shape cannot be known at the entry (it is the final profile), so any difference is an UPPER BOUND on what knowing the shape would give; 16 days, trades heavily clustered on a few days (2026-10-19..10-23 hold 268 of 340 rows), so a day-level result is the honest unit and nothing can be decisive; the days include the Stage 2 live runs and a few operational problems (2026-10-15/16 outage), so P&L is also affected by fills and gating, not only by shape. A pattern is a hypothesis for a pre-registered test on forward days, nothing else.
