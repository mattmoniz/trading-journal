Origin: the user (2026-11-10, live, after a tell short rode a selling climax into the prior-week VAL area): "if it capitulates and holds at a defended level, it's probably worth getting out and re-engaging immediately"; asked whether the re-engagement should be the bounce or the resumption: "test both honestly... not sure what's worth better". ## The one question (decomposed so both readings are scored the same way)
After a live capitulation climax at a defended level, from the climax bar forward, what does each continuation pay? (1) BOUNCE: enter against the move at once. (2) CONTINUE: stay with the move (its pre-cost value is what HOLDING through the climax earns from there: if it is <= 0, exiting at the climax was right). (3) RESUME: enter with the move again after the bounce has played out. "Holds" cannot be known at the climax, so no arm conditions on it (a post-hoc `held` split is descriptive only).

## Definitions (all causal at bar close; real functions, hr-022)
- Series: explore 500V full-session files (RTH and Globex), 2023-03-07..2025-05-16, sessions with 20 prior sessions of both tracks, `v2.continuous` + `v2.feats` at L_ROC = 10, exactly the inputs of the live turn signal (`shadow.evaluate_turn`). Excluded: sealed (> 2025-05-16), the live era (>= 2026-10-12), design dates 11-05, 11-06, 11-09, 11-10 (today's 10:50:14 live event is reproduced as a code check and never counted).
- CLIMAX = an `S1_SPIKE` event of `turn_signal.turn_events` (loose or strict, as the live log) whose extension ext (direction x 240-minute close change / ADR20, current session only) >= the track's rolling cutoff = the 67th percentile of the extensions of the track's Stage-1 events (S1_CLUSTER and S1_SPIKE) from EARLIER sessions only (last 600 events, needs >= 60; earlier events are burn-in and excluded), as live `ext_cut`. Direction s = the spike direction (the move). One climax per episode: a 30-bar lock per track. Windows as live: RTH signal bar END 09:35-(close - 10 min), Globex 18:30-09:25 ET.
- DEFENDED = at the spike bar's END time the extreme of the climax bar (its Low for s = -1, its High for s = +1) lies in the band of a LIVE confirmed swing pivot of the matching type (LOW for a selling climax, HIGH for a buying one) that had a completed prior test: `pivotAt` of `scripts/lib/pivotContext.mjs` (5-minute pivots, width 5, ZigZag 0.25 ATR, confirmed >= 30 min earlier, not replaced or broken), tests >= 1. This is the system's own defended definition (level tags, defended-pivot detectors), not a chart reading.
- Entry: Open of the bar after the climax bar (BOUNCE direction -s, CONTINUE direction +s). RESUME: after the climax, the bounce phase starts at the first bar j' with -s * Z_ROC3[j'] > 0 (ROC3 momentum against the move, Z from `v2.feats` L_ROC = 3) and ends at the first later bar j (within 30 bars of the climax) with -s * Z_ROC3[j] <= 0; entry Open[j+1] in direction +s; no trigger in 30 bars = no RESUME trade. Exit for all arms: 30 bars after the entry bar or the hard flat (RTH close - 5 min, Globex 09:29:50), `shadow.calc_outcome`; net = signed 30-bar change minus 2.0 (net_nostop); the V0 2.5 x EMA10(TR) stop variant is descriptive.
- Roll weeks: entries and exits are inside one session on 500V bars (no stitch); the pivot timeline uses the stitched minute series and excludes roll weeks by its own `buildRollWeekDateSet`; events on repo / detected roll-week dates are dropped in a descriptive sensitivity only. 2023-Q1 minute bars are sparse (about 395 a day): climaxes before 2023-04-01 are excluded (the pivot context needs minute history).

## AMENDMENT A2 (2026-11-10, AFTER the row 42 and row 43 results, declared post-hoc, DESCRIPTIVE, never counted, never in a Holm family; user question: "what if it gets out at the capitulation and then re-enters?")
Estimand: for a position held through a climax, the value of EXITING at the climax (Open of the bar after the climax bar) and RE-ENTERING with the move later, against simply HOLDING to the same end bar. Both end at the same price, so the difference is exactly the price improvement of the re-entry over the exit minus the one extra round trip: benefit = (-dir) x (re-entry open - exit open) - 2.0 per event, dir = the climax direction. Re-entries: RESUME (row 42, after the ROC3 bounce momentum fades), FAIL entry (row 43, on the fail trigger), and CONTINUE (immediate re-entry, benefit = -2.0 by construction, a sanity row). Reported per track with a day-blocked bootstrap CI and the share of events where the re-entry price was better than the exit; groups: all, strict defended (RTH 33 / GBX 14), pivot-live, at-level (row 43). Two price columns (`px_exit`, `px_entry`) were added to the row 42 / 43 builds; both rebuilds reproduce the registered results exactly on the original columns. Look tests 0.
A2 RESULT (descriptive, post-hoc, look 256, `scripts/vacuum_sim/capitulation_exit_reenter.py`, not counted): benefit of exiting at the climax and re-entering vs holding, points per event after the extra 2.0 round trip. Reading: exiting at the capitulation and re-entering never beat holding in this history: the bounce that RESUME waits for brings price back to the exit price on average (no gain to capture), and re-entering on a confirmed fail costs the move already made.
