At most one open replay position per direction at a time. Entry = close of bar t (the live entry is the intrabar price; using the bar close is the no-lookahead stand-in). Walk from bar t+1: stop at entry -/+ 68, target at entry +/- 250, same-bar tie goes to the stop, a bar that gaps through the stop fills at its open (market-fill rule), exit at the 15:58 bar close otherwise. P&L = points x 2 - 2 dollars; a second column also subtracts 4 dollars (the median real market-entry cost, 33 real fills, mean only 0.2 points, so noisy).
- **Fidelity check against the real fires (reported before anything else is interpreted):** of the real FAILED_SWEEP_REVERSAL rows since 2026-08-11 (primary and sibling, ACTIVE+SHADOW), the share whose direction and minute (within 2 minutes) matches a replay event, and the reverse. If the real-row match rate is below 50 percent the replay is described as a different (looser or tighter) rule and the tightening results are reported with that label.
- **Why it works — decomposition and controls (descriptive, with day-blocked intervals):** outcome mix and mean P&L for stop / target / time-exit; mean time-exit drift; by hour of day; by level. Controls: (1) DIRECTION PLACEBO: the same events and exits with the trade direction flipped (stop and target mirrored), because a 68-point stop with a 250-point target and a session-end exit can produce results from geometry and general drift; (2) NO-SWEEP CONTROL: the same levels and time window, first bar per level and session where price is on the reclaimed side at a distance above the level inside the 10th to 90th percentile of the real events' reclaim distance but NO bar in the last 10 had poked 3 points beyond the level. The setup's real direction counts as "shown to add value beyond the structure" only if its mean beats both controls with the day-blocked interval of the difference above zero.
- **Periods:** explore 2023-03-06 to 2025-05-31; hold-out 2025-06-01 to 2026-10-20 (minus the dropped window); the real live rows (2026-08-11 on) are a fidelity and real-detection check, not independent of the hold-out (same market period).

## Part C — does tighter criteria help (the user's hypothesis: "a little too loose")
Four candidate criteria, each defined point-in-time at the event bar and each fixed before looking at outcomes; cutoffs are the MEDIAN of the feature over EXPLORE events, frozen and applied unchanged to hold-out (no fixed numbers, no tuning):
- **H1 deeper sweep:** depth = (level - lowest low of the last 10 bars) / ATR20 for a long (mirrored for a short). Tight = depth at or above the explore median.
- **H2 decisive reclaim:** (close - level) / ATR20 (mirrored). Tight = at or above the explore median.
- **H3 expansion day (Stage 1 found balance days negative):** developing range from the 09:30 open to the event bar divided by ATR20, ranked against the previous 60 sessions at the same clock bar (the R4 definition used in the 2026-10-20 ML work; needs 40 prior sessions). Tight = rank at or above the explore median.
- **H4 rotation agreement:** the live 65-point rotation leg (`detectRotationLegs` over bars since 18:00 the previous evening, truncated at the event bar) points the same way as the trade.
Per hypothesis: mean P&L per trade tight vs loose (trade-weighted mean in each arm; the interval is a day-blocked bootstrap of the DIFFERENCE, resampling days and pooling trades per arm — not the day-weighted mean), the share of target hits, stop-outs and time exits in each arm, explore and hold-out separately. Rule: KEPT (strong) if the explore difference interval excludes zero on the favourable side AND the hold-out interval does too; KEPT (weak) if explore excludes zero and the hold-out point estimate has the same sign; otherwise REJECTED (stated as not shown). A combined filter (all strong and weak hypotheses together) is then evaluated once on hold-out and on the real live rows, with trades kept, mean P&L, target hits, stop-outs and the day-blocked interval. Home-run metrics (target-hit rate, mean of target hits) are reported next to the mean, because the user cares about the payoff shape, not only the average.
Comparisons: 4 hypotheses x 2 periods, plus the 2 placebo controls and the combined filter; 95 percent intervals. A tightening that keeps too few trades (fewer than 20 hold-out events or 20 distinct hold-out days) is reported as underpowered, not as a verdict.
