## Aim
One shared fill module (used by the roster scoreboard, the learner and the meta-labeler dollar labels) with a REALISTIC band and a STRESS band, applied to any entry, stop and target, so a setup that looks good is good for a 1-MNQ taker on a $2,000-trailing-loss account. ## DeepSeek round H: accepted, rejected (audited)
ACCEPTED
- The per-second tape has no first-print field (low, high, last only), so "slippage = first print after the trigger" cannot be measured from it. Measure slippage and through-distance from the raw `.scid` TICKS on a sample, by session and volatility state; use ticks to resolve a target-versus-stop tie inside a second (house convention for a tie is stop first).
- "Worst print in the second" is a lower bound on the adverse case: real gaps span seconds (35.75-point stop fill). STRESS must be next print at or beyond the trigger, including gaps (cv-030), at the 95th percentile of the measured distribution.
- Calibrate the fill assumptions on EXPLORE tape only (through 2025-05-16); the later window is a confirmatory look of the already-frozen model through `spendSealedLook()`. Queue position cannot be calibrated before 2026-06 (depth era) and the 277 simulator entries are all forward; state this and use structure plus sensitivity (touch, 1 tick, 2 ticks) instead of a fitted queue term.
- The backfill replay is circular if it reuses stored stops and targets: the generator sets stop = p75 MAE and target = p50 MFE on the same touches (cv-022 tautology). Use backfill only for RELATIVE sensitivity (how far P&L moves between bands), never as proof of edge. Re-label TIME_EXPIRED at mark to market (763 measurable expiries average -16.58 dollars, not 0) and re-apply the cv-070 did-it-fill gate to cluster siblings before any replay.
- Decision rule: pass iff REALISTIC EV is positive with a day-blocked CI AND the STRESS worst-case path stays inside the $2,000 trailing-loss budget; STRESS is the account-safety veto, not decoration. Fix every rule number in a pre-registration before any outcome so REALISTIC cannot be tuned optimistic.
- Honest prior: the module moves P&L by a few dollars per trade, below the roughly +/-15 dollars per row resolution of the existing studies. Its value is a risk gauge and a veto, not an edge detector.
REJECTED / CORRECTED
- "Forbidding price improvement is a worst case wearing a realistic costume": wrong for an exchange. A resting limit fills at its price; improvement occurs only when the market gaps through it. The 3 to 4 point average improvement in our records is an artifact of the Sierra simulator filling at the market (priceMultiplier.js classifyFill), which is exactly why the simulator cannot calibrate realism. Keep "at the limit price" for REALISTIC; allow improvement only at a gap.
- "Skip re-resolving the shadow and live rows": keep it, but only after the descriptive characterization, and as a model-P&L-versus-band comparison, not an edge claim.
- Wiring the STRESS veto into the live resolver is a `server/` change (restarts the server and disarms Sim1): a separate decision, not part of this scope.

Roll weeks excluded explicitly. Output tables only.
2. Sensitivity: touch, 1 tick, 2 ticks. Commission added once per round trip, slippage fitted on raw price deltas only.

4. Apply to (a) the hold-or-slice barrier trades (setup-free), (b) the live and shadow rows since 2026-07-09 re-scored by band (model P&L versus band, labelled as such), (c) backfill for relative sensitivity only, after the TIME_EXPIRED and cv-070 fixes.
