# Regime gauge (step 2 of the user's ML plan, 2026-11-02): how busy or quiet will the market be? Shadow, informs, never orders.
User: "start with volatility and regime only ... timing, sizing, and standing down; it doesn't need to know the side." The gauge has two parts; neither predicts direction, neither places or changes an order, neither needs a server restart.
## Part 1: DAY outlook at 09:25 ET (`scripts/regime/regime_lib.py`, `day_outlook.py`, table `regime_outlook`)
A small ridge model of the RTH range in ATR20 units from information known at 09:25 only: prior-day range, overnight range and volume (through 09:24), the pre-open gap, day of week. The first 120 valid days train only; walk-forward refits every 10 days. Buckets QUIET / NORMAL / BUSY are terciles of the walk-forward OUT-OF-SAMPLE predictions of the last 250 days (data-derived, never a fixed number). Output: the expected range in points and relative to the ATR20, the bucket, and plain-English meaning (a typical-range day costs about expected points x $2 per contract end to end).
Evidence it rests on (already recorded): the overnight range predicts the day's RTH range (claims `overnight_rth_range_expansion_control_20261026`, `range_budget_exhaustion_not_shown_20261102`: Spearman 0.398 vs the overnight range alone 0.397); so this part is mainly the overnight range plus a little volume and calendar; honest expectation: modest skill, no direction.
## Part 2: NOW reading (`regime_now.py`, on demand)
Rebuilds today's 500-contract bars from the live tick file (the same builder the nightly tagger uses, about 3 seconds), takes the champion 30-minute forecast (docs/FORECAST_SERVICE_20261102.md) at the last complete bar and shows: expected travel over the next 30 minutes in points, its bucket against the champion's own explore forecast terciles, the RTH range so far against the day outlook ("budget used"), and the data age. Read-only; the live per-minute version in the sidecar needs a restart and is parked (asks first).
## What it is FOR (and not)
For: sizing and standing down (a busy outlook means bigger swings and a larger cushion use; the trailing-floor planning), expectation setting, and judging whether a day is behaving unusually. NOT for: choosing the side, skipping or taking a particular setup (those links were tested and not shown: busy-forecast fades, stop scaling, tell targets and brackets), or any claim of profit. Any use that changes an order is a separate pre-registered step with the user's say-so.
## How it is checked (no retuning on the fly)
Nightly: `regime_calibration.py` fills the realized range into `regime_outlook` and prints COUNTS only until 30 realized outlooks exist; after that it prints the calibration (mean realized range by bucket, bucket hit shares) as a monitor, not as a trigger. The model is refit on a calendar (every complete day, ridge, no selection step), never on a result. The decay of the gauge is judged by the same rolling-20-day logic as the forecast service.
Historical check (walk-forward, descriptive, one look in the ledger, family `regime_gauge_20261102`): `regime_report.py` prints out-of-sample Spearman against the baselines (overnight range alone, prior-day range, clock-free) and the realized range by bucket.

Pre-registered in `docs/REGIME_RISK_TEST_PREREG_20261102.md`, claim `regime_risk_test_20261102`.
