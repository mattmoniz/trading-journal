## Trade (every number data-derived, none tuned on outcomes)
Entry = open of the first one-minute bar after the firing bar closes. Stop = `0.5 x H4` beyond the run's extreme on the defended side (H4 = mean four-hour bar range of the 20 prior trade days, the same stop scale for every bar size). Time exit = the close of the bar 2,879 minutes after entry (lenient at gaps). Realistic fills (a stop already beyond the market fills at that bar's open, stop before target within a bar), cost 1.5 points, one MNQ contract. Risk `R` = entry-to-stop distance.

## Targets (causal: every price used was known before the event)
- **POC target (user: "shot down at the POC"):** the nearest POC line beyond the entry in the trade's direction and at least `1 R` away, among the tick-volume period lines of that trade date (prior-day, prior-week, prior-month, current 3-month block and previous block POCs; `volume_at_price_daily`, all sessions, through the prior close; `scripts/build_defended_level_events_20261026.mjs`'s line set). Events with no such line (including dates without tick coverage, 2025-05..2026-04 partly) are counted and excluded from this arm.
- **LVN targets (user: "deepest LVN" or "the next possible LVN"):** profile = the composite all-session volume-at-price of the last 20 trade dates before the event's trade date (`volume_at_price_daily`, at least 17 of 20 dates present, causal), binned at `0.1 x H4` (rounded to 0.25), smoothed with a 3-bin moving average. VALLEY = a strict local minimum of the smoothed volume whose volume is at most 0.7 of the lower of its two neighbouring local maxima (a real node, not noise). A valley is ELIGIBLE if it lies beyond the entry in the trade's direction, at least `1 R` away and no more than `6 R` away. **DEEPEST LVN target** = the eligible valley with the lowest smoothed volume (price = its bin centre). **NEXT LVN target** = the eligible valley nearest to the entry. Events with no eligible valley are counted and excluded from that arm. Descriptive only: near-edge/far-edge of the valley, the Globex-only composite, and how often the chosen LVN lies within `0.5 x H4` of a named POC line (the user's "may coincide with the 3-month POC").

## Controls
EXCESS = event net points minus the mean of up to 30 clock-matched blind same-direction entries (same session clock within 30 minutes, other dates, the event's own partition) run through the IDENTICAL stop and target distances in points and the identical exit. EXCESS-FRESH = minus up to 30 fresh-extreme birth events (`k1Events`, no sequential defense) at the same clock, same exit. Printed: mirrored-direction placebo (same events traded the other way, same exit); the same events under a fixed 3R target (the earlier test's exit) as a descriptive reference only (it is NOT distance-matched to the named targets; the blind-entry control with identical point distances is the distance-matched control).

Explore = earlier 60 percent of event dates, hold-out = later 40 percent (a SCREEN: this history has been used all night); an explore event whose exit window crosses the cut is dropped from both. ## Amendment 1 (2026-10-26 ~23:00 ET, before any outcome and before the DeepSeek review) — the LVN definition now follows Sierra Chart's own
The user reads Sierra, and Sierra's Volume by Price study defines a Valley (its low volume node) simply: a volume bar with greater volume at each of the N bars above it and greater-or-equal volume at each of the N bars below it, N = the "Peaks and Valleys Sensitivity" setting (profile edges excluded unless "Include High/Low in Peaks and Valleys" is Yes; peaks and valleys always from total volume) ([docs](https://www.sierrachart.com/index.php?page=doc%2FVolumeByPrice.php); the page states no benefit or trading interpretation, it only defines and draws them). My definition above (3-bin smoothing, depth at most 0.7 of the lower neighbouring peak) is replaced by Sierra's: **VALLEY = a bin whose raw volume is smaller than every one of the N=3 bins above it and no larger than every one of the N=3 bins below it, edges of the composite excluded**, bins of `0.1 x H4`, composite = the last 20 trade dates, all sessions, causal as before. N=3 (a +/-0.3 x H4 window) is my assumption; the user's own sensitivity and ticks-per-bar settings are unknown and will be asked; a different N or the earlier smoothed/depth rule is a sensitivity, not run in this pass. DEEPEST = the eligible valley with the lowest raw volume; NEXT = the eligible valley nearest the entry; eligibility (1R to 6R beyond the entry in the trade's direction) unchanged. Nothing else changes.
