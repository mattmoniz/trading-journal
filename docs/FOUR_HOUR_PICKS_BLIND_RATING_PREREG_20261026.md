# Pre-registration: the user's blind picks on four-hour defended levels — does selection by eye add anything? — 2026-10-26

Written BEFORE any rating exists and before any outcome is looked at. Origin: user, 2026-10-26: "you're not selecting the right set ups after a large move day?" — the earlier four-hour-level study (`docs/FOUR_HOUR_DEFENDED_LEVEL_PREREG_20261026.md`, result: 0 of 4 cells, `four_hour_defended_level_not_shown_20261026`) averaged EVERY mechanically defined level (154 floors, 275 tops with K>=3), so a trained eye that picks the clear ones is diluted. This test asks the question directly: among the levels the detector flags, do the ones the user would take do better than the ones the user would step aside from, and than the unrated rest? It is the same kind of test as the Fade Rating Pilot (`fade_rating_pilot_take_vs_aside_not_shown_20261025`, 40 moments, roster fades, no higher-timeframe context), with three differences: the chart is the FOUR-HOUR chart (5 trading days of context, the scale the user reads), floors AND tops, and the outcome is the exit-free move plus the moonshot exit (`docs/FOUR_HOUR_MOONSHOT_EXIT_PREREG_20261026.md`).

## Population and sample (outcome never used to choose)
Events = first `K >= 3` event per level at band `0.25 x H4`, floors (long) and tops (short), from the same code as the four-hour study (`scripts/lib/fourHourLevels.mjs`), NQ 1-minute bars 2023-03-06 .. last complete date minus 3 days, same exclusions (roll weeks, 2025-09-28..11-19, bars < 60 minutes), plus the requirement that the +1,440-minute exit exists. A seeded random sample of **60 events (30 floors, 30 tops)**, drawn uniformly across the whole history (seed fixed in the script), shown in seeded random order so ANY prefix the user completes is itself a random sample. The remaining events (about 370) are the UNSEEN set. The user may stop at any point; only rated events are analysed, and the number rated is stated.

## What the user sees (outcome hidden, date hidden)
The 30 four-hour bars (5 trading days) ending at the bar whose close confirms `K = 3`; the level as a line; the defended bars marked (birth bar and the two retests); price shown as points above/below the level (so the era cannot be read from the price) and bar clock labels (18:00, 22:00, ...) without dates; a volume bar under each candle with the same-slot 20-day average as a reference line (the one metric the user chose: volume vs normal); direction stated (FLOOR long / TOP short); the entry is "the next open". Nothing about the outcome, the exit, the stop or the target is on the page. Controls on the page: TAKE / STEP ASIDE / UNSURE, five tick-box cues (distinct level defended several times; volume built or heavy at the level; big drop/rally into it; multi-day picture; something else), a note. Ratings are stored in the artifact's own database (`ratings` collection).

## Outcomes (kept out of the page; scratch/picks_20261026/outcomes.json)
For each event: (a) exit-free +1,440-minute forward return from the next open, in D20 units, and its EXCESS over the clock-matched blind same-direction control (same definition as the four-hour study); (b) the moonshot-exit P&L from the companion pre-registration (primary exit: stop 0.5 x D20 below/above entry, no target, 1,440-minute time exit, gap-through fills at the open, 1.5 points cost) and its excess over the same exit applied to clock-matched blind entries. Per-event features computed from information available at confirmation only (DROP = the move into the level over the 12 bars before birth in D20 units; relative volume at the defended bars; band tightness).

## Tests
Primary (one counted cell): **mean excess outcome (a) of TAKE-rated minus STEP-ASIDE-rated events**, bootstrap 95 percent CI resampling dates, and a permutation test on the call labels; UNSURE events are reported separately and excluded from the contrast. Read with the smallest detectable effect printed from the bootstrap half-width. The statement made before looking: with about 60 events the half-width will be roughly 0.15-0.25 D20 (about 55-90 points), larger than the +0.04..+0.07 D20 average edge seen for all events, so ONLY a large selection effect can be detected; a null means "could not be shown at this size", not "the eye adds nothing".
Secondary (descriptive, no verdict, no extra counted cells): the same contrast on outcome (b); TAKE-rated vs the UNSEEN events' mean (the selection-versus-population view); floors and tops separately; which cues co-occur with TAKE; the correlation of the DROP feature (the user's "large move day") and of relative volume with TAKE and with outcome across ALL events (rated and unrated); and, only if the TAKE-minus-ASIDE contrast has a point estimate above zero, a mechanical rule built from the cues the user ticked most (a simple threshold on DROP and relative volume derived from the rated set) applied to the UNSEEN events as an out-of-sample check, printed with its own CI and labelled a lead.

## Labelling and limits (stated in advance)
A positive result is a CANDIDATE lead for a selection rule, never a confirmation: the user's eyes and the history are the same one history mined all day, the user has seen four-hour charts of this market since before the test, and may recognise famous days despite hidden dates. 60 events is small; floors and tops split it again. The analysis runs ONCE on whatever number is rated at that moment (no daily peeking, no re-running as more ratings arrive: a second look needs a new pre-registration with a new random sample). Ratings of events the user has recognised as a specific day are flagged in the note if they say so and reported with and without.
